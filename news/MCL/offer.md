Index: [../MCL.md](../MCL.md).

## Summary
Offer for sale only by Coal India: up to 661,836,300 shares (10%), face value Rs 2, no money to MCL.
Band Rs 129-136, lot 110, employee discount Rs 6 (confirmed by the lead managers' pre-offer advert 2026-11-17); no shareholder or retail discount.
Coal India holders on 2026-11-13 get a quota of up to 66,183,630 shares; the user holds no COALINDIA, so it does not apply.

## Settled facts (RHP)
- Face value: Rs 2 per share. RHP cover: "THE FACE VALUE OF THE EQUITY SHARE IS Rs 2 EACH. THE OFFER PRICE IS [.] TIMES THE FACE VALUE"
  (cover, RHP p. 1 of the PDF and again on the second cover page). Shares were split from Rs 1,000 to Rs 2 by board resolution
  2026-08-22 and shareholder resolution 2026-08-27: "13,236,726 equity shares of Rs 1,000 each into 6,618,363,000 equity shares of Rs 2
  each" (RHP p. 123-124, notes to the KPI and peer tables). Coal India's DRHP letter to NSE/BSE (2026-09-02) also says "face value of
  INR 2 each". No Rs 10 face value appears anywhere.
- Offer type: offer for sale only. RHP cover table: "TYPE OF OFFER: Offer for Sale; FRESH ISSUE SIZE: Not applicable; OFFER FOR SALE SIZE:
  Up to 661,836,300 Equity Shares of face value of Rs 2 each". Selling shareholder: Coal India Ltd (promoter), weighted average cost of
  acquisition Rs 0.26 per share (split-adjusted). Risk factor 55: "We will not receive any proceeds from the Offer for Sale."
  661,836,300 / 6,618,363,000 = 10.00% of the share capital.
- Promoters: President of India (through the Ministry of Coal) and Coal India Ltd. MCL is now wholly owned by Coal India (RHP risk 5).

## Effect on Coal India (COALINDIA) holders
- Shareholder quota: up to 66,183,630 shares (10% of the offer) for "Individuals and HUFs who are public equity shareholders of our
  Corporate Promoter ... as on the date of this Red Herring Prospectus" (RHP p. 6), i.e. holders on 2026-11-13. Buying COALINDIA now
  does not qualify. Bid up to Rs 2 lakh in this quota; such holders may also bid in the retail or non-institutional portion and
  these are not treated as multiple bids. PAN must be updated with Coal India's register and a demat account is needed
  (RHP p. 585). The user holds no COALINDIA shares directly (portfolio/ has no Coal India entry), so the quota does not apply.
- Coal India gets the offer money (after expenses and tax) and keeps 90% of MCL. COALINDIA closes: Rs 403.50 on 2026-11-13, +0.9%
  in a week (Rs 400.00 on 11-06), +1.3% in a month (Rs 398.24 on 10-13) (yfinance, fetched 2026-11-15).
