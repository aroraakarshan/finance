## Discrepancies
- Grey market 11-17: Rs 4.60 (Livemint 11-17) vs Rs 3 (CNBC-TV18 11-17 07:31); 11-18 Rs 3.75 (Investorgain via ETV Bharat). All unofficial.
- MCL is not on NSE's forthcoming-issues list (11-17 about 18:30 IST) although the lead managers' advert gives an opening date of 11-19.
- Face value: India IPO (indiaipo.in) gives Rs 10; Business Standard, Livemint, Coal India's DRHP letter and the RHP give Rs 2. Settled:
  Rs 2 (RHP cover; split from Rs 1,000 on 2026-08-22/27). India IPO is wrong.
- Fresh issue: India IPO labels it "Fresh Issue + Offer For Sale"; the RHP cover says fresh issue "Not applicable". Settled: offer for
  sale only, 661,836,300 shares by Coal India, no money to MCL.
- Allotment date: India IPO gives basis of allotment 11-27; RHP gives on or about 11-25 (listing 11-27).
- FY25 revenue: scout/feed.md quotes m.Stock Rs 28,637.92 crore; RHP revenue from operations is Rs 31,353.02 crore (m.Stock's figure
  may be a different line, not verified). FY25 PAT matches closely (m.Stock 10,823.26 vs RHP attributable 10,823.58).
- DRHP date: m.Stock says filed 2026-08-31; Coal India's letter says DRHP dated 2026-09-01, disclosed 2026-09-02.
- Profit FY26: Moneycontrol Rs 10,698.1 crore (total PAT) vs Livemint Rs 10,677.74 crore (attributable to owners) - both match the RHP, different lines.
- Size vs valuation (resolved by the band): pre-band press sizes of Rs 9,500-10,000 crore implied Rs 95,000-1,00,000 crore for 100%;
  at the band the offer is Rs 8,538-9,001 crore and the company Rs 85,377-90,010 crore, i.e. the earlier issue-size estimates were high.
- Price band source: settled 2026-11-17 by the lead managers' pre-offer advert (Axis Capital, BOB Caps); it matches ET, Livemint,
  Chittorgarh and d-asia (Rs 129-136, lot 110, employee discount Rs 6). Still not on NSE/BSE as of about 18:30 IST 11-17.
- Employee discount: the brief from d-asia reported no employee/shareholder discount; ET and Chittorgarh report Rs 6 per share for
  employees. The RHP has no shareholder discount at all, so "no shareholder discount" is right; the employee discount is Rs 6 per two
  sources (advert not read).
- Issue size Rs 8,981.12 crore (ET, Chittorgarh) vs 661,836,300 x Rs 136 = Rs 9,000.97 crore: the gap Rs 19.85 crore = 33,091,815
  employee shares x Rs 6 discount, so Rs 8,981 crore is net of a fully used employee discount (computed).
- FY26 EPS: Chittorgarh 16.16 (total PAT / shares) vs RHP 16.13 (attributable to owners); P/E 8.42 vs 8.43 at Rs 136.
- Stake: Coal India's board approved selling up to 25% each in SECL and MCL (PSU Watch, 2026-03-23, headline only); this IPO sells 10%.
  More MCL sales later are possible (not verified).
- SEBI website: MCL's RHP was not yet in SEBI's "Red Herring Documents filed with ROC" list on 2026-11-15 (latest entry Crystal Crop,
  2026-11-12); the company site and Coal India's NSE letter have it.
