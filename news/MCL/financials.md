# MCL - Mahanadi Coalfields Ltd: financials, valuation and risks

Index: [../MCL.md](../MCL.md).

## Summary
Revenue and PAT fell FY24-FY26 (CAGR -1.73% / -4.95%); FY26 profit helped by one-offs; H1 FY27 up 9.3% / 6.7% y/y.
Dividends Rs 8,400-8,750 crore a year (Rs 12.69-13.22 per share); FY26 yield 9.33-9.84% at the band.
At the band 8.00-8.43x FY26 EPS vs Coal India 7.45x trailing: a cash-yield case, not growth.

## Valuation vs Coal India
At the band (details in "Price band" in offer.md): MCL 8.00-8.43x FY26 EPS (7.78-8.20x trailing 12 months), market cap Rs 85,377-90,010
crore, FY26 dividend yield 9.33-9.84%. Coal India: trailing P/E 7.45 at Rs 403.50 (2026-11-13; yfinance TZ=UTC, fetched 2026-11-16),
8.05 in the RHP peer table (Rs 406.30 on 2026-11-11, p. 123); 12-month dividends Rs 26.50 = 6.57% yield. ET quotes an industry peer
average P/E of 11.57x (FY26), which includes the foreign peers in the RHP table (Yancoal 18.34, Bukit Asam 11.40). So MCL is priced
about 13% above Coal India's trailing P/E with a higher payout and yield; MCL's 3-year earnings trend is down (see Sentiment in ../MCL.md).
The pre-band press estimates (Rs 9,500-10,000 crore issue, "up to $10 billion" valuation) are replaced by the band: Rs 136 x 10% =
Rs 9,000.97 crore; market cap Rs 90,009.74 crore matches the top of Livemint's Rs 83,000-90,000 crore.
