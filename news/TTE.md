## Note for the thesis
- The screen's EPS +123% matches Q3 diluted EPS (3.66 vs 1.64), and revenue growth is +37% in Q3 (screen +41%), but both come from war-driven prices (Brent Q3 avg USD 97 vs 69; refining margin 4x). This is a cyclical peak signal, not structural growth.
- Since results, Brent fell 18.5% and the IEA sees a surplus, which points to lower Q4/Q1 earnings; dividend and buyback are covered at gearing 9.1%.
- Low P/E (about 9-10.5) reflects peak earnings; use normalized earnings (yfinance reported EPS Q1 2026 USD 2.45, Q2 USD 2.68, Q3 USD 4.03) when checking value.
