## Note for the thesis
- Supports "margin-cycle peak earnings": Q2 GRM USD 20.41 was more than double a year earlier, flattered by USD 3.82 inventory gains; Q1 GRM was only about USD 8.8; Brent has since fallen about 18% from its 10-30 high (117.64 -> 95.84); an analyst expects GRMs to moderate; windfall taxes cap export gains. A P/E of 3.7 on these earnings is a peak-cycle multiple.
- Against: utilisation stays near 100%, Q3 has no turnaround so volumes may recover, and Hormuz disruption is still unresolved (Brent back near USD 96 on 11-13). The stock moves 6-15% a day on crude news.

## Discrepancies
- Q2 profit: headlines say Rs 2,271 crore (standalone) or Rs 2,247 crore (consolidated); both are right for their basis.
- Press release says consolidated H1 FY26 PAT Rs 694 crore; the audited consolidated statement shows PAT Rs 679.27 crore (Rs 694.02 crore is total comprehensive income).
- Multibagg AI (2026-11-05) gives June-quarter revenue Rs 27,370 crore and Brent "towards $76"; audited Q1 revenue is Rs 29,358.75 crore and yfinance Brent was 94.62-98.29 on 11-04/05. That source is unreliable.
- Oct 7 close: Business Today Rs 1,611.70 (BSE); yfinance NSE Rs 1,612.30.
- ROE: scout 51% (Yahoo TTM) vs 32% (Screener last year), as the scout already noted.
