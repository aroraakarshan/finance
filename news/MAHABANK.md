## Note for the thesis
- Supports the growth/quality screen: profit +41%, ROE about 27%, GNPA 1.25% and NNPA 0.12% are confirmed by the bank's own filing; government already at 73.6%, so no forced OFS under the 75% rule.
- Risks to the thesis: loan growth (30%) well above deposit growth (21.5%) funded by borrowings, FCNR and a USD bond; falling CASA share; fast agri gold-loan growth in a drought year; an open Rs 7,500 crore equity-raise approval could bring a QIP at a discount. Price is near its 1-year high after a 15% PSU-bank rally in three weeks.

## Discrepancies
- 11-17 high: MarketsMojo reports an intraday 52-week high of Rs 94.7, but yfinance shows the close at Rs 95.99 (the close itself is above 94.7; MarketsMojo was likely written intraday).
- How the Dec 2025 government sale was done: CNBC TV18 (2026-04-20) says "via a QIP"; DIPAM via ANI (2025-12-01) says an offer for sale (5% + 1% green shoe). The OFS version is from the seller and is used here.
- CNBC TV18 (2026-04-20) writes the equity raise as "$7,500 crore"; NDTV Profit headline says Rs 7,500 crore. Rupees assumed.
- Press release table shows provisions change q/q as "(0.03)%"; the figures (1,097 -> 1,059) are -3.5%.
- Deposits: press release gives Rs 3,76,407 crore (global) and Rs 3,75,687 crore in the top-line table (difference = overseas deposits Rs 721 crore, rounding).
- Livemint headline says "biggest weekly surge in two years"; its text and the Google News title say "seven months".
