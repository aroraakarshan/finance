Market: India (NSE GESHIP, BSE 500620). Held in: not held; trading/watchlist.md stage idea since 2026-11-15 (finance-scout, scout/ideas.md 'Stock screens 2026-11-15'). Last update: 2026-11-17.

## Summary
Score +1: Q2 FY27 profit Rs 1,269 crore (+118% y/y) with net cash, a buyback and the 19th straight dividend, but profit fell q/q and tanker rates fell 21-29% q/q.
Scout thesis "cyclical near a peak": the news supports it - Rs 308 crore ship-sale gains flattered Q2 and Nomura models tanker rates -50% in FY28.
Against a quick fall: Q3 started with record tanker rates, the stock is near 0.5x ex-cash NAV, and US-Iran / Hormuz news moves it several percent a day.
Now (11-17): close Rs 1,548.50 (+8.6% in 5 days) is above the Rs 1,530 buyback cap; 49.44 lakh shares bought so far.
Watch: buyback closes 2026-12-11 (not confirmed; cap Rs 1,530); Q3 FY27 results late Jan / early Feb 2027 (not confirmed).

## Note for the thesis
- Supports "cyclical near a peak": Q2 profit fell q/q, tanker rates fell 21-29% q/q in Q2, ship-sale gains (Rs 308 crore) flattered the quarter, the company itself avoids buying at "elevated asset prices", and Nomura models tanker rates -50% in FY28. The scout's EPS +119% is mostly the war-driven rate spike.
- Against a quick reversal: Q3 started with record tanker rates (Nomura), about 40% of Q3 tanker days already covered, large net cash, stock near 0.5x ex-cash NAV, and the buyback. The key swing factor is US-Iran / Hormuz news, which moves the stock several percent a day.

## Discrepancies
- Buyback cap Rs 1,530 vs close Rs 1,548.50 on 11-17: an on-market buyback cannot buy above its cap, so purchases may pause while the price stays above it (inference; not confirmed by the company).
- Q2 consolidated revenue: Business Upturn/FE/CNBC Rs 1,824 crore (revenue from operations) vs presentation "Operating Revenue" Rs 1,816 crore and "Revenue incl. other income" Rs 2,234 crore. EBITDA: Business Upturn Rs 1,091 crore (59.8% margin) vs company reported Rs 1,566 crore (incl. other income) and industry-format Rs 1,508 crore. Different definitions; use the company figures.
- ROE: scout used 26% (Yahoo TTM); company normalised Q2 ROE 26%, H1 28%; Screener/FE 15.9% (last full year).
- FE says shares fell 3% on 2026-11-09 to Rs 1,425.5; yfinance close 1,426.00 vs 1,428.70 on 11-06 (-0.2%). The 3% was likely intraday; FE's "-3%" not confirmed by closes.
