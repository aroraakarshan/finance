## Note for the thesis
- Supports "cyclical possibly near a peak": DRAM contract price growth is slowing each quarter (80% -> 40% -> 20%), NAND has stalled, consumer spot is below contract, CXMT is advancing, and the stock is 38% below its high despite record guidance - the market is pricing a peak in margins (86% gross margin guided).
- Undercuts it in the near term: contract prices are still rising in 4Q26, HBM prices are forecast to roughly double in 2027 under long-term agreements, and capacity is reported sold out through 2027-2028. The scout's P/E 10.5 is on peak-cycle trailing EPS; Yahoo forward P/E is 3.7, typical of a market that expects earnings to fall.
- Event risk is high: the stock moved 5% or more on many days since October; Nvidia results (11-17) and the December results are the next tests.
