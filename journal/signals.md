# Signals

## Summary
No closed trades yet, so no scores. Each closed paper or real trade adds a row below; once a
month the hit rate and average result per signal, strategy and source are scored here and
sent to the other sessions. Nothing for you to do yet.

## How it works
Which signals, strategies and sources worked. One row per closed trade in `trades.csv`;
monthly scores below, sent to `finance-scout`, `finance-news`, `finance-research` and
`finance-trader`.
