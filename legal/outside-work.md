# Outside work, employer rules and IP (legal view)

## Summary
- No Indian law bars a private employee from paid side work; your employment agreement and Microsoft's Outside Work and Conflicts of Interest policies decide. Microsoft's public Trust Code says: disclose conflicts and get advice or approval, and check outside work against the agreements you signed.
- Topmate paid calls (incl. RedHawk-SC): accepted by user 2026-11-15 (no declaration, no pause); see [Accepted risk](#accepted-risk-topmate).
- Do now: read your agreement (IP assignment, exclusivity, confidentiality) before a course or open-source release; those still need the internal approval process.
- IP: work made in the course of employment belongs to Microsoft by law (Copyright Act s.17(c)); work made on your own time, kit and knowledge is yours unless the signed clause assigns it. Patents follow the contract only.
- Your public site says "Microsoft, Bengaluru": the Karnataka shops act then applies, not Telangana's; it has no double-employment rule.
