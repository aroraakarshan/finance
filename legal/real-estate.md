# Real estate in Bengaluru: title checks, RERA, bank auctions, your rent

## Summary
- Your rent (Rs 33,000/month, no written agreement) is the only live item: get a written 11-month agreement on e-stamp paper, signed by both, with the deposit amount and refund terms, and keep the UPI trail as proof ([section 4](#4-your-rent-without-a-written-agreement)).
- If you buy (income says keep renting: [income/real-estate.md](../income/real-estate.md)): A-khata + e-khata, 30-year EC, OC, approved plan and RERA number, checked by a lawyer before any advance ([checklist](#1-title-checks-for-a-bengaluru-flat)).

## 1. Title checks for a Bengaluru flat

| Document | What it proves | Where to get it | Red flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sale deed of seller and mother deed / title chain (30+ years) | Seller owns it; unbroken chain of owners | Seller; certified copies from the sub-registrar (Kaveri 2.0) | Gaps in the chain, GPA sales, missing heirs' consent, unregistered documents |
| Encumbrance certificate (EC), 30 years | Registered sales, mortgages, court attachments | Kaveri 2.0 online (kaveri.karnataka.gov.in) or sub-registrar | Open mortgage; EC shows only registered deals (not oral mortgages, pending suits): a nil EC is not a clean title |
| Khata: A-khata and e-khata (e-Aasthi) | Property is in the civic register; A = built per law; B = tax only, not compliant | GBA e-Aasthi portal / ward office | B-khata (no loans, harder resale, limited approvals); name or area differs from deed |
| Property tax receipts | Tax paid, owner in civic records | GBA / e-Aasthi | Arrears (the buyer may inherit them) |
| Land conversion order (KLR Act s.95) or deemed conversion in plan approval | Agricultural land changed to residential | Deputy Commissioner; Bhoomi records | No conversion for a layout outside the master plan; RTC still shows farm land |
| Approved building plan and layout plan | Building approved (GBA/BDA/BMRDA) | Builder; planning authority | Floors or area beyond the plan; plan for a different survey number |
| Commencement, completion and occupancy certificate (OC) | Built per plan, fit to live in | Builder; GBA | No OC (utilities may be refused; deviations); OC only for some towers |
| RERA registration (projects over 500 sq m or 8 units) | Project registered; plans, approvals and dates filed | rera.karnataka.gov.in | Not registered; registration lapsed; complaints and recovery orders against the promoter |
| Joint development and power of attorney (builder deals) | Builder's right to sell your unit | Builder | Unit not in the builder's share; landowner disputes |
| Society / association NOC, dues, utility bills | No dues for maintenance, water, power | Association, BESCOM, BWSSB | Unpaid dues, no association formed |
| Bank NOC or loan closure (resale) | Seller's loan paid off | Seller's bank | Original deeds still with a bank |

2024-2026 changes in Karnataka:

| Change | What it means for a buyer | Source, date |
|---|---|---|
| e-khata linked to Kaveri 2.0 registration from 1 Oct 2024 in Bengaluru | Sub-registrars check the e-khata before registering; GBA says illegal-layout registrations fell to almost zero. ## 2. Pre-launch bookings and RERA

| Rule | Text (RERA Act 2016, indiankanoon.org/doc/65068227, read) |
|---|---|
| s.3(1) | No promoter shall advertise, market, book, sell or offer for sale any unit in a project in a planning area without registering it with RERA |
| s.3(2)(a) | No registration needed if the land is at most 500 sq m or at most 8 apartments: small projects sit outside RERA, so a "pre-launch" there has no RERA cover at all |
| s.13(1) | Promoter may not take more than 10% of the cost as advance or application fee before a written, registered agreement for sale |
| s.18(1) | Late or no possession: on demand, refund of all money with interest at the prescribed rate plus compensation; if you stay, interest for every month of delay |
| s.19(4) | Allottee's right to refund with interest and compensation on default |
| s.40(1) | Interest, penalty or compensation not paid is recovered "as an arrears of land revenue" (by the Deputy Commissioner through a revenue recovery certificate, RRC) |
| s.59 | Promoter breaking s.3: penalty up to 10% of project cost; continued breach up to 3 years jail |

What happens to pre-launch money: the booking itself breaks s.3 and usually s.13, so there is
no registered agreement and often no project. You can still complain (RERA s.31, consumer
commission, civil suit, police for cheating), but whether K-RERA hears a complaint on an
unregistered project is a lawyer question (R5). Recovery is weak even with an order:

| K-RERA recovery (FPCE data, 1 Nov 2026) | Count | Rs Cr |
|---|---|---|
| RRCs issued | 2,694 | 1,253.11 |
| RRCs recovered | 356 (13.2%) | 129.03 (10.3%) |
| Issued 23 Jan - 31 Oct 2026 / recovered | 369 / 74 | 171.27 / 18.76 |
| Projects registered / lapsed / applied for completion | 8,984 / 3,147 / 3,580 | - |

Source: The Hindu, 4 Nov 2026 (FPCE data). Karnataka announced a digital RRC tracking system
for about Rs 1,090 Cr of dues (MSN, 29 Aug 2026, nv). Court rulings: Newtech Promoters (SC, 11 Nov
2021) on the refund right (not read, nv).

## 3. Bank auctions (SARFAESI)

| Step | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bank takes possession | s.13(4) after 60-day demand notice s.13(2) | SARFAESI Act 2002 (nv) |
| Sale notice | At least 30 days before the sale (r.8(6), r.9(1)) | Security Interest (Enforcement) Rules 2002 (nv; widely quoted) |
| Borrower's right to redeem | Ends when the auction notice is published, unless full dues were paid before | Celir LLP v Bafna Motors, SC 21 Sep 2023 (indiankanoon.org/doc/149474401, read para 88) |
| 25% deposit | "Immediately, i.e., on the same day or not later than next working day", including the EMD; else resale | r.9(3) as quoted in AP HC, Pappula Masenu Venkanna, 21 Sep 2026 (indiankanoon.org/doc/4957650) |
| Balance 75% | Within 15 days of confirmation of sale, or up to 3 months if agreed in writing with the bank | r.9(4); same, and P&H HC, Tarsem Lal, 11 Aug 2026 (doc/68608805) |
| Default | Deposit forfeited to the bank (r.9(5)) | same |
| Sale certificate | Bank issues it on full payment; register it or file a copy (Registration Act s.89(4)); stamp duty on it | Practice and rule nv (R9) |

Risks the buyer carries:

| Risk | What the law says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "As is where is, as is what is" | You take the property with all defects and unpaid dues the notice does not list: property tax, association dues, power and water arrears | Bank sale terms (nv) |
| Tenants | A tenant protected by a Rent Act cannot be evicted through SARFAESI (Vishal N Kalsaria, SC 20 Jan 2016). A tenancy over 1 year needs a registered lease to resist the bank (Bajarang Shyamsunder Agarwal, SC 11 Sep 2019). Since 1 Sep 2016 the DRT decides tenant claims (s.17(4A)); PNB Housing v Manoj Saha (SC 15 Jul 2025): unregistered tenancy claim goes to the DRT, not the High Court | indiankanoon.org/doc/176039345, /146571650, /4697589 (read in part) |
| Borrower challenge | Borrower can go to the DRT under s.17 (45 days); the sale may be stayed or set aside | s.17 (nv) |
| Possession | Bank may hand over only symbolic possession; physical possession needs a DM/CMM order (s.14) | s.14 (nv) |
| Loans | Banks rarely lend in 15 days: have the money ready | income note |

Checklist before bidding: read the sale notice (dues, encumbrances, possession type); get the
EC, khata and tax arrears yourself; visit and see who lives there; ask the bank in writing about
DRT cases (s.17) and possession; lawyer checks title as in section 1; budget dues, stamp duty
and 3-6 months for possession.

## 4. Your rent without a written agreement

| Point | Law | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Is the Karnataka Rent Act 1999 relevant? | Probably not: it does not apply to premises whose deemed or standard rent exceeds Rs 3,500/month in Part A areas (s.2(3)(e)) or to buildings within 15 years of completion (s.2(3)(f)). At Rs 33,000 your tenancy is most likely outside it (R10) | indiankanoon.org/doc/103116945 (read) |
| If it applied | s.4: tenancy only by written agreement; s.16: rent receipt on demand, landlord fined up to double the rent if refused | same |
| Rent Act amendment 2025 | Bill passed Dec 2025 (fines up to Rs 50,000, decriminalised, Controller as adjudicator); Governor's assent and notification not confirmed | Bangalore Mirror 29 Dec 2025; TOI 17 Dec 2025 (nv) |
| Model Tenancy Act 2021 | Not adopted by Karnataka (state "open" to it, Jan 2026) | New Indian Express 5 Jan 2026 (nv) |
| Without a written lease | Oral lease with possession is valid; for housing it is month to month, ended by either side with 15 days' written notice (TPA s.106) | indiankanoon.org/doc/80042 (read) |
| Registration | Compulsory for leases from year to year, over one year, or reserving a yearly rent (Registration Act s.17(1)(d); TPA s.107). Hence the usual 11-month agreement | indiankanoon.org/doc/561156 (read) |
| Stamp duty | Karnataka Stamp Act, Article 30 (lease); rate not verified (R11). Use e-stamp paper | nv |
| Tax | New regime: no HRA, so landlord PAN and agreement have no tax use; no TDS on rent below Rs 50,000/month | [regime-and-rent.md](../tax/FY2026-27/regime-and-rent.md) (finance-tax) |

Risks today: no proof of the deposit or its refund terms; 15 days' notice either way; no fixed
rent, so increases are at will; disputes about repairs and damage; address proof for KYC or
passport is harder. Proof of rent exists: 17 UPI payments of Rs 33,000 to one SBI account since
Jul 2025 ([cost-cuts.md](../income/cost-cuts.md), D3).

| Do | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | 11-month agreement on e-stamp paper, signed by both, two witnesses: names, address, rent, due date, deposit amount, refund within a set time less listed deductions, notice period (1-2 months), lock-in if any, increase on renewal, repairs, inventory |
| 2 | Pay by bank or UPI only (already so); ask for a signed yearly rent receipt |
| 3 | Deposit: get a written acknowledgement now for what you already paid (amount needed from you) |
| 4 | Longer stay wanted: a registered lease (over 11 months) fixes rent and term, at higher stamp duty and a visit to the sub-registrar |
