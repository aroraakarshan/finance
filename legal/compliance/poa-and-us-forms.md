## Powers of attorney

- Under Indian law an agency ends when the principal dies or "becomes of unsound mind"
  (Indian Contract Act s.201) [C1]. So an ordinary Indian POA stops working exactly when it is
  needed for incapacity; India has no general "durable" POA (Mental Healthcare Act 2017 advance
  directives cover treatment only; not verified).
- Indian POA: written, stamped as per state Stamp Act; registered when it covers immovable
  property (Registration Act; not verified). Banks and depositories have their own POA formats.
- Foreign brokers: IBKR (trading authorisation / advisor-agent forms), Fidelity (its own POA
  form), Alpaca/INDmoney: not checked. A US POA for a non-US person may need notarisation and
  apostille (not verified). Death: POAs end; heirs use transmission
  ([India: transmission](../estate/india-rules.md#transmission-of-securities-sebi); [foreign brokers](../estate/foreign-assets.md#us-estate-tax-and-brokers)).
- Gap: no POA or authorised person recorded for any account. [Question](../questions.md) C9.

## US side (one item)

- Form W-8BEN stays valid from signing to the last day of the third following calendar year,
  unless facts change [U1]. Treaty rate 25% on dividends needs a valid form at each broker
  (`tax/investment-types.md`). Dates on file at IBKR, Fidelity and Alpaca: unknown (user to check).
