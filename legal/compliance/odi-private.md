# Startup and business stakes abroad (FEMA ODI)

## Summary
- Indian startups (iSAFE/CCPS): domestic, no FEMA overseas rules. Foreign startup shares (e.g. a Delaware C-corp via Wefunder/Republic) are ODI for an individual at any stake size (unlisted equity), allowed only in an operating, non-financial-services company, from own funds, via one AD bank with Form FC and a UIN before paying.
- A SAFE / Crowd SAFE is probably not "equity capital" (it is not compulsorily convertible: it converts only on a priced round or exit), so it fits neither ODI nor a clear OPI route: grey, ask the AD bank before any SAFE (O1).
- Paying a foreign platform directly by card does not fit: OI payments must go through banking channels (reg 8) with Form FC/UIN.
- Buying a foreign SaaS company's shares with control is ODI (APR yearly, CA-certified); buying only its assets (code, domain) is not on the LRS list of capital account transactions: grey, likely needs RBI approval (O4).
- Income and sale money from ODI must come back within 90 days (reg 9(4)), not 180.

## Rules

| Rule | Text (short) | Para | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capital account | "a transaction which alters the assets or liabilities ... outside India of persons resident in India" | FEMA s.2(e) | [D1] 1999 text |
| Holding abroad | No resident shall "acquire, hold, own, possess or transfer any foreign exchange, foreign security or any immovable property situated outside India" save as provided | FEMA s.4 | [D1] |
| Equity capital | "equity shares or perpetual capital or instruments that are irredeemable or contribution to non-debt capital of a foreign entity in the nature of fully and compulsorily convertible instruments" | OI Rules r.2(1)(e) | [L5] G.S.R. 646(E), 22 Aug 2022 |
| ODI | "acquisition of unlisted equity capital of a foreign entity", or 10% or more of a listed one, or control | OI Rules r.2(1)(q) | [L5] |
| Control | right to appoint majority of directors or control management/policy, incl. 10% or more of voting rights | OI Rules r.2(1)(c) | [L5] |
| Individuals' ODI | "ODI in an operating foreign entity not engaged in financial services activity and which does not have subsidiary or step down subsidiary where the resident individual has control" | OI Rules Sch. III para 1(2)(i) | [L5] |
| Barred sectors | No ODI in real estate activity, gambling, INR-linked financial products | OI Rules r.19(1) | [L5] |
| Start-ups | ODI in start-ups "recognised under the laws of the host country" only "from own funds" of the individual; AD bank takes a CA certificate that no borrowed funds are used | OI Rules r.19(2); MD para 9 | [L5][L3a] |
| Later control | Individual with ODI without control shall not acquire control if the entity sets up a subsidiary | MD para 22(1) | [L3a] |
| LRS | Capital account list for individuals: foreign currency account, immovable property, ODI and OPI per OI rules, loans to NRI relatives | LRS MD para 6 | [L2] 6 Sep 2024 |
| Payment | "by remittance made through banking channels; from funds held in an account maintained in accordance with the provisions of the Act"; cash not permitted | OI Regulations reg 8; MD para 11 | [L4][L3a] |
| Cards (LRS) | AD bank "may also accept the payment through credit /debit/prepaid card of the card holder" (paying the bank for the remittance) | LRS MD Part B para 8 | [L2] |

## Filings for an individual's ODI

| Step | Rule | Para | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Designated AD bank; all transactions for a UIN through it | Required | OI Regulations reg 9(3); MD para 26 | [L4][L3a] |
| UIN for the foreign entity before remittance or acquisition | Form FC "on or before making initial ODI"; no remittance before UIN | reg 9(2); MD para 16(3) | [L4][L3a] |
| Report the financial commitment | At the time of remittance | reg 10(2) | [L4] |
| Evidence of investment (share certificate etc.) | Within 6 months of remittance | reg 9(1); MD para 16 | [L4][L3a] |
| Annual Performance Report (APR) | By 31 Dec each year; **not required** if holding is under 10% without control and no other commitment | reg 10(4) proviso | [L4] |
| APR certification | By a CA for resident individuals | MD para 17(3) | [L3a] |
| Disinvestment | Report within 30 days of receiving proceeds | reg 10(2) | [L4] |
| Dues and sale proceeds | Realise and repatriate within 90 days | reg 9(4) | [L4] |
| Late filing | Late Submission Fee: Rs 7,500 per return, or 7,500 + 0.025% x amount x years for flow returns (Form FC etc.) | reg 11; MD para 18(2) | [L4][L3a] |

## B2: unlisted startup stakes

| Case | Finding (our reading) |
|---|---|
| (i) Indian startup (iSAFE, CCPS) | Domestic; FEMA overseas rules do not apply. Company law and tax only |
| (ii) Foreign startup shares (priced round, Reg CF equity) | ODI at any size (unlisted equity) [L5 r.2(1)(q)]. Allowed if: operating company, not financial services, no subsidiary where the user has control [Sch. III para 1(2)(i)], not real estate/gambling [r.19(1)], own funds [r.19(2)]. Needs designated AD bank, Form FC + UIN before payment, evidence in 6 months; APR not needed below 10% without control. Counts toward LRS |
| (ii) SAFE / Crowd SAFE | Converts only if a priced round or exit happens; on dissolution it returns money. So it is not "fully and compulsorily convertible" and probably not "equity capital" [r.2(1)(e)]; not on the debt list either [r.5(A)]. No clear ODI or OPI route; an AD bank may refuse Form FC. Grey (O1). Same doubt for convertible notes (debt-like) |
| Platform via AngelList fund/SPV | A US fund or LLC unit is not a "duly regulated" overseas fund in most cases [L3a 1(ix)(e)]; an LLC unit is unlisted equity -> ODI in an entity whose business is investing = financial services: likely barred (O2) |
| Card payment to the platform | Not a permitted mode (reg 8 banking channels; Form FC/UIN first). The LRS card rule only lets you pay your own bank for the remittance (O3) |

## C2: buying a small foreign online business (SaaS)

| Route | Finding (our reading) |
|---|---|
| Buy shares of the foreign company | ODI with control. Allowed for an operating, non-financial company with no subsidiary (Sch. III para 1(2)(i)); Form FC + UIN, APR every year by 31 Dec certified by a CA, dividends and sale proceeds back within 90 days [reg 9(4)]; counts toward LRS |
| Buy only the assets (code, domain, customer list) from abroad | A capital account transaction (assets outside India) [FEMA s.2(e)], but not on the LRS capital list [LRS MD para 6]. Likely needs RBI approval; an Indian proprietorship buying software/IP as an import payment may be a different (current account) route - not verified (O4) |
| Income after purchase | Via a foreign company: dividends within 90 days [reg 9(4)]. Run from India as an Indian business: foreign customer receipts are export proceeds (export rules, not read; O5) |
| Microsoft approval | Side business needs employer approval ([income/business.md](../../income/business.md)); not a FEMA point |

## Discrepancies found
- [income/business.md](../../income/business.md) cites "rbi.org.in MD id=12710" for individuals' ODI: correct (Master Direction - Overseas Investment, updated 1 Apr 2026). The legal notes cited the 2022 Directions (id=12381); same paragraph numbers, now listed as [L3a] in [sources.md](sources.md).
- [income/private-investing.md](../../income/private-investing.md) says the SAFE "must be fully and compulsorily convertible": matches r.2(1)(e). "card payment not an LRS route (nv)": the LRS MD lets the bank accept card payment for a remittance (Part B para 8), but a direct card payment to a platform does not meet OI Regulations reg 8.
