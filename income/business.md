# Owning a business or an income asset

## Summary
- Do now: nothing. No business or income asset checked here beats the plan's index funds after tax and risk (11-12% a year expected, about 9.6-10.3% after 14.95% LTCG), with zero hours.
- If you own a house with its own electricity meter: get 2 rooftop solar quotes and apply on pmsuryaghar.gov.in (subsidy up to Rs 78,000, most likely taxed at slab in the year received, up to Rs 27,986; bill savings are tax-free).
- Buying a small online business does not beat index funds unless it keeps most of its value for 3-4 years: taxed on actual profit at slab (no 6% presumptive while you have paid technical-call income), GST above about Rs 10.6 L of sales. Only with Microsoft approval, a proven operator who signs a written IP assignment, and a FEMA route (ODI for shares; an asset-only buy likely needs RBI approval).
- P2P, invoice discounting, bond/SDI platforms (old "equipment leasing") and SM REITs are already judged weak in [research/global/alternatives.md](../research/global/alternatives.md); not repeated.

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-income` from a partial raw scan, rechecked against the sources below (all read 2026-11-15). "nv" = not verified. After tax = 35.88% slab unless said. FX: USDINR 94.23 (yfinance, 15 Nov 2026). Employer status per [rules.md](rules.md). Proposals only; you decide.

Verdict: solar first if you own a roof (small but passive). The online business beats index funds only if it keeps most of its value for 3-4 years (over 4 years it pays Rs 8.8-22 L against Rs 9.2-10.4 L from index funds that also keep the Rs 25 L); with its risk and hours, keep it as an idea for after Microsoft says yes, not a plan.
