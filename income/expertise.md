The page meta description says "48 ratings", so the two numbers disagree. - Topmate prices compared. Other VLSI listings (2026-11-15): mock interview Rs 100-800, career roadmap Rs 50-999, interview guidance Rs 1,199, "VLSI job hunt" Rs 1,499. With 11 years in VLSI power integrity (akarshanarora.com, 2026-11-15), a Top 1% badge and EMIR as a rare niche, the user can charge at the top of this range or above.
- Mentoring platforms. ADPList is free mentoring, with no money. Preplaced runs paid long-term mentoring and takes a share (its page returned 404; terms unverified). Topmate is already the main channel, so no new platform is needed.
- Course (E2) and newsletter (E4): the funnel exists (akarshanarora.com, EMIR Lab, PDN Toy Tools, YAML from Zero, the free newsletter, 1.2k Topmate bookings), but only about 10-15 bookings a year are paid. Expect a free-to-paid rate of 1-2% or less (nv). That is why a pre-sale must come before any build. Selling on his own site keeps most of the revenue; Udemy pays a small share per sale.
- Confidential material. Do not use Microsoft designs, flows, run data, foundry PDK data under NDA, or Ansys/Synopsys/Cadence manuals and training material. Use open PDKs (SkyWater 130, GF180) and his own code for every example.
- The public site names Microsoft and "AI accelerator" work. The Trust Code says employees do not speak for the company without authorization. Keep content generic and add "views are my own".
- Tax: all details are in `tax/side-income.md` (finance-tax). Presumptive AF s.58(2) Sl.3 (ex-44ADA, 50% deemed, up to Rs 75 L if cash is at most 5%) fits technical calls only. finance-tax says teaching (E3, E6) does not qualify.
