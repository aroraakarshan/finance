# Private investing: startups, angel funds, unlisted shares

## Summary
- Do first: before any startup cheque, disclose the stake to Microsoft and get advice or approval (Trust Code wording; pre-approval vs notice is internal). Chip, EDA, power or design-IP startups are likely suppliers, customers or competitors, so treat them as likely conflicts and disclose first - [rules.md](rules.md), [legal/private-and-business.md](../legal/private-and-business.md) B1.
- Best route now: direct small cheques (Rs 1-2.5 L each, iSAFE/CCPS) in 6-10 Indian chip/deep-tech startups over 3 years; no SEBI test applies to direct investing. Proposed cap: 3% of the total (about Rs 12.6 L at Rs 4.21 Cr).
- You are NOT a SEBI accredited investor today: on the closest test the income and financial-asset legs pass, but net worth is Rs 4.21 Cr vs Rs 5 Cr (about Rs 79 L short). Do not push income over Rs 2 Cr to qualify (about Rs 3.10 L extra surcharge). Angel funds (IAN, LVX, IPV) need accreditation, except older funds that may still take non-accredited money to 31 Mar 2027.
- Tax, per deal: insist on CCPS (not CCD/convertible note) and keep the startup's valuation report - [tax/private-investing.md](../tax/private-investing.md).
- Expect most single startups to fail: in the best-known angel study 52% of exits returned less than the money put in. Treat this as a lottery-like slice, not income.

## Proposed allocation (for finance-main and you to decide)
- At most 3% of the total in private equity (about Rs 12.6 L now), spread over 3 years, at most Rs 2.5 L per company and at least 6 companies. Reason: [K] shows a single company is more likely than not to lose money, and only a spread of 10+ deals gives a fair chance of one large winner; 3% is below the 5% scout-ideas cap, so a total loss costs about one year of the India SIPs (Rs 7.2 L/yr) plus a bit.
- It needs a new class in [targets.md](../portfolio/targets.md) (band 0-3%), funded from yearly new savings, not from safe money.

## Notes
- Succession: nominate each unlisted holding on Form SH-13 (s.72, SCD Rules r.19), or via the depository if in demat; the nominee holds for heirs, so also list shares in the will. iSAFE/SAFE before conversion are contract rights: no SH-13, they pass under the will [L1] B4.
- Shareholders' agreement: ask for tag-along, information and pre-emptive rights; checklist in [L1] B3.
- Angel tax (tax on the startup, 1961 s.56(2)(viib)) is gone from AY2025-26 (Finance (No. 2) Act 2024 memorandum, clause 23); it never taxed the investor. [M]
- Chip startups raise mostly VC-led seed and Series A rounds; angel slots, if any, are small (raw report, ET 2026-06-02, nv).
