Tax rules behind the table (Act text read 2026-11-15):
- Own home: no deduction for loan interest in the new regime (AF s.202(2)(a)(v) bars s.22(1)(b) for a self-occupied house, s.21(6)); no principal deduction (no 80C). A 7.50% loan costs the full 7.50%; your FDs earn about 4.5% after tax, so do not borrow while FDs sit idle.
- Let-out house: rent less 30% (AF s.22(1)(a)) less interest (s.22(1)(b)), at 35.88%; a house-property loss cannot be set off against salary (s.202(2)(b)(ii)) and is not carried forward (s.202(3)).
- Section 86 (old 54F): long-term gain on any asset other than a house is exempt if you buy one house in India 1 year before to 2 years after the sale (or build within 3 years); exemption = gain x house cost / net sale value. A home loan is fine (finance-tax).
- One house can cover all QCOM/MSFT sales, tested sale by sale, with the house cost shared across the sales (ITAT Ahmedabad, Pankaj Chimanlal Patel, 2018). QCOM sale 1 (Nov-Dec 2026, about Rs 70 L), sale 2 (Apr 2027, about Rs 32 L) and MSFT (about Rs 58 L) carry Rs 3.54 L + 1.73 L + 1.11 L = Rs 6.38 L of long-term tax. Saving all of it needs a house cost of at least the total net sale value, Rs 1.59 Cr (about Rs 1.48 Cr flat price with stamp duty); a Rs 1.0 Cr flat (Rs 1.08 Cr with stamp duty) saves about Rs 5.54 L. Register between Sep 2027 and Nov 2028 to catch every sale ([tax/real-estate.md](../tax/real-estate.md)).
- Under-construction flat counts only if completed within 3 years of each sale (possession risk).
- Unspent net sale value (not just the gain) goes into a Capital Gains Account Scheme deposit by the return due date: 31 Jul, or 31 Aug if the return has business/profession income; sale 1 money (up to Rs 70 L) by 2027 if the purchase comes later.
- Only a residential house counts against you: owning more than one other house on the sale date blocks it (s.86(5)); buying another house within 2 years or building one within 3 years reverses it (s.86(6)); selling the new house within 3 years reverses it (s.86(7)).

## 4. If you buy: title checks (details in [legal/real-estate.md](../legal/real-estate.md) s.1)
- 30-year title chain and encumbrance certificate from Kaveri 2.0; e-khata (A-khata) for the flat, checked before registration since Oct 2024.
- Avoid B-khata: conversion to A-khata at 2% of guidance value is open (scheme from 16 May 2026) but few owners use it; do not buy expecting conversion.
- Occupancy certificate (OC) is required: without it water, power and sewer connections can be refused; refuse possession without OC.
- RERA number checked on rera.karnataka.gov.in; a lawyer checks all of this before any advance.
