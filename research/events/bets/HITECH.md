## (c) Exit window rule (verified, Delisting Regulations)
- Reg 26(1): holders who did not tender (or were not accepted) may tender "for a minimum period of one year from the date of delisting". Reg 26(2): the acquirer must buy "at the same price at which the equity shares had been delisted" (Rs 426). Buying now still qualifies: the right goes with the shares held at delisting.
- Reg 14(4): after accepting the price, the acquirer must top up escrow to cover all shares still with the public; reg 26(3)-(4): exit payments come from that escrow, kept for at least 1 year. Reg 27: quarterly reminders.
- Payment timing is NOT in the regulation. It is set by the Exit Letter of Offer (not yet issued for Hitech). Elpro's exit offer (12 Nov 2026): paid monthly, within 10 working days after the end of the month in which shares are validly tendered. So not "10 working days from tender".

## (d) How you get paid (AngelOne)
1. Buy in AngelOne (BE series = delivery only). Keep shares in the AngelOne CDSL demat.
2. After trading stops, the Exit Letter and form go to holders on the register (Elpro: register on the trading-stop day); also on the company website and from the registrar, MUFG Intime (registrar to this offer, per the corrigendum).
3. Usual steps in exit letters (to confirm when Hitech's letter is out): off-market transfer of the shares to the special demat account named in the letter (CDSL Easiest or a paper DIS slip from AngelOne), then send the signed form and transfer proof to MUFG Intime. Money goes to the bank account linked to the demat.
4. Request the AngelOne DIS booklet / enable CDSL Easiest now. Tender in the first month of the window.
- Stuck risk: if you miss the 1-year window you hold unlisted shares with no market and no buyer obliged to pay. Physical shares are not involved (you hold demat).
