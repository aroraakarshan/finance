## Tax and STT (answer to the question)
- An open offer is a purchase by the acquirer, not a buyback by the company. The Oct 2024 rule taxing buybacks as dividend does not apply.
- Shares are tendered through the BSE acquisition window (DPS VIII.6), a stock-exchange sale: STT 0.1% on the sale value is charged, and the gain is short-term capital gain at the listed-shares rate (user: 23.92%). LOF tax sections say this (e.g. Frontier Capital DLOF, SEBI, Oct 2026: "tendering ... on the recognized stock exchange and chargeable to STT"). Castrol's own LOF tax section not read.
- Accepted and unaccepted parts both fall in FY 2026-27, so gain and loss net off.
