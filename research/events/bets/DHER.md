# DHER - Delivery Hero (Xetra), Uber cash offer EUR 41.50

Checked 2026-11-18 by finance-research. Prices are 17 Nov 2026 closes (Yahoo); EUR/INR 102.87 (Yahoo EURINR=X, 17 Nov).

## Summary
- VERDICT: NO-GO at EUR 36.60 (Rs 1 L). After tax the bet only pays if the deal closes with more than 84% chance; the market prices about 77%. Revisit at EUR 35.50 or lower.
- No rush: tendered shares trade as a separate Xetra line (Yahoo DHE1.DE, "z" = zum Verkauf eingereichte), EUR 36.40 on 17 Nov, so Fri 20 Nov is not the last chance.
- Pay-out is at closing, "second half of 2027" (Uber 10-Q for Q3 2026); long-stop 10 Nov 2027, extendable to 10 May 2028 at the latest (BCA cl. 1.2(b)).
- Gain 13.4% before tax, 8.6% after the 35.88% tax; 10.2% a year after tax if paid 30 Sep 2027, 7.8% if 31 Dec 2027, 5.8% at the long-stop.
- If it breaks: back towards EUR 19.99 (unaffected close 8 May 2026, Yahoo; Uber says offer is a 108% premium to it), a loss of about 44%.

## The event (primary sources)
- Offer: voluntary WpUG takeover offer, EUR 41.50 cash per share; BCA signed 16 Jul 2026 (Uber 8-K, 16 Jul 2026, Item 1.01 and ex. 2.1).
- Offer document approved by BaFin and published 27 Aug 2026; acceptance period 27 Aug - 5 Nov 2026, 24:00 Frankfurt (Uber/EQS release 27 Aug 2026). The document itself was NOT read: delivering-value.com is a script-only site with a disclaimer gate.
- Minimum acceptance: 50% plus one share, counting Uber's own shares (release 27 Aug; BCA 1.2(a)). Met: Uber reported 66.56% after the first period (dpa-AFX/Dow Jones 10 Nov 2026, via marketscreener and finanzen.net headlines; Uber's own Sec. 23 notice not read).
- Additional acceptance period: 11 - 24 Nov 2026 (Delivery Hero Q3 call 28 Oct, ad-hoc-news 3 Nov; Dow Jones 10 Nov). Exact end hour not verified. Custodians set earlier cut-offs (release 27 Aug); IBKR's cut-off not verified.
- Prosus irrevocably tendered 51,116,174 shares (16.68%) (release 27 Aug).
- Payment: within 7 Frankfurt business days after the additional period has ended AND all conditions are met (BCA cl. 1.1).

## Conditions (BCA cl. 1.2; final wording in the offer document not verified)
- Merger control for the offer: European Commission, Jordan, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Argentina, and South Korea if the KFTC asks for it. KFTC received Uber's filing (Korea Times, 22 Sep 2026), so Korea is likely in.
- Merger control for the SSW carve-out of 14 markets (also a condition): Austria, Spain, Poland (or the EC on referral), Ecuador.
- Financial regulators: Singapore MAS; Bank of Greece; Central Bank of Turkey.
- FDI: the BCA lists FDI clearances among "Clearances" to be sought (cl. 4.1) but not as an offer condition. Not verified in the offer document.
- Financing: no financing condition. EUR 14.2 bn bridge (16 Jul) of which EUR 4.0 bn moved to a term loan (8-K 6 Aug 2026); German law requires a bank certain-funds confirmation (BCA annex 1.5).
- Uber must accept remedies unless they are adverse and material to the combined delivery business (BCA cl. 4.5): a regulator can still kill it.
- Break fees go to the companies, not to holders: Uber pays Delivery Hero EUR 700 m on a regulatory lapse; Delivery Hero pays EUR 200 m on a competing offer (8-K 16 Jul).

## Lock-up, exit and untendered shares
- Tendered shares are booked to the tendered line; they get EUR 41.50 only at closing. Withdrawal right: not verified (under WpUG normally only if the offer is changed or a rival offer appears).
- Exit before closing: sell on the tendered line DHE1.DE (Yahoo: first trade 3 Sep 2026, 20-day average 15,174 shares, about EUR 0.56 m a day). Its ISIN and whether IBKR can trade it are NOT verified.
- Untendered shares: no offer price. Uber committed to no domination agreement (DPLTA) for 3 years (release 27 Aug), so no squeeze-out compensation soon. A sell-out right needs 95% (WpUG sec. 39c, law, not checked in the document). How the ordinary line trades after 24 Nov: not verified.
- If the deal lapses, tendered shares go back to the ordinary line (normal German practice; not verified in the document).

## Base rate and risks
- About 89-90% of announced deals close (Alpha Architect 2023, Mitchell and Pulvino 2001; scout [D20]). This one is harder than average: many agencies, about 20 months signing-to-close.
- Precedents against: Taiwan's TFTC blocked Uber buying foodpanda Taiwan (Uber ended it Mar 2025; Bloomberg via livemint) and blocked the Grab sale of the same unit (21 Oct 2026, finanznachrichten). Taiwan is not on the condition list.
- EU: DG COMP is looking at whether the SSW carve-out (funded mostly by an Uber loan to SSW) should be judged with the main deal; MEP Schwab asked for close review (Politico via trendingtopics.eu, 23 Sep 2026). No clearance found in news to 17 Nov.
- Other: EUR/INR moves on the whole stake; IBKR may not process the tender in time; capital tied up 10-18 months.
