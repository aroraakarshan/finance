## Phase 2 strength and endpoint
- EMPhASIS (Phase 2, RRMS, 24 weeks): new active MRI lesions vs placebo, rate ratio 0.38 at 45 mg (p=0.0002) and 0.30 at 30 mg (p<0.0001); adverse events 43% vs 44% placebo (Fox et al., Ann Clin Transl Neurol 2022, PMID 35698927). Phase 3 uses the 30 mg dose.

- CALLIPER (Phase 2, progressive MS) missed its primary brain-volume endpoint in 2025 (10-Q); IMUX fell 23% on 30 Apr 2025 (Yahoo; link to CALLIPER date not verified). Not the same population as ENSURE.
