## The event (primary sources)
- PDUFA goal date Sat 28 Nov 2026: PYPD 6-K ex99.1, 29 Jul 2026 (NDA accepted, Priority Review, "no filing review
  issues"); repeated in 6-Ks of 1 Oct, 28 Oct and 12 Nov 2026. 28 Nov is a Saturday after Thanksgiving (Thu 26 Nov;
  Fri 27 Nov is a half day), so the decision can come on or before Fri 27 Nov. Buy earlier, not on 27 Nov.
- Application: NDA, first cycle, prevention of surgical-site infection in colorectal surgery. Rolling submission
  started 31 Mar 2026, completed about 1 Jun 2026 (6-Ks 31 Mar and 1 Jun 2026). Fast Track, Breakthrough Therapy, three QIDP
  designations (20-F, 25 Feb 2026; 6-K 29 Jul 2026). Active ingredient is doxycycline (an old drug), so it is probably
  not a "new molecular entity"; NDA pathway (505(b)(1) or (b)(2)) not verified.
- Advisory committee: none announced in any 2026 filing or on the 11 Nov call (not verified with FDA).
- Labeling: "Labeling discussions with the FDA are underway" (6-K 12 Nov 2026; CEO on the 11 Nov call). This usually
  means FDA has not found a reason to refuse, but CRLs after label talks do happen, mostly over manufacturing.
- Manufacturing: made in PolyPid's own sterile plant in Petach Tikva, Israel, certified by the Israeli MOH
  (20-F, 25 Feb 2026). No FDA inspection of that plant is disclosed. On the 11 Nov call (Motley Fool transcript)
  Roth asked if the pre-approval inspection was done and whether a Form 483 was issued; the CEO declined to say.
  This is the main risk: a first product from a first-time maker at its own sterile plant.
- Efficacy: one positive Phase 3, SHIELD II (n=798): primary endpoint 10.9% vs 18.1% (-40%, p<0.005); deep and
  superficial SSI 3.8% vs 9.5% (-60%) (20-F). An earlier Phase 3 (SHIELD I) missing its primary endpoint is from
  memory of 2022 news and NOT verified in the 2026 filings.

## Base rate
- First-cycle approval of new-drug NDAs 2000-2012: 50% (151 of 302); 73.5% eventually (Sacks et al., JAMA 2014,
  PMID 24449316). Eventual NDA/BLA approval 2011-2020: 90.6% overall, infectious disease 92.9%, non-NME 91.5%
  (BIO/Informa/QLS, Feb 2021, Fig. 2 and 9). These are eventual rates, not first-cycle.
- Adjustments (my judgment): up for priority review + Breakthrough, "no filing issues" and label talks; down for one
  pivotal trial with an earlier miss (not verified), a first-time sponsor and an own sterile plant with undisclosed
  inspection status. Estimate: 75% approval by 28 Nov (range 65-85%).
