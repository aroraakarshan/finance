## 2. Business
- Revenue H1 FY3/27 JPY 1,167.2 bn; 75.5% outside Japan [S1][S3]. Units: Oncology (US/EU) 422.6 bn (+54.8%), Japan 232.7
  (-6.9%), EU Specialty 149.2, ASCA (Asia, Latin America) 142.5, American Regent (US iron injectables) 77.9 (-19.5%) [S1].
- Enhertu product sales H1 JPY 464.5 bn (+45.9%; US 271.0, Europe 102.8, ASCA 66.2, Japan 24.5), FY forecast raised to
  915.3 bn; Datroway 48.3 bn (+207%), FY 118.9 bn [S2]. New approvals in 2026: Enhertu 1L HER2+ breast (US, EU, Japan,
  China), early breast cancer; Datroway 1L triple-negative breast [S1].
- **AstraZeneca overlap:** Enhertu and Datroway are co-developed and sold with AZ; where Daiichi books sales it pays the
  partner 50% of gross profit (JPY 202.4 bn in H1, 395 bn FY forecast) [S2]. AZ also pays milestones (JPY 29.8 bn in H1)
  [S2]. Three other DXd ADCs are shared with Merck (US) [S1]. For AZ these drugs are ~6% of revenue ([AZN](AZN.md)).
- Moat: the DXd linker-payload technology and a lead in ADCs ("market leader in HER2+ mBC 1L in the US" [S2]); patents
  (dates not verified). Competitors' ADCs not checked in this run.

## 7. Management and governance
- CEO Hiroyuki Okuzawa [S1]. No controlling holder; institutions 55% [S8].
- **Accounting correction:** SG&A data aggregation errors led to corrected FY3/26 results on 31 Jul 2026, with Q1 results [S2]; the
  stock fell 11.1% that day (yfinance [S4]). The board reviewed it; automated aggregation and more reviews added [S2].
- I-DXd (lung cancer ADC with Merck) US filing withdrawn in Sep 2026 [S1]; a court dismissed Northern TK Venture's claims
  (Simply Wall St, 14 Sep 2026 [S10]). Indian courts allowed a forensic audit in its Ranbaxy award case against the
  Singh brothers (Livemint headline, 27 Sep 2026 [S11]); any recovery is not modelled.

## 8. Risks
- **Concentration:** Enhertu + Datroway are most of the growth; a safety signal, a rival ADC or a lost trial hits hard.
  Enhertu patent expiry not verified in this run.
- Profit share: half of the gross profit goes to AZ in Daiichi-booked markets [S2]; margins stay below big pharma.
- Cash: inventory build, CMO payments (JPY 80 bn FY forecast [S2]) and more debt; FCF must turn up for the dividend policy.
- Yen: a stronger yen cuts reported profit (FX +22.7 bn core OP in H1 [S1]) but raises the INR value of the shares.
- US pricing/tariff policy (Daiichi's MFN status not verified); controls after the accounting correction.
