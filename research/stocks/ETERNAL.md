## Summary
- **BUY IN STEPS, not at Rs 371: up to Rs 2.0 L - Rs 1.0 L at Rs 320-335, Rs 1.0 L at Rs 280-295 (or after Q3 FY27 results, late Jan 2027, if checks in section 10 pass).**
- What: India's #1 food delivery (Zomato) and #1 quick commerce (Blinkit, 10-minute grocery) app, plus District (going out) and Hyperpure (restaurant supplies). Profitable at group level, but only just.
- Why not now: at a 52-week high, 122x EV/adjusted EBITDA, 2.3x EV/order value; consensus target Rs 384 (+3%) [Y]; DCF says Rs 150-190. Business momentum is the best in the sector.
- 3-yr return (INR) from Rs 371: low -31% / base +43% / high +116% (about -12% / +13% / +29% a year); from Rs 330 base is about +17% a year.
- Swiggy is cheaper (0.5x EV/GOV vs 2.3x) but loses Rs 900 cr a quarter in Instamart; Eternal is the safer of the two.

## 4. Margins and returns [S1][C1]
- Reported EBITDA margin 3.1% of (1P-inflated) revenue; adjusted EBITDA 1.9% of B2C NOV.
- Core business still loses money below EBITDA: TTM other income (treasury) Rs 1,416 cr > profit before tax
  Rs 993 cr. Share-based pay Rs 262 cr in Q2 (Rs 819 cr in FY26) is about 2x TTM net profit.
- ROE about 1%, ROCE 2.5% [S1]. Tax: parent's carried-forward losses run out in FY27, so tax rate stays high
  (61-66%) until Blinkit/District subsidiaries use their own losses [C1].

- Quick-commerce war: Zepto plans an IPO Feb-May 2027 (~$3 bn, Rs 5,000-8,000 cr fresh money; FY26 loss
  Rs 5,905 cr); Flipkart Minutes 1,000 stores (to 1,500); Amazon Now ~800 sites, 120+ cities; Instamart going
  1P [N1][N2]. Top-10 city store counts (CLSA, Aug 2026): Blinkit 969, Zepto 828, Minutes 627, Instamart 615 [N1].

- Food delivery growth back under 15%, or take-rate cuts.
- Rules: gig-worker social security levy (labour codes from 21 Nov 2025, state laws), dark-store inspections [C1][N1].
- Valuation: 122x EBITDA; 2022 drawdown was -74%; 2026 low Rs 215.68 (13 Mar) [Y]. Volatility 34%/yr.
