## Summary
- **WATCH, buy in steps lower: Rs 1.5 L at or below Rs 4,300 and Rs 1.5 L at or below Rs 3,900 (max Rs 3 L), only if Q3 FY27 (results about 10 Feb 2027) shows normalised jewellery EBIT margin of 11% or more and domestic jewellery growth of 15% or more.** Not at Rs 4,910.
- What it is: India's largest jewellery retailer (Tanishq, CaratLane, Mia, Zoya, beYon) plus watches and eyewear; Tata group; market cap Rs 4,35,409 Cr [S1].
- Why wait: the reported P/E 61 is flattered by Rs 1,614 Cr of one-off gains in 6 months; on clean profit the P/E is about 77 TTM / 70 FY27 (my estimate), close to its 10-yr median of 80, and none of the DCF cases tested reaches the price.
- 3-yr total return from Rs 4,910 (INR): low -13% / base +27% / high +70% (about -5% / +8% / +19% a year); from Rs 4,300 the base is +45% (+13%/yr).

## 2. Business
- Domestic jewellery Rs 16,343 Cr in Q2 FY27 (+21%; Tanishq/Mia/Zoya +20%, same-store +15%; CaratLane +31%),
  international Rs 1,152 Cr (incl. Damas 364), watches Rs 1,838 Cr (+27%), eyecare Rs 277 Cr (+29%) [C1].
  Jewellery is about 85% of income. Studded share 35% of domestic jewellery (32% a year ago) [C1].
- 3,758 stores, 51 m loyalty members [C1]. Moat: Tata trust, scale in gold sourcing and hedging. Only 7 net new Tanishq
  stores in Q2; growth comes from Mia, CaratLane, abroad. Target: double jewellery by FY30 (about 19%/yr) [N3].

## 4. Margins and returns
- Operating margin 9-12% for 10 years; ROE 37.7%, ROCE 20.5% TTM, ROE 5-yr average 32% [S1]; jewellery EBIT margin "anchored around 11%", management will not
  raise it before FY27 closes; competition "only going to increase" (smaller jewellers discounting) [C2][C3].
- Q2 Tanishq/Mia/Zoya EBIT 14.3% reported, 11.1% excl. duty gain; CaratLane 6.6% clean (10.2% a year ago) [C1][C3].

## 5. Balance sheet and cash flow
- Borrowings Rs 27,638 Cr (Sep-26) vs equity Rs 18,263 Cr [S1]; most is gold metal loans, i.e. gold borrowed to
  hedge inventory (split not verified). Inventory 222 days [S1]. Commercial paper of Rs 1,000 Cr and Rs 850 Cr
  repaid in Nov 2026 [S1]; board approved Rs 2,500 Cr NCDs [N4].
- Cash conversion is weak: free cash flow FY22-26 Rs 4,736 Cr vs profit Rs 17,378 Cr (27%); FY26 alone 93% [S1].
  Dividend yield 0.31%, payout 26-29%; promoters (Tata group) 52.90%, unchanged [S1].

## 6. Gold, weddings and lab-grown diamonds
- Gold price: COMEX USD 3,878 on 16 Nov, -27% from the 52-wk high of 5,318, at a 1-yr low [Y]; India 24K about
  Rs 1,36,800/10 g [N5]. For Titan, lower gold = smaller tickets but more buyers (new buyers enter below Rs 0.5-1 L)
  and less pressure from coin-buying; management expects buyer growth to replace ticket growth [C3].
- Titan hedges its gold (conservative, via gold loans), so gold moves mostly do not hit profit [C3]. The real gold
  risk is duty policy: the Jul 2024 duty cut (15% -> 6%) dropped Q2 FY25 profit 23% [N6]; a cut back from 15% (the
  Union Budget is usually on 1 Feb) would reverse the H1 FY27 gains.
- For your gold (target 5%, now 3.1% with GOLDIETF buys planned, `portfolio/targets.md`): weekly 2-yr correlation of
  TITAN with COMEX gold is 0.23, with GOLDBEES 0.10 [Y]. Titan is not a second gold bet.
- Weddings/festive: Diwali shifted into Q3 (Navratri 11-22 Oct, Dhanteras 6 Nov); management saw "a very strong start"
  and a soft patch on gold volatility [C3]; many weddings in Nov-Dec [N3]; Senco festive sales +26% [N7].
- Lab-grown diamonds (LGD): wholesale prices -97% since 2018, -13% y/y in Q2 2026; India LGD jewellery market Rs 2,950 Cr
  (FY25) -> Rs 6,300 Cr (FY30, Technopak) [N8] - small next to Titan's jewellery. Titan's beYon has about 7 stores, "very
  small numbers" [N8]. Risk is indirect: natural diamond prices are 30-40% below the 2021 peak [N9], which can pull
  down studded prices and margins (studded = 35% of mix). Management: LGD is not driving buyer trends [C3].

## 9. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- Normalised jewellery EBIT margin below 10.5% for two quarters, or domestic jewellery growth below 12% / buyer growth
  negative once the gold-price tailwind is gone.
- Gold duty cut (inventory loss, as in 2024); sharp gold rebound hurting volumes.
- Diamond price deflation (LGD) squeezing studded margins; Damas losses and option liability swings.
- De-rating: 5-yr max drawdown -28.6%, volatility 24% a year [Y].
