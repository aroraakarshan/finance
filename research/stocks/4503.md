# 4503 Astellas Pharma (TSE) - stock deep dive

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research` (stage 3). Research only, not advice; no orders placed.

## Summary
- **WATCH.** Large Japanese drug maker (market cap JPY 4.8 trn [S4]); prostate-cancer drug XTANDI is 42% of sales; new "strategic brands" growing 51%.
- Cheap (P/E 12.6, EV/EBITDA 6.6) for a known reason: XTANDI loses US exclusivity Aug 2027 and consensus EPS falls next year.
- 1-3 yr return (JPY, total incl. dividends): -13.5% to +10.1% a year; base +1.0% a year (section 9).
- Do not buy with the trading budget: 100-share lot (Rs 1.59 L) is 4x the trading position cap; recheck after the US LOE.
- Screen check: Rev +20%, EPS +26% (Jul-Sep 2026 quarter y/y) confirmed [S5], H1 +23%/+64% per company [S1]; P/E 12.6 confirmed (company TTM EPS 215.2).

Fiscal year runs April-March; "FY3/27" = year ending 31 Mar 2027 (Astellas calls it "FY2026").
Price JPY 2,699 = close of 13 Nov 2026 (yfinance [S4]). FX: JPY/INR 0.5879, USD/JPY 159.9 (13 Nov 2026 [S4]).
News score: +1 (`news/4503.md`, finance-news, 2026-11-15): beat-and-raise vs XTANDI decline; price above mean target.

## 2. Business
- Revenue H1 FY3/27 JPY 1,270.9 bn [S2]. By region: US 45.4%, Japan 12.7%, Europe/Canada 23.9%,
  China 5.4%, other international 12.1% [S2].
- By product (H1 FY3/27, JPY bn, y/y) [S2]: XTANDI 533.7 (+12%, but flat without FX [S3]); PADCEV
  158.3 (+55%, bladder cancer, co-promoted with Pfizer in US); mirabegron (Myrbetriq/Betmiga) 119.5
  (+38%; US +77%, reason not given in the documents read); Prograf 114.3; IZERVAY 56.2 (+65%, eye
  disease GA); VYLOY 45.9 (+73%, gastric cancer); XOSPATA 42.9 (+25%); VEOZAH 30.7 (+34%, menopause).
- Strategic brands (PADCEV, IZERVAY, VYLOY, VEOZAH, XOSPATA): JPY 333.9 bn H1, FY forecast raised to
  680 bn (from 610); goal: more than double FY3/26 level by FY3/31 [S2][S3].
- US XTANDI profit is split 50/50 with Pfizer (co-promotion fee JPY 230 bn FY forecast) [S2][S3].
- Moat: patents (time-limited), oncology and ophthalmology franchises. Competitors (general
  knowledge, not verified in this run): J&J Erleada and Bayer Nubeqa (prostate), Apellis Syfovre
  (GA); company says a VEOZAH competitor's impact is "within assumptions" [S3].

## 8. Risks
- **XTANDI US loss of exclusivity Aug 2027** plus Medicare (IRA) price cuts; ex-US expiries already
  under way [S3]. The 50/50 Pfizer split softens the US profit loss [S3].
- Pipeline must fill the gap: setidegrasib (KRAS G12D) and ASP2138 (CLDN18.2 T-cell engager) in
  Phase 3; ASP6958 (PSMA) proof of concept; EV-309 readout FY2028-2030 [S8]. Trials can fail.
- US drug pricing/tariff policy (45% of sales in US) [S2]. Yen: a stronger yen cuts reported profit
  (FX added JPY 41.2 bn core OP in H1) [S2]; for an INR holder it raises the rupee value.
- More impairments from in-licensed assets [S3].
