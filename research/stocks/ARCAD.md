## 2. Business

- Net revenue = gross revenue minus subcontractor costs. FY25: gross EUR 4,875 m, net EUR 3,760 m [A4]. Margins are measured on net revenue.
- Q3-26 segments (share of net revenue) [A2]: **Resilience** 38% (water, energy grids, environment; +5.6% organic), **Places** 35% (buildings, data centres, pharma, chip plants; property and architecture are weak), **Mobility** 27% (rail, roads; +6.7%).
- New 2027-29 plan [A5]: Core (Transport, Energy & Water) is 53% of revenue, Accelerate (data centres, life sciences, chip plants) 21%, Optimize (Real Estate) 26%. Most of Architecture and the China business will be sold (about +100 bp on the margin). About 1,000 overhead jobs go in 2027.
- **US exposure** (FY25 net revenue by origin [A4]): US EUR 1,394 m = **37%**, Canada 7%, UK & Ireland 22%, Netherlands 9%, Germany 6%, rest 19%. US revenue -2.3% in EUR (FX). US water revenue grew by double digits in Q3-26 [A2]. Federal funding risk: no figure found (not verified).
- Moat: long framework contracts with key clients (65% of net revenue) and scale in water and environment. Little capital needed (capex about 0.5% of revenue). Risk: AI tools may shrink billable design hours. Fears that AI will cut design work hit WSP and Stantec shares in May 2026 [A12].

## 4. Margins and returns

- Guidance 2026: low-single-digit organic growth; operating EBITA margin 11.7-12.0% (operating EBITDA 14.3-14.6%) [A5].
- Targets 2027-29 [A5]: mid-single-digit organic growth; operating EBITDA margin "mid- to high-teens" by 2029; payout 30-40% of NIfO; net debt/operating EBITDA 1.5-2.5x. Buybacks are reviewed after each full-year result. From 2027 the margin KPI changes from EBITA to EBITDA, so the targets are harder to compare with the past.
- "Non-operating" costs come back every year: EUR 77 m in FY25, EUR 57 m in 9M-26 [A2][A4]. Reported EBITA margin is only 9.7% for 9M-26. Count part of these as normal costs.
- ROE 19%, ROIC 11.5% (stockanalysis, TTM) [A1]. Goodwill EUR 1,328 m is above book equity of about EUR 1.05 bn [A4], so returns depend on past acquisitions.

## 5. Balance sheet and cash flow

- Net debt EUR 910 m at 30-09-26 (EUR 1,042 m at 30-06), including about EUR 250 m of leases (FY25: EUR 252 m) [A2][A4]. FY25 net debt/operating EBITDA was 1.5x, against a covenant maximum of 3.5x [A4].
- Cash flow is seasonal: H1 negative, Q4 strong. FY25 FCF EUR 288 m = 7.2% FCF yield on today's market cap (own calc). DSO 65 days (73 a year ago) [A2].
- Shareholder returns: dividend EUR 1.05 for FY25 (39% of NIfO). Buyback of EUR 175 m (4.58 m shares at EUR 38.24) finished 16-01-26 and the shares were cancelled in May 2026 [A9].
- Data trap: stockanalysis/Yahoo show EBITDA EUR 341 m and D&A EUR 19 m, which is really EBITA. The site's EV/EBITDA of 12.4x is overstated; see own calc below.

## 7. Management and governance

- **CEO change:** Alan Brookes (25 years at Arcadis; CEO start date not verified) left on 01-03-26 after a weak 2025. He got 1 year's salary as severance, and his 2024-25 LTI lapsed [A4]. **Heather Polinsky** (American, insider, ran Resilience since 2021) became CEO on 01-03-26; formal appointment was set for the 20-05-26 AGM [A4][A14]. CFO Simon Crowe is new; the previous CFO left after 5 years [A4][A5]. Peter de Wit took over as Supervisory Board chair at the May 2026 AGM (planned [A4]; he speaks for the boards in the 23-09-26 release [A13]). The new team has run the company for 8 months: early results are better, but the plan is not yet proven.
- Owners (31-12-25) [A15]: **Lovinklaan Foundation 19%** (employee foundation; reinvests its dividends in staff), BNP Paribas AM 5%, Amundi 5%. Insiders 0.08% [A1]. With the foundation's stake, a hostile takeover is hard. The boards turned down EUR 51.5 as "fundamentally undervaluing" Arcadis [A13]. If the plan slips, there may be no bid left to fall back on (own view).
- Capital allocation: steady bolt-on deals (SATEL Spain 2026 [A7]), buybacks when leverage is low, dividend 30-40% of NIfO. Restructuring costs are booked as "non-operating" every year, so watch reported, not only operating, profit.

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)

- Organic growth falls back below 2% in 2027 (Mobility order book -2.1% y/y in Q3 [A2]), or the order book stops growing.
- 2027 operating EBITDA margin does not rise from about 14.5%, or non-operating costs stay above EUR 50 m/yr.
- Architecture and China sales drag on or sell cheaply. Canada property stays weak.
- AI cuts billable hours faster than Arcadis can reprice to value-based fees (sector de-rating in 2026 [A12]).
- US: 37% of revenue; federal and state spending, USD/EUR (USD down means fewer EUR).
- Price: up 85% from the 24-03-26 low (EUR 25.4 adjusted). Max drawdown over 10 yrs is -60% (Nov 2024 -> Mar 2026); 5-yr volatility 29%/yr [A8].
