## 8. Risks

- **BIOSECURE Act (verified):** signed 18 Dec 2025 in the FY2026 NDAA [S11]. US federal agencies may not buy from, contract with
  users of, or fund through grants "biotechnology companies of concern" (BCC). 1260H-listed biotech firms are BCCs
  automatically; OMB publishes the full list by **18 Dec 2026**; 5-year safe harbour for existing contracts [S11]. Effect on US
  customers: mostly indirect (federal-funded or federal-contract work), but June 2026 showed clients pause on the label itself
  [S8]. A naming would hit the 72% US revenue.
- Customer concentration in a few products; supply reshoring to the US/India; China policy, CNY, A/H gap, HK sentiment (HSI 1y -12.7% [S9]). Volatility 61%/yr (5y); max fall -85% (2021 peak to Jul 2024)
  (own calc [S9]).
