## 2. Business
- Segments by gross profit FY3/26 (stockanalysis segment table, JPY bn) [S6]: Individual 248.5,
  Asset management 111.9, plus Corporate, Investor services, Real estate, Market (full split not
  verified). Fee income ratio 51.0% of gross business profit (H1 FY3/27) [S2] - unusual for a
  Japanese bank; less dependent on lending spread than megabanks.
- Geography: mostly Japan; international business interest income JPY 77.0 bn of 252.0 bn
  effective interest earnings (SuMiTB, H1) [S2].
- Moat: trust and custody licences, pension/asset-admin scale, real estate brokerage network.
  Competitors: MUFG (Mitsubishi UFJ Trust), Mizuho (Mizuho Trust), Resona (general knowledge).
- Growth moves: stake in an Indian PE firm and a Rs 3,000 Cr fund with One Planet (29 Oct 2026),
  aircraft fund with Orix (11 Nov 2026) (Google News headlines [S8]); AHAM Asset Management
  (Malaysia) made a subsidiary [S2].

## 8. Risks
- Earnings quality: 32% of FY3/27 profit from cross-shareholding sales [S2]; a stock market fall cuts
  both the gains and the 41%-of-net-assets equity book.
- Deposit costs rising faster than loan yields (spread -0.08 pt y/y) [S2]; if the BOJ stops at 1.25%
  (held 30 Oct 2026, `research/global/japan.md`), the rate tailwind fades.
- Market risk in securities/foreign-currency funding (profit from surplus FX funds -JPY 72 bn y/y) [S2].
- Price: +68% in 1 year, near 52-week high JPY 1,822.5 [S5]; 3-yr volatility 30% (own calc).
- Yen: profit is mostly yen; a weaker yen vs INR lowers the rupee value.
