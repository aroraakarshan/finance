## Summary
- **WATCH, buy in steps lower: Rs 1 L at Rs 2,700 and Rs 1 L at Rs 2,400 (max Rs 2 L), only if the Q3 update (early Jan 2027) shows fashion LFL at mid-single digits or better and Zudio adds on track.** Not at Rs 3,204.
- What it is: Tata Group fashion retailer - Zudio (value fashion, 999 stores), Westside (mid-price, 311), Star (grocery JV) [N1][N2]; market cap Rs 1,71,113 Cr [S1].
- Why wait: P/E 89 on TTM; growth now comes mostly from new stores (sales per sq ft -8% y/y), not same-store sales; a 10-year DCF gives Rs 740-1,960 even on generous inputs (section 6).
- 3-yr total return from Rs 3,204 (INR): low -26% / base +15% / high +67% (about -10% / +5% / +19% a year); from Rs 2,600 the base is about +42% (+12%/yr).

## 2. Business
- Own-brand clothes, footwear, beauty, innerwear in large stores. Zudio: low price, 8,000-10,000 sq ft, tier 2-3 towns [N6];
  Westside: mid-price. Beauty/innerwear/footwear = 21% of Q2 FY27 revenue [N3]. 1,342 stores, 339 cities, 18.5 m sq ft [N2].
- JVs (equity-accounted): Star (grocery, with Tesco; revenue +20% in Q2) and Inditex Trent (Zara) [N3][N2]. Pilots: Samoh, Burnt Toast [N5].
- Moat: scale and speed of store roll-out, own brands with ~44% gross margin [N4], tight inventory (74 days) [S1].

- The gap: FY26 sales +18% while retail area grew 32% and revenue per sq ft fell 11% [N4]; Q1 FY27 store
  count +26%, revenue per store -5% [N7]; Q2 FY27 revenue per sq ft -8.4% [N3]. So almost all growth is new
  space; existing stores grow 0-5%. Causes named: new products that did not sell, cannibalisation from dense
  stores, and new tier 2+ stores that ramp slowly [N5].
- Store pace: FY26 net +198 Zudio, +52 Westside [N4]. FY27 guidance 200-250 Zudio, 50 Westside, 25-40 Star [N5].
  H1 FY27 only +36 Zudio, +11 Westside [N1] - H2 needs about 165-215 Zudio (Q4 FY26 alone added 109 [N7], so
  back-loading is normal, but watch it). Long-run potential per company: 5,000 Zudio, 700 Westside; HSBC models 1,500 Zudio [N5].
- Peak? Not cyclical, but the 40-80% years are over: Bernstein calls ~20% "the new normal" [N7]; brokers model
  21-25% CAGR FY26-29 (Motilal: standalone revenue 21%, adj. PAT 22%) [N8].

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- LFL stays at 0-3% for H2 FY27 and FY28 while revenue per sq ft keeps falling -> margins stall, P/E de-rates
  toward 50-60. Brokers' downside case starts here (Goldman: risk if LFL does not reach mid-high single digits) [N7].
- Zudio adds fall below 200 in FY27 (H1 only 36) [N1][N5].
- Competition in value fashion: Reliance (Yousta), Shoppers Stop (Intune), V-Mart, V2 Retail, Max; online
  fast-delivery fashion. Trent itself flagged "high competition, rising rental costs" (May 2026, headline) [N11].
- Quick commerce: hits Star (grocery) more than fashion - DMart's margin slipped to 7.1% and Blinkit is now
  EBITDA-positive [sector note D20][D21]. Fashion on 10-minute apps is small today (share not verified).
- Input-cost inflation (fabric, war-related), weak urban demand [N3][N4]; chairman change (section 7).
- Price: 37% yearly volatility; -60% from Rs 5,479 (Oct 2024) to Rs 2,194 (30 Mar 2026), bonus-adjusted [Y].
