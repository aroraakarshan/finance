## 1. Business

- **Advice & Wealth Management (AWM)**: Q3 2026 pretax $989 m (69% of segment profit). Advisors charge wrap (advisory) fees on $729 bn. Ameriprise Bank ($23.6 bn deposits) and the certificate company ($7.3 bn) earn spread income on client cash.
- **Asset Management (Columbia Threadneedle)**: pretax $308 m, margin 45.7%; AUM+AUA $743 bn (+4%).
- **Retirement & Protection (RiverSource annuities/life)**: pretax $132 m ($198 m excluding unlocking). Variable annuity balance $92.8 bn; 43% of it carries living-benefit riders (47% a year ago).
- Source for all of the above: Q3 2026 release (8-K exhibit 99.1, 2026-10-29) and 10-Q (2026-10-30). Delaware company with its head office in Minneapolis (EDGAR), so the shares are **US-situs**.

## 3. Advisor productivity, cash and rates

- **Productivity**: TTM revenue per advisor $1.24 m, +13% (release). 79 experienced advisors recruited in Q3; 258 advisors and $28 bn joining from Huntington through early 2027, plus 29 advisors and $4 bn (Cadence) by mid-2027.
- **Attrition dispute**: BofA cut AMP two notches to Underperform with a $445 target (headlines 2026-10-19: Investing.com, scanx; articles not read). Barron's ran "Ameriprise Has an Advisor Problem" the same day (headline only). On the Q3 call (2026-10-29, Fortune transcript) the CEO said retention is "in the high 90s", the count is "consistent year-over-year" at about 10,000, and that there is no broad retention bonus. The exact advisor count is not in the release or the 10-Q (not verified).
- **Comerica**: client flows were -$8.5 bn in Q3, or +$5.3 bn excluding Comerica's exit. Comerica was bought by Fifth Third (not verified this run). Wrap net inflows were +$4.8 bn.
- **Cash and the Fed**: the Fed **raised** its target to 3.75-4.00% on 2026-09-16 and held on 2026-10-28 (FRED DFEDTARU/DFEDTARL). The CFO spoke of "those two hikes" possibly coming. US 10-year yield 5.15% on 2026-11-13 (FRED DGS10).
  - Cash sweep balances were $27.0 bn (-1% y/y). Total AWM cash was $35.5 bn: bank yield 4.61%, certificates 4.52%, off-balance-sheet sweep net fee yield 2.47%.
  - The rate AMP pays clients on sweep cash is not disclosed ("we benchmark our cash sweep rates", call). Suits over sweep rates have raised legal costs at Raymond James and others (AdvisorHub headline 2026-07-27); whether AMP is named is not verified.
- **Rate sensitivity is small** (10-Q item 3, at 2026-09-30): +100 bp adds about $68 m pretax from bank and brokerage cash, -$2 m from certificates and +$35 m from fixed annuities. Asset-based fees lose $66 m, and variable-annuity hedges add $339 m (mostly accounting). Hikes are a mild plus. The bigger risk is what hikes do to stocks: CEO: "Why are they raising rates? ... you never know what that causes".

## 4. Columbia Threadneedle outflows

- Net outflows were -$9.3 bn in Q3 (institutional -$5.5 bn, retail -$2.9 bn, legacy insurance -$0.9 bn) and **-$23.5 bn over 9M 2026** (10-Q), about 3% of AUM a year. Every quarter in the release table shows outflows.
- Earnings still rose 18%, helped by markets and Seligman technology-fund performance (10-Q). That makes part of the profit tech-linked: an indirect overlap with the user's QCOM/MSFT block.
- Fund performance: 78% of retail funds above median over 3 years, 87% over 10 years. Inflows came into ETFs, SMAs and model delivery (+$2.4 bn). The CEO expects institutional flows to improve. Outflows that continue at this rate slowly shrink the fee base.

## 5. Margins and returns (ROE judged)

- Pretax adjusted operating margin: 28% consolidated, 29.3% AWM, 45.7% Asset Management (release).
- **ROE**: Yahoo shows 72% (the scout row showed 63%); the company reports 55.2% GAAP ex-AOCI, TTM. GAAP equity is only $5.11 bn because of:
  - AOCI of -$2.64 bn (unrealised bond losses after the rate rise; it was -$1.28 bn in Q2);
  - buybacks of $21.4 bn in 2015-2025 against equity of about $7.75 bn ex-AOCI (XBRL).
- The true return on capital is high but not 63-72%: the wealth and asset-management arms need little capital, and the insurer, bank and certificate company hold the regulatory capital. Own estimate: TTM net income $4.16 bn / average ex-AOCI equity $7.59 bn = 55%. Book per share ex-AOCI is $89, so buybacks run at about 5.8x book. This is value-neutral only while earnings stay high.

## 8. Management, governance, risks

- Jim Cracchiolo is both Chairman and CEO (release); CFO Walter Berman. Tenure and succession plan not verified (the 2026 proxy was not read). Insiders own 0.55% (Yahoo). One share class.
- Legal: AMP and an advisor must pay Edward Jones $4.7 m in a trade-secret case (InvestmentNews headline 2026-09-24). A judge barred an Ameriprise broker from using Citizens client names (AdvisorHub 2026-10-29). Regulatory inquiries are "elevated" (10-Q, routine wording).
- **What proves the thesis wrong**:
  - the advisor count falls below about 9,700, or client flows excluding Comerica turn negative for 2 quarters;
  - Columbia outflows above $10 bn a quarter while markets fall;
  - payout drops below 80% (capital needed for the annuity book);
  - the ex-AOCI ROE falls below 45%.
- Other risks: S&P 500 at a record (7,652 at Q3 end, release), with a 54% drawdown in 2020 and 28% volatility over 5 years (yfinance); fee pressure from AI/robo advice; cash-sweep litigation (sector); and the long-term-care block (call; size not checked).
