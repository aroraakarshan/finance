## 1. Business in plain words
- Makes and installs the equipment that carries power from plants to cities: high-voltage transformers and reactors
  (Vadodara), gas- and air-insulated switchgear, HVDC terminal stations (long-distance DC lines), substation
  automation and grid software. One reported segment [T].
- Customers (backlog, 30 Sep 2026): central utilities and PSUs (mainly Power Grid) plus private, 98% together; state
  utilities 2% [P]. Exports 32% of Q2 revenue, 21% of Q2 orders; much of export work comes through GE Vernova group
  companies (related-party orders) [P][T].
- Parent GE Vernova (NYSE: GEV) owns 51%; it sold 24% in Sep-Dec 2024 (75% -> 51%) [SC]. No subsidiaries: standalone
  = consolidated (screener's consolidated page has data only to 2010) [SC].

- Cyclical peak? Grid capex is in a strong upcycle (India power use +21.9% y/y Oct 2026; 500 GW non-fossil target)

## 3. Margins and returns
- Gross margin 44.5% (Q2), EBITDA 27.1%, PAT 21.3% [T][P]. ROE 57% FY26, 3-yr 42%; ROCE 77% FY26 [SC]: the highest of
  its peers (section 5), helped by customer advances (low capital in the business).
- Some price pressure on regular transformers "but very little"; no HVDC tender since Barmer to judge Chinese or new
  domestic bidders [T].

## 4. Balance sheet and cash flow

- Cash from operations FY26 Rs 1,710 Cr vs profit Rs 1,233 Cr; free cash flow Rs 1,521 Cr; H1 FY27 cash generation
  Rs 1,010 Cr [SC][T]. Debtor days 128 (FY26) [SC].
- Capex: Rs 1,000 Cr capacity expansion under way, about 20% spent [T]. Dividend payout 21% (yield 0.23%) [SC].
