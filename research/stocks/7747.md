## 1. Business
- Makes very thin steel guidewires (and catheters) that doctors thread through arteries to open blocked heart vessels (PCI) and,
  more and more, leg, brain and abdominal vessels. company aims to keep its leading share in Japan [S7]; builds its own wire and coil (Device Division also sells parts to other makers) [S3].
- FY6/26 sales JPY 145.4 bn: Medical 126.8 bn, Device (outside sales) 18.6 bn [S2]. Medical by region: China 37.4 (+32%), Europe 27.9,
  US 26.2 (+14%; +12% ex-FX), Other 18.2, Japan 17.1 [S3]. Q1 FY6/27 medical: cardio +16.6%, non-cardio +38.4%, OEM +33.4% [S6].
- **China VBP:** China is sold through distributors under "centralised bidding"; the company says "for the time being, price decline
  is absorbed by distributor policy, but there will be a risk of decline in the future" [S7]. A local factory in Nanning
  (start Dec 2030) is planned to make products for China [S7]. Bid round dates and prices: not verified.
- **US expansion:** building a direct sales force; Q1 US +36.2% (ex-FX about +26%, own calc from +8.1% USD) [S6]; distributes partner products
  (QXMedical boosting catheter, May 2026; Medikit sheath, Apr 2025, headlines [S10]). US tariffs cost JPY 1.15 bn in FY6/26 [S3];
  Q1 FY6/27 had a one-off refund of earlier tariffs [S1].
- **Rocamed acquisition: not found.** Not in the company history, FY6/24-27 results, the 2030 plan or news headlines [S2][S7][S9][S10];
  no change in consolidation in Q1 FY6/27 and no subsequent events [S1]. Treat as unverified. The real M&A record: 2021 US/Japan
  deals (Pathways, Rev.1, surgical robot) were written off, JPY 9.2 bn impairment in FY6/25 [S2]; Nitta Mold (moulds) bought Jan 2025 [S9].

## 7. Risks and what proves the thesis wrong
- **Yen:** FY guide uses USD/JPY 155 vs 159.5 now; yen weakness added JPY 3.2 bn of Q1 sales [S6]. A move back to 130-140 would cut
  reported EPS by roughly 10-15% (own estimate, not company data), though it lifts the INR value of the shares.
- **China (26% of group sales):** VBP price cuts reaching Asahi's distributor prices; distributor stocking swings quarter to quarter [S1][S7].
- **Thesis is wrong if:** ex-FX sales growth falls below ~8% for two quarters; gross margin ex-FX falls back under 68%; a large new
  acquisition (from the JPY 130 bn pot) gets impaired; or US growth stalls after the direct-sales build-out.
- US tariffs on Japanese devices (rate not verified); Japan reimbursement price cuts (Japan is 13% of medical sales [S3]).
