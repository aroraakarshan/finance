## Summary

Watch, do not buy yet. Cheap (P/E 9.2, EV/EBITDA 4.5), strong balance sheet, near-zero link to QCOM/MSFT and not US-situs - a better fit than APA.
But Q3 2026 earnings are a war-driven peak (Brent USD 97, record USD 33 refining margin); base case is flat. Prefer entries near EUR 65-70 or after oil cools.
1-3 yr return (local, per yr): Low -22% / Base -2% / High +12% (INR: -20% / +1% / +15%) - section 9.

## 2. Business

- Segments (Q3 2026 adjusted net operating income, company call) [T4]: Refining and Chemicals USD 3.6 bn (best quarter ever), Exploration and Production 3.2 bn, Integrated LNG 1.9 bn (doubled q/q); Integrated Power cash flow about 0.8 bn/quarter, about 3 bn for 2026.
- Production 2.47 m boe/day in Q3 2026; Middle East conflict cut about 5% of output; LNG from 11 countries [T4]. Operating cost USD 4.7/boe [T4].
- Revenue by segment, FY2025 (USD bn, stockanalysis; segments include inter-segment sales, so they sum above the group's 182.3) [T3]: Refining and Chemicals 87.2, Marketing and Services 78.7, Integrated Power 19.6, Integrated LNG 10.1, E&P 5.6. Geography split: not verified.
- Moat: integration (own crude into own refineries, trading, a tanker fleet about 40% on fixed charters), low upstream cost, one of the largest LNG portfolios [T4].
- Competitors: Shell, BP, Exxon, Chevron, Eni, Equinor.
- NYSE: ordinary shares started trading on the NYSE on 2025-12-08, replacing the ADRs (TotalEnergies and Investing.com headlines; page itself 403) [T5]. Paris is still listed and trading (TTE.PA prices to 2026-11-13 [T7]). Which venue is "primary" not verified.
