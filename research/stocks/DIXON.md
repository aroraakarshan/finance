## 2. Business
- Contract manufacturer (EMS): builds products for brands, earns a thin fee, passes component costs through [C3].
- Q2 FY27 segments [C3]: mobile and other EMS Rs 20,093 Cr (operating profit 509, 2.5%); within it IT hardware 2,160 and
  telecom 1,837; consumer electronics (TVs, fridges) 1,115 (40); home appliances 586 (48). Lighting moved to a 50:50 JV with Signify (Aug 2025) [C2].
- Phone customers: Motorola (anchor), Transsion brands, a new Indian brand, Longcheer JV (starts Q3 FY27), Vivo JV (51:49,
  government approval 8 Jul 2026 [C5], production from Q4 FY27, about 20-21 m phones a year, capex Rs 300-350 Cr, commercials not final) [C3].
- Volumes: 9.7 m phones in Q2 (flat y/y while India's smartphone market fell 26%); FY27 33-34 m without Vivo; FY28 55-60 m with Vivo [C3].
- Components (the margin plan): Q Tech camera modules (51%) 80 -> 120 m capacity, 180-190 m by FY28, FY28 sales Rs 2,500-3,000 Cr;
  HKC display JV (government approval Mar 2026 [N4]): notebook/auto line trials Dec 2026, sales Q4 FY27; mobile line trials Mar 2027,
  sales FY28; capex about Rs 1,000 Cr over 3 years, Rs 6,000-7,000 Cr sales when ramped (mostly captive). Management: display + camera add
  80-100 bps to margin over 2 years [C3]. New: optical transceivers (Gemtek JV, trials Q4 FY27), laptops with Inventec, servers later [C3].

## 4. Margins and returns
- Operating margin 3.6-5.0% for 10 years, 3.2% TTM, 2.7% in Q2 FY27 [S1]. Washing machines fell from 10.5-11% to 8.3% (polymer, freight,
  FX; pass-through lags), IT hardware 2-2.3%, telecom 3-3.2% [C3]. Thin margins are the EMS model; returns come from negative working capital.
- ROCE 29.7%, ROE 22.2% adjusted (H1 FY27, down from 44.8% / 28.1% in FY26) [C1]. Peers' ROE 6-14% [S2].

## 5. Balance sheet and cash flow
- Net debt Rs 73 Cr (Sep-26) vs net cash Rs 773 Cr (Mar-26); working capital -2 days [C1]. H1 FY27 operating cash flow -280,
  capex 700, free cash flow -980 (strategic memory stock, PLI receivables); management expects 80-85% released by March [C1][C3].
- Free cash flow FY22-26 Rs 1,125 Cr vs reported profit Rs 3,697 Cr (30%) [S1]. Capex about Rs 1,200 Cr in FY27, Rs 1,200-1,300 Cr in FY28 [C3].
- **PLI receivable (auditor emphasis):** a subsidiary booked Rs 1,110 Cr of mobile PLI for output above its annual ceiling (allowed only if
  other applicants under-use theirs); still unpaid at 30 Sep 2026, with Rs 577 Cr owed on to a customer. Net at risk about Rs 533 Cr
  pre-tax (about 0.6 years of clean profit). The auditor drew attention to it (Note 6, review report para 6) [C2].
- Promoters 28.50% (Sep-26) vs 33.63% (Dec-23), selling in steps [S1]; pledge not checked. Dividend Rs 10/share (0.08%) [C2][S1].

## 6. Policy (PLI and MPMS)
- Mobile PLI-1 has expired [C3]; IT hardware PLI 2.0 (Dixon moving to the Rs 500 Cr "global" category) and telecom PLI continue;
  components fall under ECMS (camera, display, Gemtek approved) [C3].
- MPMS, approved 15 Jul 2026: Rs 62,500 Cr over FY27-FY31; 2.25-5% on eligible phone sales, +1.5% for local components, +3% for
  design/R&D [N2]. It pays the brand, needs about 15% yearly sales growth over a baseline (analyst on [C3]); Dixon gains through its
  customers' exports and localisation; management could not quantify the margin effect [C3].

## 7. Smartphone cycle and your QCOM/MSFT
- Yes, Dixon is tied to the same smartphone cycle as QCOM: India's smartphone market fell 26% y/y in Q2 FY27 as memory costs (AI
  data-centre demand) pushed phone prices up [C3]. That squeeze hurts handset units for both. Which chips its customers' models use is not verified.
- But Dixon earns per phone assembled and gains share (exports, Vivo, Indian brands) even when the market shrinks, and half its growth plan
  is laptops, telecom and components. Price links are weak: weekly 2-yr correlation QCOM 0.11, MSFT 0.16, QCOM+MSFT 0.17, Nifty 50 0.51 [Y].

## 10. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- Vivo JV production not started by Q1 FY28 (Apr-Jun 2027) or FY28 volumes below 50 m phones.
- Clean EBITDA margin stuck below 3% through FY28 (displays/cameras not adding the promised 80-100 bps).
- PLI receivable (net Rs 533 Cr) written off; working capital not released by Mar 2027 (free cash flow negative again in FY27).
- Memory prices stay high and India phone demand keeps shrinking; customer loss (Motorola, Transsion) or MPMS benefits going to rivals.
- De-rating of the EMS group (Kaynes restatement); 5-yr max drawdown -54%, volatility 39%/yr [Y].
