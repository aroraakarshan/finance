## Summary
- **Watch, do not buy yet.** Pune-based PSU bank (government 73.60%, Sep 2026 [S1]); Rs 91.70, market cap Rs 70,532 Cr [S1].
- Why: best ROE among PSU banks and clean asset quality, but P/B 1.9 is near the top of its 10-yr range (median 1.0) and RBI's ECL rules start Apr 2027.
- 3-yr total return (INR): low -15% / base +46% / high +105% (about -5% / +13% / +27% a year; section 9).
- Screen row: rank 3 India, passed G M V Q; Rev +33%, EPS +28%, P/E 8.8, ROE 23% (`scout/ideas.md`, 2026-11-15).

## 2. Business
- Lender with deposits Rs 3,76,407 Cr and global advances Rs 3,29,359 Cr at 2026-09-30; total business
  +25.2% y/y [N1]. Retail advances Rs 94,582 Cr (+20.9%), MSME Rs 58,049 Cr (+24.7%) [N1]; so retail
  about 29% and MSME about 18% of advances (my arithmetic). Corporate/agri split: not verified.
- Moat: low-cost deposits (CASA 46.5% [N1]) and ROA 2.02% [N2] (peer ROA not compared); no product
  moat, banks compete on price. Geography: India, Maharashtra-based (branch split not verified).
- Competitors: SBI, Bank of Baroda, Canara, Union, Indian Bank, PNB (section 6) and private banks.

## 7. Management and governance
- MD and CEO Nidhu Saxena [N2]. Government 73.60% after a 6% OFS in Dec 2025 (floor price, oversubscribed,
  Rs 2,492 Cr raised; green shoe used) [N4]. This meets the 75% minimum public shareholding rule, so
  MAHABANK is not on the Oct 2026 list of four PSU banks facing new OFS (Central Bank, UCO, IOB,
  Punjab and Sind) [N5]. Further government sales are possible but none announced (not verified).
- FII holding 0.39% about 2.5 years ago -> 6.74% (CEO) [N2]; screener shows 6.50% Sep 2026 [S1].
- Capital allocation: payout fell to 13% in FY26 [S1] (retaining capital for 18-30% loan growth).
- Controversies: none found in the news searched (1 year). Government can push policy lending or
  mergers; PSU bank consolidation talk resurfaced in Oct 2026 [N5].

## 8. Risks
- Cycle: loans growing near 30% a year tend to show bad loans 2-3 years later; credit cost is near zero now.
- Regulatory: ECL from Apr 2027 (Rs 2,500 Cr est. = about a third of FY26 profit) [N2].
- Rates: NIM gains from RBI hikes reverse when rates fall; deposit costs rise with a lag.
- Political: government is the owner (dividends, OFS supply, possible merger, farm-loan waivers).
- Funding: CASA ratio falling 3.9 points in a year [N1].
- Price: up 63% in 1 year, 2% below 52-week high, 38% yearly volatility, -40% drawdown as recently as
  Apr 2025 [Y].
