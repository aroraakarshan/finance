## 2. Business

- Revenue TTM to H1 2026, GBP 12.05 bn (stockanalysis) [C3]: Technology Sourcing (resale) 10.23 bn (85%), Professional Services 0.99 bn, Managed Services 0.83 bn.
- H1 2026 revenue by country [C3]: US 3,785 m (55%), UK 1,513 m (22%), Germany 1,060 m (15%), Western Europe 415 m, other 72 m.
- Judge it on gross profit, not revenue. H1 2026 gross profit GBP 657.9 m (+30.5%) [C4]; by segment [C5]: North America 268.6 m (41%; H1-25 156.8 m), Germany 161.7 m, UK 160.1 m, Western Europe 51.2 m, International 16.3 m. North America = 62% of adjusted operating profit before central costs (H1-25 44%) [C4].
- Gross margin fell to 9.6% from 12.6% because high-volume, low-margin hyperscale resale grew fastest [C4]. A 2.8% operating margin is normal for a reseller (peers in section 6).
- Moat: scale, vendor accreditations, integration centres (new liquid-cooling facility in Hatfield), long services contracts; 216 customers giving over GBP 1 m gross profit a year [C4][C6].
- Competitors: CDW, Insight, Softcat, Bytes, Bechtle (listed); SHI, WWT (private, not verified).
- Acquisitions 2026: AgreeYa (USD 120 m EV, services, US/India) and GAI (up to USD 92 m, US federal reseller) [C4].

## 5. Balance sheet and cash flow

- Net cash GBP 133.0 m statutory, 308.7 m "adjusted" (excludes leases) at 30-06-26 [C4]; down from 426.8 m at end-2025 because inventory jumped to GBP 1,260 m (317 m a year earlier) for large North American and UK projects [C5].
- Free cash flow: FY23 389 m, FY24 398 m, FY25 272 m (stockanalysis) [C3]; H1-26 outflow 138.9 m (seasonal unwinding of early customer payments, company) [C5]. Working capital is the swing risk.
- Dividends: policy cover 2-2.5x adjusted EPS; FY25 74.6p; H1-26 interim 27.1p (+14.8%) [C5][C3]. Since the 1998 IPO GBP 1.35 bn returned via dividends and buybacks [C5]; GBP 222 m buyback in 2024 (Yahoo [C8]), EBT bought GBP 38 m in H1-26 [C5]. Shares 114 m (FY22) -> 105 m.

## 8. Risks

- AI capex cycle: hyperscaler/neocloud spending can stop fast; backlog is non-cancellable per the company, but revenue after 2027 not verified [C6].
- Customer concentration: top-customer share not disclosed in what was read (not verified).
- Working capital: GBP 1.26 bn inventory; a delayed or failed large project hits cash [C5].
- Margin: gross margin down 300 bps in a year; vendor rebates and pricing power are thin.
- Germany: public-sector services weak, restructuring costs [C4]. Currency: 55% of H1-26 revenue in the US, reported in GBP (H1 translation impact about -GBP 3 m on FY op. profit) [C5].
