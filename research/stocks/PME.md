## Summary
- **WATCH (entry A$130, then A$110; condition below). Do not buy at A$201.66.** Rs 0 now; later up to Rs 4.2 L (1% cap) in 2 halves of about Rs 2.1 L.
- What it is: Australian maker of Visage 7, cloud software for reading hospital scans (X-ray, CT, MRI); 90% of revenue from US hospitals; EBIT margin 75%, no debt [S1].
- Why not now: the screen's P/E 79.5 counts a one-off A$174m paper gain. Real (underlying) P/E is **146**, forward 107 (FY27 consensus). DCF value A$36-64 (A$104-134 even on generous inputs).
- 1-3 yr return (Nov 2029, AUD and INR at a flat AUD/INR; dividends included): -19% to +16% a year; base +1% a year (section 9).
- Buy only after the H1 FY27 results (~10 Feb 2027) show constant-currency revenue growth of at least 25% and new wins keep coming, and the price is A$130 or lower (about 70x FY27 EPS).

## 2. Business
- Sells imaging software per scan read (fee per exam, with contracted minimums), mostly as a cloud service: Viewer, Workflow, Open Archive ("full stack"), plus cardiology, pathology and an AI reporting module [S1].
- Revenue FY26 (Jun) A$261.7m: North America 236.8m (90%), Australia 19.0m (RIS for I-MED and Lumus), Europe 5.9m [S1].
- US share about 11% of 690m exams a year; used by 11 of the top-20 US hospitals [S1]. Fast installs (Trinity Health, the largest, done in 7 cohorts by Oct 2026) are a key edge [S1].
- Contract book: A$1.34 bn of minimum contracted revenue over the next 5 years (Jun 2026, +41% in 12 months) [S1].
- **Wins and renewals:** FY26 signed A$548m (10 new A$407m incl. UCHealth A$170m/10 yr, Beth Israel Lahey A$90m/7 yr; 6 of 6 renewals A$141m, all 5-yr at a higher fee per exam) [S1].
  FY27 so far: St Luke's A$23m (12 Aug), Valley Health A$25m (21 Aug), Billings Clinic A$24m (19 Oct), Virtua Health A$43m (21 Oct 2026) = A$115m [S1][S3][S7].

## 5. Balance sheet and cash flow
- No borrowings. Cash and term deposits A$216.1m; other financial assets A$225.5m, of which A$182.0m is the 4DMedical (4DX) note [S2].
- **4DX note:** A$10m hybrid loan to ASX-listed 4DMedical (12.5% coupon, matures 31 Jul 2027, payout linked to 4DX's share price) [S1][S2].
  It produced the A$172m paper gain at 4DX A$4.59 (30 Jun). 4DX is now A$2.19 (-52%, 16 Nov [S5]), so expect a large non-cash fair-value loss in H1 FY27 (own estimate, size not verified).
  Also A$10m Echo IQ convertible note [S1].
- Operating cash flow A$127.5m; less capitalised R&D and equipment = FCF A$118.8m = 82% of underlying NPAT (own calc [S2]); company says 91% cash conversion (definition not checked) [S1].
  Receivables rose to A$100.4m from A$64.4m (+56% vs revenue +23%) [S2]: watch it.
- Dividend FY26 69c, fully franked (+25%), about half of underlying NPAT; yield 0.34% [S1][S6]. Share count flat [S6].

## 7. Management and governance
- **Founder control:** co-founders Dr Sam Hupert (CEO; 24,196,660 shares) and Anthony Hall (Technology Director; 24,174,166) hold 46.3% together (30 Jun 2026 [S2]). They own the company since 1983.
  Aligned (they take no bonus or LTI; CEO pay A$1.07m [S2]), and they bought about A$3.4m of shares at A$108-122 in Feb 2026 [S7]. A takeover needs their consent.
- Related party: head-office lease A$215k a year from a company they control, assessed as arm's length [S2].
- Board: Chairman Peter Kempen (since 2008, chairman since 2010) and 3 other NEDs with 5-10+ years; new NED Timothy Reed (Oct 2026) [S7]. On 5 Nov 2026 Kempen moved 40,000 shares between his own entities at A$208, not a sale [S3].
- CFO changed (Clayton Hatch left 15 Mar 2026; Danny English, also company secretary) [S2]. No named CEO successor found (not verified).
- Capital use outside the core: small hybrid notes in 4DMedical and Echo IQ (A$20m) [S1]. Small, but it brings accounting noise.

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- Growth fade: H1 FY27 constant-currency revenue growth under about 20%, or a year with new signings under about A$250m (FY24-26: A$245-511m [S1]).
- A lost renewal or a price cut at renewal; so far 6 of 6 renewed at higher fees (FY26) [S1].
- Multiple compression: at 107x forward EPS a slower half can cut the price by half; it fell 67% from Jul 2025 to Feb 2026 [S5].
  Volatility 51% a year (2-yr weekly, USD, own calc [S5]).
- Strong AUD: 90% of revenue in USD; each 5% AUD rise cuts AUD revenue about 4-5% [S1].
- Reported-profit noise from the 4DX note (gain FY26, likely loss H1 FY27) can mislead screens.
- Key-person: the founders run product and the company.
