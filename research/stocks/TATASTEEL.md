## Summary
- **WATCH (not now).** Entry Rs 150-160, and only after the UK unit reaches break-even (Q4 FY27 results, about mid-May 2027) or a signed Dutch deal (deadline 1 Mar 2027); then Rs 2 L in two steps (Rs 1 L at 160 or less, Rs 1 L at 145 or less).
- What it is: India's most profitable large steel maker (25% EBITDA margin at home) tied to a weak European arm (Netherlands small profit, UK loss); net debt Rs 91,423 Cr (2.4x EBITDA).
- Why wait: at 2.2x book (87th percentile of 10 years) the price already assumes Europe gets fixed; DCF at today's margins gives about Rs 120; brokers' targets Rs 210-219 are only +15-20%.
- 3-yr total return from Rs 182 (INR): low -36% / base +12% / high +73% (about -14% / +4% / +20% a year; section 9).

## 1. Business (plain words)
- Makes flat steel (car, appliance and building sheet), long steel (bars, wire) and pipes; 36 Mt a year capacity worldwide [N5].
- India (Jamshedpur, Kalinganagar, Neelachal/NINL in Odisha): Q2 FY27 revenue Rs 42,653 Cr, EBITDA Rs 10,634 Cr (25%),
  crude steel 6.18 Mt, deliveries 5.98 Mt [N1]. Has its own iron ore (low cost); about Rs 17,200 EBITDA per tonne,
  highest among Indian peers (Nuvama, Goldman) [N2].
- Netherlands (IJmuiden): Q2 revenue EUR 1,395 m, EBITDA EUR 72 m (Q1 EUR 4 m), deliveries 1.30 Mt; a key plant (DSP,
  about 18% of volume) was shut for most of Q1 over chromium-6 emissions and restarted in August [N1][N3].
- UK (Port Talbot): blast furnaces closed Sep 2024; now rolls bought-in slab. Q2 revenue GBP 411 m, EBITDA loss
  GBP 56 m, deliveries 0.38 Mt [N1]. A GBP 1.25 bn electric arc furnace (UK government pays GBP 500 m) was due end-2027;
  grid connection may push it to late 2028 or later; Tata is asking for compensation [N6][N7].

## 6. Policy, China, Europe
- India safeguard duty on flat steel: 12% / 11.5% / 11% over three years from 30-Dec-2025, ends Dec 2028 [M].
  Government will not raise it (prices already high) despite imports +29.5% in Apr-Aug 2026; China 31.8% of imports;
  a mill asked for 20%+ [N4]. Anti-dumping probe on hot-rolled steel from China, Japan, Russia since June 2026 [N4];
  result date not verified. Steel users (Punjab) want the duty waived [M].
- China: steel exports 95.5 Mt Jan-Oct 2026 (-2.3% y/y), 9.85 Mt in October, near record [M]. If its home demand falls
  further, more cheap steel comes to Asia; the duty only covers part of it.
- Europe: Dutch decarbonisation letter of intent extended to 1-Mar-2027; coke plant closure dates, slag stockpile,
  energy cost and permits still open; DRI-EAF route is the reference case, alternatives studied [N3]. EU carbon price
  (ETS) and border tax (CBAM) affect costs and imports [N3]. UK: EY hired by ministers to review Tata's funding request [N10].

## 8. Risks (what proves the WATCH/BUY thesis wrong)
- UK not at break-even in Q4 FY27, or EAF slips past 2028 with no extra state money [N2][N6].
- Dutch deal fails by 1-Mar-2027, or Tata must fund most of the green switch (multi-billion euro) [N3][N9].
- Indian steel prices fall: China export surge, duty not renewed after Dec 2028, new capacity from JSW/JSPL/SAIL.
- Coking coal up (imported, USD); a stronger rupee helps, a weaker one hurts costs.
- Net debt above 2.5x EBITDA during the NINL build. Price swings: 29% yearly volatility, -34% max fall in 5 years [Y].
