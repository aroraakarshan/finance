## 2. Business
- Builds and runs software for banks (25% of Q2 FY27 revenue), insurers (13%), travel (20%), healthcare/hi-tech
  (18%) and others; Americas 63%, EMEA 25%; 47,365 staff, attrition 10.4% [F1].
- Q2 FY27 (Sep 2026): revenue $652 m, +40.1% y/y, +10.1% q/q; organic constant-currency +3.8% q/q; EBIT
  16.0%; PAT +56.5% but EPS only +18.3% (Rs 13.3: new shares); missed the revenue poll; FY27 EBIT guide 16% (from
  15.5%), 17% aimed for FY28 [F1][N1]. 12-month order book $2.38 bn (+45%); FY30 goal $5 bn (15% organic) [F1][N2].
- Acquisitions (the user asked):
  - **Cigniti** (software testing, Hyderabad): 54% bought for cash at Rs 1,415/share in 2024 [N3]; merged 4 Jun 2026
    with 12,671,602 new shares [E1]. Company claim: its EBITDA margin rose from 11% to 19% [N4].
  - **Encora** (US, $516 m revenue in 2024, 7-10% organic growth, owned by Advent and Warburg Pincus) [N5][N6]:
    EV $2.35-2.5 bn, about 21x EBITDA and 3.9x sales, paid with 93,796,508 shares at Rs 1,815.91 (allotted 23 Apr
    2026) plus a $550 m 3-year loan at 4.6% fixed [E1][N7]. In the accounts from 1 May 2026; $100.7 m revenue in its
    first two months [N8]. Elara, JM, Morgan Stanley, DAM called it expensive and EPS-dilutive [N6].
  - **Sabre** deal: $1.56 bn over 13 years (Mar 2025), about $120 m a year, under 5% of revenue [N9]. The client is
    weak: Sabre $1.80 a share, market cap $0.72 bn, debt $4.4 bn [Y]; it refinanced its secured debt to 2032 at
    9.875% in Sep 2026 [N10]. HSBC flagged the deal's upfront savings and low cash conversion (Sep 2025) [N11].
    Coforge has since exited its data-centre business [E3].
- Moat: domain depth in insurance, travel, banking; 95.5% repeat business [F1]. Rivals: Persistent, LTM, Mphasis.

## 8. Risks (and what proves the thesis wrong)
- Organic growth fades: thesis wrong if organic constant-currency growth is under 2% q/q for 2 quarters, or the
  12-month order book stops growing y/y. Encora's pre-deal growth was only 7-10% [N6].
- Integration and goodwill: Rs 22,139 Cr of goodwill and intangibles; a write-down or EBIT margin under 15% breaks it.
- Share supply: PE holders' 93.8 m shares (Rs 18,900 Cr, about 40 days of trading at Rs 472 Cr/day [Y]); MSCI
  buying (Nuvama: $630 m, about Rs 5,900 Cr [N16]) ends 30 Nov. Either can cap the price for months.
- Sabre ($4.4 bn debt) default: about 4-5% of revenue plus unbilled assets. Also AI price cuts, hedge losses, H-1B.
- Price: 39% yearly volatility [Y]; fell 46% from Dec 2025 to 16 Mar 2026 (Rs 1,074 close) [Y].
