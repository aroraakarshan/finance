## 1. Business (plain words)
- Sells a small sensor worn on the arm for 10-15 days that reads glucose every few minutes and sends it to a phone or insulin pump. Revenue is almost all repeat sensor sales (10-Q Q3 2026), so it behaves like a consumable, not a one-off device.
- Q3 2026 revenue $1,344 m (+11%): US $970 m (+14%), international $375 m (+5%, lapping 2025 reimbursement wins) (8-K ex. 99.1 and call, 2026-10-29).
- Products: G7 and G7 15 Day (about 50% of US users switched by end-2026; Canada launch Nov 2026, Japan early 2027), Dexcom ONE+ (cheaper, abroad), **Stelo** (first over-the-counter CGM, Aug 2024, for type-2 not on insulin and pre-diabetes; cleared for children 2+ in June 2026; new app Q3 2026). Stelo revenue is not disclosed (not verified). G8 in clinical work before FDA filing (call).
- Moat: accuracy (G7 15 Day MARD 8.0%, 10-K), links to most US insulin pumps, Share/Follow for parents, payer contracts. US new-patient starts at a record in 2026 (call).
- **Type-2 non-insulin**: the big untapped market. CONNECT randomised trial (NEJM, Oct 2026): A1c -1.6 points in the CGM arm, 0.9 points better than control at 6 months (call). Dexcom expects a CMS coverage decision this year; its plan assumes coverage from mid-2027 (call; Trefis 2026-11-16). ADA Standards of Care update expected "in the coming weeks" (call).
- **GLP-1 drugs**: 10-K calls them "a companion product" but says they "could potentially compete" and reduce sales. So far no visible hit: active users grow in the upper teens (call). Long term, fewer type-2 patients progressing to insulin is a real risk (my view).

## 2. Competition: Abbott Libre and the pump makers
- Abbott Diabetes Care Q3 2026 $2,185 m (+6.2%); its CGM sales +7.3% reported (Abbott 8-K ex. 99.1, 2026-10-21) vs Dexcom +11%: Dexcom is gaining share; it says the largest gains are in type-2 incl. non-insulin (call). Abbott flagged CGM pricing pressure, mostly cash-pay abroad; Dexcom says its pricing is stable (call Q&A).
- Abbott got its own FDA warning letter on Libre CGMs (Drug Delivery Business headline 2026-02-03; not read). FDA approved Abbott's Libre Duo (glucose + ketone sensor), Aug 2026 (Abbott release).
- CVS (PBM) formulary loss: under 4% of Dexcom US revenue, small 2027 effect expected (CFO, call). New "Dexcom Direct" access service early 2027.
- FDA guidance of 2026-01-06 widens "general wellness" devices (incl. glucose readings): opens the door to Big Tech wearables (10-K risk factor).
- Pump makers are partners as much as rivals: MiniMed (Medtronic's spin-off, IPO $20 in Mar 2026, separation completed Oct 2026; Reuters, Medtronic) sells its own Simplera CGM; Insulet (Omnipod) and Tandem pair with Dexcom and Libre.

## 4. Margins and returns
- Gross margin fell 68.6% (2021) -> 60.1% (2025) during G7 ramp and quality problems, now back to 64.3% GAAP / 65.1% non-GAAP in Q3 2026, the best since Q4 2022 (release, call): scrap, freight, 15-day sensors. Ireland plant started production Q4 2026; start-up cost weighs into early 2027 (call).
- TTM: revenue $5,104 m, GAAP operating income $1,224 m (24.0%), net income about $1,002 m (own calc, XBRL).
- ROE about 38% TTM (Yahoo 37.7%), inflated by buybacks shrinking equity ($2.5 bn book). NOPAT / (equity + debt) about 24% (own calc, 21.7% tax rate from 10-Q). Stock pay $160 m (3.4% of 2025 revenue).

## 7. Risks and what proves the thesis wrong
- **FDA warning letter (March 2025)**, San Diego and Mesa plants, for manufacturing and quality-system faults and sensor changes made without FDA clearance (10-K; MedTech Dive 2025-03-26). The 10-K (Feb 2026) still lists "responding to the FDA warning letter"; the Q3 2026 10-Q does not mention it; no close-out letter found (status not verified). Escalation (import alert, consent decree) would hurt supply.
- **Litigation** (10-Q): G6/G7 user class actions (consolidated May 2026, plus one in LA) on device accuracy; securities suit on G7 reliability (Jul 2024-Sep 2025, stayed); first securities suit dismissed with prejudice 2026-08-27, under appeal (Dexcom brief due 2026-12-09). FDA flagged an app software issue (Drugwatch headline 2026-01-21).
- **Medicare competitive bidding**: CGMs added; contracts in 2027, payment cut from **2028-01-01**, paid at the 75th percentile of winning bids, about 10 contracts (10-K); bidder registration opens 2026-12-01 (HME News 2026-11-06). Size of cut not known.
- Abbott price war abroad; Big Tech wellness wearables; GLP-1 shrinking the insulin-using population; payer losses like CVS.
- Thesis wrong if: quarterly growth <9% for two quarters, gross margin back below 62%, CMS says no to type-2 non-insulin, or the warning letter escalates.
- Volatility 38% (1y) / 47% (5y); max drawdown -66% (2021-11-17 to 2025-11-10) (yfinance, own calc).
