## 2. Business
- It raises money from pension funds (72% of AUM) and wealthy UK savers (28%), invests it, and charges a yearly fee on the money (80% of revenue). On top come performance fees when it sells assets well (7%), marketing fees on retail sales (8%), and arrangement and monitoring fees [S3].
- Real Assets: AUM GBP 11.1 bn (solar, wind, batteries, forests, Australian infrastructure). Private Equity: GBP 1.9 bn (16 regional UK funds, VCTs) [S2].
  Core EBITDA split: Real Assets 72%, PE 28%; AUM by place: UK 54%, Australia 28%, Europe 18% [S4].
- Retail products are UK tax wrappers: Business Relief (BR) inheritance tax products (ITS/AITS/EITS; it is number 1 in unquoted BR fundraising) and two VCTs. These raised a record GBP 630m in FY26 and carry higher fees [S1][S4].
- Sold its public-markets unit (FCM, GBP 1.0 bn AUM) to Guinness on 4 Sep 2026; all FY25-FY26 figures here are for the remaining business unless stated [S2][S7].
- Average management fee rate 1.5% (1.1% in FY21) [S4]; own check: recurring revenue GBP 135.3m / average FUM GBP 8.73 bn = 1.55%.

## 7. Management and governance
- Co-founder Bernard Fairman is Executive Chairman (combined role) and holds 29.5%. With CEO Gary Fraser (4.1%) and CIO David Hughes (1.9%) the concert party holds 35.4% and counts as a controlling shareholder [S12].
  A Rule 9 waiver (passed by 96% of independents, 31 Jul 2026) lets buybacks lift them to 39.4% without a bid [S12].
- 2026 AGM: 27% of independent votes were against NED Michael Liston; pay report 7% against. Long-serving Guernsey NED Geoff Gavey left on 31 Jul 2026; John Le Poidevin now chairs Audit [S12][S13]. Auditor BDO.
- Follows the UK Corporate Governance Code (ESCC listing) [S3]. Insider ownership aligns interests, but outside holders cannot force a sale.

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- **UK tax rules (biggest risk):** VCT upfront relief was cut from 30% to 20% from 6 Apr 2026 (Budget 26 Nov 2025). The 28 Oct 2026 Budget did not restore it [S14][S15].
  The same Budget plans "greater compliance activity on fee arrangements" in VCT/EIS [S16]. BR: 100% relief only up to GBP 2.5m per estate from Apr 2026 (50% above) [S17]. Pensions come into inheritance tax from 6 Apr 2027 (more demand for IHT products) [S16].
  A future Budget that cuts BR further would hit the highest-fee part of the business.
- FEIP II far short of its EUR 1.25 bn target by June 2027; the FY29 GBP 118m guidance missed.
- Australian funds are paying out to investors who want their money back (AUM runs off), plus AUD swings. Listed trusts it manages (FGEN, FSFL) trade at discounts and have already cut fees (-GBP 3.2m in FY26) [S3].
- Performance fees (GBP 11.6m, 7% of revenue) are lumpy.
- Thesis wrong if: retail raise under GBP 500m a year, FY27 core EBITDA down, or BR relief cut again.
