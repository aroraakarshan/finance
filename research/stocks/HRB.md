## 2. IRS Direct File, AI and Intuit - the three threats
- **Direct File (status Nov 2026): suspended.** IRS free e-filing pilot ran in the 2024 and 2025 seasons; the administration confirmed in Nov 2025 it would not be offered for the 2026 season (140,803 returns in 2024, 12 states) [S10]; the FY26 10-K (Aug 2026) says "that program has been suspended" but may be reinstated; IRS Free File Inc. (industry-run) agreement runs to Oct 2029 [S3]. Nothing found on Direct File for the 2027 season (Bing News, 2026-11-17) - it is a political risk (a future administration could restart it), not a 2027 one.
- **AI tax prep**: the 10-K lists AI as a risk that may "reduce the need for third-party tax preparation" [S3]. Evidence so far: HRB's DIY users shrink (above), assisted grows on price, not volume. Intuit cut about 17% of staff "as AI pressure reshapes" its tax business (headlines 2026-05-20/21 [S14]); Intuit fell 12% on 2026-08-26 after guiding TurboTax units +2-3% and was -46% YTD (247wallst [S12]). No consumer AI agent that files a US return end to end was found in news (Bing, 2026-11-17): not verified either way.
- **Intuit**: about USD 92 bn company, TurboTax leads DIY; HRB's DIY is about 10% of HRB revenue. Weekly correlation HRB-INTU 0.43 (2 yr) [S7]: the market trades them as one AI-loser basket. HRB's Feb 2026 fall (-8.6% on 11 Feb, low USD 28.34 on 12 Feb) came with Q2 results (3 Feb) and AI-related software selling; the exact cause is not verified.
- Jackson Hewitt (private, office tax prep, still active - Nov 2026 press [S14]; owner and size not verified). Liberty Tax also private. No listed pure peer except Intuit.

## 8. What proves the thesis wrong
- US assisted returns fall two seasons in a row (FY26: total -0.1%, company-owned +2.0%), or NAC growth stops - price-led growth then ends.
- DIY paid returns falling faster than 5-10%/yr, or Intuit/AI agents pricing full-service help near zero.
- Direct File (or a state equivalent) restarts at national scale.
- Buybacks fund themselves with debt (net debt above 1.5x EBITDA) or the dividend raise stops.
- Free File / pixel-tracking regulators or class actions produce a large fine (accrued, amount not disclosed [S3]).
