## 1. Business
- Sells CABOMETYX (cabozantinib, a daily pill that blocks tumour blood-vessel and growth signals) in the US for kidney, liver, thyroid and neuroendocrine cancers; Ipsen (outside US/Japan) and Takeda (Japan) sell it and pay royalties.
- Q3 2026 (8-K ex. 99.1, 2026-10-29): revenue $658.7 m = US cabozantinib $604.9 m (+11%) + collaboration $53.8 m (of which royalties $51.8 m). Price rises and volume both helped.
- Next drug: zanzalintinib ("zanza"), a newer pill of the same type. Filed in 3rd-line colorectal cancer (with Roche's atezolizumab); phase 3 trials in kidney (STELLAR-304; with Merck's belzutifan: LITESPARK-033/-034), neuroendocrine (STELLAR-311), early colorectal (STELLAR-316); phase 2 in lung and meningioma (10-Q Q3 2026).
- Moat: patents and the oncologist habit around CABOMETYX; no moat beyond the patent dates in section 7.

## 3. Margins and returns
- Gross margin about 96% (COGS ~4% of product sales, guidance). Operating margin 9% (2023) -> 38% (2025) -> 40% TTM as R&D fell and sales grew. SBC $113 m in 2025 (5% of revenue); non-GAAP excludes it.
- ROE: 2023 9%, 2024 23%, 2025 35% (net income / avg equity, XBRL) -> 3-yr average 22% (the screen's figure); TTM 44% (Yahoo 44%), inflated because buybacks shrank equity to $1.83 bn. ## 8. Risks / what proves the thesis wrong
- Zanza: FDA rejection or a narrow label on 2027-03-03; STELLAR-303 missed its second primary endpoint (non-liver-metastasis group, June 2026). STELLAR-304 negative in Q1 2027. Either would leave a single drug with 4 years of patent life: P/E would fall toward 10.
- Handa capsules or another 505(b)(2) (Almatica, MSN fumarate) launching before 2030 and taking share; MSN winning on '039 (entry 2030-01-15).
- Concentration: cabozantinib 92% of revenue; US price politics (IRA from 2029).
- Volatility 36%/yr (5y); max fall -57% (2018, yfinance own calc). Beta 0.4-0.8.
