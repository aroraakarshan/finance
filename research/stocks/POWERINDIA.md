## 1. Business in plain words
- Builds the "highways" of the power grid: power transformers, gas-insulated switchgear (GIS), substations, STATCOMs
  (voltage support), HVDC converter stations (long-distance links, e.g. Khavda-Nagpur, Bhadla), grid automation and
  service. Formerly ABB Power Products (2019 Hitachi-ABB JV); listed March 2020 [S1].
- Customers Q2 FY27 (domestic orders): utilities 38%, industries 34%, transport/infra 28%; channel shifted to EPC
  contractors (62%, from 5%) as transmission moves to private tariff-bid (TBCB) projects [C1]. Exports about 25-26% of
  orders (Asia, Africa, Middle East, Europe, USA incl. TenneT) [C2]. New: data centres (hyperscaler orders), battery
  storage (first BESS order Q1 FY27), semiconductor fabs [C1][C2].
- Moat: Hitachi Energy's global technology (HVDC is a 3-4 firm world market), long test records with Power Grid.
  Demand: India approved Rs 1.86 lakh Cr Green Energy Corridor III and a 1,150 kV system (Rs 68,816 Cr) [C1].

## 6. Risks - what proves the thesis wrong
- Operating EBITDA margin below 14% for two quarters (HVDC mix, price pressure), or PAT growth staying near 20%
  while sales grow 60%: then FY27-28 consensus (+55% / +46%) falls and the P/E is re-rated.
- Orders without HVDC turning negative y/y; the next greenfield HVDC bid (management expected an award within about
  6 months of Aug 2026 [C2]) going to a rival.
- Competition: India let 4 Chinese makers (incl. TBEA) into transformers/GIS with 60-65% local content (raised on the
  Aug 2026 call; management sees no material impact [C2]); GVT&D, Siemens Energy and BHEL all adding capacity.
- Execution delays on HVDC (first-year revenue is slow by design [C2]); transmission delays and unsigned PPAs
  (`research/sectors/utilities-power/india.md` section 3).
- Price risk: volatility 44%/yr; fell 40% to Jan 2025 and traded Rs 16,214 in Jan 2026 vs Rs 38,445 in May 2026 [Y].
