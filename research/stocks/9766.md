## 6. Valuation and the P/E discrepancy
**Cause found:** Yahoo's quarterly table puts the 9-month (Apr-Dec 2025) result in the Dec-25
"quarter" (revenue 353.0 bn vs true 128.2 bn; net income 74.3 bn vs 27.9 bn) [S5][S6]. Its trailing EPS
1,322.35 therefore double-counts H1 FY3/26.
Company TTM EPS = FY3/26 737.80 + H1 FY3/27 584.54 - H1 FY3/26 342.92 = **979.4** [S1][S7] ->
P/E 29,180 / 979.4 = **29.8** (stockanalysis 29.79 matches). Forward (company guide 995.90): **29.3**.
EV/EBITDA 15.8-15.9 agrees in both sources (annual EBITDA, not affected).

  Kozuki Capital 4.91% (31 Mar 2026, of 143.5 m issued shares) [S12]. Founder Kagemasa Kozuki

  TSE capital-efficiency standards. Controversies: none checked in this run.

## 8. Risks
- Hit-driven: profit depends on a few live titles (eFootball, Yu-Gi-Oh!); a weak year cut EPS 36%
  (FY3/23) [S6]. World-Cup-year boost will not repeat in FY3/28.
- Platform fees and app-store rules; game delays (Silent Hill/Metal Gear pipeline).
- Price: 6-month +47 pts vs Nikkei (scout screen); 3-yr volatility 39.9% (own calc); 10-yr max drawdown
  -59% (Mar 2020, own calc) [S5].
- Yen: strong yen lowers overseas sales in yen; small effect on Japan-booked sales.
