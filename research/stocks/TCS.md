## Summary
- **BUY IN STEPS, Rs 2.5 L in all (cap Rs 4.2 L):** Rs 1.0 L now at Rs 2,350-2,450; Rs 75,000 at or below Rs 2,200; Rs 75,000 after Q3 results (expected about 11 Jan 2027) if constant-currency growth stays positive and EBIT margin >= 24%, or at Rs 2,050 any time.
- What it is: India's largest IT-services firm (Rs 8.69 lakh Cr market cap); Tata Sons owns 71.77%. Runs and rebuilds software for big Western clients, paid mainly in foreign currency.
- Why: P/E 15.9 (adjusted) vs a 10-yr median of 26.7, cheaper than on 97% of days since 2016; ROE 52%; no debt; trailing dividend 4.7%. Growth is slow (+2.8% y/y in constant currency) and AI is cutting prices on old work.
- 3-yr total return (INR, before tax): low -5% / base +45% / high +91% (about -2% / +13% / +24% a year; section 9).
- Choose TCS or ITBEES, not both (weekly correlation 0.89; TCS is 19.8% of ITBEES). TCS is cheaper; ITBEES spreads the risk.

## 2. Business
- Sells IT services: application build and support, cloud and data work, consulting, back-office (BPO) and some
  products (TCS BaNCS for banks), mostly for large Western firms; BFSI (banks, insurers) leads. Region split not verified.
- Q2 FY27 (Sep 2026): revenue $7,642 m, +0.5% q/q and +2.8% y/y in constant currency; Rs 73,188 Cr, +11.2% y/y
  in rupees (the gap is the rupee fall to about Rs 94/$) [N1][N2]. Annualised AI revenue $3.1 bn, over 10% of
  revenue, up from $2.6 bn a quarter earlier [N1][N3]. Deal wins (TCV) $9.6 bn [N1]. Staff 598,056, attrition 13.3% [N4].
- New bets: HyperVault data centres (Rs 18,000 Cr with TPG, TPG up to Rs 8,820 Cr, Nov 2025 [N5]; plus a 1 GW,
  Rs 70,000 Cr campus in Hyderabad, Sep 2026 [N6]; revenue from it after 18-24 months [N7]). Bought Porsche's
  consulting arm MHP for EUR 320 m with a EUR 1.5 bn 5-year Porsche contract (Aug 2026) [N1][N8].
- Moat: scale, multi-year contracts, switching cost, Tata brand. Rivals: Infosys, HCLTech, Wipro, Accenture, Cognizant.

  Rs 41,000 Cr [Y][S1]. Fixed assets up to Rs 32,872 Cr from Rs 23,053 Cr (Mar 2025): data-centre spending [S1].
- Free cash flow FY26 Rs 48,013 Cr (FCF yield 5.5% on market cap); cash from operations 92% of operating profit [S1].
- Debtor days rose to 93 (FY26) from 81-84 (FY23-25) [S1]: clients paying slower. Watch.
- Dividends: Rs 112 per share in the last 12 months (Rs 57 Jan 2026, 31 May, 12 Jul, 12 Oct) [Y] = 4.7%; FY26
  dividend Rs 110 [N7]; payout 81% of FY26 profit [S1]. Screener shows 2.66%, Yahoo 1.99%: both seem to drop
  part of the Jan 2026 payout (special part not verified). Buybacks: 5 since 2017 (Rs 16,000-18,000 Cr each;
  last Rs 17,000 Cr in 2023) [N11]; none since. From 1 Apr 2026 buyback money is taxed as capital gain again

## 7. Management and governance

  12.7% (Mar 2024) to 9.0% (Sep 2026), DIIs rose to 13.6% [S1].
- Capital allocation: high payout (81-100% most years) [S1]; first real non-organic bets (HyperVault, MHP).
  Brokerages flagged MHP integration risk and falling MHP revenue (Citi, HSBC; headline only) [N8].
- People: FY26 workforce restructuring (layoffs) [N4]; senior staff denied quarterly bonuses after missed targets
  (headline only, Oct 2026). US suspended TCS, Infosys, Wipro and others from the green-card (PERM) programme
  on 8 Oct 2026; TCS says its PERM filings are "single digits" and plans 15,000 local US hires [N12].

## 8. Risks (and what proves the thesis wrong)
- AI deflation outruns AI revenue: thesis wrong if constant-currency revenue falls y/y for 2 quarters, or AI
  revenue grows under 10% q/q while non-AI keeps falling 3%+.
- Margin: EBIT margin under 23% (MHP, AI spend, wage hikes) with no growth pickup.
- H-1B: $100,000 fee on new petitions extended to 21 Sep 2027 but blocked by courts; USCIS may collect
  retroactively if the government wins [S23]. TCS has cut H-1B registrations [S24]; cost not verified.
- Capex: HyperVault makes a cash machine capital-heavy; a dividend cut would hurt. Rupee: a stronger rupee cuts profit.
- Price: 30% yearly volatility; Rs 3,187 (Dec 2025) to Rs 1,961 (1 Jul 2026), now up 18% from the Sep close of
  Rs 2,040 [Y]. Steps, not one buy.
