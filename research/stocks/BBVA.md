## 2. Business

- Retail and corporate banking in 5 areas. Gross income, last 12 months to Q3 2026 (EUR bn) [B3]: Mexico 17.1 (40%), Spain 10.1 (24%), Turkey 6.5 (15%), South America 6.4 (15%), Rest of Business (CIB in US/Europe/Asia, digital banks in Italy/Germany) 2.4 (6%); corporate centre -0.7.
- Net attributable profit Jan-Sep 2026 (EUR bn) [B4]: Mexico 4.52, Spain 3.11, South America 0.82, Rest of Business 0.80, Turkey 0.79; group 9.07. **Mexico = 50% of group profit** (own calc 4.52 / 9.07), so "about half" is confirmed.
- Moat: number 1 bank in Mexico with a 30.7% efficiency ratio there [B4]; low-cost digital model (group efficiency 37.9%); scale in Spain. 84.0 m active customers (30-09-26) [B5].
- Competitors: Santander, CaixaBank, Sabadell (Spain); Banorte, Santander Mexico (Mexico); Isbank, Akbank (Turkey) - Mexico/Turkey rival list from general knowledge, not verified.
- Sabadell bid: **failed.** CNMV result 2025-10-16: only 25.5% of Sabadell voting rights accepted, below the minimum, so the offer lapsed [B6]. BBVA then switched the capital to buybacks.
- Hyperinflation accounting applies to Turkey and Argentina (income translated at period-end FX, net monetary loss booked in results) [B5]. This makes Turkish profit noisy.

## 7. Management and governance

- Chair Carlos Torres Vila (led the failed Sabadell bid); CEO Onur Genc [B4][B6]. No controlling shareholder: 252 holders of 500k+ shares own 68.8% (mostly custodians) [B5]; insiders 0.17% [B2].
- Capital allocation: after the bid failed, excess CET1 above 12% went to buybacks (about EUR 6 bn in 2026) [B5][B6]. Good discipline since.
- Controversy: Villarejo spying case - Spain's High Court sent BBVA (as a legal entity) and ex-chair Francisco Gonzalez to trial (2026-07-09); prosecutor seeks a fine above USD 200 m (2026-03-19) (Reuters/Euronews headlines only; trial date not verified) [B9].

## 8. Risks

- Mexico (half of profit): MXN, US tariffs and US-Mexico politics, rate cuts squeezing NII (specific Mexican policy risks not verified in this run).
- Turkey: lira, hyperinflation accounting, high cost of risk (2.55%); Argentina similar.
- Spain: rate cycle (ECB deposit rate 2.50%, hikes in 2026 help NII) [B10]; Spanish bank levy (not verified).
- Valuation: re-rated from P/B 0.9 to 2.2 in two years; a miss could cut the multiple fast. Stock fell premarket after Q3 despite beating (Investing.com headline 2026-10-29) [B9].
- Legal: Villarejo trial (section 7). Currency: EUR plus EM currencies inside the earnings.
