## 8. Risks

- HK/China property: more write-downs (Hui Xian -6.0 bn in H1 2026), thin margins on dear land, HIBOR/rates [K1][K2].
- UK (34% of revenue): pub costs (wages, business rates), GBP, water regulation (Northumbrian) [K1].
- Idle cash or a big low-return deal; control discount persists; US-China/HK sanctions risk to the CK group. Volatility 23%/yr (5y); max fall -51% (2018 peak to Mar 2020) (own calc [K5]).
