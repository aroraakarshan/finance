## 2. Business (plain words)
- Makes Pulsar/Platina/Boxer motorcycles, RE/Maxima auto-rickshaws (about 90% share in CNG passenger 3W), Chetak e-scooters, e-3W and spares (Rs 2,000 cr a quarter) [F2].
- Exports: Q2 FY27 7.8 lakh units (+41%), about USD 800 m, about 40% of revenue. Africa motorcycles +83% vs industry +35%; LatAm +44% vs +12%; Nigeria share about 60% but volumes still 60% of the old peak; MENA -24% [F2]. Bajaj is 48% of India's 2W exports and 63% of 3W exports (H1 FY27) [F2].
- Domestic 2W: deliberately losing share in 100-125cc to protect price; Pulsar range refreshed Aug-Sep 2026, 2 new brands (125-250cc) due in FY27 [F2]. October 2026: domestic 2W +10%, exports +18%, total +14% [F5].
- Chetak/EV: 23% e-scooter share, about 45K retail in Sep 2026; EV (Chetak + e-3W) is over 20% of revenue and now earns double-digit EBITDA, helped by PLI incentives at 18% of EV revenue in H2 FY27 [F2]. Capacity to 80K/month by Q4 FY27 [F2].
- KTM (Bajaj Mobility AG, Vienna/Swiss listed): Bajaj controls 74.9% since 18 Nov 2025 after KTM's 2025 insolvency restructuring; paid Rs 4,456 cr for the rest of the holding company [F3]. Q3/26: revenue EUR 351 m (+57%), EBIT EUR 11.3 m (3.2%) - turnaround, not yet a profit engine [F6].
- Triumph: Bajaj makes and sells the Triumph 400 range; about 11K units in Q2; KTM + Triumph India about 40K units (+31%) [F2].
- Bajaj Auto Credit (BACL): captive lender; AUM about Rs 21,000 cr (1.5x in a year), Q2 PAT Rs 243 cr, CAR 19.5%, ROE 26% [F2].

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- Exports fall back below 6.5 lakh units a quarter (Nigeria currency or politics, LatAm slowdown, shipping), or the rupee strengthens well below Rs 95/USD: currency was a main margin driver in FY27 [F2].
- EBITDA margin below 19% for 2 quarters: input inflation not passed on, or PLI incentives end [F2].
- Domestic 125cc+ share keeps falling after the Pulsar refresh (check SIAM/Vahan by Mar 2027) [F2].
- KTM needs more money or slips back to EBIT losses; BACL credit costs keep rising [F6][F3].
- Big capital allocation surprise (another large overseas buy).
