- What it is: US biotech: Dupixent (with Sanofi), Eylea / Eylea HD (eye), Libtayo (cancer), about 50 pipeline drugs. $955.47 (Rs 89,845 per share); market cap $98.8 bn; net cash $16.9 bn.
- Verdict: **WATCH (long-term only; kept as candidate)** - beats the non-US alternative Astellas (growth, net cash, no near patent cliff) even after US estate tax (section 10a).
- What to do: do not buy now: P/E is above its own median after +38% in 1 year, so wait for a better price (the Rs 40k cap applies only to the trading budget; as a long-term idea, one share fits the 1%-per-idea limit, research/global/README.md section 3, approved by the user 2026-11-15). Consider only as a long-term buy nearer its median P/E (about $840).
- 1-3 yr return (to Nov 2028, USD, incl. dividends): low -19% / base +22% / high +68% (section 9).

## 4. Margins and returns

- Operating margin fell 56% (2021) -> 25% (2025) as R&D rose $2.86 bn -> $5.85 bn (41% of 2025 revenue); Q3 2026 back to 40% with the Sanofi step-up.
- ROE (net income / avg equity, XBRL): 2024 16%, 2025 15%, TTM 15% (Yahoo 15%). Low because of the large cash pile ($18.9 bn of cash and investments in $33.5 bn equity).
