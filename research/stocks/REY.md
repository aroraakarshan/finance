## Summary
- **WATCH, do not buy at EUR 132.80:** buy Rs 2.0 L (about 16 shares) at or below EUR 115, add Rs 1.5 L at or below EUR 100 (Rs 3.5 L in all, under the Rs 4.2 L cap). Buy higher, up to EUR 140, only if FY2026 results (about 11-12 Mar 2027) show organic growth >= 6% and EBITDA margin >= 17.5%.
- What it is: an Italian network of about 200 small IT consulting firms (17,628 staff) building AI, cloud and digital projects for big European companies. The founding Rizzante family controls 64% of the votes. It has EUR 397 m net cash.
- Why wait: the price is up 72% from its March low. P/E 18.1 is below its own 10-yr median of 22.6, but level with IT-services peers, not "41% below". Weekly correlation with MSFT is 0.54, and he already has TCS (in steps) for IT services.
- 3-yr total return before tax: EUR low -18% / base +32% / high +79%; INR -11% / +44% / +95% (about -4% / +13% / +25% a year; section 8).

## 1. Business
- Consulting, system integration and digital services for telco/media, manufacturing, banks, insurers, energy, retail and public sector [R1]. A group of specialist units ("Data Reply", "Cluster Reply" etc.) [R1].
- 9M 2026 revenue by region [R1]: Region 1 (Italy, USA, Brazil, Poland, Romania) 62.8%; Region 2 (Germany, Switzerland, Croatia) 17.8%; Region 3 (UK, Benelux, France) 19.4%. Italy is not reported on its own.
- By business line [R1]: Technologies 58.9% (60.3%), Applications 32.6% (29.8%), Processes 8.5% (9.9%).
- AI offer: Reply Model Factory, agentic and "physical" AI (robots, digital twins) [R1]. Mistral AI partnership (Mar 2026), NVIDIA Physical AI specialisation (Oct 2026) [N5]. **AI revenue is not disclosed** (none in the Q3 report).
- 2026 deals: Cyberis and Movar (UK), FedTec (US federal IT, Jul 2026), Labyrinth (Italy, 70%) [R1]. Moat: client relationships, specialist brands, founder culture; rivals Accenture, Capgemini, Sopra Steria, Indian IT.

## 6. Management and governance
- Founder Mario Rizzante is Chairman and CEO; his daughter Tatiana Rizzante is CEO; his son Filippo Rizzante is an executive director. Board of 10: 5 executive, 5 non-executive, of whom 4 are independent. Auditor PwC [R1][R7].
- **Control:** Alika S.r.l. holds 37.08% of the capital and **63.87% of the votes** [R3]. Mario Rizzante owns 100% of Iceberg, and Iceberg owns 51% of Alika [R3].
  - Loyalty votes, adopted Sep 2024, give up to 10 votes per share for long holders [R7]. Minority holders cannot outvote the family, and a takeover premium is unlikely.
  - Alika sold 1 m shares in an accelerated sale in Jul 2025 (headline only; price not verified). Free float 60% [R7].
- Pay and related parties: executive directors EUR 10.6 m and key managers EUR 8.5 m in 2025 (about 7.6% of net profit) [R3]. Head office services are bought from Alika (exempt related-party deal) [R3]; related-party office services were EUR 2.7 m in 9M 2026 [R1].
- Record: 10-yr revenue CAGR 13.4% and 14.7%/yr shareholder return 2013-25, per the company [R8]. 2025 goodwill write-off EUR 19 m [R1].

## 7. Risks (and what proves the thesis wrong)
- AI deflation catches up. Thesis wrong if organic growth stays under 4% for 2 quarters, revenue per person falls, or the EBITDA margin (ex one-offs) drops below 17%.
- Germany shrinks y/y, or the UK pre-tax margin falls under 2%. Those regions are 37% of revenue but 15% of pre-tax profit [R2].
- M&A: earn-outs swing EBIT by EUR 13-17 m a year [R1]. Write-offs (EUR 19 m in 2025). FedTec brings US federal budget risk.
- Family control (above). Italian corporate governance: minority holders have little say.
- Price: 32% annual volatility. It went from EUR 77.15 (24 Mar 2026 close) to 132.80, and the July buyback paid EUR 97 [Y][R9]. Liquidity about 73,000 shares/day (EUR 9.7 m) [Y].
