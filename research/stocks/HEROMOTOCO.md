## Summary
- **Fit for a 17 Nov buy in steps: BUY Rs 1.5 L at the open only at or below Rs 5,450 (about 28 shares); add Rs 1.5 L at or below Rs 4,950, or after November retail data (FADA, about 5-8 Dec) show Hero's share recovering (max Rs 3 L).**
- What it is: India's biggest two-wheeler maker by dispatches (85% of the 100cc bike market), cheap commuter bikes for rural and small-town India, plus VIDA e-scooters and a 32.8% stake in Ather Energy [C1][C4].
- Why: clean P/E 18.8 vs its 10-yr median 20.2 and peers at 23-55; after taking out the Ather stake and net cash the core business trades at about 13.5x profit; 3.45% dividend yield; not held by any of your funds. Against: Honda beat Hero in retail for months and October retail fell 40.6% (industry -28%).
- 3-yr total return from Rs 5,360 (INR, with dividends before tax): low -9% / base +33% / high +71% (about -3% / +10% / +20% a year).

## 1. Business
- Bikes and scooters for mostly rural and small-town buyers; 8 plants, capacity 8.63 m units a year [C2]. Q2 FY27 dispatches
  18.68 lakh (+10%): motorcycles 16.28 lakh (+6.5%), scooters ICE+EV 2.40 lakh (+47%) [C2].
- Market share H1 FY27 (SIAM dispatches): 100cc 85.0%, 125cc 17.7%, scooters 7.1%, premium 1.9%, exports 6.0%; EV 11.0% (VAHAN) [C2].
- Other income streams: parts, accessories, merchandise Rs 1,941 Cr in Q2 (+27%) [C2]; Harley-Davidson X440 (made by Hero) [C1].
- Associates (equity method): Ather Energy (32.8% fully diluted after buying Rs 1,758 Cr more in Aug 2026 at about Rs 1,480/share [C5]),
  Hero FinCorp (unlisted lender; IPO approved by SEBI in May 2025, current status not verified [N3]), Euler Motors (3-wheeler EVs) [C1].
- Exports: 54 countries; Colombia about 25% of exports, Bangladesh also large [C3]. Board added Rs 300 Cr for Colombia/Brazil [C1].

## 3. Margins and returns
- OPM 11-16% for 10 years; 13.4% standalone EBITDA in Q2 FY27 (flat q/q) on commodity inflation (steel, aluminium, rubber, about 1%
  q/q); target corridor 14-16% "in the next few quarters"; Q3 margins guided "stable" [C2][C3].
- ROE 28.1%, ROCE 35.2% (FY26); 10-yr ROE average 23% [S1]. VIDA EV drag (EBITDA loss) Rs 168 Cr in Q2 vs Rs 232 Cr in Q1; VIDA

## 5. EV, GST, exports, competition
- EV: VIDA retail 66,531 in Q2 (+76%), 30,143 dispatches in Oct, EV share 13.6% in Oct (11.1% a year ago); capacity 30k -> 45k a
  month by Q4 FY27 [C1][C3][C6]. Industry is capacity-constrained [C3]. Ather (separate brand, no product tie-up yet [C3]): Oct retail
  31,833 (+5%) [N1]; market cap Rs 77,099 Cr, +210% in 1 yr, 52-wk low Rs 589 [S2][Y]. Hero's stake at market about Rs 25,300 Cr
  = Rs 1,263/share, 24% of Hero's value (my arithmetic): a big, volatile part of the price.
- GST cut 22 Sep 2025: bikes up to 350cc 28% -> 18% [A]. Almost all Hero volume is under 350cc, so Hero gained more than Royal Enfield
  (Eicher) and KTM (Bajaj), whose big bikes moved to 40% [A]. The benefit is now in the base: H2 FY27 compares with GST-boosted months.
- Exports: Q2 dispatches 85k (-24%) on a Colombia earthquake and a Bangladesh fuel-price slowdown; retail there +19%; H1 +8%; growth
  expected back in Q3 [C2][C3]. Bajaj is the export leader; Hero's 6% export share is small.
- October retail (FADA, 6 Nov 2026) [N1]: industry 23.21 lakh (-28.35%; festival moved from Oct 2025 to Nov 2026). Honda 6.38 lakh
  (-25%, share 27.5%), Hero 5.98 lakh (-40.6%, share 25.8% vs 31.1%), TVS 4.58 lakh (-20%, share 19.7%). Business Standard (8 Nov):
  Honda's lead now four months (headline only [N2]). Hero dispatched 7.34 lakh in Oct (+16%) vs 5.90 lakh VAHAN retail [C6]:
  dealer stock was built for Diwali (Dhanteras 6 Nov). November retail will show whether Hero lost real share or only timing.

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- Retail share: Hero's Nov 2026 retail share stays well below a year ago (Oct 2025 31.1%) and Honda keeps the lead into Q4 FY27.
- Margins: standalone EBITDA margin below 13% for two quarters (commodities, discounts such as the 0% finance offers of 31 Oct [N5]).
- EV: VIDA drag stops falling, or e-scooters take the commuter market faster than VIDA gains share.
- Ather: its price fell to Rs 589 within the last year [S2]; a halving would cut about Rs 630 from Hero's value per share.
- Demand: high base from the GST cut; rural slowdown; volatility 26%/yr, 5-yr max drawdown -42% [Y].
