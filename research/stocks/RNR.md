## 9. Munich Re or RNR: pick RNR
- `research/sectors/financials/README.md` and `global.md` call Munich Re the "cheapest quality insurer"; the scout now rates it BUY BELOW EUR 538 (price 591.6) and says RNR is the cheaper reinsurer. Weekly 2-yr correlation RNR vs Munich Re (in USD) 0.41: they overlap, own one.
- For RNR: P/B 1.37 vs 2.23 for similar operating ROE; returns come as buybacks (taxed only on sale, 14.95% after 24 months) instead of a 4.1% dividend taxed at 35.88% with 26.375% German withholding and a yearly 16.375% BZSt reclaim (`tax/new-investments.md`).
- For Munich Re: steadier (primary insurance and life besides reinsurance), less hit by property-cat price cuts. Choose Munich Re only if earnings swings matter more than cost and tax. Finance-main to drop Munich Re's Rs 0.61 L slot from the buy list if RNR is taken.
