## Summary

Largest digital bank in Latin America: 143 m customers, ROE 32%, profit +31% (Q3 2026) [N1]. **Verdict: WATCH, do not buy yet.**
At $13.22 it is fair, not cheap: the value model gives about $12.8 in the base case (range $7-18), and late loans (90+ days) have risen 2 quarters in a row. That is the scout's own "proves it wrong" signal.
1-3 yr return (USD, per yr): Low -18% / Base +9% / High +33% (INR: -16% / +13% / +37%) - section 9.
What to do: buy Rs 1.5 L (about 120 shares) only from the foreign-ideas money of a QCOM sale, and only if the price is at or below $11.50, or if Q4 2026 results (Feb 2027) show 90+ NPL flat or down and ROE at 28% or more.

- Loan book $41.6 bn (+33%): cards 67%, unsecured loans 25%, secured (payroll, FGTS) 8% [N5]. A mostly unsecured, mass-market book - high yield, high losses.
- Moat: lowest cost base in the region (efficiency ratio 19.3% vs 49% in Q3'22 [N5]), brand, data on 120 m Brazilians. Rivals: Itau, Bradesco, Inter&Co, Mercado Pago, PicPay (list from general knowledge).
- No dividend ever paid; no policy; earnings kept for growth [N3]. Brazilian units are not licensed as a bank. The central bank's brand-name rule (Joint Resolution 17) forces Nu to get a bank licence or buy a bank; Nu expects no big capital effect [N3].
