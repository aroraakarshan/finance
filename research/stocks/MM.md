## Summary
- **Fit for a buy at the 17 Nov open, max Rs 3,400: BUY IN STEPS, Rs 3 L total (about 0.7% of Rs 4.21 Cr).** Rs 1 L at the open (up to Rs 3,400); Rs 1 L at Rs 3,050-3,150; Rs 1 L after Q3 FY27 results (Feb 2027) if auto PBIT margin stays >= 8.5% and core tractor margin >= 16%.
- What it is: India's #1 SUV maker by revenue (24.3% share) and #1 tractor maker (41.5%), plus 52% of Mahindra Finance and 25% of Tech Mahindra [F1].
- Scout's drop was a **rule misfire**: standalone OCF/PAT is 1.31 (3y), consolidated 0.20 only because Mahindra Finance's loan growth counts as operating cash outflow (section 5).
- 3-yr total return (INR, before tax): low -13% / base +34% / high +90% (about -5% / +10% / +24% a year; section 9). 1-yr: -20% / +10% / +25%.
- Key risk: a weak tractor year (monsoon 87% of normal) plus commodity inflation while PLI subsidies prop up margins.

- Monsoon: season ended at 87% of normal (LPA); reservoirs 16% below normal (15 Oct); Maharashtra and Karnataka in drought [F2]. Management: NOAA puts the chance of a normal 2027 monsoon above 70%; back-to-back severe deficits happened only twice in 20 years [F3] (their claim, not verified).
- EVs: Q2 eSUVs 23,345 billed, 23.2% volume share, 30.1% revenue share (#2; H1 #1 at 32.3%); EVs are 12.7% of M&M SUVs vs 11.0% industry [F2]. XEV 9S was India's top EV (13,275 in Q2); BE 6 "Sporteq" (15 Aug) lifted bookings 93% [F2]. BE 6 / XEV 9e units alone are not broken out (not verified).
- Cyclical peak? Partly: SUV demand got a one-time push from the GST cut of 22 Sep 2025 [sector README]; tractors are already in a down year, so the farm side is near a trough, not a peak.
