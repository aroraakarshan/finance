# 6954 Fanuc (TSE Prime) - stock deep dive

Written 2026-11-17 by `finance-research` (stage 3). Research only, not advice; no orders placed.

## Summary
- **WATCH: entry JPY 4,800 or lower (100 shares = about Rs 2.83 L); condition: Q3 results (about 25 Jan 2027) show orders still above sales, no rise in cancellations and the JPY 50 bn buyback under way. Of the two robotics names, Nabtesco (6268) is the better buy now; Fanuc is the better business but is fully priced at JPY 5,343.**
- What it is: maker of CNC controls and servo motors for machine tools (market leader; share not verified), industrial robots (43% of sales), small machine tools and service. Net cash JPY 787 bn (16% of market value), no debt, operating margin 23-26%.
- Fair, not cheap: P/E 21.7 on the FY3/27 guide (18.3 without cash), EV/EBITDA 15.8 (own range 15.5-18.4); DCF JPY 4,560 (perpetuity) to 5,580 (15x exit).
- 1-3 yr (to Nov 2029, with dividends): JPY -8.4% / +9.4% / +22.0% a year; INR (yen -2%/yr) -10.3% / +7.3% / +19.6% a year (section 9).
- Key risk: the China machine-tool boom (China orders +80% y/y, partly early orders because of long lead times) turning, and parts shortages.

Fiscal year Apr-Mar; "FY3/27" = year to 31 Mar 2027 (Fanuc calls it FY2026). Price JPY 5,343 = close 16 Nov 2026 (Yahoo [S4], stockanalysis [S5]); about 5,243 on 17 Nov at 15:24 JST [S5].

## 5. Balance sheet and cash flow
- 30 Sep 2026: cash and deposits JPY 746.3 bn + securities 40.8 bn, **no borrowings -> net cash 787.1 bn = JPY 851/share (16% of the price)**; plus investment securities 242.9 bn (incl. affiliates) [S3]. Japanese GAAP.
- Operating cash flow FY3/26 250.9 bn, capex 21.2 bn, **FCF 229.7 bn** (FCF yield 4.6%); H1 FY3/27 OCF 155.9 bn [S3][S5]. Inventory is large (about JPY 300 bn), so growth ties up cash.
- Equity-method affiliates added JPY 23.0 bn in H1 [S3]; this is below operating profit (in ordinary income).
- **Buybacks**: almost none in FY3/26 (JPY 0.7 bn). Then JPY 50 bn done (8.27 m shares, May-Sep 2026) and a new JPY 50 bn / 10 m share programme (26 Oct 2026 - 30 Apr 2027); 0 shares bought by 31 Oct [S3][S7]. Dividend = 60% of profit: interim JPY 72.54 (+41%) [S7]. FY3/27 total return about JPY 237 bn (dividends about 137 + buybacks 100), about 100% of profit, 4.8% of market value (own calc).

## 7. Management and governance
- President Kenji Yamaguchi [S3]. Long known as secretive; Third Point (Dan Loeb) took a stake in Feb 2015 and Fanuc doubled its dividend in Apr 2015 (Japan Times 19 Feb 2015, WSJ 27 Apr 2015 headlines [S10]); 60% payout since [S7].
- **Activists now: none found** (news searches [S10]; EDINET large-holder filings not checked). Pressure point remains the cash pile: equity ratio 88.7%, and buybacks only restarted in 2026.
- Nov 2024: a Special Investigation Committee report on products not meeting legal and standard requirements; a separate Quality Management Division was set up on 1 Apr 2025 [S11]. Details of the breach not read.
- Reputation: a BDS campaign ("#StopFANUCNow", Feb 2026) targets its robot sales linked to Israeli arms makers [S10].

## 8. Risks / what proves the thesis wrong
- Orders below sales (book-to-bill under 1) for 2 quarters, or cancellations of the early China/Asia orders [S2].
- Parts shortages: Fanuc says it may lose some customers to rivals while lead times are long [S2]. Shares fell 14.3% on 3 Aug 2026 after Q1 ("procurement fears", Japan Times headline [S10]).
- China: 27% of sales and 30% of orders [S1]; local robot makers (e.g. Estun; scout S19) and export rules. China blocked SoftBank's purchase of ABB's China robot unit (Nov 2026, scout S22).
- Yen: guide assumes USD/JPY 150 in H2; a fast yen rally cuts profit, but helps an INR holder.
- Volatile: 3-yr volatility 42%; 10-yr max drawdown -61% (to Mar 2020) [S4].
