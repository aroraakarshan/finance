## 4. HealthCo demerger (the value-unlock catalyst)
- Plan (board, Jun 2025): HealthCo (pharmacies, 24/7) demerged and merged with Keimed (promoter-held pharma wholesale),
  listed as one company; Apollo shareholders get its shares directly. Target about Rs 25,000 Cr revenue [N7].
- Approvals: CCI Sep 2025, NCLT nod Apr 2026 [N8]. Timing slipped: "Dec 2026" (May 2026 [N9]) is now "toward the end
  of FY27", i.e. by Mar 2027 [N4]. Record date and share ratio: not announced (not verified).
- What it does: no new value by itself; it lets the market price the hospitals (24% margin) apart from the
  6% pharmacy business. Risk: Keimed is a promoter entity, so the swap ratio matters for minority holders.

## 7. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- Medicine and consumable mark-ups: the Supreme Court reserved its order on 27 Oct 2026 on a plea to cap MRP mark-ups
  (it cited a Rs 1,100 cancer drug billed at Rs 1.30 L) [N12]; brokers estimate a 2-5% hit to hospital profit [N13];
  the 30% trade-margin cap on cancer drugs (Oct 2026) was smaller than feared [N14]. A wide cap on hospital pharmacy margins
  or standard package rates (IRDAI panel, proposals by end-2026, sector note) would cut margins and the P/E.
- Hospital EBITDA growth below 15% for two quarters, occupancy below 70%, or new beds still loss-making after FY27.
- HealthCo: listing slips past FY27, a swap ratio that favours the promoters' Keimed, or 24/7 back to losses.
- De-rating: 5-yr max fall -39%, volatility 21% a year [Y]; at 40x FY28 a miss hurts.
