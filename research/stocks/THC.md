## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)

- Medicaid (OBBBA, July 2025): work requirements, stricter eligibility from 2027; caps on state-directed payments and provider taxes later (10-Q). Tenet's Medicaid supplemental revenue was $1,107 m in 9M 2026 (10-Q), about 29% of 9M adj. EBITDA; the share at risk is not disclosed (CFO). A cut here hits margin almost 1:1.
- ACA exchange subsidies expired end-2025: exchange admissions and revenue -18% y/y in Q3, now 5.5% of revenue; charity/uninsured admissions 5.5% vs 4.5% (10-Q). More losses expected in 2027 ("sizing further exchange headwinds", CEO). Industry: Reuters 2026-11-05 "hospitals brace for tougher 2027" (headline only).
- Wrong if: 2027 guidance below about $4.68 bn adj. EBITDA; USPI same-facility revenue under 3% with falling cases; buybacks stop; net debt/EBITDA above 3x.
- Politics both ways: hospital stocks rose up to 16% when Democrats took Congress in Nov 2026 (headlines, Medical Daily / 24/7 Wall St, 2026-11-05/06): delay of cuts is partly priced in. Volatility 42%/yr (2y daily); fell -83% to Mar 2020.
