## 2. Business
- Designs and assembles man-portable night-vision (image-intensifier) goggles and binoculars, thermal clip-ons and sights; since 2025-26 also vehicle cameras (Kappa Optronics, Germany), drone gimbals and counter-drone sensors [S2][S3].
- FY25 revenue EUR 443.4m: Europe 75%, Asia 19%, Americas 6%; night 93%, thermal 6% [S3]. Non-night-vision share rose to 17.6% in 9M26 (7.1% a year earlier) [S1].
- Key supply: image-intensifier tubes from Exosens (long-term deal to 2030; THEON owns 9.8% of Exosens) and its own Harder Digital (65% owned) [S1][S3].
- **Hensoldt link (correcting the brief):** THEON owns no Hensoldt shares. It owns 49.9% of Hensoldt Theon NightVision GmbH (HTN, Hensoldt Optronics 50.1%) and bids with Hensoldt as a consortium for OCCAR [S4].
  The Dec 2025 OCCAR revision added 100,000 goggles for the German army and 4,000 for Belgium, about EUR 1 bn, the largest such order in Europe [S3]. Sales to HTN H1 26: EUR 34.6m [S4].
- Acquisitions: Kappa (Jan 2026, EUR 69.9m), Merio 80% (closed by 9 Nov 2026), HGH Systemes Infrarouges (France, EV about EUR 300m, about EUR 40m revenue, EBITDA margin above 40%, closing "late Q4 2026") [S1][S5].

## 5. Balance sheet and cash flow
- Net debt EUR 251.1m (30 Sep 2026), 1.6x LTM adj. EBITDA [S1]. After HGH and Merio: about 3.0x pro forma (management), back below 2.5x in 2027 and 2.0x in 2028 [S9]. New loans: EUR 300m RCF and EUR 325m syndicated facility [S1][S3].
- Listed stakes (market value, own calc 16 Nov [S7]): Exosens 9.8% about EUR 256m (cost 268.7m), Kopin 6.19m shares about EUR 17m; Varjo convertible EUR 19.9m (book) [S4]. About EUR 3.8 a share.
  They swing reported profit: +EUR 37.3m in H1 26; at today's prices about -EUR 35m in H2 (Exosens EUR 59.80 on 30 Sep -> 51.50) (own estimate).
- **Weak cash conversion:** working capital is about 42% of revenue [S1]. Operating cash flow FY24 -26.1m, FY25 45.5m, H1 26 30.7m; less capex: free cash flow -36.8m, 26.8m, 17.7m [S3][S4].
  That is about 33% of FY25 profit. The company's "cash conversion 83%" is (EBITDA - capex) / EBITDA, which ignores working capital [S1].
- Dividend 20-30% of profit: EUR 0.31 for FY25 (paid June 2026), yield 1.1% [S1][S7].

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- FY26 book-to-bill below 0.9x or soft backlog below EUR 1.3 bn at the FY26 results: growth then fades in 2027-28.
- A Ukraine ceasefire or European budget delays slow orders; German approvals already slipped into 2027 [S5].
- Customer concentration: 38% from one European customer; 2024 had three at 30%, 15% and 10% [S3].
- Free cash flow stays weak while debt rises to about 3x EBITDA; another rights issue would hurt.
- Tube supply (Exosens, Harder Digital) or a shift to digital night vision; margin pressure as products broaden.
- Paper gains or losses on stakes make reported EPS noisy; volatility 55% a year (2-yr weekly, own calc [S7]).
