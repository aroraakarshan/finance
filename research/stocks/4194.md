## 2. Business
- **BizReach** (FY7/26 sales JPY 80.1 bn, +16.8%): paid job site for mid-career professionals. Employers and headhunters pay
  to search a database of job seekers and send "scouts"; success fees when they hire. Operating margin (before corporate
  costs) 41.3% [S1]. 45,800 registered direct employers at Q4 (+7,700 in a year) [S2][S3].
- **HRMOS** (JPY 9.3 bn, +78%, incl. Thinkings ATS bought 1 Oct 2025): HR software (hiring, attendance, payroll, talent data).
  ARR JPY 10.33 bn; 10,699 paying customers; loss JPY 0.48 bn FY7/26, guided loss JPY 1.5 bn FY7/27 (more investment) [S1].
- Other HR Tech JPY 4.4 bn (BizReach CAMPUS, Stanby jobs); Incubation JPY 5.5 bn (TRABOX logistics, M&A Succeed, Assured, yamory) [S2].
- Moat: two-sided network (largest pool of senior job seekers in Japan; 17 years of hiring data), brand; AI matching ("BizReach AI") built on own data [S3].
- Competitors: Recruit (Recruit Agent, Rikunabi NEXT), en Japan, Dip (part-time ads), JAC Recruitment (agency), LinkedIn, SmartHR (private, HR software).

## 8. Risks / what proves the thesis wrong
- BizReach growth below 12% for two quarters, or direct-employer adds stalling -> the 15% mid-term path is wrong.
- Operating margin below 20% without HRMOS growth to show for it (FY7/27 guide 22.2%).
- Large, expensive M&A with the cash (Thinkings was the largest so far; price not read).
- AI job-matching tools (LinkedIn, Recruit's Indeed, chat assistants) cutting the value of scouting.
- Volatile: fell 41% from 10,770 (19 Dec 2025) to 6,347 (24 Feb 2026), then +59%; cause of the fall not verified. 3-yr volatility 35%, max drawdown -50% (Jun 2022) [S5].
