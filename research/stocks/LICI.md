## Summary
- **BUY IN STEPS - fit for a buy at the 17 Nov open, max Rs 410. Rs 2.0 L in all (cap Rs 4.2 L):** Rs 1.0 L at Rs 390-410 on 17 Nov; Rs 50,000 at or below Rs 380; Rs 50,000 after Q3 FY27 results (early Feb 2027, date not announced) if 9-month individual APE growth is positive and VNB margin >= 21%.
- What it is: India's biggest life insurer (58.8% of first-year premium, H1 FY27), 90% owned by the government. Market cap Rs 5.06 lakh Cr vs embedded value (EV) Rs 7.97 lakh Cr: P/EV 0.63, cheaper than on 95% of days since listing; private peers trade at 1.2-2.1x.
- Why only part size: growth is slow (individual APE +0.3% in H1 FY27), the government must sell about 15% more by May 2032, and new IRDAI distribution rules and Ind AS accounting both start 1 Apr 2027.
- 3-yr total return (INR, before tax): low -9% / base +37% / high +92% (about -3% / +11% / +24% a year; section 9).

## 2. Business
- Sells life cover, savings plans and pensions through 14.5 lakh agents (43.7% of all agents in India); agents bring 91.8% of individual new premium [F2]. Total premium H1 FY27 Rs 2,74,269 Cr (+11.6%); AUM Rs 59.6 lakh Cr [F1].
- Two kinds of policies: "par" (profits shared with policyholders, low margin to shareholders) and "non-par" (fixed terms, ULIP, annuity, term cover; higher margin). LIC is moving its mix to non-par.
- Moat: brand and trust (state backing), agency reach in small towns, cost: expense ratio 10.41% [F1]. Rivals: SBI Life, HDFC Life, ICICI Life, Axis Max Life (Max Financial), Bajaj Life.

- OFS 4-5 Aug 2026: government sold 6.5% (2.5% base + 4% green shoe) at floor Rs 382, 10.9% below the 3 Aug close of Rs 428.50; institutional book 3.32x base, retail Rs 10 discount; about Rs 31,500 Cr raised [N1][N2][Y]. Stake now 90.0% (from 96.5%); DIIs 6.18%, FIIs 1.55% [S1].
- The 10% public float (SEBI deadline 16 May 2027) is now met [N1]. Still due: 25% float by May 2032 [N2] = about 15% more, about Rs 76,000 Cr at today's price. The stock fell 8.7% on the OFS day [Y]: expect each sale to come at a discount.
- Risks from state ownership: policy-driven investments (IDBI Bank stake sale and talk of banking ambitions, Nov 2026, headlines only [N2]); not checked in detail.

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- Individual APE falls again in Q3/Q4 FY27 (thesis needs growth to return) or VNB margin drops back below 20%.
- Equity market fall: EV moves about 0.6% for each 1% in equities [F1]; a 20% market fall cuts EV about 12%.
- IRDAI distribution rules (revised draft by Dec 2026, start 1 Apr 2027 [N3]): agents' unions oppose commission caps; agent losses would hit LIC most (92% agency).
- More government sales at 10% discounts; a policy order to support state firms; Ind AS from Apr 2027 changes profit; RBC may lock more capital.
- Falling persistency/surrenders: 61st-month persistency down 2.8 pts in a year [F1].
