## 2. The PTC deal (this decides the PTC verdict)

- Terms [P1][P2]: USD 205 cash per share, 42.3% over the 2 Oct close of USD 144.03; board recommends; no financing condition (USD 25 bn bridge from Morgan Stanley and Societe Generale); PTC break fee USD 700 m if it takes a better offer; no reverse fee found in the proxy text searched (not verified).
- Approvals [P2]: shareholder vote at the special meeting on **17 Dec 2026** (record date 10 Nov); HSR (US), **CFIUS** (French buyer of US design software used by defence firms), European Commission merger and foreign-subsidy rules, other foreign-investment filings. Expected close "by the third quarter of calendar 2027"; end date 4 Apr 2027, auto-extended up to 4 Jan 2028.
- PTC dropped earnings calls and guidance after the deal [P3].
- Spread: 205 / 196.38 = +4.4% in about 8-10 months (about 5-6% a year in USD). If the deal fails, the unaffected price was USD 144.03 (-27%); peers DSY and ADSK are up about 25% since 2 Oct [P5], so a standalone PTC might trade nearer USD 170-180 (-8% to -13%) - estimate.
- Evercore fairness analysis on management's plan [P2]: DCF USD 160-210 (WACC 11.5-12.5%, g 5-6%), peer P/E USD 137-189, premiums paid USD 173-202. My DCF on the same plan (management unlevered FCF FY27-36, net debt USD 1.56 bn, 109.4 m shares): USD 170 (WACC 11%, g 3%) to USD 237 (9%, 3%); USD 199 at 10% / 3%; terminal value 55-65% of EV. The plan's FCF excludes stock pay, so the value is on the high side. USD 205 is a fair, full price.

## 3. Business in plain words

- PTC sells software engineers use to design products (Creo 3D CAD, Onshape cloud CAD) and to manage product data and changes across a company (Windchill PLM, Codebeamer). Kepware and ThingWorx (factory IoT) were sold in fiscal Q2 2026 [P3]. FY2026 revenue (Sep year-end) USD 2,733 m: licences 1,105, support and cloud 1,534, services 94 [P4]. Under US GAAP (ASC 606) term licences book up front, so quarterly revenue jumps around; ARR is the cleaner measure.
- Dassault sells the same kind of tools at larger scale (CATIA in aerospace and cars, SOLIDWORKS for small firms) plus Medidata (clinical trials) and, since Sep 2026, ArisGlobal (drug-safety software, USD 1.8 bn) [P6][P9]. 2026 software revenue split: Industrial Innovation 54%, Mainstream (SOLIDWORKS, Centric) 27%, Life Sciences 19%; by region Americas 39%, Europe 38%, Asia 23% [P6].
- Moat for both: engineers trained on the tool, decades of design files, high switching cost; 80%+ of software revenue recurring. Peers: Autodesk (ADSK), Siemens Digital Industries Software (inside Siemens AG, not listed separately), Nemetschek (NEM), Bentley (BSY).
