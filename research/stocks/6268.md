## Summary
- **BUY IN STEPS: lot 1 (100 shares, about Rs 2.06 L) at JPY 3,500 or lower; lot 2 (about Rs 1.77 L) at JPY 3,000 or lower, or after the 12 Feb 2027 results if reducer orders are still up y/y. - What it is: No. 1 maker of precision reduction gears (RV gears) for industrial-robot joints (about 60% world share, company estimate), plus ship and aircraft parts, rail brakes and automatic doors. Robot gears are only about 28% of sales.
- Cheap: P/E 17.3 on the FY2026 guide, EV/EBITDA 8.3, DCF base JPY 4,750 vs JPY 3,574. Weak spots: ROE 7.5%, Q3 profit missed by 24%, Chinese gear makers are growing fast.

- Key risk: the robot and machine-tool cycle (RV gear sales fell 27% in 2024) and cheap Chinese gears.

Fiscal year = calendar year. Price JPY 3,574 = close 16 Nov 2026 (Yahoo [S3], stockanalysis [S4]); it fell 13.9% that day, the first day after Q3 results, and traded at about 3,411 on 17 Nov at 15:24 JST [S4].

Source: Q3 deck, 13 Nov 2026 [S1]. Robot customers: Fanuc, Yaskawa, Kawasaki, KUKA, ABB [S1]. Hydraulics (Comtesco) was sold in 2026, so FY2024-25 are restated without it [S1].
- Moat: RV gears for mid/large robots are a near-standard part (60% share). Small robots and humanoids use strain-wave gears, where Harmonic Drive (6324) leads, not Nabtesco.
- Humanoid hype vs orders: RVmini (a small gear for cobots and humanoids) started shipping in H2 2026 with a 2026 sales goal of only **JPY 0.2 bn** (0.2% of reducer sales) [S2]. The CEO sees "opportunities in cobots and humanoids" (Nikkei headline, 19 Oct 2026 [S5]). Today's orders come from car and EV makers in China and Korea, machine tools and chip tools [S1][S2], not humanoids.
- Chinese competitors: Leaderdrive (688017.SS, strain-wave and RV gears) revenue +56% y/y, market value CNY 46 bn (2.5x Nabtesco), P/E about 296; Shuanghuan Drive (002472.SZ) +14%, P/E 25 (Yahoo [S3]). Chinese makers win in small robots on price; Nabtesco's China plant (Changzhou) still runs at 95% [S1].

## 8. Risks / what proves the thesis wrong
- RV gear orders below last year for 2 quarters (Q3 was flat q/q); JARA robot orders (late Jan 2027) turning down.
- China: EV-maker capex is the main driver now [S2]; China is 15% of sales, and RMB moves hit profit (JPY 1 per RMB = JPY 0.19 bn profit) [S1]. Chinese gear makers moving up to mid/large robots.
- Margin stuck below 10% (ROE under 8%) despite price rises.
- Yen: the company assumes USD/JPY 150 in Q4 vs 159 now; a strong yen cuts profit (USD: JPY 44 m per yen) but helps an INR holder [S1].
- Volatile: 3-yr volatility 43%; -14% on 16 Nov 2026, -10% on 23 Jun and 28 Jul 2026; 10-yr max drawdown -61% (to Apr 2025) [S3].
