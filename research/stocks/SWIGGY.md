## Summary
- **WATCH, do not buy now at Rs 190.70.** Food delivery is profitable and cheap; Instamart (quick commerce) loses about Rs 900 Cr a quarter and is losing ground to Blinkit.
- Cheap vs Eternal: EV about 0.5x yearly order value vs Eternal 2.3x, and cash is 27% of market cap (section 6). The price follows Instamart losses, not the cheapness.
- 3-yr return (INR, no dividends): low -42% / base +47% / high +120% (about -17% / +14% / +30% a year; section 9).
- Do: no money now. After Q3 FY27 results (late Jan / early Feb 2027, date not announced), buy Rs 1.0 L at Rs 170-210 only if Instamart losses fall as guided (section 10); Rs 1.0 L more after Q4 FY27 (May 2027). Max Rs 2 L.

## 2. Business (plain words)
- One app for food delivery (Swiggy, plus Toing, a budget-meals app), 10-minute grocery delivery (Instamart), dining out and events (Dineout, Scenes), and a B2B supply arm [L1].
- Earns commission from restaurants and shops, ads, delivery and platform fees, and the Swiggy One subscription [L1].
- Q2 FY27 (Jul-Sep 2026), by segment [L1]:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Food delivery | GOV Rs 10,054 Cr | +17.7% | +Rs 341 Cr (best ever) | 3.4% of GOV |
| Instamart | GOV Rs 9,230 Cr; NOV Rs 6,594 Cr | +31.4%; NOV +34.8% | -Rs 895 Cr | -9.7% of GOV |
| Out-of-home (Dineout) | GOV Rs 1,508 Cr | +34.9% | +Rs 8 Cr | 0.5% |

| Platform innovations (Toing etc.) | revenue Rs 138 Cr | - | -Rs 162 Cr | - |
| **Consolidated** | B2C GOV Rs 20,793 Cr | +24.6% | **-Rs 760 Cr** | - |

GOV = order value before discounts; NOV = after discounts. 27.8 Mn monthly users (+21%); 1,240 dark stores [L1].

## 4. Margins and returns
ROE -29%, ROCE -24% (FY26) [S1]. Operating margin -10% of revenue (Q2 FY27) vs -23% (Q2 FY24) [S1]. Share-based pay Rs 194 Cr a quarter, excluded from "adjusted" EBITDA [L1].

## 5. Balance sheet, cash runway, capital raises
- Cash Rs 13,662 Cr at 30 Sep 2026 (company figure; Rs 15,053 Cr at Mar-26) [L1]. Borrowings Rs 2,514 Cr, mostly leases [S1].
- Burn: Rs 606-903 Cr a quarter over the last 5 quarters (about Rs 700 Cr in Q1-Q2 FY27) [L1]. FY26 free cash flow -Rs 3,809 Cr [S1].
- Runway at Rs 700 Cr a quarter: about 19 quarters (~4.9 years), plus about Rs 500 Cr from the Lynks sale [L1]. No raise needed before breakeven unless competition forces more spend (my arithmetic).
- QIP: Rs 10,000 Cr at Rs 375 a share in Q3 FY26 (Dec 2025; 25.56 Cr shares; net Rs 9,931 Cr) [L1]. Today's price is 49% below it. Rapido stake sold for Rs 2,399 Cr (FY26) [L1].
- Inventory model (IOCC): foreign holding capped at 49.5%, domestic ownership now over 58%; Instamart moves to owning stock from mid-Q3 FY27 over 2-3 quarters, about +80 bps to Instamart contribution margin, little extra working capital (management) [L1].

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- Quick commerce price war: Blinkit (2,751 stores), Zepto (IPO money coming), Flipkart Minutes, Amazon Now (120+ cities, orders 2x a quarter [N5]); Swiggy expects no easing near term [L1].
- Food entrants: Rapido is live in food; Flipkart testing [L3]. Toing burns about Rs 400 Cr a year (analyst estimate on the call [L3]).
- Thesis is wrong if: Q3 FY27 Instamart adjusted EBITDA loss stays above Rs 850 Cr or margin does not improve about 100 bps; food GOV growth below 18%; quarterly burn above Rs 900 Cr; any new QIP (dilution at low prices).
- Regulation: gig-worker social security costs; GST on delivery cut 18% -> 5% (proposed, a tailwind; date not verified) [L1].

## 10. Verdict and plan
- WATCH. The stock is cheap on order value and cash, and food is solid, but every quarter since listing Instamart has burnt cash while Blinkit pulls away; buying before proof is a bet on management's "peak losses" claim, after two quarters of target cuts.
- Trigger (Q3 FY27 results, late Jan / early Feb 2027; Q3 FY26 call was in Feb 2026 [S1]): Instamart adjusted EBITDA loss below Rs 850 Cr and margin about -8.7% of GOV or better, NOV +10% q/q, food GOV +18% or more, burn under Rs 800 Cr.
- If met: Rs 1.0 L (0.24% of total) at Rs 170-210. Second Rs 1.0 L after Q4 FY27 (about May 2027) if the trend holds. Cap Rs 2 L (0.48%), below the 1% (Rs 4.2 L) cap given the risk.
- If not met: drop. Below Rs 150 without the trigger is not a buy signal.
- Sector view (`research/sectors/consumer-discretionary/README.md`: skip quick commerce): agree for now; I differ only in that Swiggy's cash and food arm make it a WATCH with a clear trigger, not a flat skip. Prefer Eternal? No: Eternal at ~725x P/E and 2.3x order value also fails on price.
