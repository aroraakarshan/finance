## Summary
- **WATCH, do not buy at Rs 381.55.** Tamil Nadu private bank (921 branches), Rs 36,879 Cr market cap [S1]; best ROA (2.10%) and cleanest book (GNPA 0.69%) of its peers [C1].
- Why wait: P/B 2.40 is the highest in 10 years (10-yr median 1.1, 99.8th percentile) [S2]; the price already assumes ROE of about 19% for ever (section 6); ROA is at a 10-yr peak and the stock sits at its 52-week high.
- 3-yr total return (INR): low -17% / base +44% / high +83% (-6% / +13% / +22% a year; section 9).
- Do: Rs 1.5 L in two halves - Rs 75,000 if it closes at or below Rs 330 (from the Rs 1.94 L silver leftover, or the Punjab Gramin Bank savings surplus if the user extends rule 4 of portfolio/targets.md), Rs 75,000 after the Q4 FY27 results (about May 2027) if the ECL switch is absorbed (section 10).

## 2. Business
- Lender based in Karur, Tamil Nadu; 54% of branches semi-urban/rural [C1]. Gross advances Rs 1,11,589 Cr, deposits Rs 1,28,123 Cr at 2026-09-30 [C1].
- Loan mix Sep 2026 (y/y growth) [C2]: commercial/MSME Rs 39,407 Cr, 35% (+19%); retail Rs 29,024 Cr, 26% (+21%; LAP +52%, home -2%, vehicle -22%, BNPL -13%); agriculture Rs 27,145 Cr, 24% (+20%); corporate Rs 16,013 Cr, 14% (+24%).
- Gold (jewel) loans across segments: Rs 32,153 Cr = 29% of advances, +23% y/y; agri gold 24,887 (LTV 67%), non-agri 7,266 (LTV 59%); SMA 30+ 0.08% [C2][C3]. Self-cap: 35% of advances [C3].
- Moat: granular, secured, small-ticket book (67% of commercial loans under Rs 5 Cr) and long local relationships; weak on deposits (CASA 27.5%). Competitors: City Union, TMB, South Indian, Federal, gold NBFCs (Muthoot, Manappuram).

## 8. Risks
- Valuation: P/B at a 10-yr high; a de-rating to P/B 1.6 (its 2023-24 level) is -33% with no earnings miss.
- Cycle: ROA at peak; credit cost 0.43% vs 1.45% in FY23; the 2018-20 bad-loan cycle took ROA to 0.3% and the price -85% [C2][Y].
- Gold: 29% of loans; a 20% gold fall lifts LTV from 65% to about 81% (my arithmetic), margin calls and auctions. Adds to the user's gold (target 5% of total).
- Margins: gold repricing done, NIM guided down from 4.26% toward 4%; deposit competition (FCNR inflows) [C3].
- New lines: BNPL/personal loans with Axio (target Rs 2,000 Cr, Aug 2026 headline) [N2]; LAP +52% y/y.
- ECL (Apr 2027) amount unknown; CEO succession 2028; Tamil Nadu concentration.
- Price: 37% yearly volatility, 52-week low Rs 236.63 (-38%) [Y].
