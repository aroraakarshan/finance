## Summary

Max Rs 2.5 L direct (cap Rs 4.2 L incl. funds, section 10).
- What it is: India's largest private bank (Rs 12.6 lakh Cr market cap), merged with HDFC Ltd (mortgages) on 1 Jul 2023; no promoter.
- Why: P/B 2.09 and P/E 15.7 are cheaper than on 95-96% of weeks since Nov 2016 (10-yr median 3.4 and 23.1). Weak spots: ROE 13.5% (ICICI 16.5%), NIM 3.16% is a record low, profit +4.7% y/y. Up 20% since 27 Oct (Rs 680).
- The screen's ROE drop is a data misfire: Yahoo shows 11.0% (its equity likely includes subsidiaries' minority holders, per the sector note). ROE: FY25 14.4%, FY26 13.7%, TTM 13.5% (3-yr screener avg 15%), above the 12% rule.
- 3-yr total return (INR, before tax): low -4% / base +36% / high +85% (about -1% / +11% / +23% a year; section 9).

## 2. Business
- Lends to retail (home, personal, card, auto), small and mid firms (SME) and big companies, funded by 9,706 branches' deposits [N3].
  Q2 FY27 (Sep 2026, standalone): gross advances Rs 32.20 lakh Cr +16.3% y/y (retail +11.7%, SME +23.5%, corporate +16.4%);
  deposits Rs 33.27 lakh Cr +18.8% [N2][N3]. Home loans are the largest retail product after the merger (share not verified).
- Subsidiaries in the consolidated numbers: HDFC Life, HDFC AMC, HDB Financial, HDFC Ergo, HDFC Securities. Market caps on
  16 Nov: HDFC Life Rs 1.31 lakh Cr, HDFC AMC Rs 1.14 lakh Cr, HDB Rs 0.47 lakh Cr [Y]. At roughly 50% / 52% / 74% stakes
  (not verified this session) the listed ones are worth about Rs 1.5 lakh Cr to the bank, about Rs 95 a share (12% of the price).
  Moat: low-cost deposits and reach; but CASA is only 31.6% since the merger brought HDFC Ltd's loans without deposits.

## 8. Risks (and what proves the thesis wrong)
- Margin: NIM fell again in Q2; RBI's 7 Oct hike to 5.50% raises deposit costs, and the old HDFC Ltd mortgage book is slow to reprice
  (share of fixed-rate loans not verified). Thesis wrong if NIM stays below 3.2% through Q4 FY27 (results about Apr 2027).
- Returns: ROE TTM below 13% by Q4 FY27, or LDR back above 100% = the merger drag is not fading.
- Credit: SME loans +23.5% a year usually show bad loans 2-3 years later; credit cost above 0.7% would cut ROE about 1 point (est.).
- ECL rules from 1 Apr 2027: extra provisions from net worth; HDFC Bank's estimate not disclosed (not verified) [I5].
- Governance: US class action findings, an RBI penalty or more senior exits. Stress case: P/B 1.5 = Rs 587 (-28%).
- Price: 1-yr volatility 23%; max fall in 3 yrs -33% [Y]; it just rose 20% in 12 sessions, so a pull-back to the 200-day average
  (Rs 775) is likely; hence the steps.
