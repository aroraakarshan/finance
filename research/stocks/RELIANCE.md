## Summary
- **WATCH. Buy only at Rs 1,200 or below: Rs 1.0 L there, a second Rs 1.0 L at Rs 1,100 or after Q3 (about 15 Jan 2027) if O2C EBITDA stays at or above Rs 20,000 Cr; Rs 2 L in all.**
- What it is: India's largest company: refining and chemicals (O2C), 64.5% of Jio (listed 28 Oct 2026), 83.6% of Reliance Retail, KG-D6 gas, media, new energy.
- Why wait: up 16% from its 52-week low (about Rs 1,160, 29 Oct) in under three weeks on a record O2C quarter that rests on war-level fuel margins; Jio (46% of the price) is at P/E 41; you already own it as 7.3% of Nifty 50.
- 3-year total return (INR): low -21% / base +19% / high +58% (about -8% / +6% / +16% a year; section 9).
- Key risk: O2C margins fall back as Brent eases (forecasts $70-86 for 2027) while capex stays about Rs 1.4 L Cr a year.

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- O2C: Q2 rode fuel cracks 3-3.7x a year ago [N10]. If O2C EBITDA drops below Rs 17,000 Cr a quarter
  (Q2 FY26: Rs 15,011 Cr, my arithmetic) as Brent eases to $70-86 [E], EPS falls 10-15%.
- Jio: forward P/E 29 vs Bharti 26; lock-in sales or a late tariff hike can cut Jio 20% = about 9% off RIL.
- Capex never falls (13-14% of revenue): the DCF then says far less (section 5). Retail margin keeps
  falling to quick commerce; windfall tax or price caps; holding discount stays 25% or widens.
- Bull side: if O2C holds above Rs 20,000 Cr a quarter through 2027 and retail margins recover, the high case applies.
