- Why steps, not all now: quality is top-tier and P/E 47.7 is at the 18th percentile of its own 5-yr range, but it is at a 52-week high, analysts' mean target is only Rs 2,381 (+6%) and a plain DCF gives far less (Rs 640-1,230).

## 1. Business (plain words)
- Turns steel coil into about 1,500 kinds of pipes and hollow sections (10x10 mm to 1,000x1,000 mm) used as columns,
  beams, frames, roofs, fences, solar and warehouse structures [IP]. Capacity 5 Mt a year, going to 8 Mt by FY28 [IP].
- H1 FY27 use: housing 64%, commercial buildings 19%, infrastructure 13%, others 4% [IP].
- Q2 FY27 volume 963 kt: APL Apollo brand 775 kt, roofing 135 kt (both premium, 92-93% of volume), SG Premium
  (low-grade) 27 kt, UAE plant 26 kt [IP][CC]. Its black pipe sells about Rs 70,000/t vs 68-69,000 for rivals [CC].
- UAE (Dubai) plant runs at about 40% of normal and exports from India are hard (Middle East disruption, container
  freight) [CC]; UAE capacity goes from 0.3 to 0.5 Mt by Mar-2027 regardless.

## 7. Sector view (research/sectors/materials/)
- The materials README sees Indian metals near a profit peak (Nifty Metal P/B 2.7, 88th percentile) and prefers
  Hindalco. APL is in Nifty Metal but is not a metal producer: it earns a conversion spread, so I treat it as a
  building-products compounder rather than a steel-cycle bet. I agree with the caution on steel itself; the link to
  APL is that a steel price crash causes inventory losses for 1-2 quarters (Q2 FY25), and a weak SG Premium mix.
- Safeguard duty (ends Dec 2028 [M]) keeps coil prices high in India; APL passes this on, so it is a minor risk.

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- Q3 FY27 (about 21-Jan-2027) EBITDA/t below Rs 5,500 or FY27 EBITDA below Rs 2,000 Cr (guide 2,200) [CC].
- Brand + roofing volume growth below 10% in H2 FY27 (guide 15-20%): new 8 Mt capacity would sit idle (now 70-75% used [CC]).
- De-rating: P/E falling to 30-35 (its 2019 and 2023 lows) takes 25-35% off the price even if profit grows.
- Steel price crash (inventory loss), housing slowdown (64% of sales), rivals in branded tubes (JSW, Tata Steel structural - not verified).
- Middle East: Dubai stays at 40%; or worse, FX and freight costs. Promoter selling continues; FIIs own 34%.
- Price: 30% yearly volatility; worst fall 28% in the last 5 years [Y].
