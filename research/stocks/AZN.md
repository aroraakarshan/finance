## 2. Business
- Revenue 9M 2026 $46.9 bn (+7% CER) [S1]. By area: Oncology 47% (+16%), CVRM (heart, kidney, diabetes) 19% (-14%),
  Respiratory & Immunology 16% (+10%), Rare Disease 16% (+11%), Vaccines/Infectious 2% [S1].
- By region: US 42%, Europe 22%, China 12%, other emerging 15%, other rich 9% [S1].
- Biggest drugs (9M 2026, $ bn, CER growth): Tagrisso 5.86 (+8%), Farxiga 5.60 (-15%, US generic), Imfinzi 5.54 (+27%),
  Ultomiris 4.02 (+16%), Calquence 3.01 (+16%), Enhertu 2.69 (+33%), Lynparza 2.42 (-2%) [S1].
- **Daiichi Sankyo overlap:** Enhertu and Datroway are shared with Daiichi (50/50 profit split). For AZ they are about
  6% of revenue ($2.86 bn 9M) [S1]; for Daiichi over half (see [4568](4568.md)). Owning both doubles this one bet.
- Moat: patents, a wide pipeline (12 positive Phase III readouts in 2026, 47 approvals since Q4 2025 [S1]),
  global sales force. Ambition: $80 bn revenue in 2030 (risk-adjusted, Q1 2024 FX), "on track" [S2].

## 7. Management and governance
- CEO Pascal Soriot since 2012 [S10]. No controlling holder; institutions 74.5% [S8]. Listed LSE, Stockholm, NYSE [S1].
- Deals: talks with Bristol Myers Squibb for a ~$400 bn merger were reported 2 Aug 2026; shares fell 9% in a day, the
  most since 2020; investors opposed it [S10]. Talks collapsed ("no deal", Reuters via BioSpace, 2 Sep 2026) [S11].
  Smaller deals continue: $2 bn stake in Summit (29 Sep), Omniose vaccine buy (10 Nov 2026) (headlines [S12]).
- Lesson: management is open to very large deals; a repeat is the main governance risk.

## 8. Risks
- Generics and price cuts: Farxiga -15%, Soliris -26%, Brilinta -62% (9M 2026) [S1]; later patent dates for Tagrisso,
  Imfinzi, Lynparza not verified in this run.
- US pricing: the 2025 US government (MFN) agreement is now cutting gross margin [S1]; IRA Medicare talks (`pharma.md`).
- Pipeline misses happen: SERENA-4 failed, volrustomig and Ultomiris AWAKE stopped (Q3 2026) [S1].
- Big M&A (BMS) could dilute returns [S10]. Higher US/UK rates (gilt 5.24%, Sep 2026 [S9]) cap the multiple.
- For an INR holder: GBP/INR (+22.7% over 5 yrs, own calc [S4]) can move the result either way.
