## Summary
- **Not a buy at the 17 Nov open (do not pay more than Rs 620). BUY IN STEPS lower: Rs 1.25 L at or below Rs 620 and Rs 1.25 L at or below Rs 540 (max Rs 2.5 L), only while the Q3 update (about 7 Jan 2027) shows India growth of 20%+ and the promoter pledge does not rise.**
- What it is: India's no. 2 listed jeweller (365 Kalyan stores in India, 38 Middle East, 4 US/UK, 138 Candere), growing through franchise-owned, company-run stores; family-controlled (62.85%) [C3][S1].
- Why wait: the stock is up 115% from its June low and 33% in seven sessions; at P/E 48 on clean profit (43 on FY27) and 48% yearly volatility, it fell 40% in Jan 2025 and 28% in Jan 2026. 24.85% of the promoters' shares are still pledged, against a Feb 2026 promise to cut it within six months.
- 3-yr total return from Rs 708 (INR): low -18% / base +41% / high +109% (about -6% / +12% / +28% a year); from Rs 620 the base is +60% (+17%/yr).

## 1. Business: plain words
- Sells gold and studded jewellery (studded share rising [C2]); about 60% of sales are weddings [C2]. Brands: Kalyan, Candere (lifestyle, online and 138 stores), and the new Akshaya Thanga Maligai (ATM), a lower-price Tamil Nadu brand (first store Aug 2026, four more by Mar 2027) [C3][C2].
- Franchise model (FOCO = franchise-owned, company-operated): the franchisee pays for the store and the inventory, Kalyan runs it and shares the margin; franchisee ROCE about 14% [C4]. All new Kalyan stores are FOCO; target 84 Kalyan and 50 Candere stores in FY27 [C4]. Share of FOCO stores in the network: not verified.
- Old-gold exchange is 42% of sales (gold for cash 7-8%) [C2]; coins are single digit [C2]. The non-GML debt (debt other than gold loans) went to zero in Q2 FY27 [C3].

## 3. Margins, returns, balance sheet and cash
- Operating margin 6-8% since FY18 (Q2 FY27 6.2%) [S1][N6]; PBT margin about 5.1% excl. bullion in Q1, flat y/y in Q2 on clean basis [C4][C2].
- ROE 24.8% (5-yr avg 17%), ROCE 21.2% [S1]; ROCE "about 20%, will improve" with asset-light growth [C4].
- Borrowings Rs 6,127 Cr (Sep-26) vs equity Rs 6,638 Cr [S1]; of which leases about Rs 2,006 Cr (Mar-26, Yahoo balance sheet [Y]); the rest is mostly gold metal loans (my inference from "non-GML debt zero" [C3]). Inventory 167 days (Titan 222) [S1].
- Cash conversion is good: free cash flow FY20-26 Rs 4,564 Cr vs profit Rs 3,452 Cr (132%; Titan 27% FY22-26) [S1]. Dividend payout about 20%, yield 0.35% [S1]. Rating IND AA-/Positive (India Ratings, 22 Sep 2026) [C6].

## 5. Middle East, gold price and import duty
- Middle East: 38 stores, about 11% of revenue with US/UK [C3]. Q2 revenue Rs 960 Cr (+12%, all same-store), PAT Rs 12 Cr vs 15; Q1 PAT 25 vs 22, so H1 flat at Rs 37 Cr [C2][C4]. In talks with Gulf investors for franchise-led growth (headline, Feb 2026 [N2]). The Gulf conflict hit Titan's Damas (TITAN.md); Kalyan's sales there held up.
- Gold: COMEX USD 3,867 on 16 Nov, -6% in a year [Y]; correlation of KALYANKJIL with gold -0.07 (weekly, 2 yrs) [Y]. Management: buyers come with a fixed rupee budget, so lower gold = more grams; buyers wait 2-3 weeks when prices swing [C2].
- Import duty: raised 6% -> 15% on 13 May 2026 [N7]; Kalyan's gain was about Rs 40 Cr in Q1 (mostly passed to buyers) and the rest in Q2, "does not flow through Q3" [C4]. The government is reviewing a cut (industry asks for 6%) as smuggling rises [N7]. A cut means an inventory loss: after the Jul 2024 cut, Q2 FY25 PAT fell to Rs 130 Cr from 135 [S1][N3]. Next decision point: Budget, 1 Feb 2027 (usual date).

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- India same-store growth below 10% or revenue growth below 20% for two quarters (management's own floor is 10% SSG / 25% revenue).
- Clean PBT margin falling (exchange-heavy sales, discounting by rivals); Q4 FY27 laps a +66% quarter.
- Pledge rising, or the Dec-26 pattern (out about 21 Jan 2027) still showing 24.85%; any new rumour-driven sell-off.
- Gold duty cut (inventory loss); sharp gold swings delaying wedding buying; Middle East profit falling further.
