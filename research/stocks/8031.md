## 1. Business (plain words)
- Buys stakes in mines, gas fields and businesses worldwide and trades what they produce. Much profit comes as its share
  of partly-owned companies (equity method JPY 292 bn = 41% of H1 pre-tax profit; dividends received 101 bn = 14%) [S1].
- H1 FY3/27 profit by segment (JPY bn) [S1]: Mobility/Digital/Infra 153.7, Mineral & Metal 139.7, Energy 103.3,
  Chemicals 70.2, Innovation & Corp Dev 60.9, Wellness 20.8, Iron & Steel 10.5. Total 574.9.
- **Iron ore**: equity production ~64 Mt/yr (63.8 Mt FY3/26), rising toward FY3/29; Robe, Vale stake, BHP JVs; Rhodes
  Ridge (Australia) added in MTMP2026 [S2 p29][S3]. Sensitivity: JPY 3.0 bn profit per USD 1/t; H1 avg USD 101/t [S2 p28].
- **LNG/oil**: 211 kboe/d FY3/26 -> 247 FY3/29 plan, mostly gas/LNG (Waitsia, US shale, Ruwais UAE, Mozambique LNG,
  Tangguh, Oman; Sakhalin II still held at JPY 50 bn fair value) [S2 p31][S1 p18]. Oil: JPY 0.9 bn net per USD 1/bbl.
- Other sensitivities: copper 0.5 bn per USD 100/t; **USD/JPY 4.6 bn per 1 yen**; AUD 1.8 bn per yen [S2 p28].
