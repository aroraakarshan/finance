# ZAB - Zabka Group S.A. (Warsaw Stock Exchange: ZAB; Yahoo ZAB.WA)

Written 2026-11-17 by `finance-research`. Research only, not advice; no orders placed. Prices: close 2026-11-16 (PLN 31.86). "not verified" = no source confirmed it. Sources [Z#] at the end. News score: pending finance-news (no `news/ZAB.md`).

## Summary

SKIP (Rs 0): Zabka is being bought out. Couche-Tard (Circle K) bids PLN 32.00 cash per share. The offer is now unconditional and closes for acceptances on 2 Dec 2026 [Z1].
The price is PLN 31.86, so buying now earns at most 0.44% (PLN 0.14) in 3 weeks. Fees, slab tax on a short-term gain and PLN/INR moves wipe that out. Once the deal completes, the shares will probably be squeezed out and delisted.
1-3 yr return: base +0.4% (tender at PLN 32); low -21% if the buyer stays below 95% and the stock trades as a thin minority line (DCF PLN 25); high +0.4% (bid is final). INR is the same plus PLN/INR moves.
The scout's BUY missed the bid. As a business it is good (sales +14%/yr, 13,405 stores), but you cannot buy it for the long term. Remove ZAB from the global list; look at Jeronimo Martins (Biedronka) or Dino for Polish grocery exposure.

## 2. Business

- Poland's largest convenience chain: 13,405 stores on 30 Sep 2026 (including Nano unstaffed stores and Froo stores in Romania: about 307 by own count, 204 at end-Q1 2026 [Z9] plus 103 opened in Q2-Q3 [Z3]), +1,410 in a year [Z3]. Food to go, coffee, drinks and services (parcels, bill payment). Digital arms: Maczfit and Dietly (meal plans), Jush/delio (quick commerce) [Z3][Z6].
- **Franchise model**: about 11k franchisees run the stores. They own the stock, employ the staff and serve the customers. Zabka picks the sites, holds the leases, supplies the goods and sets up the stores [Z6]. Franchisee pay is about 16.7-17.3% of store sales (Q3/9M 2026) [Z3]. The risk is that franchisees must stay profitable as Polish wages rise. The company runs a "take-home pay" programme for this [Z6].
- **Romania (Froo)**: entered in 2024. 240+ stores by Jun 2026 [Z6], 135 opened in 9M 2026 [Z3]. Long-term target raised from 4,000 to 7,600 stores (Romania Insider 2026-01-23 [Z9]; investor deck [Z6]). Still loss-making: the company says Romania "temporarily affects segment profitability" [Z3].
- Market share 11.3% (rolling LTM, Sep 2026, packaged food, drug and cigarettes basket), up from 10.0% in Mar 2025 [Z3].

But FY25 tax was only PLN 49 m on PLN 1,106 m of pretax profit (4.4%), including a PLN 140 m tax credit in Q4-25 (Yahoo [Z7]). Pretax profit grew +26%/yr. FY24 included IPO costs [Z6]. Per-share history before 2024 uses the pre-IPO share count (FY22 EPS PLN 5.79 on stockanalysis), so EPS growth rates are meaningless. Consensus FY26 EPS PLN 1.18 is only +7% (13 analysts, Yahoo [Z7]).
- Like-for-like sales: Q3-26 +3.5%, 9M +3.6% (9M-25 +5.5%) [Z3]. Medium-term guidance: 1,300+ new stores a year, mid-to-high single-digit like-for-like growth, adjusted EBITDA margin at the top of 12-13% of store sales, adjusted net margin about 4.5% by 2028 [Z3]. Sales are defensive, not cyclical. Q1 is seasonally weak (losses in Q1 every year).

## 8. What would prove this view wrong

- A higher or competing bid before 2 Dec. This is unlikely: the offer is unconditional, the period will not be extended, and the market price is below PLN 32. Couche-Tard's CEO did say in July that he expected investor pressure to raise the bid (Bloomberg headline 2026-07-31 [Z9]).
- Couche-Tard ends up below 95% and keeps Zabka listed. A long-term holder would then own a minority stake next to a controlling buyer, in a thin market.
