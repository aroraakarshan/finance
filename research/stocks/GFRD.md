## 1. Business
- UK-only contractor (FTSE 250). Building GBP 951m (49% of revenue): schools, defence, prisons, health, affordable homes, facilities management; Morrison Construction in Scotland. Infrastructure GBP 972m (50%): Highways and Environment (water/wastewater, AMP8 frameworks). Investments GBP 8.5m: PPP stakes worth GBP 37.2m and rental-housing (PRS) development [S1].
- Order book GBP 4.3bn (2.2x revenue): 95% public/regulated, about 90% through frameworks, 92% repeat clients; 90% of FY27 and 62% of FY28 revenue secured [S1].
- Mix: fixed-price and cost-reimbursable/target-price contracts (split not disclosed) [S1]. Small bolt-on deals in higher-margin specialist niches (fire safety: Nene Valley, GBP 9.4m, Feb 2026) [S1].
- Since the Jan 2020 sale of its housebuilders to Vistry it has been only a contractor, so only 2020-26 history is comparable.

## 4. Balance sheet and cash flow (cash quality)
- 30 Jun 2026: cash GBP 259.0m, no bank debt, leases GBP 47.8m, PPP assets GBP 37.2m, no defined-benefit pension; GBP 25m RCF undrawn [S1][S2].
- **The cash is mostly float.** Working capital is -GBP 274.8m [S2]: payables GBP 609.6m (accruals 338.1, contract liabilities 140.7, trade 77.3) vs receivables GBP 398.7m (contract assets 283.6) [S1].
  Year-end cash is seasonally high: 12-month average month-end cash was GBP 216.2m and 31 Dec 2025 net cash GBP 211.7m [S1][S3].
  If revenue shrinks, or the company pays suppliers faster (27 days now), the cash goes out. Do not value it at 100%.
- FY26 cash: operating cash GBP 82.0m, capex GBP 1.2m, lease payments GBP 23.3m -> **FCF after leases GBP 57.5m (1.4x net income)**, not GBP 80.8m (Yahoo/stockanalysis "2.0x" leave out lease payments) [S1][S5].
  FY26 also had no cash tax (old tax losses used up; FY27 cash tax "in line with" the GBP ~14m P&L charge) and a GBP 15.3m rise in provisions [S1]. Normal FCF: about GBP 40-55m (6-8% of market cap).
- Uses: dividend cover 1.8x adjusted EPS; GBP 35m of buybacks done since 2021 (GBP 50m with the new one [S1]); a GBP 15m buyback is running (GBP 3.98m done by 10 Nov 2026) [S1][S8].

## 5. Contract risk
- Rectification provisions keep rising: GBP 34.7m (Jun-24) -> 47.6m -> 61.0m (Jun-26); FY26 additions GBP 27.9m, used GBP 2.5m, released GBP 12.0m [S1].
  GBP 13.1m is for one joint-venture road/structure scheme with a 12-year defect warranty (5 years left; range GBP 7.6-19.1m) [S1].
- Contingent: 3 fire-safety (cladding) claims "cannot be valued" and "material settlements" cannot be ruled out; other claims GBP 0-2.2m; bonds GBP 144.1m [S1].
  27 Oct 2026: Galliford Try lost a bid to strike out a hotel cladding claim (Construction News headline; body not read) [S10].
- Delivery signals (headlines only [S10]): replaced on GBP 100m of Sizewell C road work by Kier and Breheny (25 Aug 2026); a bypass job faces a 6-month delay and a cost increase from utility diversions (6 Nov 2026). Contract terms (who pays) not known.
- History: in 2018 it needed a rights issue after losses on big fixed-price road jobs (prospectus on the company site [S2]; details not re-read). The current strategy (frameworks, target-cost work) is a response to that.

- Divisional margin falls below 3.3% in H1 FY27, or a profit warning on a fixed-price job.
- Average month-end cash falls below about GBP 180m (float unwinding) or the order book falls below GBP 4bn / FY28 cover falls well under 60%.
