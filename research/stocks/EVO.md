## Summary
WATCH: buy only at SEK 750 or lower AND if the 4 Feb 2027 Q4 report shows y/y growth with Brazil contained; then up to Rs 4.2 L (about 60 shares, 1% cap) from a QCOM sale slot. SKIP if you do not want gambling shares.
What it is: the world leader in live online casino games (filmed blackjack, roulette and game shows) sold to betting sites. It is very profitable (66% EBITDA margin, net cash) and cheap against its own past (P/E 14.6 vs a 10-yr median of about 27).
Why wait: revenue has been flat for 2 years. About 51% of revenue comes from players in unregulated markets (37% Asia). The UK regulator fined it for AML failures in 2026, Brazil's rollback shut its studio there, and the stock is up 69% from its Feb 2026 low and above the analysts' mean target.
1-3 yr return (2-yr horizon, per year): Low -20% / Base +8% / High +26% in SEK; INR -19% / +11% / +28% (section 9).
Ethics, plainly: this is a gambling business. Its revenue is players' losses, and about half comes from places where online casinos are not licensed.

## 2. Business
- Evolution films dealers in about 24 studios (Riga, Malta and Tbilisi hubs; also the US, Canada, LatAm, Philippines) and streams the games to licensed operators. It takes a commission on the operator's gaming win [E2].
- Revenue by player IP, Q3-26: Asia 37%, Europe 33%, North America 16%, LatAm 9%, Africa 5%. Regulated markets are 49% (up from 39% in 2023) [E1][E9]. By customer location, 64% is Europe: Asian play comes through operators licensed elsewhere (e.g. Malta). The company says 100% of revenue comes from licensed operators [E9].
- Moat: scale (about 2,000 live tables), the game-show catalogue and its brands (Hasbro/Monopoly deal from mid-2025). Competitors: Playtech, Pragmatic Play (private, not checked), Ezugi (its own second brand), Light & Wonder (RNG).

## 5. Balance sheet and cash flow

- Capital return: no dividend for FY2025, which breaks the 50% payout policy because "buybacks create more value" [E2]. Instead there is a EUR 2 bn buyback (from 18 May 2026): EUR 1,324.7 m spent by 6 Nov, EUR 675.3 m left before the April 2027 AGM [E5]. 16.17 m shares were cancelled 29 Sep 2026; 2026 buybacks are about 14% of today's market cap. Policy: 100% of excess cash goes back to shareholders, mainly through buybacks [E10].
- Discrepancy: the IR dividend table shows EUR 2.94 for May 2025, but the annual report says EUR 2.80, which matches EUR 572.5 m paid [E2][E10].

## 7. Management and governance
- Founders: Jens von Bahr (chair, not independent) and Fredrik Osterberg hold 10.9% via Osterbahr Ventures. CEO Martin Carlesund (with the group since 2015) owns 784,710 shares and 325,000 warrants; his 2025 pay was EUR 8.6 m including social charges [E2][E13]. Auditor: Ohrlings PwC [E2]. One share class.
- Kenneth Dart (Candle Lake, Cayman) owns 30.07% (15 Sep 2026). Crossing 30% forced a SEK 695 mandatory bid (13 Aug 2026). The board advised shareholders to reject it, and few accepted at a price far below the market [E5][E14]. He calls it a financial investment and would delist only above 90% [E14]. Buybacks lift his share of outstanding shares (about 33% now). Above 30% he can keep buying without another bid, so the free float shrinks over time.
- Compliance record [E1][E15]:
  - UK Gambling Commission review (Dec 2024) found weak AML and customer checks; its games were reachable on unlicensed UK sites. Settled for GBP 4.75 m (EUR 5.6 m booked in Q3-26). The Commission said it had considered suspending the licence.
  - An unsealed Spectrum Gaming report (Sep 2026), commissioned by Evolution itself, found no illegal practice. But it confirmed its games were reachable and earning in prohibited places (Hong Kong, Singapore, UAE, Saudi Arabia) and that it did not monitor customers proactively.
  - Evolution is suing Playtech and Black Cube for defamation in New Jersey; the trial date is not verified.

## 8. Risks
- Regulation: half of revenue is unregulated (Asia), so a crackdown there or by its licensing regulators (Malta, UK, New Jersey) could cut revenue quickly. Europe keeps shrinking (-4.7% y/y). Brazil is closed until the rules are clear.
- Cyber: stolen streams in Asia hit 2024-25 growth; progress is "slow and methodical" [E2].
- Growth slowdown: 2 flat years. Growth now depends on North America (Ezugi relaunch, a large New Jersey studio approved Nov 2026 [E15]) and LatAm.
- Concentration and control: top 5 customers 39%; Dart owns 30%+ and the free float keeps shrinking.
- FX: EUR reporting, SEK price, USD/Asian currencies in revenue; constant-FX growth was 8.6 points above reported in Q4-25 [E4].
