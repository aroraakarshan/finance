Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Research only, not advice; no orders placed. Prices: close 2026-11-13 (EUR 131.28). Fiscal year ends 30 Sep (FY26 = Oct 2025 - Sep 2026). "not verified" = no source confirmed it. Sources [S#] at the end. News score: pending finance-news (no `news/ENR.md`).

## Summary
Siemens Energy makes gas turbines, grid equipment (transformers, switchgear, HVDC) and, through Siemens Gamesa, wind turbines; FY26 was a record (net income EUR 4.1 bn, backlog EUR 164 bn).
**Verdict: BUY, staged, Rs 1.5 L (11 shares), not the full Rs 3 L.** The price is 30% below the April high and only prices about a 15% margin (the FY27 guide), not the 20-22% FY30 target; but it is an AI-power bet that moves with your VRT and CEG (weekly corr 0.49 / 0.41).
1-3 yr return (3 yr to Nov 2029, per yr): Low -11% / Base +14% / High +33% in EUR (INR: -9% / +18% / +37%) - section 8.
Do: buy 6 shares (about Rs 82k) at or below EUR 140 when the money is there; buy the other 5 after Q1 FY27 results (11 Feb 2027) if book-to-bill stays above 1, or earlier below EUR 115.

- Backlog EUR 164 bn = 3.7 years of revenue; about 85% of FY27 revenue already covered; service backlog about EUR 71 bn; about EUR 15 bn (9%) is data-centre related (Gas plus Grid) [S2].
- Gas turbines: 65 GW firm backlog plus 39 GW slot reservations (to convert in FY27). Market 64 GW (FY24) -> 108 (FY25) -> 140-150 GW (FY26); company expects 130-150 GW a year to FY30 and 120-150 to FY35, i.e. a high plateau, not more growth [S2]. Q4 booked only about 2 GW of new units, no large turbines [S2].
- AI: data centres are driving North American gas and grid orders (company outlook) [S1]; data centres hunt for used turbines and jet engines (Reuters headline 2026-11-09) [S8].
- **Cyclical peak?** Gas new-unit orders look near a peak (market plateau, Q4 moderation); Grid and service look structural (grid replacement, 60% power-demand growth to 2040 per DNV, company slide) [S2].

## 4. Margins, returns, balance sheet, cash
- ROE 33.8% (Yahoo NI / equity), 36.9% stockanalysis; ROCE 26.1% [S3][S4]. Margins are high now because the backlog was priced in a seller's market (backlog margin Gas new units +26 pts, Grid +12 pts since FY22) [S2].
- Net cash about EUR 8 bn (cash 12.8, debt 3.0 bn, 30-09-26); ratings BBB+ (stable) / Baa1 (positive) [S2][S3]. Pension deficit: not verified.
- Free cash flow: FY24 1.4, FY25 4.1, FY26 8.0 bn (Yahoo, after tax) [S3]; boosted by customer prepayments and reservation fees [S1] - these reverse if orders slow.
- Shareholder returns: buyback EUR 3.1 bn in FY26; new up to EUR 9 bn to FY30 (about EUR 3 bn a year); dividend EUR 2.30 for FY26 (FY25 0.70), 40-60% payout; AGM 25 Feb 2027 [S1][S2]. Yield 1.75% [S4].
- **German guarantee (2023)**: shares fell 35-39% on 2023-10-26 when it asked for state help [S6]; on 2023-11-14 Berlin backed EUR 7.5 bn of a EUR 15 bn guarantee package (banks 12 bn, others 3 bn) for customer prepayment bonds, not cash [S6]. Replaced by a bank facility; Bloomberg headline 2025-06-05 "closes chapter on Germany's EUR 7.5 bn rescue" [S10] (exact date not verified).
