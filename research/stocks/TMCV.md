## Summary
- **WATCH - buy in steps only on a dip.** Rs 1.5 L at Rs 375-390 (about 16x FY28 consensus EPS); Rs 1.5 L more after Q3 FY27 results (late Jan 2027, first quarter with Iveco) if Iveco is at least break-even and the debt plan is clear. Cap Rs 3 L (0.7%).
- What it is: India's largest truck and bus maker (the demerged CV arm of old Tata Motors, renamed Tata Motors Ltd), listed 12 Nov 2025 [F7]. Since 30 Oct 2026 it owns 89-96% of Iveco (Italy), a business bigger than itself [F4].
- India business is excellent (Q2 standalone revenue +42%, EBIT 9.7%, ROCE 65%, net cash) [F2]; but growth is tapering [F3], and the loss-making Iveco (EUR 3.8-3.9 bn, debt-funded) changes the stock [F4][N1].
- 3-yr total return (INR): low -30% / base +26% / high +81% (about -11% / +8% / +22% a year; section 9).
- Better buy of the two Tata Motors listings; TMPV is a riskier JLR turnaround bet.

## 2. Business
- India trucks (HCV, ILMCV), small cargo and pickups (SCV), buses, spare parts and services, exports; Tata Cummins engine JV; e-buses [F2].
- Q2 FY27 wholesales 135.1K (+42%): HCV 33.8K (+41%), ILMCV 20.8K (+24%), SCV 44.0K (+27%), buses 15.4K (+31%), exports 21.1K (+177%, an Indonesia order) [F2].
- Iveco (from 30 Oct 2026): trucks, vans, buses, FPT engines, Iveco Capital; 33,000 staff, 16 plants; 2025 revenue EUR 13.4 bn = Rs 1,42,471 cr, about 1.5x TMCV's own LTM revenue [F4]. Defence arm not included [F4].
- Moat: India market leader with the widest network and parts business; Amazon picked it for 3,000 e-trucks (27 Oct 2026) [F6].

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- Iveco stays loss-making through 2027 (Europe truck slump, light-van weakness) while interest on EUR 3.8 bn of debt runs; an equity raise dilutes holders.
- India CV downturn: high base after the GST cut, diesel prices, rate hikes, a freight slowdown; truck cycles have fallen 30%+ in past downturns (not verified for this company).
- Commodity costs (590 bp hit in Q2, offset so far) [F3].
- Accounting noise: consolidated profit swings with the Tata Capital stake value [F2]; Iveco consolidation from Q3 FY27 makes year-on-year comparisons hard.
