## Summary
- BUY IN STEPS: Rs 2.1 L (about 22 shares) at or below EUR 92 now, and Rs 2.1 L at EUR 80 or after the 24 Feb 2027 results if 2027 guidance keeps about 6% organic growth and a margin rise; total Rs 4.2 L (1% cap), from the QCOM sale 1 slot, not new money.
- Dutch publisher turned software firm for doctors, tax accountants, lawyers and compliance teams (UpToDate, CCH Axcess). The P/E fell from about 30 to 16 because the market fears AI (Claude, OpenEvidence) will undercut paid expert content, not because results got worse: organic growth has held at 6% and guidance was never cut.
- 1-3 yr return per yr: local Low -8% / Base +12% / High +32%; INR -5% / +16% / +36% (section 9). The price already rose 62% from the June 2026 low (EUR 56.44).
- Key risk: AI tools take share in Health and Legal or force price cuts; 2-yr weekly correlation with MSFT is 0.43 (the scout's -0.01 is wrong for this window), so it does not diversify the MSFT AI-software risk as much as the screen says.

## 1. Business
- Five divisions, H1 2026 revenue EUR 3,033 m [W2]: Tax & Accounting 28% (CCH Axcess, CCH iFirm), Health 26% (UpToDate clinical decision support, Lexicomp, medical journals), Financial & Corporate Compliance 19% (CT Corporation, lending compliance), Legal & Regulatory 17% (European legal research, Libra AI workspace, Brightflag), Corporate Performance & ESG 11% (CCH Tagetik, Enablon, TeamMate).
- 85% of revenue recurring; cloud software 24% of revenue, growing 15% organically; print 4% and shrinking [W3]. North America 63%, Europe 29%, rest 8% [W3].
- Moat: trusted expert content embedded in daily professional workflow (tax filing software, hospital EHRs via Epic, Abridge, Suki, Doximity) [W2][W3]. AI is the threat to content alone; workflow software is harder to replace.

## 8. Risks and what proves the thesis wrong
- AI substitution: OpenEvidence (free to doctors), Claude/ChatGPT legal and tax agents. Wrong if: Health organic growth under 4% or UpToDate renewal price cuts; group recurring growth under 6%; 2027 guidance below "good organic growth".
- Spending: AI product spend at 12-13% of revenue may not earn a return; margin guidance cut would hurt.
- Leverage 2.0x and more acquisitions; FX (weak USD cuts EUR earnings).
- Sentiment: the stock moves with the AI-software theme, as on 3 Feb 2026 when MSFT also fell [W8].
