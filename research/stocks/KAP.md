## 2. Business
- Mines uranium by in-situ recovery (acid pumped through sand, no pits) at 27 deposits / 14 assets, all in Kazakhstan, many in JVs with foreign partners, including Rosatom's Uranium One and China's CGN and SNURDC [S18][S21] (full partner list not checked). Sells U3O8 under long-term contracts (part spot-linked, some with price caps) via Astana and TH Kazakatom (Switzerland) [S5].
- 2025: production 25,839 tU (100%) / 13,519 tU attributable; group sales 18,494 tU; group realized price about $63/lb vs spot avg $72.75 [S7][S8]. 2026 guide: 27,500-29,000 tU (100%), 14,500-15,500 attributable, revenue KZT 2,100-2,200 bn (cut from 2,200-2,300 in Aug on a strong tenge) [S3][S5].
- 9M26: production +10% (100%), group sales +10%, group realized $73.51/lb (+17%) vs month-end spot $87.41 (+22%); Q3 realized $81.50 [S5].

## 7. Kazakhstan, state and supply risks
- **Samruk-Kazyna (state fund) holds 75%**. New governance code Dec 2025 (fewer Samruk roles, competitive independent directors) [S8]. Oct 2025: company weighed an extra listing of part of the 25% free float (Bloomberg) [S12]: placement overhang, not decided.
- **MET (subsoil tax)**: 6% (2023) -> 9% (2025) -> 2026 tiered by mine output: 4% (<500 t) up to 18% (>4,000 t), plus 0.5-2.5% when price > $70-110/lb (Kursiv/MINEX, Feb 2026) [S16]; KAP's effective rate 9% -> 12.4% in H1 26 [S3]. A windfall tax in practice: higher prices raise the tax.
- **Subsoil law** (26 Dec 2025 and 7 Jul 2026): JV licence extensions or output increases need KAP at 90% or a technology deal from the foreign partner; new licences only to entities > 75% KAP [S3][S8]. Good for KAP's share over time, bad for partners' goodwill.
- **Sulphuric acid**: TQZ acid plant delayed from 1Q 2027 to 3Q 2027-1Q 2028 (fossils found on site) [S3]. Russia requires government approval for acid exports until 31 Dec 2026 (Resolution 1158); suppliers say 2026 deliveries will go ahead, 2027 terms under negotiation [S17]. Acid shortages cut output guidance in past years [S3]. The 2027 production guide will include this.
- **Russia / sanctions**: exports go through Russia and the Trans-Caspian route; both open [S7]. US Act of Sep 2026 on Russian uranium imports was waived by EO 14436 on 16 Oct 2026 [S5]. Rosatom (Uranium One) sold Zarechnoye and Khorasan stakes to China in 2024 [S18] but still partners JVs (Akbastau, Karatau, SMCC) [S21]; a U3O8 supply contract to Uranium One did not pass the Oct 2026 EGM (88% of votes withheld) [S6]. Rosatom builds Kazakhstan's first nuclear plant [S5].
