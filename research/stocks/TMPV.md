## 2. Business
- JLR (Range Rover, Defender, Discovery, Jaguar): Q2 FY27 revenue GBP 5.9 bn, 82.5K wholesales; US is about a third of volume; China local production ended June 2026 [F3].
- Tata PV and EV (India): #2 carmaker, Vahan market share 14.3%; EV leader with 42.8% EV share; Punch was India's #1 model in Q2 [F3].
- Others: Tata Technologies stake and similar (Rs 1,077 cr revenue in Q2) [F3].
- Moat: JLR luxury brands and Range Rover pricing power; in India a broad EV range and the Tata brand. Weak spots: JLR cash break-even rose from 300K units (FY23-24) to 425K (FY26) [F3].

## 8. Risks (what proves the thesis wrong)
- JLR margin stays under 3% in H2 FY27 or FY28 FCF is not positive -> book value keeps falling, net debt does not peak.
- China (luxury imports, Chinese EV makers in Europe), US tariffs, Middle East disruption, higher VME (discounts) [F3][N1].
- Another cyber or IT failure (Sep 2025 attack stopped JLR plants for weeks) [F3].
- India PV: commodity inflation keeps EBITDA near 4%; demand growth fades after the GST-cut base (company: "growth moderating on a high base") [F3].
- Positive surprise risk (for a short or a skip): new Range Rover Electric (4,300 orders, deliveries from Dec 2026) and Jaguar Type 01 sell well [F3].
