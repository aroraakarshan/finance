## Summary

- What it is: oil and gas producer (US Permian, Egypt Western Desert gas-heavy, UK North Sea; 50% of Suriname GranMorgu, first oil mid-2028, operated by TotalEnergies). $48.44 (Rs 4,555); market cap $16.3 bn (Yahoo).
- Verdict: **DROP** - the +251% EPS is a low base plus a war-driven oil price (Brent $95.8); consensus sees 2027 EPS -19%.
- What to do: do not buy. For oil exposure, look at TotalEnergies instead: same oil and GranMorgu exposure, half the volatility, twice the dividend, no US estate tax (section 10a).
- 1-3 yr return (to Nov 2028, USD, incl. dividends): low -50% / base -12% / high +34% (section 9).

## 2. Business

- Q3 2026 production (8-K exhibit 99.1, 2026-11-04): 450,276 BOE/day reported: US 294,268 (65%), Egypt 133,610 (30%, incl. noncontrolling partner and tax barrels), North Sea 22,398 (5%). Adjusted (ex Egypt NCI and tax barrels): 381,000 BOE/day.
- Q3 2026 revenue $2,769 m: oil $1,934 m (70%), gas $236 m, NGL $190 m, purchased oil and gas resold $409 m (same release). Oil is what drives profit.
- North Sea output -27% y/y; Egypt oil -17% y/y (higher Brent cuts APA's share under the production sharing contracts, release). US oil 128,371 b/d, +6% y/y.
- Suriname GranMorgu: APA 50%, TotalEnergies operator; "capital carry" for APA; first oil mid-2028 (release; Reuters headline 2026-09-20).
- Moat: none beyond low-cost Permian acreage and Egypt gas contracts. Price taker.
- Competitors: DVN, OXY, EOG, COP (US E&P); TotalEnergies is both partner and alternative.
