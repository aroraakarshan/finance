## Summary
- Most attractive: Information Technology. Its price-to-earnings ratio (18.0) is far below its own 5-year average (25.4) while analysts expect 42% earnings growth in 2027. No other sector is this far below its own history.
- Least attractive: Consumer Staples (paying 23.7x for 6% growth, well above its history) and Energy (earnings expected to fall 8.5% in 2027 after an oil-price jump). Consumer Discretionary is also weak: top P/E (25.1) with falling 2027 earnings.
- Health Care has had the best run (+31% in 6 months) and still grows well, but it now costs more than its history: hold, do not chase.
- The market as a whole is not expensive on forward earnings: S&P 500 forward P/E 18.3 vs 5-yr 19.8 and 10-yr 19.1 (FactSet, 13 Nov 2026).

## How to read the table
- Returns: SPDR sector ETF total return (dividends included, Yahoo adjusted closes via yfinance) to the close of 13 Nov 2026; 1y from 13 Nov 2025, 6m from 14 May 2026. "vs SPY" = sector return minus S&P 500 ETF return (SPY: 1y +13.5%, 6m +2.6%), in percentage points (pp).
- Valuation: forward 12-month P/E (price / next 12 months of expected earnings) from FactSet Earnings Insight, 13 Nov 2026, against the sector's own 5- and 10-year averages and the S&P 500 (18.3).
- Growth: analysts' expected 2027 (CY 2027) earnings growth, same FactSet report. S&P 500: +16.2% earnings, +10.0% revenue.
- The ETFs hold S&P 500 sector members but cap the biggest stocks, so returns differ a little from the FactSet sector figures.

## What changed since 30 Sep 2026 (FactSet)
- S&P 500 price -0.8%, 12-month EPS estimate +3.3%, so P/E fell from 19.0 to 18.3 - the lowest in more than 3 years (FactSet blog, 9 Nov 2026).
- IT (price about -10%, EPS about +7%) and Communication Services (price about -10%, EPS about -1%) fell most; Consumer Staples rose most (about +14%) with flat EPS; Energy EPS rose most (about +11%). Values read from FactSet's chart, so approximate.
- Bottom-up analyst price targets: IT +35.8% and Communication Services +29.2% upside; Staples +4.6%, Health Care +8.3%, Financials +8.9% (market +22.2%). Targets are usually too high, so use them only to compare sectors.
