## Summary
- Most attractive: Financials. Asian banks and insurers are cheap (Hang Seng financials P/E 9.6, yield 4.1%; China financials forward P/E about 6x, at its 15-yr average), earnings grow steadily (+7% a year), and they beat the market: +23% in 1 year and +11% in 6 months while the region fell 9%.
- Least attractive: Consumer Discretionary (China e-commerce, cars, travel: -18% in 1y, -21% in 6m, earnings flat in 2026), Materials (miners and chemicals -17% in 6m, earnings growth slowing from 42% to 11%) and Industrials (Korea shipbuilding, defence and nuclear names down 45-55% in 6m).
- Tech is the big call: earnings expected +239% in 2026 and +41% in 2027 (memory-chip boom), but prices fell 16% in 6m and Korea looks cheap only because profits are at a peak. Neutral; for this user (57% in semis) it adds to the same bet.
- The region as a whole is cheap: MSCI Asia Pacific ex-Japan forward P/E 10.5 vs about 13 on 15-yr average (JPM, 30 Sep 2026).

## How to read the table
- Returns: no free MSCI Asia ex-Japan sector index or ETF prices were reachable, so each sector is an equal-weight basket of its 7-10 largest stocks across China/HK, Korea, Taiwan, Australia and Singapore (list below), in USD, Yahoo adjusted closes (dividends included) via yfinance, to 13 Nov 2026; 1y from 14 Nov 2025, 6m from 14 May 2026. Market = iShares MSCI All Country Asia ex Japan ETF (AAXJ): 1y +14.1%, 6m -9.4%. "pp" = percentage points vs AAXJ. Equal weights make baskets differ from cap-weighted MSCI sectors.
- HSCI = Hang Seng Composite industry index (Hang Seng's own classes, e.g. Tencent is in IT, not Comm Services): 1y / 6m return, trailing P/E and dividend yield, 30 Oct 2026. HSCI overall: 1y -15.7%, 6m -13.0%, P/E 14.1, yield 3.1%.
- Valuation vs history: forward P/E of the MSCI China sector vs its 15-yr average (JPM chart, read by eye, about +-1x). MSCI does not publish free Asia ex-Japan sector P/E history (msci.com blocked by a challenge page); pan-Asia sector P/E vs history is "not verified".
- Earnings growth: MSCI AC Asia Pacific ex-Japan sector EPS growth, FactSet consensus for 2026 and 2027 (JPM Guide to the Markets Asia, 30 Sep 2026). Region: +68.0% 2026, +25.5% 2027.
