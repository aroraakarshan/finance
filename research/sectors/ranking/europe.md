# Europe - sector ranking (STOXX Europe 600, UK included)

Written 2026-11-15. Market research, not advice; the user decides. Region context:
[Eurozone](../../global/eurozone.md) and [UK](../../global/uk.md). Sources [S#] at the end.

## Summary
- Most attractive now: **health care** (cheaper than usual vs the US, momentum turning up, defensive) and **financials, mainly insurers** (P/E about 13, beating the market).
- Least attractive: **real estate** (rising rates, flat for a year), **autos** (-16% in 1y, Chinese EV and tariff pressure) and **industrials** (P/E about 28, defence rally fading).
- Energy and materials led the last year on the oil shock; that is now in prices, so neutral.
- Region overall: Europe ex-UK forward P/E 14.9 vs 14.7 average; UK 12.2 vs 13.7 (30 Sep 2026 [S2]). Use sector ETFs only as small tilts on top of one pan-Europe ETF.

## How to read the table
- Returns: total return in EUR of the iShares STOXX Europe 600 sector ETF on Xetra (dividends
  reinvested), close 2026-11-12 vs 2025-11-12 (1y) and 2026-05-12 (6m) [S1]. "vs mkt" = minus
  the STOXX Europe 600 ETF (EXSA): 1y +17.3%, 6m +9.7%, 3m +0.8%.
- P/E: trailing P/E of the ETF from Yahoo (2026-11-12 [S1]; not checked against iShares, so
  "not verified"). Market (EXSA) 18.0; worldperatio puts Europe at 17.7 vs 10y average 14.2 [S3].
- "vs US now / avg": J.P. Morgan chart of MSCI Europe ex-UK sector forward P/E as a premium (+)
  or discount (-) to the same S&P 500 sector, now vs its average since 1995 (30 Sep 2026 [S2]).
  Read off a chart, so about +-2 points. A discount bigger than usual = cheap vs its own history.
  UK figures are the same chart for MSCI UK [S2].
- STOXX uses ICB sectors; the ETFs are mapped to the 11 GICS sectors as shown.
- Next-year (2027) earnings growth by sector: no free source found (not verified). Region-level
  consensus 2027 EPS growth: Europe ex-UK about +11%, UK about +7% (2026: about +20% and +21%)
  [S2]. "Revisions" = J.P. Morgan global (MSCI ACWI) sector contribution to 2026 forecast
  changes this year [S2]; global, not Europe-only.

## How to act (for this user)
- Base: one pan-Europe ETF (MEUD / SMEA, see [eurozone.md](../../global/eurozone.md)); its
  sector weights were not checked here (not verified).
- If tilting, health care and insurers are the candidates; sector ETFs above are German-domiciled
  (DE) UCITS on Xetra, distributing. Check the listing, costs and tax on IBKR first.
- Skip real estate and autos; do not chase defence.
