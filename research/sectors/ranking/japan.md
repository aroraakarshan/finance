## Summary
- Most attractive: Health Care (drugmakers at their usual P/E, cheaper vs the market than usual, rising trend), Financials (banks: profits and forecasts rising with each BOJ rate hike) and autos (below-average price-to-book, Toyota raised its forecast).
- Least attractive: steel and nonferrous metals (price at 2x its usual P/E after a -35% fall in 6 months), Consumer Staples (foods at a record price-to-book for slow growth) and Real Estate (rising rates, weak trend).
- Electronics (Japan's tech) earns the most from AI but costs 2.2x the market's P/E; hold, do not add.
- The market is a little above its own history: Prime market P/E 19.8 vs median 18.6 since 2020, P/B 1.70 vs 1.30 (JPX, Oct 2026). Country view: ../../global/japan.md.

## How to read the table
- Returns: NEXT FUNDS TOPIX-17 sector ETFs (1617.T to 1633.T), Yahoo adjusted closes (dividends included) via yfinance, to 13 Nov 2026; 1y from 13 Nov 2025, 6m from 15 May 2026. "vs mkt" = minus the TOPIX ETF 1306.T (1y +19.3%, 6m +3.3%), in percentage points (pp). In yen, not INR.
- These ETFs trade thinly (median 286 to 4,403 units a day), so a price can lag its index. Spot checks of big members agree: Fujikura 6m -49.7%, Sumitomo Electric -44.2% (steel and nonferrous); Mitsubishi Heavy -28.2%, Kawasaki Heavy -40.0% (machinery); MUFG +20.2% (banks) (Yahoo, 13 Nov 2026).
- Valuation: JPX "PER/PBR by industry", Prime market, cap-weighted, Oct 2026. P/E is trailing (last full-year profits, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026), not forward: no free forward P/E by Japanese sector was found (not verified). History = median of 13 half-year readings, Apr 2020 to Apr 2026 (Apr 2020 to Oct 2021 = old First Section, then Prime). JPX's 33 industries are summed into the 17 TOPIX-17 groups. Market: P/E 19.8, P/B 1.70.
- Growth: company forecasts for the year to March 2027, compiled by SMBC Nikko (431 of 1,106 TOPIX March-year companies, as of 5 Nov 2026): total net profit +16.9%, a 7th year of growth; first-half profit +43.2%. Next year (to March 2028) is expected to slow; no number found (not verified).
- Sectors: GICS has no exact Japanese match. Each row uses the closest TOPIX-17 group(s). Communication services is not separate: telecoms (NTT, KDDI, SoftBank Corp) sit in "IT and services, others" with SoftBank Group, Recruit and software.

## Notes and discrepancies
- Trailing P/E can mislead when profits swing: energy, utilities, steel and transport had loss years in the history, so their medians are low and P/B is the better guide for them.
- "IT and services, others" P/E fell from 23.4 (Apr 2026) to 17.1 (Oct 2026): JPX's Information and Communication industry shows 12.1, likely held down by SoftBank Group's earlier gains (cause not verified).
- 1306.T and 1629.T show one bad Yahoo price around 31 Mar 2026 (a near-zero close); the 1y and 6m start and end dates are not affected.
- Returns are in yen. For an INR investor add the yen move (JPY/INR +2.7% over 1y to 13 Nov 2026, ../../global/japan.md).
