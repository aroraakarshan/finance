# India: sector ranking (11 GICS sectors)

Written 2026-11-15. Market research, not advice; no orders. Market-wide view, fund picks and more sector
detail: [India market now](../../themes/opp-india-market.md) and its [sectors part](../../themes/opp-india-market/sectors.md) (not repeated here).

## Summary
- Most attractive: private banks (P/E 13.5 vs 5y median 15.8, earnings +17% expected FY28, price beating Nifty) and FMCG (cheapest vs own 10y history, earnings recovering, but price still falling: buy slowly).
- IT is cheap (P/E 19.4 vs 27.0) but expected earnings growth is only ~5%: neutral.
- Least attractive: pharma/health care (most expensive in 5y after +26% in 1y), defence and capital goods (2-2.7x market P/E), chemicals.
- Everything else (autos, energy, metals, power, realty, telecom, PSU banks) is about fair: neutral.

## How to read the table
- Mood: price index return to 13-Nov-2026, 1y from 13-Nov-2025 and 6m from 13-May-2026; "vs mkt" = minus the Nifty 50 (1y -2.8%, 6m +7.4%) [PX].
- Valuation: trailing P/E of the sector index (last 12 months' profits, not forward) vs its own 5y and 10y
  MEDIAN, "pctl" = share of the last 5y days with a lower P/E, and "x mkt" = sector P/E / Nifty 50 P/E (20.5) now vs its 5y median [PE].
- Earnings: analyst consensus EPS growth for FY27 (Apr-26 to Mar-27) and FY28, as the weight-weighted
  median of the top 5 stocks of the index (34-80% of the index by weight; most 55-80%) [FS, YF]. A rough guide, not an index forecast.
- Verdict compares cheapness, growth and trend; "attractive" means worth a closer look, not a buy order.

## Notes and gaps
- The 11 GICS sectors map to Nifty indices that are not GICS-based; telecom has no Nifty sector index
  (Nifty India Digital mixes telecom, IT and internet; its 5y P/E median 118 is distorted by loss years).
- Earnings: FY28 growth recomputed as FY28 / FY27 consensus EPS, because Yahoo's own "growth" for SBI used a
  different base (yearAgo 107.0 vs FY27 estimate 95.1). Standalone vs consolidated basis: not verified.
  Yahoo FY28 figures are the analyst average on 2026-11-15; coverage 5-40 analysts per stock (MTAR 5).
- Index P/E is trailing; sector forward P/E: not verified. P/E basis over 10 years: not verified (see
  [India sources](../../themes/opp-india-market/sources.md)).
- Nifty Media top names Nazara, Saregama and Tips are outside the Nifty 500 symbol list, so media growth
  uses PVR Inox, Zee, Sun TV and Prime Focus only (not in the table).
