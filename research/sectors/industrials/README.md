## Summary
- Order books are at records almost everywhere (L&T Rs 8.5 lakh crore, Lockheed $292 bn, Airbus 9,300+ planes), but
  share prices already assume this: US industrials trade at 21.7x next-year earnings vs a 17.2x 10-year average.
- Defence has cracked in 2026: Rheinmetall -56%, Mazagon -30%, BDL -32%, Korean names down hard, on delays and
  thin margins. Demand is real; the problem is the price paid and the speed of delivery.
- Best value now: the global industrials fund XDWI (Irish), L&T in India (23x FY28 earnings), and Japan's trading
  houses (Mitsui 13.5x). Add defence and aerospace only in small steps (HAL, BAE, Safran).
- Avoid Indian power-equipment names at 55-67x forward earnings (ABB India, Siemens, Hitachi Energy India).
