# Industrials: growth outlook and risks

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Back to the index: [README.md](README.md). Source tags: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- Demand is not the problem: India's government plans Rs 12.22 lakh crore of capital spending in FY27, NATO
  members pledged 5% of GDP for defence by 2035, and order books are at records from L&T to Lockheed.
- The problem is turning orders into profit on time: Rheinmetall, Mazagon, BDL and Hanwha all disappointed in
  Oct-Nov 2026 on delays and margins, and their shares fell 30-60%.
- Biggest risks: high prices (India defence 56x), delivery delays, peace talks, and the AI-power link (Hitachi
  Energy, Cummins, MHI move with the AI trade the user already owns through tech shares).

## 4. Growth outlook

### India: capex cycle and order books
"Capex" = spending on long-lived assets (roads, rail, plants, power lines). "Order book" = signed work not yet billed.
- Government capex FY27 (Apr-2026 to Mar-2027): Rs 12.2 lakh crore in the Budget, fiscal deficit target 4.3%
  (News18, 2026-01-31) [N22]; restated as Rs 12.22 lakh crore by the finance minister (headline, 2026-11-02) [N22].
- Railways: record Rs 2.77 lakh crore allocation (Financial Express, 2026-02-01) [N23].
- Private capex: RBI estimates Rs 3.2 lakh crore planned for 2026-27 (BusinessLine, 2026-09-25) [N24]: private
  companies still spend far less than the government.
- Consensus EPS growth (Yahoo, 25 analysts) for L&T: +20.1% in FY27, +19.8% in FY28 [Y].

### Defence budgets
- NATO: The Hague 2025 pledge is 5% of GDP by 2035 (3.5% core defence + 1.5% wider security) (CSIS, 2026-07-06)
  [OST]. The US is expected to spend 3.17% of GDP on core defence in 2026 (News18, 2026-07-10) [N25]; only two
  members already spend 5% (NDTV headline, 2026-07-10) [N25].
- World: military spending USD 2.887 trillion in 2025, +2.9% after inflation, Europe USD 864 bn (+14%) (SIPRI via
  Bursa, 2026-11-06) [N20]. Budgets include pay and running costs, so only part becomes equipment orders.
- India: defence budget Rs 7.85 lakh crore with Rs 2.19 lakh crore for modernisation (headline, 2026-11-02);
  targets of Rs 3 lakh crore output and Rs 50,000 crore exports by 2029 (Business Standard headline, 2026-11-03)
  [N26]; output Rs 1.78 lakh crore in FY26 (+15.6%), exports Rs 38,424 crore (PIB, 2026-11-11) [OST].
- Backlogs (Q3 2026): Lockheed USD 292 bn record [N11]; RTX about USD 340 bn record, 2026 guide raised [N12];
  General Dynamics USD 220 bn record [N13]; BAE GBP 33 bn of orders in 2026, cash-flow guide raised to GBP 3 bn
  (11-Nov) [N9]; Saab orders +216% in Q3, backlog +80% (Gripen for Ukraine, GlobalEye) [N15]; Thales raised its 2026
  sales outlook (21-Oct) [N16]; Rheinmetall: full order book but the German Boxer contract slipped into 2027 [N10].

### Aerospace (civil planes)
- Airbus targets about 870 deliveries in 2026; 621 done by October, so 249 needed in two months; engine supply is
  the limit; backlog above 9,300 planes (6-Nov) [N19].
- GE Aerospace raised its 2026 guide to adjusted EPS USD 8.00-8.10 (20-Oct) [N14]; Safran Q3 revenue EUR 9.0 bn,
  guide confirmed, shares hit by aftermarket worries (Reuters headline, 22-Oct) [N14]. Consensus EPS growth 2027:
  GE +14%, Safran +21%, Airbus +18% [Y].

### Transport and logistics (India)
- IndiGo (InterGlobe): Q2 FY27 loss Rs 1,879 crore (narrower), revenue +26%, fuel cost +67% (29-Oct) [N27].
  Consensus expects a return to profit (FY28 EPS Rs 216, 16 analysts) [Y]; oil is the swing factor.
- Rail: record budget [N23] but rail PSU shares fell (IRFC -29%, RVNL -36% in 1y [Y]); RVNL at 45x trailing has one
  analyst. Container Corp is the only one below its 3-year-ago price (-14%/yr) [Y].

### Japan
- Trading houses: Berkshire held near 10% of all five (2025) and its CEO Greg Abel said in Sep-2026 it will hold
  them for decades and may buy more [N18]. Mitsubishi Corp cut its share count 6% in a year (buybacks) [SA].
- Mitsubishi Heavy: H1 revenue +19.4%, net profit +76.9%, full-year profit guide raised to JPY 440 bn, buyback up
  to JPY 300 bn; yet the stock fell 8% on 6-Nov to an 18-month low in the AI sell-off [N17].
- Hitachi Ltd (Tokyo 6501, not Hitachi Energy India): consensus EPS +24% in FY27 and +20% in FY28 (grid and digital) [Y].

## 5. Key risks
| Risk | What it means | Most exposed |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Good news is in the price; a miss gets punished (Cummins -14% in a day) | ABB India 67x, Hitachi Energy India 63x, Siemens 55x fwd; Nifty India Defence 56x |
| Delivery delays | Revenue slips a year; margins drop; cash conversion weak | Rheinmetall, Mazagon, BDL, Hanwha (Q3 operating profit -46% [N21]), Airbus engines |
| One customer | Indian defence PSUs sell mainly to the Ministry of Defence; orders come in lumps | HAL, BEL, BDL, Mazagon |
| Peace or politics | Ceasefire talk cut defence shares (Korea fell on prospects of the Iran war ending, 5-Nov [N21]); US midterm result hit US/EU defence [OST] | DFNS, Korean and European defence |
| AI-power link | Grid and generator demand is partly AI data centres; moves with the user's tech shares | Hitachi Energy India, Cummins (data-centre gensets [N7]), MHI, Siemens Energy |
| Commodities and oil | Trading-house profits follow metal and energy prices; airline profits follow fuel | Mitsui, Mitsubishi Corp; IndiGo |
| Currency | Returns for an Indian investor also depend on EUR, GBP, JPY and USD vs INR | All foreign names and XDWI |
| Single-name risk | One bad quarter can cut a stock by a third | All single stocks: keep each small; prefer XDWI for the core |
