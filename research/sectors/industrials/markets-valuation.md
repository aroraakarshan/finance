# Industrials: markets, returns and valuation

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Back to the index: [README.md](README.md). Source tags: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- Industrials beat or matched their markets over 3 years in every region; in 2026 the picture split: world and
  US industrials up 12-15%, defence down (DFNS -10%, Rheinmetall -56%), Indian defence and rail PSUs down 30-36%.
- US industrials cost 21.7x next-year earnings vs 17.2x 10-year average and vs 17.7x for the whole S&P 500:
  a rare premium to the market [FS]. European defence (BAE 19.7x vs 11-17x before 2025) is also above its past.
- India: defence index 56x and capital goods about 39x are expensive; L&T (23x forward) and HAL (28x) are the
  sensible-priced leaders. Japan's trading houses are the cheapest group (13.5-16.4x) after a strong run.

Mood in words:
- **Capital goods (India).** Results in Oct-Nov 2026 were split: L&T profit +32%, record orders [N1]; Hitachi Energy
  India order inflow +86% [N5]; ABB India record orders [N6]; but Cummins India profit -27% and shares -14% on cost
  pressure [N7]. Nifty Capital Goods fell 35.1% in 2024-25 and is recovering [OIM].
- **Defence.** A "rearmament paradox" (CNBC via Bursa, 2026-11-06): budgets rise while shares fall on delays, cost
  overruns and drones changing what armies buy [N20]. Rheinmetall cut its 2026 sales guide a second time (to about
  EUR 13.2 bn, margin 18.5%) and fell over 17% intraday on 5-Nov [N10]; Mazagon profit -54% [N4]; BDL profit -15%
  [N4]; Hanwha Aerospace Q3 operating profit -46% [N21]. US defence fell after the Democrats won the midterms
  (Bloomberg headline, 2026-11-04) [OST].
- **Aerospace.** Steady: Airbus is near its high, Safran and GE 13-16% below theirs; GE raised its 2026 guide
  (adjusted EPS $8.00-8.10) but the stock fell 2% that day [N14].
- **Japan.** Trading houses near highs (Berkshire says it will hold them for decades [N18]); machinery and MHI fell
  with the Nov-2026 AI sell-off (Nikkei -7.1% on 6-Nov) [N17].
