## Summary
- Main risks: AI price cuts in IT services, AI agents shrinking software seats, the chip cycle
  turning, US visa and trade policy, and his own concentration in US chips and MSFT.
- Gaps marked "not verified" below; check them before buying.

## 3. Tax (from tax/investment-types.md and tax/new-investments.md)

| Type | Gains | Dividends |
|---|---|---|
| Indian listed equity, Indian equity ETFs | STCG 20%; LTCG 12.5% above Rs 1.25 L after 12 months | Slab |
| Foreign shares (SAP, Keyence, Accenture, TSMC) | LTCG 12.5% after 24 months, else slab | Slab, credit for treaty withholding: Germany 10% (26.375% taken, reclaim rest), Japan 10% (15.315% taken), Ireland 10% (25% taken), Taiwan 12.5% (21% taken), US 25% |
| Irish UCITS accumulating | No tax until sale; then LTCG 12.5% after 24 months, else slab | None |
| US-incorporated shares (MSFT, CRM, ADBE, NVDA) | as foreign shares | US 25%; **US-situs (estate tax)** above $60,000 |

Accenture plc is listed in New York but incorporated in Ireland: under 26 USC 2104(a) shares of a
non-US company are not US-situs [S20]. Treating it as US-situs (because it is US-listed) would be
wrong; whether IBKR applies Irish withholding at 10% or 25% is not verified.
