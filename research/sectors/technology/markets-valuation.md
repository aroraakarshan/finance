# Technology: markets, mood and valuation

Part of [information technology](README.md). Written 2026-11-15. Sources [S#] in
[risks and sources](risks-sources.md).

## Summary
- The past year split in two: chip stocks and Asian chip markets rose 40-105%; US software was
  down 5% and Indian IT down 13%.
- The last month turned: world IT fell 11.9% (Nvidia's CEO quit on 12 Nov) while Indian IT ETFs
  rose 13% [S10][S11][S14].
- US IT is at its lowest forward P/E since March 2020 and below its 25-year average; Nifty IT
  is cheaper than 80% of days in 10 years. Chip profits are at a peak, so their low P/Es can mislead.

- Forward P/Es for US chip and software names use analysts' "adjusted" EPS, which is higher
  than reported (GAAP) profit; trailing P/E in the tables uses reported profit.
- Nifty P/E history: NSE's earnings basis for index P/E may have changed over the decade
  (not verified); the 10-year comparison is approximate.
- Chips: low forward P/Es (NVDA 10.3, Samsung 2.9) assume peak profits continue. Peak-cycle
  profit makes P/E look cheap; this is the classic chip-cycle trap.
