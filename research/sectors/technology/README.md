## Summary
- Chips drove 2026 (US IT profits +69% y/y in Q3, chips +133% [S2]); software fell on AI fear and mostly recovered; Indian IT fell 13%.
- Cheap vs history: US IT forward P/E (price / next 12 months' expected profit) 17.3 vs 25y avg 20.0 [S3]; Nifty IT P/E 19.4 vs 10y avg 25.0 [S4].
- The cheapness is in software and IT services, not chips; he already holds QCOM, AVGO, NVDA and MSFT, over a third of any world IT fund [S9].
- Buy what does not repeat them: Indian IT (ITBEES or TCS), SAP, Accenture; in steps, as AI price cuts and the H-1B fee can still hurt.
