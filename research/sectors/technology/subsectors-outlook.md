# Technology: subsectors and growth outlook

Part of [information technology](README.md). Written 2026-11-15. Sources [S#] in
[risks and sources](risks-sources.md). P/E = market cap / last-12-month net income (Yahoo,
13 Nov 2026) in the stock's own currency; fwd = price / next-year consensus EPS (Yahoo) [S1],
with stockanalysis [S8] where Yahoo's EPS was wrong.

## Summary
- Spending forecast: Gartner sees 2027 IT spending +15%, but most of it is data-centre hardware
  (+55.5%); software +14.6%, IT services only +4.9% [S7].
- Software: the AI-replaces-apps fear hit hard in 2026; profits keep growing (US software EPS
  +17% y/y in Q3 [S2]), so several large names trade near 9-14x forward profit.
- IT services: AI cuts the hours clients pay for ("AI deflation"); Infosys guides 1-2% growth. The
  H-1B visa fee is extended but blocked in court. Prices already reflect much of this.
- Chips: the boom is real but he already owns it through QCOM, AVGO and NVDA.

## 2. Software (SaaS = software rented as a subscription)

- Fear: AI agents (e.g. Anthropic's tools, Feb 2026) could let firms build their own apps or need
  fewer paid user seats [S15]. The software index fell over 26% (late Jan - Apr), then hit 2026
  highs by 6 Oct as profits beat [S16]; ServiceNow then fell 27% on 29 Oct [S18].
- Likely winners: firms that own data and the system of record (SAP, Microsoft, Oracle's cloud),
  security software (CrowdStrike, Palo Alto: "triple-digit" gains in 2026 [S16]). Most exposed:
  per-seat apps whose work AI agents can do (not verified per company).

## 3. IT services

- AI deflation: clients expect AI to do part of the coding and support they paid people for, so
  prices per project fall even when volumes hold. TCS and Infosys say AI is over 10% of Q2 revenue
  [S22]; TCS AI revenue crossed $3 bn (whether annualised: not verified) [S12].
- Results: TCS Q2 FY27 revenue +11.2% y/y to Rs 73,188 Cr, profit +15% to Rs 13,884 Cr [S12]. Most of the +11.2% is the rupee's fall: constant-currency growth was +2.8% ([TCS.md](../../stocks/TCS.md)).
  Infosys: +1.2% q/q in constant currency, FY27 guidance cut to 1-2% [S17]. Accenture: Q4 revenue
  +7% to $18.7 bn, bookings $22.2 bn, FY27 guidance 3-6% (was 3-4%); stock about 56% below its
  late-2021 peak (Bloomberg) [S13].
- H-1B fee: a $100,000 payment on new H-1B petitions from Sep 2025; extended on 18 Sep 2026 to
  21 Sep 2027, but blocked by a Massachusetts court (Jun 2026, upheld on appeal 24 Jul) and an
  N.D. California court (30 Sep 2026); government appeals continue and USCIS says it may collect
  retroactively if it wins [S23]. India has 71% of H-1B approvals (HT) [S24]; TCS, Infosys and
  Cognizant filed fewer H-1B registrations (Livemint, 18 Oct 2026) [S24]; cost effect not verified.

## 4. Internet platforms

Under the GICS sector rules, Alphabet and Meta sit in communication services and Amazon in
consumer discretionary, so they are not in IT funds (XDWT top 10 has none of them [S9]). They
belong in the communication-services note; not covered here.
