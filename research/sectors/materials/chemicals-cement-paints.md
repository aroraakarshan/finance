# Materials part 2: chemicals, cement and paints

Part of [materials](README.md). Written 2026-11-15. Prices: closes of 12-13 Nov 2026 [Y]; source tags in
[sources](sources.md).

## Summary
- Indian specialty chemicals recover unevenly: Deepak, Aarti, Navin profits jumped; PI and SRF disappointed.
- Prices have not reset (Nifty Chemicals 41.5x earnings); only PI looks cheap (16x forward) after a 71% profit
  fall. Wait for proof of recovery. Globally, Shin-Etsu is the quality pick; BASF gained from the Iran war.
- Cement and paints are still expensive (UltraTech 40x, Asian Paints 50x) with competition rising: not now.

## Growth outlook
- India chemicals: volume recovery visible (Aarti +30% volumes [N18]); pricing still set by China [N16].
- Cement: demand tied to housing and infrastructure; Ambuja and UltraTech adding capacity [N22] keeps prices in
  check.
- Paints: growth depends on price rises and housing; competition caps margins [N23].

## Risks
- Chinese overcapacity and export push (chemicals) [N15][N16]; anti-dumping duties help only some products.
- Agro-chemical exports weak (PI) [N17]; refrigerant gas supply in India to rise into 2027 (SRF) [N16].
- War-driven price rises reverse (BASF) [N21]; high valuations in cement and paints leave no room for misses.
