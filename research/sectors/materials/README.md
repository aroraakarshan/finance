## Summary
- Metals rose strongly for 3 years; copper set a record in Sep 2026 (now 6% below); miners fell 20-30% since.
- Prices are about average, not cheap: US materials 17.4x next-year profit vs 17.8x 10-year average; Indian metals
  15.0x vs 15.8x median, but at 2.7x book value (88th percentile), a sign profits may be near a peak.
- Indian chemicals, cement and paints still cost 30-56x profit after a 2-3 year slump: wait. A fair time to buy
  metals in steps: Irish fund XDWM as core, mining fund GDIG, Hindalco (about 10x) or a Nifty Metal ETF in India.

## Key risks (details in the parts)
- Metal prices: copper is mostly tariff stockpiling and AI data-centre hopes; Goldman sees downside to its
  $15,400/t 2027 forecast [N6]. Gold already fell 27% from January; miners swing 2-3x as much.
- China: record trade surplus over $1 trn in 2026 [N4]; steel exports 95.5 Mt in Jan-Oct (-2.3% y/y) [N3];
  chemical exports surging [N15]. Oversupply caps prices outside India's 11-12% steel safeguard duty [N2].
- India: safeguard duty ends Dec 2028 [N2]; chemicals face Chinese pricing (SRF, PI); paints face Birla Opus.
- Currency: XDWM/GDIG are in USD; a stronger rupee reduces INR returns.

## Discrepancies found
- The brief says "copper at a record": LME copper's record was $14,533/t on 7-8 Sep 2026 [N1]; on 12-13 Nov it was
  $13,612 [N6], about 6% below. COMEX futures: $6.09/lb vs $6.80 high on 9 Sep [Y].
- "iShares MSCI Global Metals and Mining Producers" is US-listed (PICK, US-situs). No UCITS version appears in
  justETF's list of 18 materials UCITS ETFs [JE3]; GDIG is the closest Irish fund.
- Vedanta demerged into five listed companies in June 2026 [N9]; Yahoo's trailing P/E (4.7) mixes pre-demerger
  profit, so it is not used.
- Yahoo Indian prices stop at 12 Nov; NSE index data runs to 13 Nov. Other data issues: see the parts.
