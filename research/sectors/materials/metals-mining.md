## Summary
- Copper and Indian steel are the strong stories; gold and aluminium have fallen from 2026 peaks.
- Indian steel makers had a strong September quarter on volumes and the 11-12% safeguard duty (a temporary
  import tax); Hindalco is the cheapest large Indian metal stock (about 10x earnings).
- Global miners fell 20-30% from their highs in 3 months: buy an Irish mining fund (GDIG) in steps.

## Copper
- Record on 7-8 Sep 2026 on US tariff stockpiling, tight mine supply and demand from AI data centres, power
  grids and electric cars [N1]. US distributor Border States reports copper prices +35% and steel +31% as data-centre
  demand strains supply (Nov 2026) [N7].
- Forecast: Goldman Sachs $15,400/t for 2027 but sees downside risk (about $14,500-14,600) as AI-linked
  positioning and US tariff hopes fade after the midterm elections [N6]. Banks are split between a 2027 surplus and
  a later mine shortfall (headlines only, mining.com 403) [N8].
- Ways in: Global X Copper Miners UCITS (COPX on LSE, IE0003Z9E2Y3, TER 0.55%, EUR 912 m, Acc) or iShares Copper
  Miners UCITS (IE00063FT9K6, TER 0.55%, EUR 591 m) [JE3]. Proxy valuation: US-listed COPX fwd P/E 10.95, 52-week
  high $99.99 vs $69.23 now [SA4]. In India: Hindustan Copper, Hindalco (copper smelting).

## Steel
- India: safeguard duty on flat steel imports of 12% in year 1, 11.5% year 2, 11% year 3, notified 30 Dec 2025
  (stainless and some developing countries excluded) [N2]. worldsteel sees Indian steel demand +7.9% in 2026 and
  +8% in 2027, the fastest of big markets, while world demand is flat at 1,722 Mt in 2026 and +1.7% in 2027 [N5].
- A new steel policy targets about 600 Mt capacity by 2047 [N11]. Users (Punjab industry) want the duty waived
  as prices rose (Sep 2026) [N11].
- China: steel exports 9.85 Mt in Oct 2026, 95.5 Mt in Jan-Oct (-2.3% y/y), still near record levels [N3]; China
  planned export licences to curb record shipments (Dec 2025) [N3].
- September quarter (Q2 FY27): Tata Steel profit +16% to Rs 3,700 cr, revenue Rs 65,436 cr; JSW Steel profit
  Rs 5,279 cr (+225%), EBITDA margin 18.6% vs 15.8%; SAIL profit +315% [N12].

## Aluminium
- LME cash aluminium trades above 3-month (backwardation, a sign of tight near-term stocks) at a 36-year high,
  awaiting new supply (Nov 2026, headlines) [N13]. COMEX aluminium is still 26% below its May peak [Y].
- Hindalco: record Q2 FY27, copper EBITDA at a record, aluminium EBITDA +53% [N10]; Novelis is selling two
  Chinese units for $265 m [N10].

## Growth outlook
- India: steel demand +7.9% (2026) and +8% (2027) per worldsteel [N5]; infrastructure, housing and the duty
  support prices; Q3 FY27 expected stronger after the monsoon [N12].
- Copper: long-term demand from grids, data centres and electric vehicles; short-term price depends on US
  stockpiles and AI sentiment [N6]. Aluminium: tight LME stocks [N13].

## Risks
- Prices fall back: copper's 2026 rise leaned on tariff stockpiling [N1][N6]; if US stocks flow back, Goldman
  sees $14,500/t [N6]. Steel duty ends Dec 2028; Indian steel users lobby against it [N11].
- China: weak home demand pushes exports (steel, aluminium products) [N3][N4].
- Cycle: Nifty Metal P/B at the 88th percentile of 10 years [NI]: profits may be near a peak.
- Company: Tata Steel Europe; Vedanta's debt and demerger; Glencore coal and legal cases (not verified here).
