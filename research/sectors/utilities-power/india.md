- Buy NTPC and Power Grid slowly at AngelOne; PowerGrid InvIT only for income. Avoid Adani Green and JSW
  Energy at current prices; IEX only if you accept the market-coupling court risk.

## 3. Growth outlook
- Demand: October 2026 consumption 159.8 bn units, +21.9% on a year; peak 260.7 GW in October and a record
  270.8 GW in May 2026 [I5]; August peak 258.3 GW, +12.4%, hottest August since 1901 [I6].
- Capacity: the government says India is on course for 500 GW of non-fossil capacity by 2030 (minister,
  2026-11-13/14 headlines) [I7]. More solar and wind needs more transmission: Power Grid's core business.
- NTPC: Jul-Sep 2026 consolidated profit Rs 7,030 Cr (Yahoo [I1]; headlines say +38.7% on a year, while one
  says +14%, probably standalone; not verified [I8]); revenue Rs 54,270 Cr, +21% [I1]. NTPC Green (listed
  unit) profit +56-60% in the quarter (headlines) [I8].
- Renewables auctions: winners get a letter of award, but states must then sign power purchase agreements
  (PPAs). A CERC staff paper (Nov 2025) found about 45 GW of grid connections blocked by unsigned PPAs
  (Rajasthan 18.75 GW) [I9]. Status in 2026 not verified. This slows renewable developers (Adani Green,
  JSW, Tata Power, NTPC Green) more than Power Grid. 300 GWh of renewable power was lost in Q1 2026 to
  transmission delays [I10].
- IEX: real-time market volumes grew 41% in FY26 and are 43% of IEX volume (Q1 FY27); management expects
  25-30% a year [I4].

## 4. Key risks
| Risk | Who it hits |
|---|---|
| Market coupling: a CERC plan to pool prices across power exchanges; APTEL ruled it invalid without a framework (Feb 2026), now in the Supreme Court; IEX says at least 2 more years [I4] | IEX (it holds most of the market) |
| Unsigned PPAs and transmission delays [I9][I10] | Renewable developers |
| Government as owner: dividends, tariffs and capex set by policy | NTPC, Power Grid, NHPC, CPSE ETF |
| High debt and rich prices (net debt 6-8x EBITDA, P/E 40-123) | Adani Green, JSW Energy |
| Adani group: governance questions since the US charges against group executives in Nov 2024; current status not verified | Adani Power, Adani Green |
| Coal stocks low at many plants in Sep 2026 [I6] | Thermal generators |
| InvIT: fixed-life transmission assets; Q1 FY27 revenue Rs 311 Cr, -0.6% on a year (headline) [I11]; payouts partly return capital | PGINVIT |
