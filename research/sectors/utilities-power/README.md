## Summary
- Power use is rising again everywhere (AI data centres in the US, heat and industry in India), and
  grid owners are spending record sums. A utility is a company that makes or moves electricity.
- Prices already reflect much of this: US utilities trade near the whole market's P/E, above their
  25-year average; Indian state power firms and Japan's Kansai Electric are still cheap.
- Good time for slow, steady buying of the cheap, regulated names; not for the AI-power stars.
- Buy: XDWU (world utilities, Irish) as the core; NTPC and Power Grid in India; National Grid,
  Kansai Electric or CLP as single names. Skip more CEG/Vistra/VRT: you already hold that AI-power bet.
