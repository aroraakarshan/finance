# Sectors: what they are and which look good now

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Proposals only: the user decides and places every order.
Region rankings with numbers and sources: [India](ranking/india.md), [US](ranking/us.md),
[Europe](ranking/europe.md), [Japan](ranking/japan.md), [other Asia](ranking/asia.md). One folder per sector
(below) has the "what to buy" table.

## Summary
- Best now: financials (banks and insurers) are attractive in India, Europe, Japan and Asia: cheap, profitable,
  rising. Health care is attractive in Europe and Japan, but expensive in India.
- Technology is the cheapest US sector against its own history, but you already hold about 43% in chip and tech
  shares, so do not add; let your QCOM sales fund other sectors instead.
- Avoid for now: real estate (rising rates), industrials and defence (expensive after a big run), consumer
  discretionary outside Japan autos, and US energy and staples (expensive or peak profits).
- What to do: keep the plan's global split (research/global/README.md section 3); for extra money, add a
  financials fund and health care in stages (see the sector folders), and in India buy private-bank and FMCG
  index funds in stages.

## 1. The sectors in plain words
| Sector | Main parts | What it is | What drives it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Information technology | software, IT services, semiconductors, hardware | Companies that make chips, software and computers, or run IT for others | Business tech spending, the AI build-out, interest rates (high valuations) |
| Communication services | telecom, internet platforms, media | Phone and internet networks; search, social media, streaming | Advertising, subscriber growth, data prices |
| Consumer discretionary | autos, retail, e-commerce, luxury, hotels | Things people buy when they feel rich | Jobs, wages, interest rates, confidence |
| Consumer staples | FMCG (food, drinks, soap), alcohol, tobacco | Things people buy every week | Steady demand; input costs (palm oil, wheat); rural incomes in India |
| Health care | pharma, biotech, medical devices, hospitals, insurers | Medicines, equipment and care | Ageing, new drugs, patents running out, drug-price rules |
| Financials | banks, insurers, NBFCs (lenders), asset managers, exchanges | Lend, insure, manage and trade money | Interest rates, loan growth, bad loans |
| Energy | oil and gas, refining, coal | Produce and sell fuel | Oil and gas prices, wars, OPEC |
| Materials | chemicals, metals and mining, cement, paints | Raw materials for everything else | China demand, building activity, commodity prices |
| Industrials | capital goods, defence, aerospace, construction, transport | Machines, weapons, planes, railways, logistics | Government and company investment, defence budgets |
| Utilities | power generation, grids, gas and water | Electricity and water supply | Regulated returns, power demand (AI data centres), rates |
| Real estate | developers, REITs (rent-earning property trusts) | Build and rent property | Interest rates, housing demand, office use |
| Themes across sectors | AI infrastructure, electrification | Chips, power and grid gear, cooling for data centres; EVs and renewables | AI spending, power shortages; see [themes](../themes/opp-sectors-themes.md) |
