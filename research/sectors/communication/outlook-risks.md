# Communication services: outlook and risks

Part of [communication services](README.md). Written 2026-11-15; source tags in [sources](sources.md).

## Summary

- India telecom: three players; tariffs (prices per plan) expected to rise about 15% around Dec 2026 (Ambit forecast); Jio listed in Oct 2026; Vodafone Idea survives on AGR relief but still loses money [C9][C6][C7][C8].
- US/Europe/Japan telecom: low growth (revenue +1-8%) but steady cash and 3-6% dividends; prices fell this year on competition (T-Mobile) [C2][C14].
- Internet: revenue still growing 20-25% (Alphabet, Meta), but AI spending eats free cash and Meta is now selling shares to pay for it [C11][C12].
- Biggest risk for this user: the internet half of the sector moves with his chip and software stocks; telcos do not [C1].

## Key risks

| Risk | Who it hits | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| AI spending without payback | Alphabet, Meta, XWTS, Tencent | Alphabet's last-12-month free cash flow was $19.8 bn on $179.6 bn operating cash flow [C2]; Meta is issuing shares [C12] |
| Overlap with what he holds | XWTS, Alphabet, Meta | XWTS correlation 0.64 with QCOM and 0.59 with MSFT over 5 years; Alphabet A+C 32.2% and Meta 17.5% of XWTS (29 Sep 2026) [C1][C5] |
| India tariff hike delayed | Bharti, Jio, Vi | Forecast only (Ambit); India CPI inflation went above 6% in Oct 2026 and another rate hike is expected (headline), which may make hikes politically harder [C9][C18] |
| Vi failure or rescue | Vi, then Bharti/Jio | Vi needs bank funding; failure would leave two players (good for Bharti/Jio prices) but hurts banks and the government, a large Vi shareholder (not verified this session) [C8] |
| Jio lock-in expiry | Jio | Anchor and pre-IPO holders' lock-ins end in coming months (dates not verified); supply of shares can weigh on price |
| US competition | T-Mobile, Verizon, DT | Higher churn and promotions; heavy debt (TMUS debt/equity 2.1, VZ 1.8) [C2][C14] |
| China and US rules | Tencent | US AI-chip limits and China regulation; the HK line is the home listing, so US delisting rules do not apply [C15] |
| Currency | All foreign | Returns in INR depend on USD, EUR, JPY, HKD vs INR |
| Interest rates | Telcos | High-dividend, high-debt stocks fall when bond yields rise |
| Content and ads cycle | Netflix, Meta, media | Weak show slate hit Netflix in 2026 [C13]; ad spending falls in recessions |
