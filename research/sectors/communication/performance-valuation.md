# Communication services: performance and valuation

Part of [communication services](README.md). Written 2026-11-15; source tags in [sources](sources.md).

## Summary

- Over 1 year the sector lagged everywhere it is internet-heavy (US -1.7%, world -1.8%) while telcos did well in Europe (+13%) and Japan (+10-15%) [C1].
- Over 3 and 5 years the sector roughly matched the world market (XWTS +77% / +47% vs MSCI World +74% / +63%) [C1].
- US sector forward P/E 16.8 is below its 5-year (18.3) and 10-year (17.7) averages and below the S&P 500 (18.3) [C4].
- Telco P/Es outside India are 10-14x forward; Bharti and Jio are 38-41x trailing [C2][C3].
