## Summary

- The sector is two businesses: phone companies (telcos: slow, steady, high dividends) and internet/media (Alphabet, Meta, Netflix, Tencent: fast but tied to the AI spending cycle).
- Internet names fell after Q3 2026 on AI spending (capex) worries; US sector forward P/E 16.8 is below its 10-year average 17.7 [C4].
- A world sector fund (XWTS) is about half Alphabet + Meta and moves with QCOM/MSFT (correlation 0.6) [C5][C1], so it adds little diversification.
- Telcos move almost independently of QCOM/MSFT (correlation -0.1 to 0.2) and are cheap outside India: Deutsche Telekom, KDDI, T-Mobile US at 11-14x forward earnings [C2].
- If buying: telcos first (Bharti Airtel in INR; Deutsche Telekom or KDDI at IBKR); Tencent as a small, higher-risk value bet; XWTS only if he wants the whole sector.
