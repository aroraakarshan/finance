## Summary
- Biotech has recovered: Nasdaq Biotech index +31% in 1 year and +90% in 3 years, driven by takeovers and big pharma's need to refill pipelines, even though US 10-year rates rose to 5.15%.
- Obesity drugs: Lilly is pulling away from Novo; prices are falling (US deals, generic semaglutide in India, Canada, China, Brazil) but volumes grow faster.
- Medical devices are the opposite: most big names fell 6-57% in a year and trade below their own 5-year forward P/E. That is where value is now.
- Buy idea (in steps, small): biotech via the Irish UCITS BTEC; devices via Stryker or Olympus (non-US); Novo only as a cheap, risky turnaround. REGN stays WATCH ([REGN.md](../../stocks/REGN.md)).

## 2. Biotech: recovery, takeovers, rates
- Why it rose: big drugmakers face patent losses (2028 is one of the heaviest years since 2012-14,
  Morningstar 2026-08-02) and buy growth. 2026 started with a takeover rush, paused in summer
  (biotech fell from its August peak), and Investor's Business Daily (2026-10-13) expects a busy
  winter. October 2026 alone: 97 pharma deals worth $25.45 bn (Express Pharma, 2026-11-08).
  Citywire (2026-11-09) calls 2026 "a record year for M&A" (headline only; total not verified).
- Lilly turns its profits into deals (AtaiBeckley, Curevo, Merida; Q3 acquired R&D charge $4.57 per
  share, Lilly Q3 release via RTTNews 2026-10-29). Abbott closed its $21-23 bn Exact Sciences buy
  in Sept 2026 (ad-hoc-news 2026-09-28; ET Health 2025-11-20).
- Rate sensitivity (own calculation, weekly data 2021-11 to 2026-11): XBI moved about -6.6% and IHI
  about -5.3% for each +1 point in the US 10-year yield, vs -1.7% for the S&P 500 (correlation
  -0.22 / -0.27 / -0.10). The yield rose from 4.15% to 5.15% in the last year (^TNX), yet biotech
  rose 31%: takeovers outweighed rates. A further rate rise is still a headwind; cuts would help.

## 4. Cancer drugs: ADCs and China
- ADCs (antibody-drug conjugates) are antibodies that carry a chemotherapy drug straight to the
  cancer cell. One 2026 report counts 180+ companies and 290+ ADC drugs (GlobeNewswire 2026-10-14);
  a market forecast of $34.8 bn by 2035 is a research-firm press release (2026-11-12; low quality).
- China now supplies many of these drugs: Chinese firms signed 110+ out-licensing deals by
  2026-08-05 worth $6.2 bn upfront, vs 92 deals / $5.6 bn in all of 2025 and 59 in 2023 (Norstella,
  via Mint/WSJ 2026-09-22). Pfizer, AbbVie and BMS each paid at least $600 m upfront for one Chinese
  asset in 2026. Hengrui's Q3 profit rose 88.9% on licensing income (Reuters 2026-10-27).
- Risk: US outbound investment rules (COINS Act 2025) are being written; licensing deals stay exempt
  so far (MSN headline 2026-10-23); a bill (BINSA) would add Defense Department review.

## 6. Key risks
- Biotech: trial failures; takeover pause (summer 2026 showed it); higher US rates; US drug-price
  rules and China-deal restrictions.
- Obesity: price war from generics and pills; Novo's cash cow erodes; Lilly is priced for success
  (fwd P/E 40.8 on 2026, 28.5 on 2027) and is 11% of global health care funds (themes note).
- Devices: slowing growth at former winners (BSX, ISRG); tariffs; hospital budgets; cyberattacks.
- For this user: US-listed names add US estate tax exposure ([us-estate-tax](../../themes/us-estate-tax.md)).
