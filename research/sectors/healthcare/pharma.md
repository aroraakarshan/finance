# Pharma: India generics and CDMOs, big pharma in the US, Europe and Japan

Written 2026-11-15 by `hc-pharma` (sector research). Market research, not advice; no orders. Prices are
13-Nov-2026 closes unless stated. "not verified" = no source confirmed it. Wider sector view (health care
as a diversifier vs his QCOM/MSFT): [opp-sectors-themes](../../themes/opp-sectors-themes.md), not repeated here.

## Summary
- India pharma: most expensive in 5 years (P/E 45.8 vs 10y median 35.1) after +26%; US generic tariff from Aug 2028.
- Big pharma in Europe/Japan is cheaper (forward P/E 9-21) on patent expiries (Keytruda 2028) and US price deals.
- Not a time to chase India; a fair time to add global pharma slowly, as it diversifies his tech holdings.
- Buy in steps: XDWH (Irish UCITS) core; AstraZeneca (LSE) and Daiichi Sankyo (TSE) as single names.
- India: PHARMABEES only after a 15-20% fall; Lupin is cheap but a watch (falling profit, US tariff).

Terms: P/E = price / yearly profit per share (trailing = last 12 months, forward = analysts' next 12
months). CDMO/CRDMO = contract firm that develops and makes drugs for other drug makers. LOE = loss of
exclusivity (patent ends, copies arrive). Generic = copy of an old drug; price erosion = yearly price fall.

## 4. Growth outlook
- **US generics (about 35% of Indian pharma revenue, ~$11 bn in FY26; India fills ~47% of US generic
  prescriptions)** [S5]. Price erosion continues: a few US distributors and PBMs (insurer middlemen) force
  price matching; no annual contracts; some US prices below Europe/Africa (New Indian Express, 7-Sep-2026
  [S6]). Ind-Ra expects erosion to *moderate* as tariff uncertainty keeps new entrants out [S5].
- **US tariffs.** Branded/patented drugs: 100% Section 232 tariff from 29-Sep-2026, generics exempt for now;
  India among 20 countries with 0% on some specialty drugs (orphan, ADCs, cell/gene) [S8]. Generics: 0% until
  Aug 2028, then 100%, then 200% from Aug 2029 [S5][S7]. Ind-Ra: credit neutral, small firms hurt most [S5].
- **MFN deals.** 26 drug makers (incl. Pfizer, AZ, Lilly, Novo, Merck, Novartis, Sanofi, Astellas, Sun Pharma)
  agreed "most favoured nation" prices (US price no higher than other rich countries) in return for tariff
  relief; covers ~89% of the branded market (White House via RTTNews, 1-Sep-2026 [S13]). Sun was the first
  Indian firm; others may follow [S6].
- **IRA Medicare negotiation.** 2nd-cycle prices (15 drugs) start 1-Jan-2027; 3rd cycle (first Part B drugs:
  Cosentyx, Kisqali, Entyvio, Erleada, Verzenio, Trulicity and 9 more, $27 bn Medicare spend) takes effect
  1-Jan-2028 (CMS, 27-Jan-2026 [S14]). Leerink: only Gilead's Biktarvy is material (~8% of sales) [S15].
- **Patent cliffs 2026-2030.** Keytruda (Merck, $8.6 bn in Q3 2026) loses US exclusivity in 2028; Merck
  aims to move 30-40% of US patients to the injectable Keytruda Qlex (later patents) by end-2027 [S16].
  Keytruda, Opdivo, Darzalex, Ocrevus, Cosentyx lose exclusivity 2027-2029; ~$236 bn of drug sales go
  off-patent in 5 years (Pharmabiz, 30-Sep-2025 [S17]). Eliquis US generics: 2028 (not verified). This is
  the opportunity for Indian complex generics and biosimilars (CareEdge: ~$5 bn, FE 19-Jun-2026 headline [S10]).
- **Japan.** Daiichi Sankyo H1 FY3/27 revenue +19.7%, core operating profit +46.9%, Enhertu/Datroway oncology
  +54.8%; full-year guide JPY 2.34 trn revenue, profit JPY 251 bn (Investing.com, 30-Oct-2026 [S12]).
  Astellas: see [4503](../../stocks/4503.md).
- **India domestic**: industry eyes ~10% growth in FY27 as India offsets the US slowdown (FE, 28-Sep-2026
  headline [S10]).

## 5. BIOSECURE and the China+1 CDMO shift
- **Law:** signed 18-Dec-2025 in the FY2026 NDAA. US federal agencies may not buy from, contract with, or
  fund via grants "biotechnology companies of concern"; OMB list due **18-Dec-2026**; 5-year safe harbour for
  existing contracts (Latham & Watkins, via [2359 note](../../stocks/2359.md)).
- **New (10-Nov-2026):** the Pentagon removed WuXi AppTec from the 1260H Chinese-military list after a court
  loss, so WuXi is no longer an automatic "company of concern". Macquarie: re-listing is rare and an OMB
  designation in 2026 looks unlikely (Fierce Pharma [S18]). The rule only bites on US-government-funded work.
- **Reality check:** WuXi AppTec + WuXi Biologics revenue >$9 bn vs $3-3.5 bn for India's leading CDMOs
  combined; Indian firms get many RFQs (price requests) but few signed orders, and lack biologics scale
  (Moneycontrol, 9-Sep-2026 [S19]). Korea's Samsung Biologics, not India, is the clearest winner so far
  (headlines [S10]).
- **What it means:** Indian CDMOs (Divi's, Laurus) are growing fast on their own customer wins (peptides,
  GLP-1 ingredients, not verified per customer) but trade at 74-78x forward profit, so a weak BIOSECURE
  outcome is a valuation risk. Syngene shows the downside when one client slows.
