## Summary
- Bank shares fell behind in 2026 (HDFC Bank -30% year to date by 9 Sep) on FPI selling, a record-low rupee, RBI's 7 Oct hike to 5.50% and the ECL rules from 1 Apr 2027; but bank profits kept rising.
- Result: Nifty Bank P/B 1.84 is in the cheapest 6% of the last 10 years (avg 2.61), P/E 13.5 vs median 22.2. PSU banks are the opposite: P/B 1.28 vs 10-yr avg 1.01 (pricey vs their own past).
- Buy: BANKBEES (Nifty Bank ETF) in 3-4 steps through Apr 2027; HDFC Bank as a single-stock option.
- Wait: NBFCs, life insurers, AMCs, BSE, CDSL (P/E 25-68). Hold ICICI Lombard (user holds ICICIGI).
