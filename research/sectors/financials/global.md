## Summary
- Banks abroad had a huge 3 years: Europe banks about +200%, Japan banks +201%, US financials +82%. Rising rates in 2026 (Fed 3.75-4.00%, ECB 2.50%, BOJ 1.25%) help lending margins but hurt bond values.
- Prices are no longer cheap: US financials P/E 15.1 vs 25-yr avg 12.9; Japan's MUFG went from 0.59x book (2023) to 1.72x; Europe banks are about fair (Morningstar 0.92 of fair value, JPM 1.5x tangible book).
- Best route: XDWF (world financials, Irish, accumulating). Single names: Munich Re (cheapest quality insurer). Europe bank fund and MUFG only on a pullback. US-listed names (JPM, BAC, ICE, CME) are US-situs.
