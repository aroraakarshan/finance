# Consumer staples (sector research)

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Market research, not about the current portfolio; not
advice; no orders. Prices are 13-Nov-2026 closes. "Staples" = things people buy in good and bad
times: food, drinks, soap, toothpaste, household goods. Wider sector and theme view (not repeated
here): [../../themes/opp-sectors-themes.md](../../themes/opp-sectors-themes.md).

## Summary
- India FMCG is at its cheapest in 10 years (Nifty FMCG P/E 32.9 vs 41.9 median) after a -15% year,
  and volumes are back after the Sep-2025 GST cut; costs and weak rural demand are the catch.
- Global staples already rallied (+18% in USD, +25% in EUR in a year); US staples cost 23.7x
  next-year earnings vs a 20.1 average. GLP-1 weight-loss drugs cut grocery buying about 5%.
- What to buy: Indian FMCG ETF or HUL in parts now; abroad, Unilever (cheaper, more personal care)
  and Kao; XDWS only in small steps. Staples also offset his heavy US tech weight.
