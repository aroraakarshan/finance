# Consumer staples: India FMCG

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Market research, not advice; no orders. Prices are
13-Nov-2026 closes unless stated. Source keys ([PE], [SC], [YF], ...) are listed in section 7.
Back to the index: [README.md](README.md).

## Summary
- FMCG ("fast-moving consumer goods": soap, biscuits, tea, noodles) is the cheapest it has been in
  10 years: Nifty FMCG P/E 32.9 vs a 10-year median of 41.9 [PE], after a -15% year.
- Sales are recovering since the Sep-2025 GST cut (HUL volumes +7%, Britannia +13% in Jul-Sep 2026),
  but costs are rising (crude, food inflation) and rural volumes fell 5% in Apr-Jun 2026 [NIQ].
- Buy slowly: the Nifty FMCG ETF (FMCGIETF) or HUL in 3-4 parts over 6-12 months; ITC is cheap
  for a reason (cigarette tax).

## 4. Growth outlook

| Driver | What is happening | Source |
|---|---|---|
| GST cut (22-Sep-2025, "GST 2.0") | Slabs merged into 5% and 18% (plus 40% for "sin goods"); toothpaste, soap, shampoo, biscuits, snacks and ghee went to 5% [N18]. Volumes dipped during the switch (Oct-Dec 2025) [LM], then recovered; BNP expects the gain to last as big brands take share from small local ones | [N18], [LM], [FE] |
| Rural vs urban | NielsenIQ Apr-Jun 2026: rural volumes -5%, 68% of categories declining, home and personal care volume -4.3%. Urban held up through modern trade (+17.5% volume) and e-commerce (+34.8%) | [NIQ] 27-Sep-2026 |
| Company results (Jul-Sep 2026) | The big listed firms grew faster than the market: HUL volume +7%, Britannia +13%, Nestle India double-digit with "strong momentum in rural" | [BW], [CN], [HBL] |
| Quick commerce (10-minute delivery apps) | E-commerce is 21% of FMCG sales in the top 8 metros [NIQ]; HUL's quick-commerce sales grow 40-50% a year and it designs packs only for these apps [MN]. Pressure: new online-first brands (Honasa +66% in 2026 [ZB]) and app trade terms (margin effect not verified) | [NIQ], [MN] |
| Costs | Crude-linked inputs (palm oil, LAB for detergents) up; HUL raised detergent prices 2-8%; HUL, Dabur, Britannia, VBL margins down; HUL expects inflation to stay "elevated" | [FE], [BW] |
| Forecasts | Industry hoped for "high single-digit volume growth in 2026" [BS2, Dec-2025]; HUL expects FY27 better than FY26 (FY26 volume +4%) [BW]; analyst consensus EPS for HUL is Rs 53.3 (next 12m), forward P/E 37 [YF] | |

Reading: NielsenIQ (whole market, Apr-Jun) and the company results (big brands, Jul-Sep) disagree.
Both can be true: the market is weak but leaders gain share. This is a discrepancy to watch in the
Jul-Sep NielsenIQ report (not yet found).

## 5. Key risks
- Input costs and inflation: margins already fell 1-1.5 points at HUL and VBL [BW, CN2].
- Rural demand: NIQ shows a broad slowdown; a weak monsoon or food inflation would deepen it.
- ITC: cigarette excise from 1-Feb-2026 (Rs 2,050-8,500 per 1,000 sticks) cut profit 20%; ITC
  raised prices twice in 2026 (MSN headline, 11-Nov-2026) [NIE, FE2]. Tax can rise again.
- Quick commerce and online brands take share and press margins.
- GLP-1 weight-loss drugs: generic semaglutide launched in India after the 2026 patent expiry; the
  whole GLP-1 market is only Rs 2,492 cr a year (Sep-2026) [FE3], so the food-demand effect in India
  is small for now (see [global.md](global.md) for the US evidence).
- Valuations are low vs history but still above the Nifty 50; "cheap" can stay cheap if growth
  stays at mid-single digits.
