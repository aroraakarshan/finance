# Consumer discretionary: retail, luxury, travel

Part of [consumer discretionary](README.md). Tags: [sources](sources.md).

## Summary

- Indian consumer names grow fast but are priced for it: Trent P/E 89, DMart 72, Titan 61, Indian Hotels 57 [D1].
- Quick commerce (10-minute delivery): Blinkit is now EBITDA-positive; Swiggy's Instamart still loses about Rs 900 cr a quarter [D21][D22].
- Luxury: LVMH and Hermes shares fell 35-37% in a year on weak China [D1][D23][D24]; Titan (Indian jewellery) rose 27% [D1].
- Travel is the strongest corner: Marriott +48% in a year and raised guidance; Indian Hotels +25% [D1][D27].

## 5. Key risks

- Valuation: Indian consumer leaders at P/E 57-89 leave little room for a slowdown [D1].
- Quick commerce: heavy discounting and dark-store spending; DMart and Trent face its competition.
- Luxury: China demand, price fatigue after years of price rises [D28]; no sign of a turn yet [D23][D24].
- Gold price: Titan's jewellery revenue rises and falls with gold (not quantified here).
- Travel: cyclical; a recession or oil shock hits hotels fast.
- US names (Amazon, Marriott): US estate tax on holdings above USD 60,000 (tax/investment-types.md).
