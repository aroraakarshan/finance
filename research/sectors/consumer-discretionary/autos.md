# Consumer discretionary: autos and EVs

Part of [consumer discretionary](README.md). Tags: [sources](sources.md).

## Summary

- The GST cut of 22 Sep 2025 turned Indian auto demand up: cars +25.5% in Apr-Oct 2026, scooters +31.6% in October [D10].
- Volumes are not profits: Maruti's Q2 profit fell 26% on commodity costs [D12]; Tata PV is loss-making on JLR charges [D13].
- Two-wheelers (TVS, Bajaj, Eicher) did best over 1 year; carmakers (Maruti, Tata PV) worst [D1].
- Abroad: Toyota is cheap (P/E 9.3) and raising forecasts [D15]; Tesla is priced for robotaxis, not cars (P/E ~480) [D1]; BYD is cheap but sales are flat [D17].

## 1. The GST cut (effective 22 Sep 2025) [D11]

| Vehicle | Before | After | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small cars (under 4 m; petrol up to 1200cc, diesel up to 1500cc) | 28% + 1-3% cess | 18%, no cess | Price cut up to Rs 60,000-1 lakh on a Rs 6-10 lakh car if passed on |
| Mid and large SUVs | 28% + 17-22% cess (45-50% total) | 40%, no cess | Tax 5-10 points lower: helps M&M, Maruti, Tata, Hyundai SUVs |
| Motorcycles up to 350cc | 28% | 18% | Helps Hero, Bajaj, TVS, Honda commuter bikes |
| Motorcycles over 350cc | 28% + 3% cess | 40% | Hurts Royal Enfield (Eicher) big bikes and KTM (Bajaj) |
| Electric vehicles | 5% | 5% (unchanged; text truncated, not verified) | EV price edge vs petrol cars narrowed |

Result one year later (SIAM, Oct 2026 vs Oct 2025) [D10]: passenger vehicles 5,19,060 (+12.4%, first
October above 5 lakh); two-wheelers 25,77,810 (+16.1%); three-wheelers +25.7%; car exports +21.8%,
two-wheeler exports +26.5%. Apr-Oct 2026 cars 30,82,634 (+25.5%).

## 2. Tata Motors after the demerger

Tata Motors split into two listed companies. The old listing became Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles
(TMPV: Tata cars, EVs and Jaguar Land Rover); the commercial-vehicle arm TMCV listed on 12 Nov 2025
[D14 headline]. TMPV's long-run price history on Yahoo is not comparable (the demerger cut its price).
- TMPV Q2 FY27 (Jul-Sep 2026): net loss Rs 5,531 cr on exceptional and impairment charges; revenue +34%;
  EBITDA margin 6.2% [D13]. The year-ago "profit" of Rs 76,170 cr was mostly a one-off demerger gain [D13],
  so TMPV's trailing P/E means nothing (shown as NM). Consensus EPS for FY27 Rs 21.3 (8 analysts) gives a
  forward P/E of 13.5 [D1] (not verified against the exchange filing).
- TMCV (trucks, buses): +36.7% since listing, P/E 25.3 [D1].

## 6. Key risks

- Commodity and oil costs from the West Asia war: already cut Maruti's Q2 margin [D12] and Toyota's quarter [D15].
- Festival and GST boost may fade in 2027 (high base); October data are dispatches to dealers, not retail
  sales (one report says two-wheeler retail fell in Oct 2026, headline only, not verified).
- Tata PV: JLR restructuring charges, losses [D13].
- US tariffs (Toyota), China price war (BYD, and Chinese exports into Europe hurting EXV5 names).
- Tesla: price depends on autonomy promises; LTM profit tiny vs USD 1.5 trn market cap [D1].
- Sector is cyclical: Nifty Auto P/E swung from 31 to above 80 and back in 2016-2022 [D2].
