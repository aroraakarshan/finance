## Summary

- The 2026 Iran war (Strait of Hormuz attacks) lifted Brent crude from about $61 at the start of 2026 to a peak of $118 (31 Mar); it is $95.84 now [Y].
- Oil company profits jumped (S&P 500 energy Q3 earnings +151% [FS1]), so P/Es look low, but these are peak, war-driven earnings.
- Forecasters see Brent falling to $70-86 in 2027 (EIA, Morningstar DBRS, SBI) [EIA][OGJ][SBI]; a peace deal could make that fast.
- Not a time to buy a lot: world energy is up 48% in 1 year [Y]. If you want some, buy small and in steps: cheap European/Japanese majors, Irish UCITS XDW0, and net-cash Coal India.
- Indian fuel retailers (BPCL, HPCL, IOC) are the opposite bet: they gain if crude falls, because pump prices have been frozen since May [ETE].
