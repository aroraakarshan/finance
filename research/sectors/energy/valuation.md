# Energy: valuation vs history

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Back to [README](README.md). Source tags: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary

- On today's war-boosted profits energy looks cheap: S&P 500 energy forward P/E 12.2 vs 25-yr average 13.9; European majors P/E about 8-9 [FS1][FS2][Y].
- On 2025 profits (Brent $68) the same prices look full: Exxon 24x, Chevron 35x, Shell and Total 15-16x [Y].
- India is not cheap on its own history: Nifty Energy P/E 13.45 vs 10-yr average 13.7, and higher than usual against the Nifty 50 [NI].
- Cheapest groups: Indian oil and gas (index P/E 9.9 vs 12.1), Indian upstream (ONGC, EV/EBITDA 3.2) and Coal India (3.3) [NI][SC].

Terms: EV/EBITDA (enterprise value / earnings before interest, tax, depreciation) is the usual
energy measure because it ignores debt levels and big depreciation charges. LTM = last 4 quarters.

How built (comps method, `equity-analysis` skill):
- Global: market cap = price x Yahoo implied shares, converted to the USD reporting currency at
  2026-11-13 FX (GBP/USD 1.3212, EUR/USD 1.1050, NOK/USD 0.1044; GBp quotes / 100); EV = market cap
  + total debt - cash and short-term investments + minority interest (latest quarter); LTM from 4
  consecutive quarters (Dec-25 to Sep-26) [Y]. Next-FY P/E uses Yahoo `earnings_estimate` +1y, not
  `forwardEps` (which gave Shell 4.48 and Inpex 268, far from the +1y estimates 6.00 and 436) [Y].
- India: P/E and market cap from screener.in consolidated pages; LTM EBITDA = operating profit +
  other income, last 4 quarters (Dec-25 to Sep-26) from screener; net debt from Yahoo (Sep-26).
  Minority interest not added now (understates EV for Reliance and ONGC), while the Yahoo 4-yr
  history includes it, so Indian 'now vs history' is biased low by a little [SC][Y]. Petronet and Oil
  India 4-yr history and Petronet LTM are Yahoo only. Indian consensus EPS may be standalone, not
  consolidated (market-data skill warning) - not verified.
- 10-yr US history: year-end price x shares (net income / diluted EPS) + debt - cash, over pre-tax
  income + D&A + interest, all from SEC XBRL [ED].
- TotalEnergies note ([TTE.md](../../stocks/TTE.md)) has P/E 9.2, EV/EBITDA 4.5 from stockanalysis;
  the small gap is a different data source and date.
