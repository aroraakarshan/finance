## 3. Mood

- Global: energy was the best S&P 500 sector for Q3 earnings growth (+151.2%) and revenue growth
  (+35.7%); refiners' earnings +542%, integrated majors +141%, producers +83%, oil services -3% [FS1].
  Exxon and Chevron are up about 38-39% in 2026 (Yahoo Finance article title, 2026-11-07) [NEWS].
- Shell and TotalEnergies posted their highest quarterly profit since 2022 (headline 2026-10-29) [NEWS];
  Aramco Q3 adjusted net income $31.2 bn, +9% (Upstream, Xinhua headlines 2026-11-02/03) [NEWS].
- India: the market ignored most of the oil boom. The three state fuel retailers lost a combined
  Rs 18,150 Cr in April-June, then earned Rs 30,106 Cr (standalone) in July-September; petrol and
  diesel prices have been frozen since May and they lost Rs 4-5/litre on petrol and Rs 18-20 on
  diesel until October; unpaid LPG under-recoveries reached Rs 67,900 Cr [ETE].
- Reliance rose most in 9 months after record Q2 profit (ET EnergyWorld headline 2026-11-01) [NEWS];
  Coal India fell 3-4% after Q2 on weak EBITDA and cost pressure despite profit +53% (Moneycontrol
  headlines 2026-10-29/30) [NEWS].
