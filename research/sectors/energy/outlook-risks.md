# Energy: growth outlook and risks

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Back to [README](README.md). Source tags: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary

- Every named forecaster expects lower oil in 2027: EIA $77 by Q4 2027, Morningstar DBRS $71, SBI about $70; Goldman is highest at $88, with $130-140 only if the war gets worse [EIA][OGJ][SBI][GS].
- Analysts already expect 2027 profits 8-16% lower at most majors (BP -33%) [Y]; a fast peace deal could cut more.
- The last war spike shows the pattern: world energy (XDW0) +55% in 2022, then about flat for three years (-0.7%, +9.5%, +0.5%) [JE].
- Parts that hold up better when oil falls: refining (EIA sees fuel supply tight through 2027), Indian fuel retailers, LNG import terminals, and coal.
