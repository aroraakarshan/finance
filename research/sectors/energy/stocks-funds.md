## Summary

- Cheapest in India: IOC, ONGC and Coal India (P/E 4-7.5, EV/EBITDA about 3.2-3.5); Coal India also has net cash [SC].
- Cheapest abroad: Shell, TotalEnergies, Equinor and Inpex (P/E about 8-9); Exxon and Chevron cost about twice as much [Y].
- Easiest single buy: Irish UCITS XDW0 at IBKR. In India, the liquid ETF is CPSE ETF (a basket of state-owned companies, not only energy); the pure energy ETFs are small (Rs 270 Cr each) [GW][NSE].
- Dropped earlier and still dropped: APA and CHENNPETRO (peak war profits); MCL IPO default skip.
