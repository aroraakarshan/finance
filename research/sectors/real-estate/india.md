## Summary
- Homes sold well for four years: the big listed builders booked a record Rs 1.95 lakh crore of sales in FY26 (+17%) [R9].
- The cycle is now cooling: Jul-Sep 2026 sales were flat to down, unsold homes rose 12% to 6.31 lakh, and the RBI raised rates in Oct 2026 for the first time in about 4 years [R4][R5][R8].
- Realty shares are not cheap: the Nifty Realty index trades at 4.0x book value vs a 10-year median of 2.85x [R2].
- What to buy: at most a small, staged position in Lodha (second-lowest P/E of the big five, highest ROE); wait for a lower price before a realty ETF.

- "Pre-sales" = value of homes booked in the period. Indian builders book profit only when a building
  is handed over, often 3-4 years later, so bookings, not reported profit, show the cycle.
- Reading: listed builders keep taking share from small ones (their bookings still grow while city-wide
  sales are flat), but supply now runs ahead of demand, mostly in luxury homes (unsold Rs 20-50 crore
  homes +52% in H1 2026, BusinessWorld headline [R7]).
- Rates turned: the RBI raised the repo rate (its lending rate to banks) by 0.25 point to 5.50% on
  2026-10-07, its first hike in about 4 years; October CPI inflation was 6.09%, and SBI Research and ICICI
  Bank expect another 0.25 point in December [R8]. Higher home-loan rates hurt affordability. The 10-year
  government bond yields 7.32% (2026-11-13) [R29].

## 6. Growth outlook and risks
- **Drivers:** listed builders gaining share; FY27 pre-sales +22% expected [R10]; Godrej plans Rs 32,000
  crore of launches in H2 FY27 (headline [R12]); office demand from global capability centres (REIT note).
- **Risks:** (1) rate hikes and 6% inflation hurt affordability [R8]; (2) unsold stock +12% and launches
  above sales for 16 quarters [R4][R6]; (3) a luxury glut [R7]; (4) building-cost inflation from the West
  Asia conflict (headlines [R15]); (5) profits are lumpy and can be lifted by one-offs (DLF Q2) [R11];
  (6) realty shares often fall 40-60% in downturns (Godrej is 45% off its high now) [R1].
