## Summary
- India: builders sold a record Rs 1.95 lakh crore of homes in FY26, but sales are now flat, unsold stock is up 12% and the RBI started raising rates; realty shares cost 4.0x book vs a 10-year median of 2.85x.
- World: REITs (rent-paying property trusts) are fairly priced (US forward P/E 18.0 vs 10-year 18.6), but a 5.15% US bond pays more than their 3.5% yield.
- Best value: Japan (Tokyo office vacancy 1.75%, rents rising) and Singapore REITs after a 2-year lag.
- Not a time for a big bet; small, staged buys. Core: HPRA (cheapest global REIT UCITS); add Mitsui Fudosan, CICT; in India only Lodha, small.

## How to use this
- Real estate in total (with any Indian REITs from [opp-real-assets](../../themes/opp-real-assets.md))
  is a small slice; buy in 3-4 steps over 6-12 months, because rates are still rising in India and the US.

  names over US REITs (no US estate tax, lower link to your tech shares).
- Watch: the RBI decision in December 2026, US 10-year yield, Tokyo vacancy, Indian unsold inventory.
