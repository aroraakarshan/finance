## 2. Break PGB FDs early or let them mature

Each FD's rate, booking date, maturity and PGB's premature-withdrawal penalty are unknown (pending from the user). Rule per FD:

- Gain from moving = principal x (after-tax yield of the new use - after-tax FD rate) x years left.
- Cost of breaking = principal x (contracted rate - break rate) x years held x 0.6412. The break rate is the bank's rate on the booking date for the period actually held, minus the penalty (HDFC: 1%; PGB: nv).
- Break only if gain > cost. Otherwise move the FD on its maturity date.

| Situation | Likely answer |
|---|---|
| Less than about half the term left (most FDs booked 6-12 months ago) | Wait: the penalty plus the lower short-tenor rate cost more than a few months of extra yield. HDFC's case: about -Rs 2,500 to +Rs 1,700 on 9 FDs ([hdfc-fds.md](hdfc-fds.md)) |
| Most of a long term left and the new use earns much more after tax | Can pay: work out the rule with the FD's figures |
| Matured money already covers the next months of buys in plan.md | Wait: the plan buys monthly over 12-24 months, so there is no rush |
