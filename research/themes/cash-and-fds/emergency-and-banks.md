Rule (decided 16 Nov): in an emergency the HDFC credit card pays first; sell the emergency arbitrage the same day and repay
the card in full when the money arrives (1-2 working days). Never withdraw cash on the card; always pay the full bill.
Why: arbitrage gains are taxed about 15% (14.95%) after 1 year, vs the 35.88% slab on a liquid fund or FD; the card covers the wait.
The Rs 3,02,496 lot turns long-term on 23 Sep 2027 and the Rs 1 L lot on 16 Nov 2027; sold earlier, gains are taxed 23.92%.
