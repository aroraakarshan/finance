# India market: valuations, returns, flows, expected returns

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Market research, not advice; no orders. Proposals are conditional.

## Summary
- Large caps are below their 5-15 yr P/E medians (Nifty 50 20.5, Next 50 17.7, 13-Nov-2026 [PE]); mid caps at median; small caps above (83rd pctl).
- 2026: Nifty -3.8% YTD, record FPI selling offset by DIIs, RBI hike to 5.5%; November bounce +11% in 12 sessions.
- Expected return by rough building blocks: 13-15%/yr INR, about 8-11%/yr USD; no strong edge vs US large caps (JPM 7.5%).
- What to do: add to India in steps, large caps first; see [instruments.md](instruments.md).

## 0. Why this matters for you (context, from repo files)

- Rs 2.23 Cr total; QCOM + MSFT 81.1%; US about 88%; Indian MF book about Rs 20.5 L = 9.2% of total
  (`mutual-fund-overlap.md`, updated 2026-11-15). The MF cut targets UTI Nifty 50 35% / PPFAS 30% /
  HDFC Mid Cap 25% / Quant Small Cap 10% (same file, section 4).
- So new India money diversifies AWAY from US tech. The question is mostly "how much more India and in
  what shape", less "which hot sector".
- INR fell vs USD: 89.77 (31-Dec-2025) -> record 96.88 (24-Jul-2026) -> 94.24 (14-Nov-2026) [YF].
  INR loss per year vs USD: 6.1% (1y), 4.2% (3y), 4.9% (5y), 3.4% (10y) [YF]. Your USD holdings gained
  from this; Indian assets carry this currency risk if you measure in USD.

  major markets in 2026", 8 straight weekly falls (BBC, 2026-10-05). Then the "strongest weekly rally in

  Calendar 2026 FPI outflow Rs 3.12 lakh Cr to mid-Oct (Times of India, 2026-10-18). November turned:
  +Rs 53,739 Cr by 10-Nov, highest since Sep 2024, linked to US AI-spending worries (Business Today, 2026-11-10).
  FII ownership at a 17-year low (Business Standard, 2026-08-17, headline).
- Macro: RBI HIKED repo 25 bp to 5.5% on 7-Oct-2026, GDP forecast 7.1% (Business Standard / IIFL, 2026-10-07).
  Brent about $95.5 on 13-Nov after >$110 earlier (HDFC Sky 2026-11-13; IIFL 2026-11-02). Oil is India's
  main swing risk (Bloomberg headline, 2026-10-22).
- Street targets: Citi Nifty 28,000 (+12%) (ANI, 2026-11-10); BofA 26,200 year-end, prefers large caps

  to overweight (CNBC-TV18 post, 2026-11-09). All headlines only; bodies blocked.
