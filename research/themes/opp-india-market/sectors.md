# India market: sectors and factors vs their own history

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Market research, not advice; no orders. Proposals are conditional.

## Summary
- Cheap vs history: banks/financials, IT, FMCG (P/E pctl 4-16 of 5y, 13-Nov-2026 [PE]).
- Expensive: defence (P/E 56), pharma/healthcare (100th pctl), capital goods (~39x, short history).
- Defence, CPSE, PSE, capital goods, power fell 30-38% in 2024-25 and are rising again.
- What to do: avoid the hot themes; quality and low-vol factors are cheap vs their past.
