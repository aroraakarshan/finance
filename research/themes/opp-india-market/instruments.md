## 6. Ranked ideas for NEXT India money (conditional)

Only if you first decide that more India (vs cutting QCOM/MSFT into anything) is the plan. All "if" items.
1. **Fill the UTI Nifty 50 core first** (already in the target). Cheapest broad segment besides Next 50:
   P/E 20.5 vs 15y median 22.3, FPIs coming back, low cost. Spread buys over 3-6 months: 1-month +11%
   bounce and oil/RBI risk remain.
2. **Keep (do not sell) Kotak Nifty Next 50 (A)** if you want one more value tilt: P/E 17.7, the lowest in
   10y (2nd pctl). This reverses one exit in `mutual-fund-overlap.md`; the trade-off is one more fund.
3. **A low-vol / quality factor fund, 10-15% of new India money (B1 or B2)**: both at 24-28th pctl of their
   own P/E; 5y 8.4-8.5%/yr vs Nifty 6.8% [PX]. But in 2024-25 they fell MORE than Nifty (Low Vol 30
   -18.4%, Alpha LV -24.9% vs -15.8%), so "low vol" did not protect then. Cost: 0.5-0.6%/yr TER.
4. **Banks via index (C), small, only if you want a value bet**: P/B 1.84 vs 2.77 10y median. But Nifty 50
   is already 37% financials [FS], so this doubles a bet you have.
5. **Multi-asset (D)** only if you want one fund that rebalances itself; costlier, active.
