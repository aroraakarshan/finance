# IBKR global products for an Indian resident: what is open, what LRS allows

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Research only, not advice; no orders placed. Details,
sources [S#] and dates are in the parts below; Indian tax from `tax/investment-types.md`.

## Summary

- LRS allows listed foreign stocks, ETFs and regulated funds; bars options, futures, event
  contracts and margin; OTC bonds, CDs and crypto are grey ([rules](ibkr-global/rules.md)).
- Long-term money: Irish accumulating UCITS ETFs (CSPX/VUAA/VWRA) ([equities](ibkr-global/equities.md)).
- Idle USD earns 0% below USD 10k; bond ETFs and money funds risk always-short-term tax
  ([cash and bonds](ibkr-global/cash-and-bonds.md)). To do: confirm entity and cash account type.
