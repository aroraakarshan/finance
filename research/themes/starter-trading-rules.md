# Starter trading rules (proposals)

Written 2026-11-15. These are PROPOSALS for the user to decide. Nothing here allows a trade
until the user fills the AGENTS.md trading rules table. Agents never place orders.

## Summary
Rules for part-time trading: a fixed Rs 2 L budget (about 4.5% of Rs 44.6 L market investments; employer stock, bank money, EPF and physical gold left out), max loss Rs 2,000 a trade, stop set at entry, limit orders only.
Why: INDmoney Aug 2024 - Nov 2026 realised -$542; 19 of 30 stocks lost; 16 of 19 losers sold more than 20% down; 97 of 98 market orders.
AGENTS.md (2026-11-15) marks the rules APPROVED for paper trading; real money only after 8 weeks / 20 paper trades and a new approval.

## Rules card (paste into AGENTS.md if agreed)

| Rule | Proposed value |
|---|---|
| Trading budget (separate from long-term funds) | Fixed cap Rs 2,00,000 (about $2,120), about 4.5% of market investments excluding employer stock, HDFC and Punjab Gramin Bank savings/FDs, EPF and physical gold (Rs 44.6 L, 10.6% of the total). Raise to at most Rs 4 L only after 6 months of rule-following and a positive result after costs |
| Maximum loss per trade | 1% of trading budget = Rs 2,000 (about $21). First 3 months real money: 0.5% = Rs 1,000 |
| Stop-loss on every trade | Set at entry, below the recent swing low or 2 x ATR(14), whichever is tighter; never wider than 10% from entry. Placed as GTT (AngelOne) or stop order (Alpaca/IBKR) the same day |
| Position size | shares = max loss per trade / (entry - stop); one position max 20% of budget (Rs 40,000) |
| Max open positions | 5 (total open risk at most 5% of budget) |
| Order type | Limit orders only (entry and planned exits; stop orders only as the protective stop) |
| Excluded | Stocks under $5 or Rs 50; OTC/pink sheets; F&O and any derivatives; margin; leveraged/inverse ETFs |
| No averaging down | Never add to a position that is below entry |
| Earnings | No new entry within 5 trading days before results; reduce or exit before results if size is above half the normal risk |
| Max new trades | 3 new entries per week, decided outside work hours |
| Loss limits | 3 stopped-out trades in a row -> 1 week pause. Month loss of 4% of budget (Rs 8,000) -> no new trades until next month. Budget down 10% (Rs 20,000) -> stop and review |
| Journal | Every trade logged before entry: reason, entry, stop, target, size, risk; exit and lesson after |
| Paper trading before real money | 8 weeks AND at least 20 paper trades, pass criteria in [rules.md](starter-trading-rules/rules.md) item 13 |
| Review | Weekly 15 min (journal), monthly (rules check, P&L vs budget), quarterly (keep, change or stop) |
