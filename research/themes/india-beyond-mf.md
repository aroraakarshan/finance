# India investments beyond mutual funds: what is open, how to get in, what it pays

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Research only, not advice; no orders placed. Details, sources
(URL + publish date, all read 2026-11-15) and tax notes are in the parts linked below.

## Summary

- Best safe after-tax options: EPF/VPF 8.25% (exempt within Rs 2.5 L/yr contribution) and PPF 7.1% tax-free (Rs 1.5 L/yr; not chosen: you decided 16 Nov 2026 not to use PPF (no 15-year lock; EPF is your locked safe money)).
- Ask the employer about NPS: its contribution up to 14% of salary is deductible in the new regime.
- Taxable interest (FRSB, G-secs, FDs) loses 35.88% to tax; secondary-market SGBs lost their tax-free redemption.
- For learning to trade with small sums: REITs and G-secs/T-bills on the exchange (fit 3).
- Skip: more overseas equity (already ~$208k US stock), digital gold, SME IPOs, P2P, PMS/AIF (below minimum).
