# Corporate bonds, tax-free bonds, NBFC FDs (sections 5-7)

## Summary

- Corporate bonds/NCDs from Rs 10k face value carry credit risk; unlisted ones are always short-term for tax; fit 2.
- Tax-free bonds pay exempt interest, worth more at a 35.88% slab; current yields not verified; fit 3.
- NBFC FDs 6.95-7.35% (2026-11-13) are not DICGC-insured; ~4.5-4.7% after tax; fit 2.

## 5. Corporate bonds / NCDs via online bond platforms (OBPP)

- SEBI cut face value of privately placed debt securities to Rs 10,000 (ICICI Direct,
  https://www.icicidirect.com/research/equity/finace/sebi-reduces-the-face-value-of-debt-securities-to-rs-10000-to-boost-retail-participation, pub 2024-07-11).
  SEBI circular itself not opened (sebi.gov.in URLs not found).
- Platforms named by the user (Wint Wealth, GoldenPi, Grip): SEBI OBPP registration and current yields
  **not verified** (sites need JavaScript).
- AngelOne: CEO said it will "launch corporate bonds in a fortnight" (Moneycontrol headline,
  https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/angel-one-to-rollout-us-stock-investing-to-everyone-in-45-days-to-launch-corporate-bonds-in-a-fortnight-says-ceo-ambarish-kenghe-article-14055688.html,
  pub 2026-10-19; page blocked, headline only). Live today: not verified.
- Risk: credit risk (issuer default; high-yield = low rating), thin secondary market, no deposit insurance.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): interest at slab 35.88%, TDS 10%; listed bonds LT after 12
  months at 14.95%. **Unlisted bonds and market-linked debentures (MLDs) are always short-term (AF s.76(2))**:
  many privately placed NCDs sold on bond platforms may be unlisted, so check listing before buying.

## 6. Tax-free bonds (secondary market)

- No new issues since about 2016 (not verified). Trade on NSE/BSE (e.g. NHAI, REC, PFC, IRFC series).
  Current yields: **not verified** (no 2026 source found; Moneycontrol 2025-04-15 article blocked).
- Risk: sovereign-like PSU issuers; thin volumes; long maturities.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): **interest is exempt** (1961 s.10(15)(iv)(h); 2025 Act row
  unverified); sale of listed bonds LT after 12 months at 14.95%, else slab. At a 35.88% slab, a
  tax-free yield Y equals Y / 0.6412 taxable (e.g. 5.5% tax-free = ~8.6% taxable; example only, current
  yields not verified). **Change vs first draft:** fit rises 2 -> 3, if a real yield check shows it beats
  post-tax G-sec yields.

## 7. Corporate / NBFC fixed deposits

- Rates 2026-11-13 (3-yr, regular): Shriram Finance 7.35%, Bajaj Finance 6.95%; for comparison SBI 6.3%,
  Bank of Baroda 7.25%, Jana SFB 8.2% (5-yr) (Business Standard / Stable Money,
  https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/your-fd-could-earn-8-6-check-which-banks-are-offering-the-highest-rates-126111301560_1.html, pub 2026-11-13).
- NBFC FDs are **not covered by DICGC** insurance; check the credit rating (Upstox,
  https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/investing/senior-citizen-fixed-deposit-rates-what-are-4-nbf-cs-offering-in-july-2026/article-196371/, pub 2026-07-03).
- Access: issuer website or apps; not AngelOne (not verified). Liquidity: early exit with penalty (not verified).
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): interest at slab 35.88%, TDS. Shriram 7.35% -> ~4.71%,
  Bajaj 6.95% -> ~4.46% after tax.
