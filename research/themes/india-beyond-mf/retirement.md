## 8. PPF

- Rate **7.1% for Oct-Dec 2026**, unchanged 11 quarters (Livemint,
  https://www.livemint.com/money/ppf-scss-ssy-interest-rates-kept-unchanged-for-dec-quarter-how-are-small-savings-rates-set-why-are-they-frozen-11790833954731.html, pub 2026-10-01).
  NSC 7.7%, KVP 7.5%, 5-yr POTD 7.5% same source.
- Rules: Rs 500 to Rs 1,50,000 per FY; matures after 15 full FYs; loan in yr 3-6; withdrawal from yr 7;
  extend in 5-yr blocks (National Savings Institute, https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?ID_PK=55, undated page).
- Access: post office or bank (not AngelOne). Risk: sovereign; rate reset quarterly.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): interest exempt; deposit deduction (AF s.123, old 80C) only
  in the old regime. 7.1% tax-free = ~11.1% taxable at 35.88%. **Change vs first draft:** fit rises 4 -> 5
  (best safe after-tax rate here; capped at Rs 1.5 L/yr).

## 9. NPS (Tier 1 / Tier 2) and UPS

- New scheme classification (PFRDA circular 2025-08-28 per Livemint): Lifecycle, Active Choice, Sanchay,
  Multiple Scheme Framework (MSF) and 4A schemes. MSF categories by equity: A 80-100%, B 60-80%,
  C 35-60%, D 10-35%, E 0-10%; max two MSF schemes per category per pension fund per tier
  (Livemint, https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/nps-schemes-get-a-new-classification-what-pfrdas-revised-framework-means-for-your-investment-choices-11788171304290.html,
  pub 2026-08-31). So up to 100% equity is possible via MSF Category A. Active Choice equity cap
  (75%?): not verified.
- Charges: CRA annual maintenance charge rules from 2026-07-01; Tier 2 AMC same as Tier 1, nil if Tier 2
  balance <= Rs 1,000; each scheme under a PRAN is a separate account for AMC (Upstox,
  https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/latest-updates/national-pension-system-pfrda-clarifies-nps-charge-structure-effective-from-1-july-2026/article-193003/,
  pub 2026-04-30). Fund management fee amounts: not verified.
- Employer contribution 80CCD(2): up to 14% of basic+DA, available in the new regime (Business Today,
  https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/retirement-planning/story/how-employer-contributions-to-nps-can-push-effective-tax-free-income-to-rs13-5-lakh-536714-2026-06-13,
  pub 2026-06-13); employer contribution is first added to salary, Sec 17(2)(vii) Rs 7.5 L combined cap
  (ET, https://m.economictimes.com/wealth/tax/the-rs-7-50-lakh-myth-why-employers-nps-contribution-must-be-included-in-salary-for-claiming-deduction-under-section-80ccd2/articleshow/132362111.cms,
  pub 2026-07-14).
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): own contribution Rs 50,000 (80CCD(1B), AF s.124(3)) is
  **old regime only**; employer contribution up to 14% of salary is deductible **in the new regime**
  (AF s.124(1)-(2), s.202); employer PF+NPS+superannuation above Rs 7.5 L a year is a taxable perquisite
  (AF s.17(1)(h)). At exit 60% lump sum exempt, annuity taxed at slab (AF s.124(6)). Tier 2 (private
  sector): no deduction, sale tax unclear (CA). Consistent with the first draft; the 14% route needs the
  employer to offer NPS (not verified).
- Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) is for central government employees only; 1.18 lakh chose it (TOI headline,
  pub 2026-08-03). Not relevant for a private-sector employee.
- Liquidity: Tier 1 locked to 60 (partial withdrawal rules not verified); Tier 2 withdrawable (not verified).
  Access: eNPS / employer / bank; not AngelOne.

## 10. EPF and VPF

- EPF rate **8.25% for FY 2025-26**, ratified by Finance Ministry (The Hindu,
  https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/govt-ratifies-825-epf-interest-rate-for-fy26-to-be-credited-this-month/article71116840.ece, pub 2026-06-18).
  FY 2026-27 rate: not declared (no source found).
- VPF = extra voluntary employee contribution into the same EPF account at the same rate, via employer
  payroll: **not verified** on epfindia.gov.in in this run.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): interest on employee contributions (EPF+VPF) above Rs 2.5 L a
  year is taxable at slab (old Rule 9D -> r.277); below that it is exempt. 8.25% tax-free = ~12.9% taxable
  at 35.88%. **Change vs first draft:** fit rises 4 -> 5 for VPF up to the Rs 2.5 L total.
