# Gold: SGB, ETF, digital gold, physical (sections 1-2)

## Summary

- No new Sovereign Gold Bonds since Feb 2024; old ones trade thinly on NSE/BSE.
- Secondary-market SGB buyers lose the tax-free redemption (Finance Act 2026), so SGB fit drops to 2.
- Digital gold is unregulated (SEBI warning 2025-11-08); avoid.
- If gold is wanted at all (metals are being cut), a gold ETF is the liquid, regulated choice.

## 1. Gold: Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB)

- What: Government of India bonds in grams of gold, issued by RBI; paid in rupees at gold price.
  (RBI SGB FAQ, https://www.rbi.org.in/commonperson/English/Scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=1658, dated 2019-02-04)
- Status: **new issues stopped.** Last tranche SGB 2023-24 Series IV, subscription Feb 12-16 2024, issue
  price Rs 6,213/g online (RBI PR, https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=57299,
  pub 2024-02-09). "Issuance suspended in early 2024 and remains discontinued as of ... early 2026"
  (Business Today, https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/investment/story/budget-2026-with-gold-prices-rising-is-bringing-back-sovereign-gold-bonds-feasible-for-the-government-512106-2026-01-21,
  pub 2026-01-21). Moneycontrol headline "SGBs discontinued in Budget 2025" (pub 2025-02-05, page
  blocked, headline only). No new-issue press release found on rbi.org.in for 2024-2026 months checked.
- Interest: fixed 2.5% a year on issue price (Business Today, above). Term 8 years; final redemption
  of SGB 2018-19 Series III on 2026-11-13 at Rs 14,024 per unit = 3-day IBJA average
  (RBI PR, https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63964, pub 2026-11-12).
- Early exit to RBI: allowed after the 5th year, only on interest dates; e.g. SGB 2021-22 Series VII
  on 2026-11-02 at Rs 14,167/unit (RBI PR, https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63897,
  pub 2026-10-30). RBI posts one press release per window (many in Sep-Nov 2026).
- Secondary market: listed on NSE/BSE as SGBxxx symbols, bought like a share via any broker incl.
  AngelOne (AngelOne support not verified). Current premium/discount to gold: **not verified** (NSE API
  and Yahoo returned no SGB data; Value Research article on exchange buying, pub 2026-10-01, blocked).
  Thin volume and wide spreads are typical (not verified for today).
- Min: 1 unit = 1 g (about Rs 14,000 at the Nov 2026 redemption price). Risk: gold price, no credit risk.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2 and section 0 item 3): 2.5% interest at slab (35.88%), no TDS.
  Redemption exempt only for **original subscribers who hold to maturity** (Finance Act 2026).
  **Secondary-market buyers lose the exemption**: exchange sale or redemption taxed, LT after 12 months
  at 14.95%, else slab. Whether RBI early redemption after year 5 counts as maturity: open CA question
  (section 7 item 8, "probably not"). Matches the Value Research headline (pub 2026-06-05).
  **Change vs first draft:** buying SGBs on the exchange no longer gives tax-free gold, so its fit drops
  from 3 to 2 (summary table in [the index](../india-beyond-mf.md)); a gold ETF is taxed much the same and is far more liquid.

## 2. Gold: ETF vs digital gold vs SGB vs physical

- SEBI warned on 2025-11-08 that "digital gold / e-gold products" (apps like Jar, Gullak) are "neither
  notified as securities nor regulated as commodity derivatives"; SEBI points to gold ETFs, commodity
  derivatives, Electronic Gold Receipts instead (Groww quoting SEBI, https://groww.in/blog/holding-digital-gold-sebi-issues-warning,
  pub 2025-11-14). Digital gold sales still Rs 2,590 Cr in Sep 2026 per WGC (Outlook Money,
  https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/digital-gold-sales-surge-to-rs-2590-crore-this-festive-season-as-retail-buyers-defy-sebi-warning, pub 2026-10-27).
- Compare (costs not verified unless cited):

| Route | Regulation | Extra return | Costs and risks | Liquidity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gold ETF | SEBI-regulated | none | expense ratio | exchange |
| SGB | GoI bond (RBI) | +2.5% interest; no new issues | gold price | thin exchange market |
| Digital gold | unregulated | none | platform/counterparty risk, 3% GST on purchase (tax/investment-types.md section 2) | app-dependent |
| Physical | - | none | making charges, storage, purity risk (RBI SGB FAQ above lists these as reasons SGB is better than physical) | not covered |

- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): digital gold LT after 24 months at 14.95%, else slab 35.88%.
  Gold ETF and physical-gold rates are not in the tax file (pending CA). Original-subscriber SGBs held
  to maturity are the only tax-free gold route, and no new SGBs are issued.
