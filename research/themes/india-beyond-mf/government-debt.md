# Government debt: RBI Floating Rate Savings Bonds, G-secs, T-bills, SDLs (sections 3-4)

## Summary

- FRSB pays 8.05% (Jul-Dec 2026) but is locked and taxable: ~5.16% after tax, below PPF; fit 2.
- T-bills 5.87-6.64% (2026-11-11), 10-year G-sec 7.32% (2026-11-13), SDLs 7.6-8.15% (2026-11-09).
- After 35.88% tax these give about 3.8-5.2%; no credit risk; RBI Retail Direct has no fees.
- Fit 3: useful to park cash and learn bond auctions.

## 3. RBI Floating Rate Savings Bonds 2020 (Taxable)

- Rate: **8.05% for 2026-07-01 to 2026-12-31** = NSC 7.70% + 0.35%, reset every half year
  (RBI PR, https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63065, pub 2026-07-01).
- Open: yes (rate still announced each half year; buy via RBI Retail Direct / banks per BusinessLine,
  https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/portfolio/personal-finance/how-to-use-rbis-retail-direct-platform/article69629493.ece, pub 2025-05-31).
- Lock-in 7 years, early exit only for senior citizens, minimum Rs 1,000, not tradable: **not verified**
  in this run (from memory of the 2020 notification). Not on AngelOne (not tradable on exchange).
- Risk: sovereign; rate can fall if NSC rate is cut.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): interest at slab 35.88%, TDS deducted; not transferable.
  8.05% -> about 5.16% after tax, below PPF's tax-free 7.1%. **Change vs first draft:** fit drops 3 -> 2.

## 4. G-secs, T-bills, SDLs

- Access: RBI Retail Direct (rbiretaildirect.org.in; page needs JavaScript): free account, no brokerage
  or maintenance fee, Aadhaar+PAN KYC, primary auctions (non-competitive bid at weighted average yield)
  and secondary market; also NSE goBID and BSE Direct apps (BusinessLine above, pub 2025-05-31).
  NSE trades T-bills and SDLs in the capital market segment (NSE notice title, 2020-07-28,
  https://www.nseindia.com/static/nse-introduces-trading-of-t-bills-and-sdls-in-capital-market-segment, not opened).
  AngelOne G-sec buying: not verified.
- Yields (primary auction cut-offs):
  - T-bills 2026-11-11: 91-day 5.87%, 182-day 6.52%, 364-day 6.64%
    (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63955, pub 2026-11-11).
  - 10-year: new 6.94% GS 2036 cut-off yield 7.32% on 2026-11-13
    (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63969, pub 2026-11-13).
  - SDLs 2026-11-09: about 7.61% (4-yr Haryana) to 8.15% (long Haryana); most 10-30 yr at 7.8-8.1%
    (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63948, pub 2026-11-09).
- Min: Rs 10,000 face value per bid on Retail Direct: not verified. Liquidity: T-bills hold to maturity
  (91-364 days); long bonds can be sold but retail secondary depth thin (not verified). Risk: no credit
  risk; price risk on long bonds if rates rise.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): interest at slab 35.88%; listed G-secs sold early are LT after
  12 months at 14.95%, else slab. T-bill discount: interest or capital gain is unsettled (CA, section 7
  item 10). After tax: 364-day 6.64% -> ~4.26%, 10-yr 7.32% -> ~4.69%, SDL 8.1% -> ~5.19%.
  The AF s.76 "always short-term" risk in the tax file covers foreign/unlisted bonds and debt funds
  (section 0 item 1, section 1), not Indian listed G-secs (section 2 gives them the 12-month rule).
