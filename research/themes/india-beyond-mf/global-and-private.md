## 13. International funds via Indian MFs

- Industry limit: $7 bn for overseas securities plus separate $1 bn for overseas ETFs (SEBI, per Livemint).
  As of 2026-11-04 only two schemes took fresh money: Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua FoF (lump sum + new SIP)
  and PGIM India Global Select Real Estate Securities FoF (lump sum only); Invesco paused three FoFs
  from 2026-11-04 (Livemint, https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/looking-to-invest-in-international-funds-only-one-scheme-remains-open-for-new-investors-amid-fresh-subscription-curbs-11794052868670.html, pub 2026-11-07).
  "$1 bn per fund house" limit: not verified. Both open funds are thematic (water, real estate).
- Fit: user already holds ~$208k US stocks -> low need.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): unlisted international FoF: LT after 24 months at 14.95%, else
  slab 35.88%; outside the AF s.76 debt-fund rule; no foreign tax credit. Listed international ETFs in
  India: LT after 12 months.

## 14. GIFT City (IFSC)

- RBI (Jul 2024) allows LRS remittance to foreign-currency accounts in IFSC for deposits, securities,
  property etc.; residents may not buy Indian companies listed in IFSC (TOI,
  https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/rbi-allows-forex-accounts-in-gift-city-widens-lrs-scope/articleshow/111646641.cms, pub 2024-07-11).
  So USD FDs at IFSC bank units are allowed; current IFSC FD rates: not verified.
- NSE IX unsponsored depository receipts on US stocks started 2022-03-03 with 8 stocks (TOI,
  https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/explained-how-indian-investors-can-directly-trade-in-8-major-us-stocks-from-today-via-nse-ifsc/articleshow/89961118.cms, pub 2022-03-03).
- NSE IX Global Access (Feb 2026): US-listed stocks and ETFs, fractional, no demat, no derivatives or
  crypto, under LRS $250k/year, more markets later (TOI Ahmedabad,
  https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/ahmedabad/nse-ix-opens-door-to-30-global-markets-for-retail-nri-investors/articleshow/128792471.cms, pub 2026-02-26).
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): IFSC fund distributions at slab 35.88%; units LT after 24
  months at 14.95%, else slab; probably not reported in Schedule FA; whether an IFSC exchange is a
  "recognised exchange in India" is unsettled (CA). USD FD interest at slab (section 2 corporate-FD /
  section 1 bank-CD rows by analogy; IFSC FD not listed). US-listed stocks/ETFs are US-situs for US estate
  tax with no Indian credit (section 4); the user already has about $192.5k QCOM+MSFT exposure (section 4).
  How NSE IX holdings count for US estate tax: not verified.

## 15. PMS, AIF, accredited investors

- Minimums: PMS Rs 50 L, AIF Rs 1 Cr; accredited investors can get some PMS/AIF from Rs 10 L.
  Accreditation: income >= Rs 2 Cr, or net worth >= Rs 7.5 Cr (Rs 3.75 Cr financial), or income Rs 1 Cr +
  net worth Rs 5 Cr (Rs 2.5 Cr financial), or securities holdings >= Rs 5 Cr; from SEBI circular of
  2026-11-05 AIF/PMS/SIF managers can accredit directly (BusinessLine,
  https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/portfolio/personal-finance/accreditation-opens-more-investment-doors/article71701573.ece/amp/, pub 2026-11-14).
- New MF-only PMS route "PRIM" with Rs 25 L minimum approved 2026-09-24 (Fortune India,
  https://www.fortuneindia.com/markets/sebi-clears-pms-fpi-accredited-investor-reforms-broadens-avenues/161093, pub 2026-09-24).
- Fit: user's Indian assets (~Rs 26 L) are below these minimums.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): PMS taxed per underlying security, fees generally not
  deductible; AIF Cat I/II pass-through (AF s.224), Cat III taxed at fund level.

## 16. P2P lending

- RBI 2024-08-16: platforms may not sell P2P as an investment with assured returns or liquidity options;
  no credit guarantee; lender bears all losses; lender cap Rs 50 L across all platforms; above Rs 10 L
  needs CA certificate of net worth >= Rs 50 L (ET BFSI,
  https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/rbi-bars-nbfc-p2p-to-flex-features-like-assured-returns-liquidity-options/112571700, pub 2024-08-16).
- RBI sent show-cause notices to six platforms in 2024 (Livemint,
  https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/p2p-peer-to-peer-lending-rbi-action-liquiloans-lendenclub-rbi-notices-p2p-lending-platforms-rbi-guidelines-nbfcp2p-11728987012577.html, pub 2024-10-16).
- Current returns: not verified. Risk: high credit risk, no early exit.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): interest at slab 35.88% (TDS unverified); **defaults are not
  deductible**, so losses do not reduce tax on the interest earned.
