## 11. REITs and InvITs

- Six listed REITs: Brookfield India, Embassy Office Parks, Mindspace, Nexus Select, Knowledge Realty
  Trust (listed Aug 2025), Bagmane Prime Office (listed May 2026); paid Rs 3,136 Cr in Q1 FY27; must
  distribute >= 90% of NDCF (Business Standard,
  https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/six-listed-indian-reits-distribute-3-136-crore-to-unitholders-in-q1-126081700878_1.html, pub 2026-08-17).
- Yields: "4% to 7%"; four of five gained 13.9-16.2% in the year to 2026-05-29 (Business Today,
  https://www.businesstoday.in/magazine/deep-dive/story/how-reits-are-becoming-a-rewarding-investment-proposition-542517-2026-07-13, pub 2026-07-13).
  Per-REIT current yield: not verified.
- Lot: public REIT/InvIT trade in 1 unit; IPO minimum Rs 10,000-15,000 (Livemint on SEBI board,
  https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/sebi-reduces-minimum-application-amount-in-reits-and-invits-11624976527810.html, pub 2021-06-29).
  SEBI reclassified REITs as equity (ET, https://m.economictimes.com/wealth/invest/has-reits-role-in-your-investment-portfolio-changed-after-sebi-reclassified-them-as-equity-/articleshow/124011296.cms, pub 2025-09-22).
- Privately placed InvITs: minimum lot Rs 25 L (ET Realty,
  https://realty.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulatory/sebi-lowers-minimum-investment-for-invits-to-rs-25-lakh-enhances-regulations/123799106, pub 2025-09-09).
  Listed public InvITs (e.g. IndiGrid, PowerGrid InvIT) and their yields: not verified.
- Small and Medium REITs (SM REITs): SEBI framework, schemes from Rs 50 Cr, min 200 investors (Livemint,
  https://www.livemint.com/money/sebi-notifies-small-and-medium-reits-11709999868347.html, pub 2024-03-09).
  Rs 10 L minimum per investor: not verified.
- Access: AngelOne demat, bought like shares (listed REITs). Risk: office demand, rates, unit price falls.
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): distributions split by type: SPV interest at slab 35.88%; SPV
  dividend exempt if the SPV did not opt for s.115BAA; debt repayment taxed (AF s.92(2)(k)); other
  receipts reduce cost (AF s.72(4)). Units: LT after 12 months at 14.95% above Rs 1.25 L, else 23.92%.
  So the 4-7% headline yield is partly taxed; after-tax yield per REIT not computed.

## 12. IPOs: mainboard and SME

- Apply via ASBA/UPI in the broker app (AngelOne offers IPO applications: not verified; UPI limit Rs 5 L: not verified).
  Mainboard retail minimum about Rs 14-15k per lot: not verified.
- Mainboard listing: median listing-day gain 6.8% across 131 mainboard IPOs in 2026; 375 mainboard+SME
  issues raised Rs 2.23 trn (Multibagg AI, https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/indian-ipo-valuation-listing-gains-cmwa5xlq600cn2mphh2r4b76h, pub 2026-11-10; secondary data site).
- SME 2025 rules: EBITDA >= Rs 1 Cr in 2 of 3 years, OFS <= 20% of issue, sellers max 50% of holding,
  phased promoter lock-in (The Hindu, https://www.thehindu.com/business/sebi-amends-rules-to-tighten-sme-ipo-norms/article69312781.ece, pub 2025-03-10).
- SME proposal 2026-10-14: cut minimum application to Rs 1 L, 500 allottees (from 200), end compulsory
  market making (ET CFO, https://cfo.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy/sebi-mulls-major-overhaul-of-sme-ipo-framework-lowers-minimum-application-size/134942151, pub 2026-10-14).
  Current SME minimum (user said Rs 2 L+): **not verified** in a primary source; still a proposal.
- SME returns 2026: of 126 SME IPOs, 65 above and 61 below issue price; average 24.4% but median 3.9%
  (NiftyTrader citing ET/Prime Database, https://www.niftytrader.in/markets/real-sme-ipo-return-2026-3-9-percent/, pub 2026-09-10).
- Tax (tax/investment-types.md section 2): listing-gain sale within 12 months = STCG 23.92%; LT after
  12 months at 14.95% above Rs 1.25 L; dividends at slab, TDS 10%.
