## 1. Rules that decide everything (LRS / FEMA)

- LRS: up to USD 250,000 per financial year for permitted current or capital account
  transactions [S1 para 1, S2 Q1]. LRS Master Direction last updated 2024-09-06 [S1].
- Not allowed under LRS: "remittance for margins or margin calls to overseas exchanges / overseas
  counterparty" [S1 para 5, S2 Q2 item 2] and "remittance for trading in foreign exchange abroad"
  [S2 Q2 item 4]. The Master Direction update list includes 2018-06-20 [S1]; the 2018 circular
  text itself was not opened (not verified which words it added).
- Portfolio investment (OPI) rules, OI Directions 2022 (dated 2022-08-22; page shows no later
  update) para 1(ix)(a): OPI shall not be made in (1) unlisted debt instruments, (2) securities
  issued by an Indian resident not in an IFSC, (3) "any derivatives unless otherwise permitted by
  Reserve Bank", (4) "any commodities including Bullion Depository Receipts" [S3].
- Para 1(ix)(e): units of an overseas investment fund regulated in its home country count as OPI;
  resident individuals may invest [S3]. Para 1(ix)(f): individuals do OPI within the LRS limit
  under Schedule III of the OI Rules [S3]. Para 1(x): "listed" = listed on a recognised stock
  exchange outside India [S3].
- LRS FAQ Q4: unused foreign exchange (e.g. sale proceeds, idle cash) must be reinvested or
  repatriated within 180 days [S2]. Relevant to the USD 795 idle cash.
- OI Rules 2022 PDF and FEMA (Permissible Capital Account Transactions) Regulations: rbidocs.rbi.org.in
  returned a CAPTCHA page, so not read (not verified beyond what [S1][S3] quote).
- Crypto: no RBI text found that permits or bans buying crypto abroad under LRS. Para 1(ix)(a)
  bars commodities and derivatives; whether crypto is a "foreign security" is not stated [S3].
  Status: grey, not verified.

## 2. Which IBKR entity, which account

- IBKR has a separate Indian entity (IB India, site interactivebrokers.co.in) that sells Indian
  stocks/F&O at Rs 6-20 per order [S16]. The SYEP page lists "IB India" clients as a separate group
  from "Indian clients who trade with IB LLC" [S7]. So Indian residents can hold a global account
  with Interactive Brokers LLC (US). Which entity holds THIS user's account: not verified - check the
  entity name on the Client Portal statement header.
- IBKR Lite (free US stock trades) is "available to US residents" only [S4 note 2]. The user is on
  IBKR Pro (Fixed or Tiered).
- Cash vs margin: IBKR offers both (SYEP page names cash accounts and margin accounts) [S7].
  Whether the account was opened as cash or margin: not verified - check Settings > Account Type.
  A margin loan abroad sits badly with [S1 para 5]; use a cash account.
