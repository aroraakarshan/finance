# IBKR global: options, futures, crypto, event contracts, margin

Part of [IBKR global products for an Indian resident](../ibkr-global.md). Source tags [S#] are listed in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary

- Options, futures, ForecastEx/event contracts and margin borrowing are NOT allowed under LRS
  (derivatives barred unless RBI permits; margin remittance barred) [S1 para 5][S3 Part I para 1(ix)(a), third item].
- Crypto at IBKR (Paxos/zerohash) is grey under LRS and eligibility for India is not verified;
  tax 35.88% flat with no loss set-off (tax/investment-types.md section 3).
- Use a cash account; IBKR Lite is not open to non-US residents [S4].

## 3. Instruments (derivatives, crypto, margin)

### 3.9 Options and futures
- Available at IBKR with options/futures permissions [S26]. LRS: NOT ALLOWED - derivatives barred
  for OPI unless RBI permits [S3 Part I para 1(ix)(a), third item; legal/compliance/derivatives-abroad.md: no hedging exception]; margin remittance barred [S1 para 5]. This
  includes covered calls and cash-secured puts. Tax (if ever done): business income, possibly
  speculative; losses only against speculative income [tax/investment-types.md section 3].

### 3.10 IBKR crypto (Paxos / zerohash)
- Spot crypto via Paxos Trust or zerohash "based on account type and residence"; 0.12-0.18% of
  value, min USD 1.75 (capped at 1%); Paxos passes a USD 0.15/month fee; not SIPC-protected;
  custody is outside IBKR [S22, S23]. Available to Indian residents: not verified (eligibility FAQ
  is JavaScript-only). Crypto futures/options: derivatives, not allowed [S3].
- LRS: grey, not verified (see [rules.md](rules.md) section 1). Risk: very high. Tax: flat 30% (35.88% here), only
  cost deductible, losses cannot be set off or carried forward [tax/investment-types.md section 3].

### 3.11 ForecastEx and other event contracts
- Yes/No contracts priced USD 0.01-0.99, settle at USD 1 or 0, on ForecastEx, Kalshi, CME [S24];
  IBKR itself calls them "event-based derivatives" [S24]; ForecastEx says they are CFTC-regulated
  [S25]. Pays a 3.38% "incentive coupon" [S24]. US election contracts are US-residents only; other
  availability "varies by affiliate and country" [S24] (India: not verified).
- LRS: NOT ALLOWED (derivative) [S3].

### 3.12 Margin account vs cash account
- Margin = borrowing from IBKR against holdings; also needed for short selling and options.
  Remitting for margin is barred [S1 para 5, S2 Q2]. Use a cash account (exists at IBKR [S7]);
  Lite is not open to non-US residents [S4].
