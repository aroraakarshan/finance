Rules [TX 1], tax year 2026-27 under the Income-tax Act 2025 (1961 sections in brackets):
- Equity MFs: long term after 12 months; LT 12.5% above Rs 1.25 L (s.198; 1961 s.112A), shared with
  listed shares; ST 20% (s.196; 1961 s.111A). Your income is in the 15% surcharge band, so with 4% cess
  LT = 14.95% and ST = 23.92% [TX header note]. My first version used 20% + cess only (no surcharge).
- ST loss sets off against any gain, LT loss only against LT gain; set-off comes before the Rs 1.25 L
  exemption, so loss set against exempt MF LTCG is wasted [TX 1]. FIFO is compulsory [TX 1].
- This year net short-term is already negative (INDmoney losses about -Rs 2.17 L) and the only taxable
  gain is NXPI LT +Rs 2.76 L, so any new loss saves only 14.95% [TX 2].

  rates [TX 1]; see [silver-gold.md](../silver-gold.md) (metals unrealised about -Rs 81,600 at 13 Nov prices [TX 3]).
