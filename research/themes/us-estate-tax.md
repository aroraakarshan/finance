# US estate tax on US-held shares (non-resident alien)

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Research only, not tax or legal advice; no orders placed.
Values from `portfolio/summary.md` (2026-11-13 closes, USD/INR 94.0338), updated after

## Summary
If you die holding US shares, the US taxes everything above $60,000 at 18-40%, with no Indian credit.
Today that is about $46,057 (Rs 43.3 L) on $213,304, or $87,716 (Rs 82.5 L) counting unvested
Microsoft awards, which vest at death. Heirs also wait about 1.5-3 years for the shares (estimate).
Proposal: term cover of about Rs 1 Cr now; move QCOM, then MSFT lots as they turn long-term, and new
vests into Irish UCITS ETFs (not US-situs). Details: [proposal](us-estate-tax/proposal.md).
