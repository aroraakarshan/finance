**Why India beat world gold this year: the import duty.** India raised the customs duty on gold and
silver from 6% to 15% on 13 May 2026 (New Indian Express, 2026-05-13). GOLDBEES vs world gold in Rs
rose 8.3% from Mar to Nov 2026 (own calc: 0.00879 -> 0.00952 units of ratio), matching 1.15/1.06 = +8.5%.
Axis MF via Moneycontrol (2026-11-11): gold -21.4% globally from its peak, only -14.9% in India.
The government is weighing a cut (Indian Jeweller, 2026-09-01: "early stage, no final decision").
**A return to 6% would knock about 7.8% off Indian gold ETFs in a day** (own arithmetic: 1.06/1.15 - 1).
Gold bought abroad does not carry this premium.

**Drivers now.** - Central banks: 321 t in Q3 2026, 675 t year-to-date (+6% y/y); ETFs +211 t in Q3 after -47 t in Q2;
  jewellery -17% y/y; WGC expects central-bank buying to "at least match 2025" (WGC Gold Demand Trends
  Q3 2026, 2026-11-03, https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-demand-trends/gold-demand-trends-q3-2026).
  Goldman: 60 t/month expected for 2026-27, nowcast 85 t vs 17 t pre-2022 (Kitco, 2026-11-13).
- Rates: the Fed has turned hawkish and is hiking; Goldman's base case includes another hike in Dec 2026
  (Kitco 2026-11-13); markets priced >80% for a Dec hike (CNBC-TV18 2026-11-09). US 10-y real yield
  (TIPS) 2.86% on 2026-11-12, up from 1.83% avg in Q4 2025 and -1.0% in 2021 (FRED DFII10).
- USD: dollar index 103.4, +4.3% in a year (own calc, DX-Y.NYB); US 10-y yield 5.15% (^TNX, 2026-11-13).
  India flows: gold ETF inflows Rs 3,474 Cr in Oct 2026 (-22% m/m), Rs 49,399 Cr Jan-Oct, already above
  all of 2025 (Rs 42,961 Cr) (Fortune India on AMFI data, 2026-11-11).

**Valuation.** (1) Vs real yields: before 2022 gold usually fell when real yields rose. From 2022 real yields went from
  -1% to +2-2.9% while gold roughly doubled (FRED DFII10; own calc). The usual explanation is
  central-bank buying; Goldman calls today "like 2022": central banks support the price, high rates
  hold investors back (Kitco 2026-11-13). On the old pattern gold is expensive; the pattern has
  not held since 2022. (2) Vs US inflation: $850 in Jan 1980 (widely cited high; not checked against LBMA here) x CPI 337.5
  (Oct 2026) / 78.0 (Jan 1980) = **~$3,680 in today's money** (FRED CPIAUCSL). Gold at $3,886 is ~6%
  above its 1980 real high; the Jan 2026 high was ~45% above it. So gold is not cheap on this measure.

USD vol 19% a year (5 y, own calc). Indian ETFs add duty-cut risk (-7.8%) and rupee risk.
