Part of [Real assets: what to add next](../opp-real-assets.md). 2026-11-15, `finance-research`; "nv" = not verified; "own calc" = yfinance closes to 2026-11-13, pulled 2026-11-15.

## Summary
- India REITs yield 5.2-7.0% (13 Nov 2026); Mindspace (P/NAV 0.88) and Bagmane (0.94) trade below NAV, occupancy 96-99%.
- InvITs yield 8.4-10.3%, but part is capital coming back; 5 y prices -9.1% to +3.4% a year.
- SEBI: REITs are equity for MFs from 2026-01-01; InvITs stay hybrid. Office tenants are mostly tech GCCs (tech-cycle link).
- Global REITs (IWDP 2.26% yield) and US REITs: thin margin over 5.15% Treasuries, correlate with Nifty 0.43-0.48.
- What to do: India REIT basket 2-3% of total, InvITs 0-1% optional, skip IWDP/VNQ.

- Debt/LTV per trust: nv (Bagmane claims the "lowest leverage in sector"). **What the yield is:** e.g. Mindspace Q2 payout = 62.5% dividend, 37.5% return of capital (company,
  via Investing.com). InvIT assets (toll roads, power lines) run on fixed-term concessions, so part of the
  10% yield is your capital coming back; the 5 y price falls above show it (concession terms: nv).
- **SEBI:** REITs count as **equity** for MFs/SIFs from 2026-01-01; InvITs stay **hybrid**; REITs could
  join equity indices after 2026-07-01 (ETLegalWorld/IANS on the SEBI circular, 2025-11-28). Actual index
  inclusion: nv. This can bring MF buying into REITs; it does not change your tax.
- **Fit:** Embassy vs QCOM 0.09, Nifty 0.14; PGINVIT vs Nifty 0.21; IndiGrid vs QCOM -0.12 (5 y monthly,
  own calc). But office REIT tenants are largely global tech capability centres (GCCs 96% of Bagmane's
  Q2 leasing; 42.9% of India office leasing Jan-Sep 2026, Mindspace slides): a hidden link to the same tech
  cycle as QCOM/MSFT. Mixing in Nexus (shopping malls) reduces this.
- **Risks:** interest rates (yields must beat G-secs), office oversupply, sponsor deals, unit price falls
  (Embassy's 2020 high 480 is still not regained, own calc), payouts are only partly tax-free.

## 3. Global and US REITs (IBKR)
- **iShares Developed Markets Property Yield UCITS ETF (IWDP)**, IE00B1FZS350, TER 0.59%, distributing
  quarterly, Ireland, EUR 811 m, current yield 2.26% (justETF, checked 2026-11-15). Price per year: 1 y +4.0%,
  5 y -3.0%, 10 y +0.2% (own calc, LSE line, price only).
- US valuation: listed-REIT implied cap rate 5.74% vs private appraisals 4.47% in Q2 2026; US 10-y at 4.76%
  at end Aug (Nareit "Triple D", 2026-09-03, https://www.reit.com/news/blog/market-commentary/real-estates-triple-d-valuation-divergence-denial-and-discounts).
  The 10-y is now 5.15% (^TNX, 2026-11-13): above the private-appraisal cap rate (4.47%) but below the
  listed-REIT implied cap rate (5.74%), a gap of only ~0.6 pp, so listed REITs get little reward over
  Treasuries (long-run spread: nv). VNQ yield 3.5% (own calc), also below the 10-y.
- Current REIT picks (cheapest Irish global REIT fund: HPRA, IE000G6GSP88, TER 0.24%) are in
  [sectors/real-estate](../../sectors/real-estate/README.md).
- Correlation (5 y monthly, own calc): VNQ vs QCOM 0.33, MSFT 0.30, Nifty 0.43; IWDP vs Nifty 0.48. VNQ is
  US-situs with 25% US withholding (`tax/investment-types.md` s.0). **Verdict: skip** (weak diversifier, low yield).
