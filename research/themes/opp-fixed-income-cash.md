# Fixed income and cash: rates now, where to park money, how much to hold (2026-11-15)

## Summary
- Rates are rising in both countries (RBI repo 5.50%, Fed 3.75-4.00%, more hikes expected): keep duration short.
- India money for under 3 years: arbitrage fund (5.1-5.7% after tax) beats liquid funds/FDs (~4.0-4.7%). No PPF (your decision 16 Nov 2026).
- USD cash from QCOM sales: IB01 (UCITS T-bill ETF, ~2.8-3.7% after tax) beats IBKR idle cash (2.17%); ask a CA on AF s.76 first.
- Avoid long duration, HY/IG credit funds and US-listed SGOV/BIL (US estate tax).
- Build cash + high-grade bonds to 15-25% of net worth (Rs 35-55 L) over time; details in sections 3-5.
