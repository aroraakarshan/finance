# India market now: valuations, flows, sectors, and where NEXT India money could go

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Market research, not advice; no orders. Proposals are conditional.

## Summary
- India large caps are cheaper than usual: Nifty 50 P/E 20.5 vs 22-23 medians, Next 50 17.7 (10-yr low); small caps are above their median (13-Nov-2026 [PE]).
- 2026 was weak (Nifty -3.8% YTD, record FPI selling, RBI hike to 5.5%), then +11% in 12 sessions in Nov.
- India is only 9.2% of your Rs 2.23 Cr; new India money mainly diversifies away from QCOM/MSFT (81.1%).
- Do (if you add India): fill UTI Nifty 50 first in steps over 3-6 months, keep Kotak Nifty Next 50, optionally a low-vol factor fund.
- Avoid defence, capital goods, pharma, more small cap, PSU themes, momentum and IT funds.
