conditional and the user decides. Prices from yfinance closes to 2026-11-13 ("YF").
Starting point: total Rs 2.23 Cr, about 88% US (QCOM + MSFT 81%), Indian MFs about 9%, silver,
and no Europe, Japan, EM, value or small-cap exposure (`mutual-fund-overlap.md`, 2026-11-15).

## Summary
- You are about 88% US; the next money diversifies most in non-US developed stocks (Europe + Japan), then India.
- US is the most expensive region (CAPE about 40); Europe/Japan are near their averages; EM is cheap only on peak chip earnings and adds to QCOM risk.
- Forecasters disagree: Vanguard, GMO, AQR favour non-US; BlackRock and J.P. Morgan 2027 favour the US.
- What to do (your choice): EXUS (or IMEA/VWCG + IJPA/VJPA) at IBKR, NIFTYBEES or UTI Nifty 50 at AngelOne, a small AVGS/IWVL value slice; avoid more US and EM ex-China.
- Use Irish UCITS accumulating ETFs: no tax until sale, 12.5% after 24 months, no US estate tax.
