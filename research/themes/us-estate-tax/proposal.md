Proposal only: term cover of about Rs 1 Cr now; sell QCOM first in tranches; hold each MSFT lot

## Details

Updated 2026-11-15 after `tax/investment-types.md`. **Conflict with the first version:** it said
to sell existing MSFT and QCOM "in steps" without order. The tax note (section 5.2) says QCOM first
and hold each MSFT lot until it is long-term. This plan now follows the tax note; the estate-tax
cost of waiting is shown so the user can choose.

1. **Now, cheap:** term cover for the liability including unvested awards (about Rs 1 Cr, or add
   it to existing cover; Rs 43.3 L on current assets alone). It also covers the waiting in step 3.
2. **QCOM first, in tranches** (98% long-term by value): highest-Indian-cost long-term lots first
   (e.g. ~$49,300 / 286 sh costs about Rs 1.43 L tax vs Rs 2.34 L FIFO); selling all QCOM costs about
   Rs 5.8-5.9 L extra tax. Specific-lot vs FIFO for Indian tax is a CA question. Leave the 14 DRIP
   shares until long-term (2026-12-20 to 2027-12-19). Spreading over years only defers tax (same
   rate band). A Nov-Dec 2026 sale needs advance tax in the 15 Dec and 15 Mar instalments.
   (`tax/investment-types.md` sections 5.1, 5.2.) Estate effect: with all QCOM moved to UCITS the
   US estate falls to about $82,100 and the tax at death to about $5,800 (from $46,057); with the
   unvested awards, to about $206,700 and $43,956 (from $87,716).
3. **MSFT: hold each lot until it turns long-term** (first lot 2027-01-01, main 48.8-sh lot
   2027-08-16, last 2028-09-01). This saves about Rs 1.70 L; net of the losses' higher value against
   short-term gains, holding beats selling now by about Rs 1.08 L (section 5.2). Estate cost of
   waiting: about $61k stays US-situs, adding roughly $20,800 (Rs 19.6 L, 34% marginal with the awards) tax only if
   death happens before the sale; step 1 covers it. Sell earlier only if concentration risk matters
   more to the user than a saving of about Rs 1 L.
4. **New MSFT vests/ESPP: sell at once** (about zero gain) and move to Ireland accumulating UCITS
   ETFs at IBKR, not US ETFs or US stocks. New LRS money: same.
5. **Idle USD (conditional):** the cash is small ($5,369) and its situs is unclear. T-bills
   would be non-US-situs, but they are LRS-grey and may be taxed always short-term (AF s.76), so
   consider them only if a CA confirms both. Avoid US money market funds (US-situs and probably
   always short-term). Simplest option: keep little idle USD at US brokers.
6. End state: held US-situs assets near or below $60,000. While employed at Microsoft the unvested
   awards (now $124,683 gross) stay US-situs and vest at death, so a 706-NA and transfer certificate
   would still be needed; with QCOM and held MSFT moved, the estate would be about $145,500 and the
   tax about $24,500 (Rs 23.0 L). The term cover in step 1 is what handles that part.
7. Write down for heirs: list of US accounts, broker contacts, this note, and that a 706-NA may be
   due within 9 months of death. Ask IBKR and Fidelity for their NRA estate checklist.
8. Before acting on (d) or gifts, get a US/India cross-border tax adviser.
