# US estate tax: Process after death

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Research only, not tax or legal advice; no orders placed.
Part of [US estate tax](../us-estate-tax.md). Sources read 2026-11-15 unless another date is given; "not verified" = no source confirmed it.
Short names: "IRS page" = https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/some-nonresidents-with-us-assets-must-file-estate-tax-returns ; "i706na" = Form 706-NA instructions, https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i706na (rev. 09/2025).

## Summary
Heirs must file Form 706-NA within 9 months; US brokers hold the shares until the IRS issues a
transfer certificate. Expect US accounts frozen for roughly 1.5-3 years (estimate). Broker procedures
not verified.

## Details

1. **Form 706-NA** due 9 months after death; automatic 6-month extension to file with Form 4768,
   but tax is still due at 9 months, with interest after (i706na "When To File"; IRS FAQ,
   https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/frequently-asked-questions-on-estate-taxes-for-nonresidents-not-citizens-of-the-united-states ,
   updated 2026-09-20). Penalty 20% for big undervaluation (i706na).
2. **Transfer certificate.** US companies, transfer agents, banks and custodians "can insure
   avoidance of liability ... only by demanding and receiving transfer certificates" before moving a
   nonresident decedent's property; not needed if US-situs assets were $60,000 or less (26 CFR
   20.6325-1, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/part-20/section-20.6325-1). The IRS issues it
   once the tax is paid; request by faxing the filed 706-NA. Where no 706-NA is due, the IRS says
   processing takes **12 to 18 months** (https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/transfer-certificate-filing-requirements-for-the-estates-of-nonresidents-not-citizens-of-the-united-states ,
   updated 2026-05-05). The form number "5173" could not be confirmed: irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-5173
   and f5173.pdf return 404.
3. Closing letter: request no earlier than 9 months after filing; fee $56 (i706na).
4. So heirs should expect the US accounts to be frozen for roughly 1.5-3 years (9 months to file
   + IRS processing; the 12-18 month figure is for the no-706-NA case; time for a 706-NA case not
   published - estimate, not verified). Heirs need an executor, a US tax ID (not verified) and
   probably a US tax adviser.
5. IBKR and Fidelity deceased-account pages: not verified. IBKR URLs tried returned 404; Fidelity
   returned 403/Service Unavailable. Ask each broker for its NRA estate checklist (and whether
   transfer-on-death/beneficiary designation is offered to non-US clients; a TOD does not remove
   the estate tax or the transfer certificate need, per 20.6325-1).
