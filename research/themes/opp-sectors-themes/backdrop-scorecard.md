Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Back to the index: [opp-sectors-themes.md](../opp-sectors-themes.md). Source tags [S#]: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary

- US stocks look expensive on most long-run measures (Vanguard 99th percentile); value, small caps and ex-US look cheaper.
- Lowest correlation with QCOM/MSFT: energy (~0), utilities (~0.1), health care (~0.2), small-cap value (~0.2).
- Use the scorecard to compare valuation, returns and overlap before picking an area.

## 1. Backdrop (dated views)

- S&P 500 forward 12m P/E 18.3 on 2026-11-13, below its 5y (19.8) and 10y (19.1) averages [S2]; it fell
  because forward EPS rose 33.6% since 2025-12-31 while price rose 7.4% [S3]. If AI-era profit
  estimates fall, the P/E rises again. Trailing P/E 25.4, above 5y/10y averages [S2].
- FactSet 2026-11-05: only two sectors below their 25-year average P/E: IT (17.3 vs 20.0) and
  Energy (11.7 vs 13.9) [S3].
- Vanguard (2026-10-21): US equities at the 99th percentile vs fair value; US value (64th) and
  small caps (69th) the least expensive US styles; ex-US developed 79th, EM 96th [S5].
- GMO 7-year REAL return forecast, as of 2026-09-30, "normal rates": US large -7.3%, US small -5.1%,
  US deep value 1.0%, intl large -0.9%, intl small 1.3%, intl deep value 4.0%, Japan small value 5.4%,
  EM 0.2%, EM value 4.2%, US bonds 3.5%, cash 1.9% [S6]. GMO favours value, ex-US and small value.
- J.P. Morgan 2027 LTCMA (2026-10-19, 10-15y, USD nominal): US large cap 7.5%, global equity 7.5%, EM
  7.1%, global core infrastructure 7.1%, gold 5.5% [S7]; sector/factor numbers not verified.
- Research Affiliates (via Fortune, 2026-05-21, 10y nominal): US large cap 3.2%, US large growth
  1.7% (last of ~40 classes), intermediate Treasuries 4.6%, cash 3.5%; EM value, Europe and US small
  cap "easily beat" Treasuries (exact numbers not in article; not verified) [S8].
- FTSE Russell (2026-11-06): Q3 Russell 1000 +1.8% vs Russell 2000 -7.2%, value beat growth [S9].
