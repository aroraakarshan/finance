# Sectors and themes: factor evidence

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Back to the index: [opp-sectors-themes.md](../opp-sectors-themes.md). Source tags [S#]: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary

- Value and profitability earned a premium over decades; size alone did not.
- Recent years: value recovering, quality sharply negative, small caps weak.
- For him: prefer small cap combined with value, not size or quality alone.

## 3. Factor evidence (long run vs recent)

Ken French data library, long-short factor returns, %/yr, monthly data to 2026-09 [S10]:

| Factor | US full (1963-) | US 10y | US 3y | US 1y | Developed full (1990-) | Dev 10y | Dev 3y | Dev 1y |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Size (SMB) | 1.6 | -3.1 | -5.0 | 1.2 | -0.2 | -3.4 | -2.4 | 2.7 |
| Value (HML) | 3.0 | -1.1 | 4.2 | 9.7 | 2.8 | 0.7 | 6.7 | 16.2 |
| Profitability/quality (RMW) | 2.7 | 1.8 | -7.8 | -15.7 | 3.5 | 1.5 | -4.9 | -9.3 |
| Investment (CMA) | 2.7 | -0.9 | -2.8 | 2.9 | 2.2 | 0.2 | 0.4 | 9.5 |

- Value: positive over 60+ years, lost a decade, now recovering (developed HML +16.2% in 12m) [S10].
  Value and momentum premia across countries and asset classes: Asness, Moskowitz, Pedersen,
  "Value and Momentum Everywhere" (DOI 10.2139/ssrn.2174501) [S11].
- Size alone: weak; developed SMB about zero since 1990 [S10]. Small + value (or + profitability)
  is the better-evidenced combination; that is what Avantis small cap value targets (fund aim, not verified).
- Quality: long-run positive (RMW 2.7-3.5%/yr), but sharply negative in the last 1-3 years: junk
  led [S10]. Asness, Frazzini, Pedersen, "Quality Minus Junk" (DOI 10.2139/ssrn.2312432) [S11].
- Low volatility: Frazzini, Pedersen, "Betting Against Beta" (DOI 10.3386/w16601) [S11]; lower
  drawdowns, lower long-run return than the market in recent 5y (MVOL 6.1%/yr vs SWDA 10.4%) [S1].
