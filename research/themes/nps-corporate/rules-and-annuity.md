# NPS corporate: exit rules and annuities

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Research only, not advice; no orders.

## Summary
- Normal exit (60, superannuation or 15 years): at least 20% buys an annuity, up to 80% lump sum; all cash if corpus is Rs 8 L or less.
- Early exit: 80% must buy an annuity (all cash if Rs 5 L or less). Defer to 85 allowed.
- Partial withdrawal is 25% of own contributions; with employer-only money it may be nil (not verified).
- Annuity at 60 today: about 8.6% life only, 6.4% with return of price, 7.4% joint life (LIC, 2025-08-20).
- A fixed annuity keeps only 23-30% of its buying power after 25 years; it is taxed at slab.

## 3. Rules (exit and withdrawal)

Source: PFRDA (Exits and Withdrawals under NPS) Regulations 2015, consolidated "last amended 20 July 2026"
(regulation 4 replaced from 2025-12-16), and NPS Trust exit pages (read 2026-11-15).
- **Normal exit** (non-government): after 15 years in NPS, or at 60, or on superannuation (reg 4(1)(a)).
  Corpus above Rs 12 L: at least 20% buys an annuity, the rest as lump sum or staged (SLW / SUR). Rs 8-12 L:
  up to Rs 6 L lump sum, the rest as SUR over at least 6 years or annuity. Up to Rs 8 L: all as lump sum.
  Discrepancy: NPS Trust says corporate subscribers exit at retirement age, the regulation also lists
  "15 years"; whether an employed corporate subscriber can exit at 15 years: not verified.
- **Defer / stay invested:** lump sum and annuity can be deferred to 85 (reg 4(1)); old "75" text is still
  on the PFRDA corporate page and part of the NPS Trust deferment page (out of date). Charges during
  deferment are paid by the subscriber.
- **Premature exit** (before the above): at least 80% to annuity, 20% lump sum; all lump sum if corpus is
  Rs 5 L or less (reg 4(1)(b), NPS Trust). No minimum years in the new text.
- **Partial withdrawal:** after 3 years; up to 25% of **own** contributions; up to 4 times before 60 with
  4 years between; purposes: children's education or marriage, first house, illness, disability, loan
  lien (NPS Trust page; reg 8). The 2015 text excluded employer money; with employer-only contributions
  this likely means **no partial withdrawal at all** (not verified; ask the PoP).
- **Death before exit:** whole corpus to the nominee, who may take lump sum, staged payouts or annuity
  (reg 4(1)(c)). Disability over 75%: treated as normal exit (old text; new clause (d) not fully read).
- **Job change:** the PRAN is yours and stays; employer money vests at once (tax note s2). Next employer can
  continue on the same PRAN; else it runs as an All Citizen account (process not verified).

- Rates are fixed for life once bought; NPS buyers use the same plans (LIC brochure s9(ii)); ASPs include LIC,
  HDFC Life, ICICI Pru, SBI Life (NPS Trust list). Today's rates may differ from the 2025-08-20 brochure
  (repo hike 2026-10-07 per scout feed); live NPS quotes (CRA sites) were not reachable.
- Inflation: a fixed annuity keeps 30% of its buying power after 25 years at 5% inflation, 23% at 6%
  (1/1.05^25, 1/1.06^25). After 15 years: 48% / 42%.
- Tax: annuity income taxed at slab (tax note s2; up to 35.88% at today's income).
