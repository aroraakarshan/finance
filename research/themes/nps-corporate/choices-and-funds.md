# NPS corporate: scheme choices and pension funds

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Research only, not advice; no orders.

## Summary
- Employer money can hold at most 75% equity (Active Choice); 100%-equity MSF schemes are for voluntary money only.
- Pick Active Choice E 75 / C 25 / G 0 now; LC75 would start cutting equity within about 2 years.
- Pension fund: ICICI first (best equity 5y, 7y, 10y to 2026-11-13), HDFC second; SBI last in every period.
- Charges 0.24-0.32% a year (PoP 0.20% from 2026-12-01 + fund fee 0.04-0.12%) plus Rs 100-500 CRA.

## 1. Scheme choices for a corporate Tier 1 subscriber

Sources: PFRDA circular PFRDA/2026/47/REG-PF/10 dated 2026-08-28 ("Standardised framework ...", replaces
the MSF circular of 2025-09-16 and the LC circular of 2025-10-17), amended 2026-11-02 (PFRDA/2026/52/REG-PF/12,
in force by 2026-12-01); PFRDA corporate page (pfrda.org.in/schemes/national-pension-system/nps-for-corporates,
read 2026-11-15).

| Choice | Equity (E) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Active Choice | up to 75% in Tier 1 (100% only in Tier 2); C up to 100%, G up to 100% | Asset class A is no longer listed. Age taper after 50 (old rule): not in the 2026 circular, not verified |
| LC75 - High (15E/55Y) | 75% up to 35, then -4 pts/yr: 55% at 40, 35% at 45, 20% at 50, 15% at 55+ | Glide table on PFRDA corporate page |
| LC Aggressive (35E/55Y) | 50% up to 45, then 48% at 46 ... 40% at 50, 35% at 55+ | PFRDA page shows "-" for ages under 45; circular says "50% till 45" |
| LC50 / LC25 | 50% / 25% up to 35, down to 10% / 5% at 55 | Too low for a 27-year horizon |
| MSF (Category A 80-100%, B 60-80%, C 35-60%, D 10-35%, E 0-10% equity) | up to 100% (Cat A) | Max 2 schemes per category per fund per tier. Examples (NPS Trust list, AUM as on 2026-09-30): HDFC Equity Advantage 85-100% E (Rs 359 Cr), UTI Wealth Builder 90-100% (Rs 37 Cr), Kotak Kuber 80-100% (Rs 41 Cr) |

- For this user (27 years to 60): LC75 holds 75% E for about 2 more years at most, then is about 55% in
  7 years, 35% in 12, 20% in 17 and 15% from 22 years on. LC Aggressive holds 50% for about 12 years,
  then falls to 35% by about 22 years from now.
- **Who can pick MSF:** PFRDA circular of 2025-11-07 (PFRDA/2025/20/PDES/03, quoted by Upstox 2025-11-10 and
  ET 2025-11-11): money under the employer co-contribution set-up cannot go to MSF; employees may put
  *voluntary* money into MSF. The 2026-08-28 circular (para 5.6) keeps that limit for corporate accounts.
  So for employer NPS the real ceiling is **75% equity** (Active Choice). Voluntary own money gets no
  deduction in the new regime (tax note s2).
- **Who chooses:** employer or employee, by "formal and mutual agreement"; the employer may let the
  employee decide fully (2025-11-07 circular, Upstox/ET above). If the employer chose, the employee may
  revise after 365 days (PFRDA corporate page).
- **Switching:** at most 2 change requests (fund and/or scheme) per account per financial year
  (2026-08-28 circular para 5.4). Only one of Lifecycle or Active Choice at a time per PRAN (para 5.2).

**Choice, given 81% of investments in QCOM + MSFT** ([employer-stock-concentration.md](../employer-stock-concentration.md), 2026-11-13 values):
- For max equity: the NPS is locked for 15-25 years, and Scheme E holds Indian large caps (top 5 of every
  fund: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Reliance, Bharti Airtel, SBI/Infosys; NPS Trust Scheme E PDF, 2025-10-31),
  so it adds a different market and currency to a US-tech-heavy portfolio. EPF already puts about Rs 7.4 L/yr
  (employee + employer, tax note s1/s5) into fixed-rate debt, so debt is already covered.
- Against: total equity risk is already high, and an Indian equity crash near 60 hits the 80% lump sum.
  Answer: cut equity in the last 5-8 years, not now.
- **Verdict:** **Active Choice E 75 / C 25 / G 0** now. LC75 would start cutting equity within about 2
  years and be at 55% in 7; LC Aggressive gives only 50%. Review in about 17 years; from about 19 years
  from now move 5 points a year from E to G/C (or switch to LC Aggressive), aiming at about 40% E by
  year 27.

## 2. Pension funds, charges and returns

Ten pension funds are registered: SBI, LIC, UTI, HDFC, ICICI, Kotak, Aditya Birla, Tata, Axis, DSP
(NPS Trust "PFs under NPS", read 2026-11-15). **Max is not on the list.**

Charges (corporate employee, Tier 1):
| Charge | Rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| PoP annual charge | 0.20% a year of AUM, taken through the NAV (+ GST) | PFRDA/2026/46/REG-POP/08 (2026-08-28); start moved to 2026-12-01 by PFRDA/2026/53/REG-POP/09 (2026-10-26) |
| PoP one-time onboarding | Rs 200 per PRAN | same circular |
| Investment management fee (IMF), non-government | 0.12% (fund AUM up to Rs 25,000 Cr), 0.08% (to 50,000), 0.06% (to 1,50,000), 0.04% (above) | NPS Trust charges page, read 2026-11-15 (circular 2026-03-06) |
| Total PoP + IMF | 0.24% to 0.32% a year | 2026-08-28 circular, Annexure A |
| CRA | Rs 100 to Rs 500 a year | same Annexure A |
| NPS Trust / custodian | 0.003% / about nil | same |
