## 5. Expected outcome (Rs 3,79,956/yr, flat, 27 years to 60; rates after charges, own calculation)

Exit: 80% lump sum, 20% annuity. Annuity per month at 8.63% (life) / 6.40% (life + return of price).
"Today's Rs" = life annuity / 1.06^years. Index fund: same pay taken as salary, Rs 2,43,628/yr after
35.88% tax, same return, all sold at 60, 14.95% tax on gains. NPS after tax: 60% tax-free, 40% taxed at
35.88% (worst case, tax note s4).

Total paid in: Rs 1.03 Cr over 27 years.

| Rate | NPS corpus | Lump sum 80% | Annuity 20% | Annuity /month life / ROP | Life in today's Rs | NPS after tax | Index fund after tax |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9% | Rs 3.90 Cr | Rs 3.12 Cr | Rs 0.78 Cr | Rs 56,100 / 41,600 | Rs 11,600 | Rs 3.34 Cr | Rs 2.23 Cr |
| 11% | Rs 5.44 Cr | Rs 4.35 Cr | Rs 1.09 Cr | Rs 78,200 / 58,000 | Rs 16,200 | Rs 4.66 Cr | Rs 3.06 Cr |
| 12% | Rs 6.44 Cr | Rs 5.15 Cr | Rs 1.29 Cr | Rs 92,600 / 68,600 | Rs 19,200 | Rs 5.51 Cr | Rs 3.61 Cr |

- 5 years either side: 22 years gives Rs 2.39 / 3.09 / 3.51 Cr at 9 / 11 / 12%; 32 years gives
  Rs 6.23 / 9.40 / 11.58 Cr. The NPS-to-index-fund ratio barely moves (1.48-1.54x).
- The edge is the tax saving (see the tax note): NPS ends about 1.5x the index fund at the same return.
  With 75% equity NPS will likely earn less: 1 point lower still leaves NPS about 1.29x ahead. Break-even
  NPS return vs an index fund at 12%: 9.5%.
- Not modelled: pay rises, the Rs 1.25 L LTCG exemption, annuity tax over time, future rule changes.

## 6. Verdict and what to fill in the employer form (conditional)

1. Opt in at the cap-fit amount (tax note s6). Ask HR first: does Microsoft let the employee choose fund
   and scheme, which PoP it uses, who pays the 0.20% PoP charge, and the change window.
2. Scheme: **Active Choice, E 75% / C 25% / G 0%**. If the form offers only Auto Choice: LC75 (75% E for
   up to 2 more years, 55% in 7); switch to LC Aggressive in about 9 years, when LC75 drops below 50%.
3. Pension fund: **ICICI Pension Fund**; HDFC Pension Fund as the second choice.
4. Tier 2 / MSF 100% equity: skip (no deduction, and employer money cannot go to MSF).
5. Nominee: add one at opening (whole corpus goes to the nominee on death).
6. Review in about 17 years (equity taper from about year 19; 2 changes a year allowed); decide annuity type at 60 (joint life if a spouse
   depends on it).
