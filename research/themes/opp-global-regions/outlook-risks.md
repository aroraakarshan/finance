# Global regions: expected returns, drivers and risks

Back to the index: [opp-global-regions.md](../opp-global-regions.md). Written 2026-11-15.

## Summary
- Vanguard (Oct 2026), GMO (Oct 2026) and AQR (Jan 2026) expect non-US stocks to beat US stocks over 5-10 years.
- BlackRock (Aug 2026) and J.P. Morgan LTCMA 2027 (US 7.5%, EM 7.1%, Oct 2026) put the US at or above the rest.
- EM is the lowest for Vanguard and J.P. Morgan; GMO's best are value and small value.
- Main risks: AI-trade unwind (US, EM chips), BoJ and yen (Japan), premium P/E and oil (India).
- What to do: do not bet on one forecast; split the next money across the cheaper, less correlated regions.
