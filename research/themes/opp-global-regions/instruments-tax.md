- Buy foreign regions through Irish UCITS accumulating ETFs at IBKR (LSE USD lines), India through AngelOne.
- Main candidates: EXUS (ex-US developed), IMEA/VWCG (Europe), IJPA/VJPA (Japan), EIMI (EM), AVGS/IWVL (value), NIFTYBEES (India).

- Tax: Irish UCITS acc pays nothing until sale, then 12.5% (14.95% all-in) after 24 months; Indian ETFs 12.5% above Rs 1.25 L after 12 months.
- What to do: pick one ticket per chosen region from the table; check the CA questions before large sums.

## 8. One-line tax note per instrument type (tax/investment-types.md sections 0-2, tax year 2026-27)

- Irish UCITS ETF, accumulating (EXUS, IMEA, IJPA, EIMI, AVGS...): no Indian tax until sale (no
  deemed-income rule found, CA to confirm); LTCG 12.5% (14.95% with surcharge/cess) after 24 months,
  else slab (35.88%); Ireland withholds 0%; Schedule FA; not US-situs.
- Irish UCITS ETF, distributing (e.g. VEVE/VDEV): dividends taxed at slab every year; same gains rules.
- US-listed ETFs (SPY, VGK, EWJ, EEM, INDA): dividends at slab after 25% US withholding (credit
  allowed); 24 m / 12.5%; US estate tax above $60,000 - avoid.
- Indian equity ETF / Nifty index fund (NIFTYBEES, UTI Nifty 50): LTCG 12.5% above Rs 1.25 L a year
  after 12 months; STCG 20%.
- Indian international FoF: 24 m / 12.5% / slab; no foreign tax credit.
