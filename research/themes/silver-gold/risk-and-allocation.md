## 5. How much is sensible

- World Gold Council (2026-03-17): over 19 years, an Indian rupee portfolio had better risk-adjusted returns
  and smaller drawdowns with **7.5-15% gold** (of the whole portfolio).
  https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/why-gold-2026-anchor-indian-portfolios
- SEBI multi-asset allocation funds: at least 3 asset classes, **min 10% each** (circular
  SEBI/HO/IMD/DF3/CIR/P/2017/114, 2017-10-06): https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2017/1509103633344.pdf
- Typical gold/silver share inside Indian multi-asset funds: not verified.
- No credible source found recommending a large silver share; silver is usually a small add-on to gold.
