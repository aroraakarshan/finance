## Summary
- Opting in at Rs 3.8 L/yr (12.3% of basic) beats investing the same take-home pay in an index fund by about 1.5x after tax, because of the tax saving.
- Employer money is capped at 75% equity: choose Active Choice E 75 / C 25 / G 0 now; cut equity from about 19 years from now.
- Pension fund: ICICI Pension Fund (best equity returns 5y-10y to 2026-11-13); HDFC as second choice.
- At 60, at least 20% buys a fixed annuity (about 8.6% today, taxed at slab); up to 80% comes as a lump sum.
- Ask Microsoft HR who picks fund and scheme, which PoP, and who pays the 0.20% PoP charge.
