# Employer stock: staged selling proposal

## Summary
A proposal only; the user decides and trades. Sell QCOM in two steps: 432 shares in Nov-Dec 2026 and 195 in Apr 2027,
highest-Indian-cost lots first, down to 134 shares (~5.2%). Sell each MSFT lot on its long-term date (under 10% from
Aug 2027, about 0 vested by Sep 2028); sell new MSFT vests and ESPP shares at once. Selling all QCOM costs about
