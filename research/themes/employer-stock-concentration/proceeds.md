# Employer stock: where the sale money goes

## Summary
Option A: keep the money abroad in Ireland-domiciled UCITS ETFs at IBKR (low cost, no US estate tax, 0%
Irish withholding). Option B: bring it to India; idle sale cash abroad must be reinvested or brought home
within 180 days under LRS. Avoid parking it in bonds, T-bills or money-market funds without a CA (may be
taxed as short-term at 35.88%).
What to do: option A unless the money is needed in India.

Back to the index: [employer-stock-concentration.md](../employer-stock-concentration.md). Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`;
research only, not advice; no orders placed. "not verified" = could not confirm from a source.

## Options

| | Option A: stay abroad (IBKR) | Option B: bring to India |
|---|---|---|
| What | Ireland UCITS ETFs at IBKR, e.g. iShares Core S&P 500 UCITS (CSPX, LSE, USD, accumulating, Ireland, TER 0.07%; iShares page, 13 Nov 2026, checked 2026-11-15). A world fund (e.g. VWRA) would cut US-tech weight further; its TER not checked | Repatriate USD to an Indian bank account |
| Indian tax on holding | Accumulating UCITS has no deemed income until sale (inferred from absence of a rule); Ireland withholds 0% on distributions; US-listed ETFs suffer 25% US withholding on dividends (tax note section 0 items 5-6, section 1) | Depends on the Indian investment chosen |
| US estate tax | US shares above $60,000 at death -> 18-40%, with **no Indian credit**; QCOM + MSFT alone are about $192,500 (over 3x the threshold); Irish UCITS are not US-situs (tax note section 4; see also `research/themes/us-estate-tax.md`) | Removed once the money leaves US assets |
| Rules on timing | - | RBI LRS FAQ Q4 (updated 2023-04-06, https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/FAQView.aspx?Id=115, checked 2026-11-15): income/proceeds of LRS investments may be retained and reinvested; unspent/unused foreign exchange, unless reinvested, must be repatriated within 180 days of receipt. So sale cash left idle at IBKR for over 180 days is a problem; reinvesting is allowed. The OI Regulations 2022 90-day repatriation rule (Reg 9(4)) is for ODI (control stakes), not portfolio shares like these (RBI Master Direction id=12380, checked 2026-11-15). Whether the same 180-day rule applies to employer shares not bought with LRS money: not verified (ask finance-tax/CA) |
| Costs | USD-traded share class: no FX needed. IBKR FX if needed: 0.20 basis point of value, minimum USD 2 per order (interactivebrokers.com spot currency commissions, checked 2026-11-15) | Wire-out fees and Indian bank FX spread: not verified |

## What not to use for the proceeds

| Item | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| T-bills, bonds, US money-market funds, bond UCITS | May be "always short-term" under AF s.76 (35.88% here); CA before buying | Tax note (`tax/investment-types.md`) section 0 item 1, section 1 |
| IBKR share lending (SYEP) | May count as a transfer (sale) and reset the holding period | Tax note section 0 item 2 |
