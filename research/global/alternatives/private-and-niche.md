## Summary

- None of these fits him now: angel funds are closed (not accredited); unlisted shares have repeatedly been priced above the later IPO, with no SEBI protection;
  royalties and tokens are grey under LRS; carbon and sports are not open to retail; domains are speculation.
- If he still wants startup exposure: at most 2-5% (ET experts [N3]), only through a SEBI angel fund once accredited.
- Growth is better served by the listed equity options in other pages.
