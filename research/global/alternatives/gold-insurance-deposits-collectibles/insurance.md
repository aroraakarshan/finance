# Insurance-linked products

Part of [Gold, insurance, deposits and collectibles](../gold-insurance-deposits-collectibles.md). Written 2026-11-15. Source codes [Sx]: [sources](sources.md). "nv" = not verified. Fit 1 (skip) to 5 (strong fit).

## Summary

- Buy term cover only if someone depends on your income, and invest separately (fit 5 if cover needed).
- ULIPs, endowment, money-back and guaranteed plans: skip (fit 1); typical XIRR about 5%, taxed or locked in.
- Annuities pay ~6-7% at 60 (~4.2% after your tax) and lose to inflation: not now. NPS annuity minimum is now 20%.

- **Annuity details.** Current LIC rate for age 60: nv (LIC calculator needs JavaScript). The RBI raised
  the repo rate 25 bp to 5.50% on 2026-10-07 [S17]; annuity quotes may have moved since the Dec 2025
  6-7% figure (nv). A level annuity loses half its buying power in ~14 years at 5% inflation (own calc,
  ln 2 / ln 1.05). Increasing-annuity options step up 3% or 5% a year at a lower start rate [S16].
- **NPS (new since india-beyond-mf).** PFRDA amendment notified 2025-12-12: non-government subscribers
  may take 80% as lump sum, annuity minimum 20% (was 40%), deferral to age 85 [S18]. The Retirement
  Income Scheme lets the rest stay invested and pay out till 85, default rate 1/(85 - age), e.g. 4% at 60
  [S19].
- **Why term + invest.** In [S9] a surrender at year 5 plus term cover plus an equity fund beat staying in
  the ULIP; the ULIP's edge is only tax on small premiums (under Rs 2.5 L/yr), which you exceed if you
  invest a meaningful sum. Traditional plans mix cover and a ~5% return; at 35.88% slab a taxable FD or
  G-sec is close and liquid (opp-fixed-income-cash).
