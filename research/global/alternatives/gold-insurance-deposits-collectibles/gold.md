# Gold and silver variants side by side

Part of [Gold, insurance, deposits and collectibles](../gold-insurance-deposits-collectibles.md). Written 2026-11-15. Source codes [Sx]: [sources](sources.md). "nv" = not verified. Fit 1 (skip) to 5 (strong fit).

## Summary

- Gold ETF is the best route (fit 4): SEBI-regulated, liquid, TER 0.49-0.59%.
- Exchange SGBs cost a premium now (median +1.5%, up to +7.2% on 13 Nov 2026) and secondary buyers are taxed: fit 2. EGRs: new and thin, fit 2.
- Digital gold, jeweller savings schemes, coins and bars: unregulated or costly (3% GST, spreads): skip (fit 1).

- **SGB premium, measured.** IBJA 999 gold PM rate on 13 Nov 2026: Rs 1,35,809/10 g = Rs 13,581/g [S7].
  NSE closes the same day [S1]: median series +1.5% above IBJA, range +0.4% to +7.2% (own calc).
  Series maturing 2026-29 (old enough for early exit to RBI at the IBJA price) trade at +0.4% to +2.7%;
  series maturing 2030-32 (no RBI exit yet) at +2.5% to +7.2%: SGBFEB32IV +7.2%, SGBDE31III +6.2%.
  For comparison RBI paid Rs 14,024/unit on the 2026-11-13 redemption (IBJA average of 10-12 Nov) [S8];
  gold fell 3.4% over those days (IBJA 1,40,603 -> 1,35,809, 11 -> 13 Nov PM [S7]).
  So: no discount is on offer now. A buyer pays ~1.5-7% over spot for 1-1.1% interest that is taxed at
  35.88%, plus taxable gains. A gold ETF at 0.5% TER is cheaper over most horizons (own reading).
- **EGR:** NSE launched EGRs in May 2026 (News On AIR headline, 2026-05-26 [S6]); NSE calls the segment
  "at an early stage of development" [S6]. WGC wants GST clarity on conversion [S6]. Tax rules for EGRs: nv.
- **Jeweller schemes:** Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules 2014 r.2(1)(c)(xii) excludes an advance
  for goods supplied within 365 days; that is why schemes stop at 11 months. A longer scheme would be a
  deposit, which a jeweller company cannot take (s.73 Companies Act); the Banning of Unregulated Deposit
  Schemes Act 2019 also applies [S5]. No deposit insurance, no regulator to claim from.
- **Silver, briefly.** Silver ETF/FoF: see silver-gold. IBJA silver 999: Rs 1,78,000/kg (13 Nov PM [S7]).
  Physical silver: same 3% GST and buyback-spread problem as gold [S3]. Digital silver: unregulated like
  digital gold (whether SEBI's note names silver: nv). No silver SGB or silver EGR found. Fit 1-2.
