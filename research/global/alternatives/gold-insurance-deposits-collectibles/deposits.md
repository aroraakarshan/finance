# Deposits: GIFT City, abroad, RFC, small finance banks

Part of [Gold, insurance, deposits and collectibles](../gold-insurance-deposits-collectibles.md). Written 2026-11-15. Source codes [Sx]: [sources](sources.md). "nv" = not verified. Fit 1 (skip) to 5 (strong fit).

## Summary

- Small finance bank FDs: 8.25% pre-tax (~5.3% after tax); keep principal + interest under Rs 5 L per bank (fit 3).
- GIFT City USD FDs pay 2.9-4.5%, not insured, below T-bills: fit 2. FDs abroad under LRS: allowed today, a bar is only proposed: fit 1.
- RFC accounts are for returning NRIs: not for you.

- **LRS foreign-FD bar: status on 2026-11-15.** The idea first appeared as "RBI plans rules to stop
  Indians parking money in foreign FDs using LRS" (ET BFSI headline, 2025-06-13 [S25]). Oct-Nov 2026
  reports: a Rs 50 L sub-limit for some LRS uses; "some reports suggest" investments may need RBI
  approval; "only a proposal with
  no start date" (Livemint 2026-10-28 citing Zee Business [S26]; The Hindu 2026-11-12 says RBI proposed it
  to the Finance Ministry [S27]). No draft on RBI's draft-directions page and the LRS FAQ is still dated
  2023-04-06 (both checked 2026-11-15 [S28]). So: today foreign deposits and IFSC deposits under LRS are
  allowed (platforms.md, RBI LRS MD para 6, 11); a future bar is possible, timing unknown.
- **GIFT FD vs alternatives.** 3m US T-bill 4.32% and IBKR Pro USD cash 3.38% (cash-and-currencies s.2)
  match or beat 3-4.5% GIFT FDs, and a T-bill ETF is not exposed to one bank. The FD's merit is staying
  inside India's system. Resident eligibility differs by bank (HSBC: NRIs only [S20]; others: nv).
- **SFB FDs.** Keep principal plus interest to maturity under Rs 5 L per bank (insurance includes
  interest [S23]); deposits in different branches of one bank are added together [S23]. Rates are about
  1-2 points above big banks (big-bank 3 y 6.3-7.25%: opp-fixed-income-cash).
