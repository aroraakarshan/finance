## Summary

- SM REITs (SEBI-regulated, Rs 10 L units) are listed but barely trade; PropShare Platina projects about 9%, one building each.
- Fractional deals outside an SM REIT (e.g. Strata after its licence surrender) and managed farmland are UNREGULATED; SEBI calls farm pooling a possible illegal CIS.
- For this user: listed REITs first; SM REIT only with money locked for years (fit 2); farmland fit 1, avoid.

## 2. SM REITs and fractional real estate

- **Rule (SEBI REIT Regulations 2014, Chapter VIA, as amended to 2026-10-15, read 2026-11-15):** any
  person pooling Rs 50 Cr or more from at least 200 investors to own rent-earning property is a "REIT"
  (reg. 2(1)(zm)). An SM REIT scheme holds assets of Rs 50 Cr to below Rs 500 Cr, needs 200+ unit
  holders, **minimum unit price Rs 10 lakh**, must not portray guaranteed returns, and must pass on
  **100%** of scheme net distributable cash flow (SPVs at least 95%) (regs. 26P, 26ZK). SM REIT chapter
  notified March 2024 (Livemint 2024-03-09, via india-beyond-mf/reits-and-ipos s.11).

## 3. Farmland / managed farmland - UNREGULATED

- **SEBI view:** pooling money for farm projects and sharing profits can be a Collective Investment
  Scheme needing SEBI registration (CIS Regulations 1999). SEBI froze Growpital's accounts and called it
  a "veiled", "illegal" and "fraudulent" scheme; it had raised over Rs 180 Cr through LLPs, from
  Rs 5,000, with ~15% "returns" sold as tax-free (Finshots 2024-01-31, read). MCA fined Growpital
  Rs 1.48 Cr for marketing unlisted securities (Livemint 2024-06-06, headline). SEBI ordered the
  scheme wound up (BusinessLine 2026-09-28, headline).
- **Land law:** many states limit buying agricultural land to farmers or residents (e.g. Himachal
  Pradesh: Down To Earth 2025-12-04, headline; Karnataka eased its law in 2020: Vidhi 2020-08-07,
  headline). State-by-state rules: nv - a buyer must check the state's land revenue act. A
  "managed farmland" plot may give a share in land, a lease or nothing enforceable.
- Return: nv (no audited data). Liquidity: none. Fees: nv. **Fit 1.**
