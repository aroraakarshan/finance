## 7. Private credit and venture debt AIFs (Cat II)

- Regulated by SEBI (AIF Regulations 2012, amended to 2026-10-15). Minimum Rs 1 Cr; accredited
  investors can get some AIFs from Rs 10 L (india-beyond-mf/global-and-private s.15).
- **Returns:** "up to 22%" advertised; an 18% interest return is ~12.6% after 30% tax, before
  surcharge, cess, fees, delays and idle cash (Livemint 2026-08-29, read). At 35.88%: 18% -> 11.5%.
  The asked 12-16% target range: nv from a fund document.
- **Liquidity:** lock-in typically 5-7 years, minimum about 3 (Livemint/PPFAS 2026-08-27, read).
- **Risk:** light regulation, collateral can be unlisted or promoter shares, loans with bullet repayment
  look "performing" until maturity; one family office caps credit AIFs at 5-6% of its portfolio
  (Livemint 2026-08-27). Market: 166 deals, USD 12.4 B in 2025 (EY via Livemint 2026-08-29).
  Named fund defaults: nv. Venture debt (e.g. Trifecta Fund IV, BS 2024-08-02, headline): returns nv.
- Fees: management fee plus carry, per fund (nv). **Fit 1:** Rs 1 Cr = 45% of his assets.

## 8. Market-linked debentures (MLDs) / structured products

- SEBI regulates MLDs as listed non-convertible securities; exact current SEBI ticket size and
  disclosure rules: nv (NCS Regulations text read did not show them).
- **Tax change:** Finance Act 2023 added s.50AA: MLD gains on transfer, redemption or maturity are
  short-term, at slab, from AY 2024-25; before that they were taxed as long-term at 10%
  (Budget 2023 memorandum, indiabudget.gov.in, read). Now AF s.76 under the 2025 Act (tax file).
  Market built workarounds after the change (Livemint 2023-05-01, headline).
- Return: nv. Risk: issuer credit plus a payoff formula you cannot easily price. **Fit 1.**
