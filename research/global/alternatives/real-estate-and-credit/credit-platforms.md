# Invoice discounting, bond platforms (OBPPs), SDIs and P2P

Index: [real-estate-and-credit.md](../real-estate-and-credit.md). Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. "nv" = not verified;
"(headline)" = Google News headline and date only. After-tax at 35.88% slab = pre-tax x 0.6412.

## Summary

- Retail invoice discounting is UNREGULATED; frauds (Bizongo complaint, Falcon about Rs 850 Cr) hit investors. Fit 1.
- OBPP bonds are SEBI-regulated, from Rs 10,000 face, 9-14% stated, but that yield means lower credit and thin resale. Fit 2; SDIs fit 1.
- P2P (RBI) pays 9-12% before defaults; NPAs hit a record Rs 1,163 Cr in FY24. Fit 1.
- After 35.88% tax, 10-12% becomes 6.4-7.7%.

## 4. Invoice discounting - UNREGULATED (retail platforms)

- **What:** you buy a business's unpaid invoice at a discount; the big buyer pays you in 30-90 days.
- **Regulated route:** RBI's TReDS (Trade Receivables Discounting System). KredX now runs an
  RBI-licensed TReDS platform (DTX) with banks and NBFCs as financiers (kredx.com, read 2026-11-15).
  Whether individuals can fund on TReDS: nv (financiers shown are institutions).
- **Unregulated retail route:** TradeCred offers invoice deals at "10-12% p.a." illustrative, 30-90 days,
  "access your funds anytime*" (tradecred.com, read 2026-11-15). No regulator protects the investor.
- **Bond platforms:** SEBI lets OBPPs offer only products regulated by SEBI, RBI, IRDAI, IFSCA or
  PFRDA (Moneycontrol 2026-08-15, read), so invoice deals must sit outside the OBPP. Jiraaf's site now
  shows bonds and FDs only (read 2026-11-15). The asked-about "SEBI 2024 scrutiny": nv as a separate event.
- **Observed losses:** TradeCred filed an EOW complaint alleging Bizongo diverted buyer payments from
  escrow (Rs 124 Cr per Moneycontrol, Rs 69 Cr per ET 2025-07-03 headline); Bizongo denies it.
  Falcon invoice discounting scam about Rs 850 Cr (TOI 2025-02-15, HDFC Sky 2026-10-16, headlines).
- After tax at 35.88%: 10-12% -> 6.4-7.7%. **Fit 1.**

## 5. Bonds on online bond platforms (OBPPs) and SDIs

- Regulated by SEBI as OBPPs (framework Nov 2022: circular text not read, see india-beyond-mf/bonds-and-deposits s.5).
  Since Aug 2026 OBPPs may also sell IFSCA (GIFT City) products, labelled "international", and 54EC
  bonds (Moneycontrol 2026-08-15, read).
- **Minimum:** private-placement face value cut to **Rs 10,000** (from Rs 1 L, which was cut from
  Rs 10 L in Oct 2022), only for plain-vanilla interest-bearing NCDs with a merchant banker (ICICI Direct
  2024-07-11, read). Wint, Grip, Jiraaf and GoldenPi all advertise "start with Rs 1,000" (sites read
  2026-11-15); which instruments allow that: nv.
- **Stated yields (sites, 2026-11-15):** Grip bonds 9-12.5% (3-36 months), SDIs 10-12.5% (6-48 months),
  FDs 7-9%; Jiraaf 10-14%; GoldenPi 9-14%. Wint and GoldenPi say "zero defaults till date" (Wint adds
  this does not guarantee the future). Altifi: site did not load (nv).
- **Risk:** yields above 9% mean lower-rated or unrated NBFC and small-company credit; resale is thin.
  SDIs are slices of loan pools (SEBI aligned securitisation rules with RBI: PSU Watch 2026-06-19,
  headline). Unlisted bonds and MLDs are always short-term for tax (tax file, AF s.76).
- **Fees:** built into price/yield (GoldenPi says zero brokerage); spread nv. After tax: 12% -> 7.7%.
- **Fit 2** for bonds (same as india-beyond-mf), **1** for SDIs.

## 6. P2P lending

- Regulated by RBI (NBFC-P2P Directions 2017, tightened 2024-08-16: no assured returns, no liquidity
  options, Rs 50 L cap across platforms; india-beyond-mf/global-and-private s.16).
- **Observed:** lender returns usually 9-12%; P2P NPAs hit a record Rs 1,163 Cr in FY24; platforms had
  pooled money and offered loss guarantees, which RBI then banned (Capitalmind 2024-12-05, read).
- Defaults are not tax-deductible (tax file). 12% -> 7.7% before any default. **Fit 1.**
