## Summary

- None of these is a growth asset: they pay interest or rent taxed at 35.88%, so 10-12% becomes 6.4-7.7%, close to a debt fund or arbitrage fund but with default and lock-in risk.
- Indian property: listed REITs first (daily trading, 1-unit lots); an SM REIT only with money he can lock for years and only from a SEBI-registered SM REIT.
- Avoid: farmland schemes, unregulated invoice discounting, fractional deals outside an SM REIT, P2P. Private credit AIFs: not at Rs 2.23 Cr (Rs 1 Cr minimum).
- Check before any buy: SEBI intermediary list for the platform, credit rating, listed or unlisted.
