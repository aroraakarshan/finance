# Royalties, litigation finance, carbon credits, domains, tokenised assets

Back to the index: [private-and-niche.md](../private-and-niche.md). Sources [N#]: [sources.md](sources.md). Written 2026-11-15; "nv" = not verified.

## Summary

- Music royalties (Royalty Exchange, SongVest, JKBX) and tokenised assets are grey under LRS; no audited return data found (nv).
- Litigation finance: only listed route is Burford (BUR), -58% in 1 year after the YPF ruling was voided [N17].
- India carbon market (CCTS) is closed to individuals [N19]; voluntary credits and domains are UNREGULATED speculation [N18, N23].
- What to do: skip all of them.

## 4. Music and film royalties (abroad)

- Royalty Exchange (US auctions of royalty streams): no accreditation needed; non-US citizens
  may buy; income likely ordinary income (site FAQ, 2026-11-15) [N13]. Fees for buyers: nv.
- SongVest: SEC-qualified Reg A+ Tier 2 "royalty share units"; open to foreign investors except
  Canada; needs a US bank account or card to buy and a US bank or PayPal to get paid (FAQ,
  2026-11-15) [N15]. JKBX: launched 2024-03-06 after SEC approval (Billboard headline) [N15].
- Return range: no audited return history found (nv). Risks: song popularity decay, platform
  failure, US withholding on royalties (rate for India nv), FX.
- LRS/FEMA: **grey**. Overseas portfolio investment for resident individuals covers listed
  foreign securities and regulated funds ([infrastructure-private-markets.md](../../infrastructure-private-markets.md) section 2);
  an unlisted Reg A+ unit or a bare royalty contract is neither. Whether it is ODI, a permitted
  capital account deal or not allowed: nv. PayPal receipt of investment income: nv.
- Film royalties: no retail platform found (nv).

## 5. Litigation finance

- Funds: private, offshore, usually for institutions; minimums and India access nv. India has no
  retail product; a platform was planned (Deccan Herald headline, 2026-03-03) [N16].
- Listed route: Burford Capital (NYSE/LSE: BUR) can be bought under LRS as a listed share.
  It fell about 49% intraday on 2026-03-27 when a US appeals court voided the $16.1 bn YPF
  judgment against Argentina (Legal Business, 2026-03-30; Reuters headline) [N17]. yfinance:
  USD 3.61 on 2026-11-13, -58% 1y, -35%/yr 3y, -77% from USD 15.97 peak (2024-04-10) [N17].
- Lesson: returns hang on a few big court cases; one ruling can halve the value. No growth
  case for him.

## 6. Carbon credits

- India CCTS (compliance): set up under the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act 2022 and the
  2023 scheme [N20]. Intensity targets (not a cap): FY26 1-3% cut vs FY24, FY27 2-8% (Saur
  Energy, 2026-09-02) [N19]. Trading of Carbon Credit Certificates only on power exchanges (IEX,
  PXIL, HPX), CERC-supervised, no OTC; BEE runs it [N19]. Monthly trading schedule set (headline,
  2026-11-02) [N21]. Price estimate Rs 250-1,500/t [N19].
- **Discrepancy:** Saur Energy says about 740 entities in nine sectors [N19]; ET Climate
  (2026-10-18) says about 490 units in seven sectors [N20]. Official BEE count: nv.
- Retail access: only obligated entities and offset project developers are named; no route for
  individuals found (nv). IEEFA (2026-07-14) says financial intermediaries are not yet in and
  would come later [N22].
- Voluntary market: quality problems; a Guardian analysis found over 90% of Verra rainforest
  offsets likely "phantom" (2023-01-18) [N18]. **UNREGULATED.** Skip.

## 7. Domains

- Pure speculation; no income. NIXI bans buying and selling .in domains, applied to all .in
  names, and warned of suspensions (Domain Name Wire, 2026-06-08; NIXI advisory 2026-05-11)
  [N23]. Return data: nv. **UNREGULATED** (no investor protection). Skip.

## 8. Tokenised real-world assets

- GIFT City: IFSCA consultation paper is the only Indian policy text; Asset Tokenisation
  (Regulation) Bill 2026 is a private member's bill (Rajya Sabha, 2026-03-14), not law [N24].
  Shipfinex got an IFSCA limited use authorisation for ship tokens (ANI headline, 2026-10-13;
  page 403) [N25]. Retail access for residents: nv.
- Abroad (US tokenised funds etc.): LRS status grey, as for crypto ([crypto.md](../../crypto.md) section 2).
- Tax: if a token counts as a VDA, 30% flat (35.88% for him), no loss set-off ([crypto.md](../../crypto.md)).
  Whether a tokenised share or bond is a VDA: nv. Skip.
