# Gold variants, insurance-linked products, deposits and collectibles

Market research, not advice. Written 2026-11-15. User: salaried, about Rs 2.23 Cr, 35.88% slab.

## Summary

- Gold: keep using a gold ETF (fit 4); exchange SGBs trade at a premium now and are taxed for secondary buyers.
- Insurance: term cover only if needed, invest separately; skip ULIP, endowment, guaranteed plans; no annuity now.
- Deposits: small finance bank FDs within the Rs 5 L DICGC cover are fine (8.25%, ~5.3% after tax).
- GIFT City USD FDs pay less than T-bills and are uninsured; foreign FDs under LRS are allowed but may be barred.
- Collectibles (art, watches, wine, coins, cars, fractional art): unregulated, costly, illiquid: skip.
