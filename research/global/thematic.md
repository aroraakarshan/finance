# Thematic ETFs (beyond chips and sectors)

Market research, not advice. Data fetched 2026-11-15. Sectors, factors, AI infrastructure,
electrification and India manufacturing are in [opp-sectors-themes.md](../themes/opp-sectors-themes.md);
not repeated here. Access, IBKR and UCITS basics: [ibkr-global.md](../themes/ibkr-global.md),
estate tax: [us-estate-tax.md](../themes/us-estate-tax.md), concentration: [employer-stock-concentration.md](../themes/employer-stock-concentration.md).

## Summary
- Thematic ETFs lose about 30% (risk-adjusted) in their first 5 years after launch (RFS 2023); most themes here lagged MSCI World over 5y.
- AI/robotics funds (XAIX, RBOT) add more MSFT and semis to an 81% QCOM+MSFT portfolio: skip.
- Skip clean energy, uranium/nuclear, water, EM consumer, space, ageing; defence: no new money.
- If a satellite is wanted (themes <= 5% in total): broad health care XDWH (0.25%) or cyber LOCK (0.40%), Irish UCITS acc.
- Better use of money: broad UCITS ETFs and non-US regions to cut the QCOM+MSFT concentration.

## The base rate: thematic ETFs after launch
Ben-David, Franzoni, Kim and Moussawi, "Competition for Attention in the ETF Space",
Review of Financial Studies 36(3), 987-1042, 2023 (online 2022-08-04), doi:10.1093/rfs/hhac048
(NBER w28369, 2021). Abstract (via Crossref, checked 2026-11-15): "over their first 5 years,
specialized ETFs lose about 30% (risk-adjusted)"; not explained by fees or hedging, but by the
underlying stocks being overvalued at launch. Providers launch what is already popular.
Practical rule: a theme with a big recent run and a new wave of launches is the riskiest time
to buy. Survivorship: the funds below are the large survivors; closed funds are not shown.

## Per theme
**1. AI and robotics (beyond chips).** Trend: IFR World Robotics 2026 (ifr.org, 2026-09-24):
industrial robot stock +9% to 5 million in 2025, installations +11% to 600,000+; service
robot sales +24% to ~250,000 (IFR, 2026-09-30). Valuation: BOTZ (US) trailing P/E 33.9 vs
ACWI 20.6 (Yahoo, not verified). Crowding: high; AI is the most crowded theme of 2023-26.
Performance: above; BOTZ (US, USD) 1y -10.8%, 3y +19.2%, 5y -12.6% (Yahoo). Overlap: XAIX
top 10 = MSFT 5.75%, Meta, Apple, NVIDIA, AMD, Amazon (45% in top 10): same bet the user
already has. RBOT top 10 has AMD, NVIDIA, Intel, Advantest, Teradyne (semis again); QCOM not
in any top 10 checked, full lists not verified. Verdict: **skip** - adds more US tech/semis to an
81% QCOM+MSFT book; robotics-pure funds (ROBO, BOTZ) lagged MSCI World badly over 5y.

**2. Cybersecurity.** Trend: steady software spend; no primary market-size source fetched
(not verified). Valuation: CIBR (US) trailing P/E 27.2 vs ACWI 20.6 (Yahoo). Crowding: high
after ISPY +56.9% YTD 2026 (justETF). Performance: best of the themes (5y +52 to +71% EUR,
about level with MSCI World; CIBR US USD 5y +87.5%, Yahoo). Overlap: CIBR US holds MSFT 2.17%,
Broadcom 7.9%, Cisco 7.3%, no QCOM (ftportfolios.com holdings 2026-11-13; UCITS CIBR tracks
the same Nasdaq CTA index). 2025 was negative for all three (-1% to -5%). Verdict: **small
satellite at most (0-3%)**, LOCK cheapest (0.40%, 115 names, less concentrated); still US
software, so it does not cut the user's main risk.

**3. Clean energy / energy transition.** Trend: clean energy investment about $2.2 trillion in

**4. Nuclear / uranium.** UCITS exists: URNU (Global X, IE000NDWFGA5) yes, plus URNP and

## Instruments for this user (if any)
- 1st choice: none of the themes; diversifying away from QCOM+MSFT is better done with broad
  UCITS ETFs (SWDA/VWRA) and non-US regions (see the region notes in research/global/).
- If a satellite is wanted (total themes <= 5% of portfolio): XDWH/XWHS health care (0.25%)
  for low overlap; LOCK (0.40%) for cyber. Avoid XAIX/RBOT (MSFT/semis overlap).
- Prefer Irish UCITS accumulating lines over US ETFs (BOTZ, CIBR, URA, ICLN, PHO, UFO are
  US-situs; estate tax above $60k).
- Tax (from tax/investment-types.md): foreign ETFs LTCG 12.5% after 24 months, else slab;
  Irish UCITS acc: no tax till sale; distributing (INRG, IH2O): dividends at slab.
