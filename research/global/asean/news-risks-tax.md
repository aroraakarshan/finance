# ASEAN-5: growth, news, risks, withholding tax

Part of [ASEAN-5 index](../asean.md). Written 2026-11-15. Market research, not advice. Sources [A#]: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- Vietnam grows fastest (Q3 GDP +9.95%) and joined FTSE Emerging on 2026-09-21; inclusion phases run to Sep 2027.
- Indonesia's main risk is MSCI: possible standalone reclassification; JCI -41% YTD, rupiah at record low.
- Philippines slowed to 2.1% growth; Thailand has election-rerun risk.
- Dividend WHT India-treaty rates: Indonesia 10%, Vietnam 10%, Thailand 10%, Malaysia 0%, Philippines 15/20%.

## 5. Growth drivers and news

- Vietnam: Q3 2026 GDP +9.95% (Nation Thailand, 2026-10-02 [A8]). FTSE Russell upgrade to Secondary
  Emerging took effect 2026-09-21, phased 10% (Sep 2026), 20% (Mar 2027), 35% (Jun 2027), 35%
  (Sep 2027); up to $6 bn inflows (Nation Thailand, 2026-09-21 [A8]). MSCI kept Vietnam OFF its
  watch list: foreign ownership limits hit >10% of market cap (Viet Nam News, 2026-06-24 [A9]).
- Indonesia: Q3 GDP +5.47% (Reuters/Bloomberg headlines, 2026-11-04). But see risks.
- Malaysia: Q3 GDP +5.3% advance (Reuters headline, 2026-10-15); KLCI at a 14-month low 1,582.6
  on 2026-11-13 (The Edge headline).
- Thailand: "worst to best" Asian market by July (Bloomberg headline, 2026-07-16); KResearch sees
  2% growth in 2026 and 2027 (Nation 2026-08-20; Bangkok Post 2026-10-30, headlines).
- Philippines: Q3 GDP slowed to 2.1% (Inquirer, 2026-11-09); PSEi at a 2026 low (Inquirer, 2026-11-13).

## 7. Risks

- Indonesia (main risk): MSCI flagged free-float and ownership transparency (Jan and Jun 2026),
  then warned in Oct 2026 of broader exclusions next year and possible reclassification to a
  standalone market; stocks had their worst day since 1998 (Reuters/FT headlines, 2026-10-16). On
  2026-11-12 MSCI cut Barito Pacific and 9 small caps (effective after 2026-11-30); JCI -41% YTD,
  "world's worst-performing major market" (Business Times [A10]). FTSE kept Indonesia Secondary
  Emerging, off its watch list, but froze IPO adds and free-float increases until at least Mar 2027
  (InvestorTrust, 2026-11-14 [A11]). JCI +5.8% that week to 5,344 [A11].
- Vietnam: foreign ownership caps, state-bank credit boom, single-country fund size.
- Thailand: court case could force an election rerun (Straits Times headline, 2026-09-25).
- Philippines: growth slump, weak peso. Malaysia: slow earnings, small market. All: small ETFs
  (closure risk); 1y vol Indonesia ~33%, Vietnam ~22%, Malaysia ~11% [A2].

## 8. Dividend withholding (PwC tax summaries [A12]) and tax

| Country | Non-treaty WHT on dividends | India DTAA rate |
|---|---|---|
| Indonesia | 20% | 10% (needs Certificate of Domicile) |
| Vietnam | 5% PIT (shown for residents; non-resident rate not verified); share sale 0.1% of proceeds | 10% |
| Thailand | 10% | 10% |
| Malaysia | 0% | 0% |
| Philippines | 15/25% | 15/20% |

Inside a UCITS or US ETF the fund suffers this; you cannot reclaim it. India tax: foreign
shares/ETFs LTCG 12.5% after 24 months, else slab; dividends at slab with FTC; Irish UCITS acc no
tax till sale (Lux acc treated the same: not verified, CA to confirm). US ETFs: 25% US WHT, US-situs.
