## Summary
- IBKR trades none of IDX, HOSE, SET, PSE directly; Bursa Malaysia is listed (India-resident access not verified).
- Use non-US UCITS ETFs on LSE (USD lines); US-listed EIDO/VNM/THD/EWM/EPHE and ADRs are US-situs.
- Best fits: XVTD (Vietnam, 0.85%), HIDD (Indonesia, 0.50%); broad CSEMAS (EM Asia, 0.20%) already holds ASEAN.
- Many single-country funds are under EUR 50 m: closure risk.
