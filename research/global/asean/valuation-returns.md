## Summary
- Indonesia (CAPE 12.4) and the Philippines (10.4) are among the cheapest markets; Thailand and Malaysia near EM (17.4).
- No source gives an ASEAN return forecast; J.P. Morgan EM 7.1%/yr USD, GMO EM -1.8%/yr real.

- THB, MYR and VND gained against INR over 10 years; IDR and PHP kept pace.
