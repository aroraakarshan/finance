# Eurozone - access and valuation

Written 2026-11-15. Part of [Eurozone (and wider Europe)](../eurozone.md). Sources [E#]: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- He can buy every big European market at IBKR; one accumulating ETF (MEUD or SMEA) covers it.
- MEUD/VEUR/IMEU are pan-Europe (UK 22%, CH 15%); CSEMU is eurozone only.
- P/E 17.7-19.1 is at the top of Europe's 10-20 year range but about 25% below MSCI World.
- Direct shares carry withholding of 12.8-35% by country; reclaims are slow or unverified.

## 1. Access for an Indian resident

- IBKR: Netherlands, Italy, Spain, Sweden, Denmark, Norway have tables on the IBKR Europe
  commission page [E1]; Germany and France in ibkr-global/equities.md (Fixed 0.05%, min EUR 3; see
  [../themes/ibkr-global/equities.md](../../themes/ibkr-global/equities.md)). Finland not found on that page (not
  verified). LRS: listed shares/ETFs allowed; no derivatives or margin abroad.
- Note: MEUD, VEUR and IMEU are **pan-Europe** (UK about 22%, Switzerland about 15%), not
  eurozone. CSEMU is the eurozone-only fund.
