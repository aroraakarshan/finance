# Eurozone - expected returns, performance, rates and risks

Written 2026-11-15. Part of [Eurozone (and wider Europe)](../eurozone.md). Sources [E#]: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- Long-run forecasts are low for all non-US large caps (GMO -0.9% real) but better than US large.
- 2025 winners (defence +74.5%, banks +65.7%) have cooled; momentum is flat.
- ECB rate 2.50% and rising on the energy shock; France is the main political risk.
- Build Europe in steps; do not chase defence.

## 7. Risks

- France: debt, 2027 election, weakest big economy [E14][E6]. Energy: oil back above USD 100
  [E14]; bond yields at multi-decade highs in Sep 2026 [E16].
- Crowded 2025 winners (defence, banks) are the most fragile. ASML 9.4% of CSEMU [E2].
  No capital controls.
