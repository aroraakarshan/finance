# Global bonds (outside US and India)

Market research, not advice. Written 2026-11-15.

## Summary
- Bond yields are near the top of their 20-year range (UK 5.21%, Bund 3.43%, JGB 2.84%; Nov 2026), and JPM expects 5.3%/yr from global aggregate bonds.
- But bond ETFs may be taxed always short-term at 35.88% for this user, and he is already 81% in USD assets: post-tax about 5.6%/yr in INR vs about 9.4% for global equity held 24 months.
- What to do: global equity UCITS held 24 months or Indian tax-free options give more after tax; if a bond sleeve is wanted for stability, one accumulating USD-hedged line (AGGU) or short T-bills (IB01) is enough. Ask a CA about AF s.76 first.

and RBI bonds: see [opp-fixed-income-cash](../themes/opp-fixed-income-cash.md). IBKR bond rules,

## Verdict per type (conditional; the user decides)

| Type | Best instrument | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Developed govt bonds (UK, Bund, JGB, Canada, Australia) | AGGU (acc, USD hedged) | Neutral, small | Yields at 15-20y highs and JPM 5.3% is good, but 35.88% tax and user already has 81% USD |
| Inflation-linked (TIPS, linkers) | ITPS (acc) | Neutral | Real yields 2-2.9% are high; same tax drag |
| Global agg unhedged | AGGG | Skip | Adds EUR/JPY risk and distributes at slab; hedged acc line is cleaner |
| IG corporate | LQDE | Skip | Spread 0.89% is thin; distributions taxed yearly |
| High yield | IHYU | Skip | Equity-like risk at bond tax rate; spread 3.2% near low end |
| EM USD sovereign | IEMB | Neutral-small | Highest Vanguard forecast, but distributing and credit risk |
| EM local currency | SEML | Skip | Currency risk on top of INR risk; local yields not verified |
