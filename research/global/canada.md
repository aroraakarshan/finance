## 8. Dividend withholding / DTAA

Canada withholds 25% on dividends to non-residents; India-Canada treaty rate 15% only for 10%+
company holdings, otherwise 25% (PwC tax summaries, reviewed 2026-06-12 [C11]), so an Indian
individual pays 25%, FTC in India, dividend at slab. Irish CCAU: Canadian tax is suffered inside
the fund, nothing to him; accumulating = no Indian tax till sale; LTCG 12.5% after 24 months.
