- Estate tax: Singapore-listed and Irish/Luxembourg funds are not US-situs. Singapore estate duty

## 5. Growth drivers

- Banks: high rates kept net interest margins and dividends high; banks are over half the index.
- Economy: GDP +5.4% y/y in Q3 2026, led by AI-related manufacturing orders; MAS expects growth
  to moderate through 2027 [S16].
- REITs: rate-sensitive; DBS analysts (2 Sep 2026) saw a multi-year-high yield gap in favour of
  S-REITs over banks (Edge Singapore / Business Times headlines).
- Little "growth" exposure: few tech names except Sea.

## 6. Currency

- MAS manages the S$ against a basket (S$NEER) with a slowly appreciating band; on 14 Oct 2026 it
  left policy unchanged, with the S$NEER in the upper half of the band; core inflation 2.1% in
  Jul-Aug 2026 [S16].
- USD/SGD 1.288 on 14 Nov 2026; SGD +1.0%/yr vs USD over 5 years. Vs INR: SGD +7.2% in 1 year,
  +5.9%/yr over 5 years (INR 73.2 per SGD) [S14]. The most stable currency of these markets.

## 7. Risks

- Concentration: three banks about half the index; rate cuts would squeeze margins.
- Valuation: P/B at a 15-year high after a 24%/yr 3-year run.
- REITs: refinancing costs if rates stay high; US 10-year yields drove the 2026 REIT slump [S9].
- Small, export-led market; exposed to China/AI trade cycle. Political risk low; no capital controls.

## 8. Dividend withholding and DTAA

Singapore withholds 0% on dividends to non-residents (one-tier system) [S17], so no FTC needed;
S-REIT distribution treatment for non-resident individuals: not verified.
