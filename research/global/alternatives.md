# Alternatives: everything else an Indian resident can invest in

Written 2026-11-15 by `finance-research`. Beyond `research/themes/investment-universe.md` and the
rest of `research/global/`. Proposals only: the user decides. Details and dated sources in the
three group notes; **UNREGULATED** items are marked. Fit 1-5 for this user (salaried, Rs 4.21 Cr,
35.88% slab, about 43% in two US stocks, wants growth). Tax: one line from `finance-tax` in section 3.

## Summary
- Worth using: a gold ETF (within the 5% gold plan) and small finance bank FDs up to 8.25%, under Rs 5 L per bank.
- Regulated but weak for you: SM REITs, bond platforms, P2P, private credit AIFs, ULIP and endowment plans, annuities now.
- Unregulated, skip: farmland, invoice discounting, pre-IPO platforms, digital gold, collectibles.
- Angel funds are closed to you (not an accredited investor). None of these beats a broad equity index for growth.

## 2. Bottom line (proposal)
None of these is a growth asset that beats a broad equity index for this user. Worth using: a gold
ETF (within the gold plan in `research/global/README.md`) and small finance bank FDs under Rs 5 L
per bank for short-term cash. Everything above fit 2 is optional; everything at fit 1 is either
closed to him, unregulated, or returns less than its risk after the 35.88% slab. Term life cover
(not an investment) is still needed for the US estate tax (`research/themes/us-estate-tax.md`).

## 3. Tax (one line per item; finance-tax)
One line per item in `tax/property-and-alternatives-2026-11.md` (finance-tax, 2026-11-15; 21 rows, 9 flags;
"unv" = unverified). Main points: interest-type returns (FDs, P2P, OBPP bonds, invoice discounting,
GIFT USD FD) at slab 35.88%; MLDs always short-term at slab; SM REIT probably taxed like a business
trust (unv); ULIP with premium above Rs 2.5 L taxed like listed equity; art and collectibles are capital
assets (Schedule AL), long-term after 24 months; tokenised assets as VDA at 35.88% with no set-off.
