# ASEAN-5 (Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Philippines)

Written 2026-11-15. Market research, not advice; the user decides.
User context: 81% of Rs 2.23 Cr in QCOM + MSFT; IBKR cash account; prefers non-US UCITS.

## Summary
- ASEAN is cheap and fast-growing but small and noisy; for this user it is a neutral, minor slot.
- Hold it through a broad EM / EM Asia UCITS ETF (e.g. CSEMAS, 0.20%), not single countries.
- Optional: a small Vietnam satellite (XVTD, <=1-2%) for FTSE inclusion flows to Sep 2027.
- Skip Indonesia until MSCI decides on its status; skip Malaysia and the Philippines.
