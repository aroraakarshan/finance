## 2. Access for an Indian resident

- **Listed infra / listed PE UCITS ETFs**: regulated fund units -> allowed under LRS
  (`research/themes/ibkr-global/rules.md` section 1). Bought at IBKR on LSE/Xetra/SIX.
- **BDCs**: listed US shares -> allowed, but US-situs (US estate tax above USD 60k,
  `us-estate-tax.md`) and 25% treaty withholding on dividends (`tax/investment-types.md` US row);
  whether part of BDC payouts is exempt "interest-related dividends": nv.
- **US interval funds**: not exchange-listed; sale to non-US persons via IBKR: nv. Skip.
- **Unlisted foreign startups**: a resident individual buying even one unlisted foreign share
  makes an **ODI** (not portfolio investment); allowed within LRS USD 250k only in an operating
  company, not in financial services, real estate or gambling; needs fresh remittance through an
  Indian bank (not idle LRS cash abroad), Form FC within 30 days, UIN, arm's-length valuation,
  yearly reporting [P2, P3]. SPV/fund route (e.g. Vested SPVs; SpaceX ticket USD 20,000, others
  under USD 10,000) counts as LRS fund investment [P3]. Primary RBI OI Rules text: nv (rbidocs
  CAPTCHA).
- **Indian angel platforms (LetsVenture, AngelList India, IPV)**: angel funds are SEBI Cat I AIFs;
  since 2025 they take money **only from accredited investors**; minimum corpus/investment rules
  removed [P4]. Accreditation needs income >= Rs 2 Cr or net worth >= Rs 7.5 Cr etc.
  (`research/themes/india-beyond-mf/global-and-private.md` section 15). User (Rs 2.23 Cr total) likely does not
  qualify (income test nv). Direct angel cheques via syndicates: platform minimums nv.
- **Indian AIFs**: Rs 1 Cr minimum (Rs 10 L for some with accreditation); see
  `india-beyond-mf/global-and-private.md` section 15. Below his Indian assets (~Rs 26 L).
- **GIFT City funds**: 7 retail outbound schemes, all equity FoFs (S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, MSCI
  World/EM, global active, Greater China), USD 500-10,000 minimum, count against LRS, ~2% FX
  spread each way [P5]; ABSL added EM and India FoFs from USD 500 (2026-10-18) [P6]. No retail
  GIFT infra or PE fund found (nv). See `india-beyond-mf/global-and-private.md` section 14.

## 5. Risks

- Listed infra: rate-sensitive (utilities 53%); 61% US, so not a big escape from US exposure.
- Listed PE: really a bet on US alternative managers (finance 98%); fell 80% in 2008-09; high
  beta to credit markets.
- BDCs/private credit: credit losses, leverage; US-situs.
- Startups/angel: most fail; ET case (2026-06-08): an investor with 25+ startups saw three fail
  completely [P4]; no liquidity; ODI paperwork; FX and valuation risk.
- Tax: UCITS ETFs 24 m / 12.5% LTCG / slab, dist at slab, acc no tax till sale; AIF Cat I/II
  pass-through; unlisted foreign shares 24 m / 12.5% (tax/investment-types.md sections 1-2, CA).
