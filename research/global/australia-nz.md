## 6. Rates and currency

- RBA raised the cash rate to 4.85% on 3 Nov 2026: inflation broad and elevated (seen above the
  2-3% band until late 2027), house prices have fallen significantly, unemployment rising [A12].
  Higher rates hurt banks' loan books and housing; they support the AUD.
- AUD: 1.476 per USD (AUD/USD about 0.68) on 14 Nov 2026; AUD +3.7% vs USD in 1 year, -1.5%/yr over
  5 years. Vs INR: AUD +10.1% in 1 year, +3.3%/yr over 5 years (INR 63.8 per AUD) [A11]. AUD fell
  3% in one week in mid-Nov 2026 (StoneX headline 14 Nov 2026) - a commodity/risk currency.
- NZD: 1.81 per USD; -4.8% vs USD YTD. Vs INR: +3.7% in 1 year, flat over 5 years [A11].

## 7. Risks

- Concentration: top 10 = 65%; banks + miners = 70% of the market.
- China: biggest buyer of iron ore; a China slowdown (see china-hong-kong.md) hits miners.
- Housing downturn plus rate hikes -> bank bad debts.
- Commodity and currency swings move together (AUD falls when miners fall).
- Political and capital-control risk: low.

## 8. Dividend withholding and DTAA

Australia: franked dividends 0%; unfranked 30%, cut to 15% under the India DTAA; "conduit foreign
income" exempt [A13]. Franking credits themselves are not refunded to non-residents - ATO page
blocked (403), not verified from ATO. NZ: 15% on fully imputed dividends to <10% holders, else
30%, India DTAA 15% [A14]. FTC in India up to the Indian tax on it.
