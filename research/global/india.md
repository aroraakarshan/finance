# India vs EM and world - short global comparison

Written 2026-11-15. Market research, not advice; the user decides. Sources [I#] at the end.
Detail on Indian valuations, sectors, flows and funds is in
[../themes/opp-india-market.md](../themes/opp-india-market.md); this note only places India
against emerging markets (EM) and the world, in USD.

## Summary
- India trades about 50-60% above EM (P/E, CAPE), level with the world; the premium has shrunk.
- In USD India lagged EM and the world over 1-5 years; INR loses about 3-5%/yr vs USD.
- Verdict: neutral. Size India money to INR goals; the bigger issue is QCOM + MSFT concentration.
- For diversification, broad world or EM UCITS ETFs do more than extra India.

- India trades about 50% above EM on trailing P/E and 60% above on CAPE (my arithmetic), about
  level with the world index and below the US. ETF view (Yahoo, 2026-11-15 [I2]): INDA P/E 23.1,
  EEM 14.3, ACWI 20.6.
- The premium has shrunk: India CAPE 27.9 vs 35.1 a year earlier; year-end range 2015-2025 was
  20.2-35.1 [I1]. Siblis EM forward P/E (8.8) looks low next to its trailing 13.8: cross-check.
- India's weight in MSCI EM fell to 11.29% after the Aug 2026 review (Moneycontrol headline,
  2026-09-03), as Taiwan/Korea AI stocks grew.

## 4. INR

- USD/INR 94.24 on 2026-11-14 (Yahoo [I2]); record low past 94.29 on 2026-03-27 during the Iran
  war oil spike (NDTV/Reuters headlines). INR lost 6.1% vs USD in 1y, 4.9%/yr over 5y, 3.4%/yr over 10y.
- So an Indian stock must beat a USD asset by about 3-5%/yr in INR terms just to tie in USD.
  For a resident who spends in INR, a falling INR raises the INR value of foreign holdings.

## 5. Why home bias (and why not), for this user

For holding India:
- Goals, spending and salary are in INR: INR assets match INR needs and avoid FX swings.
- Indian funds are simple to hold and tax (equity MF: 12.5% LTCG after 12 months over Rs 1.25 L);
  no LRS limit, no Schedule FA, no US estate tax.
- India's earnings growth is higher than most markets ([opp-india-market/valuations.md](../themes/opp-india-market/valuations.md), earnings).

Against more India:
- The user's job and salary are already in India; adding Indian stocks adds to the same economy.
- India is about 11% of EM (above) and a small slice of world indices, yet trades at a premium.
- Recent USD returns trail EM and world (section 3), and the INR trend works against it.

Position now: Rs 26 L Indian MFs and metals = about 12% of Rs 2.23 Cr; the rest is mostly US tech.
The problem is concentration in two US stocks, not too little India.

## 6. Verdict (global allocation view)

Neutral on India vs the world. Keep India money sized to INR goals (next 5-10 yrs of spending)
and add through the funds chosen in opp-india-market.md. For diversification away from
QCOM + MSFT, broad world or EM UCITS ETFs do more per rupee than extra India, because
India already sits in the user's income and spending. Revisit if India's CAPE premium to EM
falls well below its current ~60%.
