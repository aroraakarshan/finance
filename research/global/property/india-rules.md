# Buying property abroad: Indian rules for a resident (Nov 2026)

Written 2026-11-15. Market research, not legal or tax advice. Every rule has a source tag [R#]/[T#]
(list at the end, all read 2026-11-15). "not verified" = no primary text read. "Reading" = our
interpretation of the text, not RBI's words. LRS basics, platforms and TCS on investing:
[platforms](../platforms.md) s.1-2; idle foreign cash and the 180-day rule: [cash](../cash-and-currencies.md) s.1.
US estate tax on US real estate: [us-estate-tax](../../themes/us-estate-tax.md) (US real estate is US-situs).

## Summary

- You may buy property abroad with LRS money (USD 250k each per FY; resident relatives can pool), or with rent/RSU money already abroad.
- No loan: no Indian bank loan, no foreign mortgage; only up to USD 250k from a relative abroad. TCS is 20% above Rs 10 L (a tax credit).
- Rent and sale money: reinvest it or bring it home within 180 days.
- Report it in Schedule FA every year: missing property means a Rs 10 L Black Money penalty (the Rs 20 L relief does not apply).
- To do: each co-owner pays from their own account; get a local lawyer for succession and probate.

## 1. Can a resident buy property abroad? Yes - from these sources only

Old FEMA 7(R)/2015 (21 Jan 2016) was superseded on 22 Aug 2022 by the Overseas Investment (OI)
Rules/Regulations 2022 [R4 para 2]; the rule is now OI Rules rule 21 [R4 para 25], read directly
[R8 r.21]. RBI's FAQ [R3, 2023-04-06] copies rule 21(2) word for word.

| Source of money / route | Allowed? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Own money sent under LRS (purpose: acquisition of immovable property abroad) | Yes | r.21(2)(ii)(c) [R8]; LRS MD para 6(ii) [R1] |
| Income or sale proceeds of other assets acquired abroad under FEMA (not ODI) - e.g. rent, dividends, sale of listed shares or employer RSUs held abroad | Yes | r.21(2)(ii)(e) [R8]; ESOP/RSU shares up to 10% without control count as OPI [R4 1(ix)(f)] |
| Foreign currency in an RFC account | Yes | r.21(2)(ii)(b) [R8] |
| Inheritance from a person resident outside India | Yes | r.21(2)(ii)(a) [R8] |
| Jointly with a relative who is resident outside India | Yes | r.21(2)(ii)(d) [R8]: no condition on how the resident pays his share (old FEMA 7(R) "no outflow of funds from India" [R5] is gone); funding route open - legal/questions.md C11 |
| Gift, inheritance or purchase from a resident who acquired it lawfully | Yes | r.21(2)(i) [R8] |
| Gift from a person resident outside India | NO - no such route in r.21(2) (was allowed under old FEMA 7(R) [R5]) | [R8 r.21(2)] |
| Property bought while non-resident, or inherited from a non-resident | May keep, own, sell | FEMA s.6(4) via FAQ Q1 [R3] |
| Lease of 5 years or less | Outside the restriction | r.21(1) proviso (iii) [R8] |

Reading: buying from RSU sale proceeds or rent already abroad needs no new remittance, so it does
not use the LRS limit or attract TCS; this is not stated in the text - confirm with the bank.

## 2. LRS limit and family pooling

- USD 250,000 per person per FY (Apr-Mar), all purposes together; minors included (guardian signs
  Form A2); not for HUFs, firms, trusts [R1 para 1, 3]. Once used, bringing money back does not
  restore the limit [R2 Q8]. Above USD 250k needs RBI approval [R1 para 7].
- Pooling for property: "Remittances for acquiring immovable property outside India from a person
  resident outside India, may be consolidated in respect of relatives if such relatives, being
  persons resident in India, comply with the terms and conditions of the Scheme" [R8 r.21(2)(ii)(c) proviso; R2 Q5].
  Others: club only as co-owners [R1 para 4]. "Relative" = Companies Act s.2(77) [R3].
- So 3 resident relatives = up to USD 750k in one FY, each with own funds, PAN, Form A2, name on title (reading).
- Funds must belong to the remitter [R1 B.8; R2 Q12]; a resident may not gift foreign currency to
  another resident's account abroad [R1 para 4; R2 Q2(7)]. Gifting rupees in India first to a family
  member who then remits: not verified; income-tax clubbing may apply (ask finance-tax).

## 3. TCS

- Income-tax Act 2025 s.394 Table Sl.7 (old 1961 s.206C(1G)): LRS remittance above Rs 10 lakh per
  FY -> 20% TCS for purposes other than education/medical (property = 20%); education/medical cut
  5% -> 2% from 2026-04-01 [T1, Finance Bill 2026 memo, Clause 73; Bill text, enacted Act not
  read]. Threshold per remitter (reading). Example: Rs 2 Cr in one FY -> 20% x Rs 1.9 Cr = Rs 38 L TCS, a tax credit (Form 27D) [platforms](../platforms.md) s.2; each family member has own Rs 10 L (reading).

## 4. Borrowing to buy

- Indian bank loan for the purchase: NOT allowed. "Banks should not extend any kind of credit
  facilities to resident individuals to facilitate capital account remittances" [R1 para 12, B.10;
  R2 Q16 - credit is allowed only for current-account remittances].
- Foreign mortgage from a bank abroad: B&L Regs reg 3 bars a resident from borrowing in foreign
  exchange "save as otherwise provided"; reg 4.B lists the exceptions and has no home-loan route
  for individuals [R7 reg 3, 4.B, as amended to 2026-02-09]. The only individual route is reg
  4.B(v): up to USD 250,000 from relatives outside India, on RBI terms (terms not verified).
- An owner may "create a charge" on the property only "in accordance with the Act or the rules or
  regulations" [R8 r.21(2)(iv)(b)], so a mortgage still needs a permitted loan. Open points: legal/questions.md C12.

## 5. Rent and sale proceeds: keep or bring back?

- 180 days: a resident individual must surrender received/realised/unused foreign exchange within
  180 days of receipt [R6 reg 7]; regs 5-6 (7/90/60 days) apply to non-individuals only [R6].
- "Unless reinvested": comes from LRS MD para 17 ("can retain, reinvest the income") [R1] and OI
  Rules Sch. III para 1(2)(ii) ("OPI, including by way of reinvestment") [R8], not from reg 7.
- So rent can be reinvested abroad (e.g. into listed ETFs) or brought home within 180 days; idle
  rent in a foreign account past 180 days is a breach (reading of the same text).
- Paying local costs (tax, repairs) out of rent: not addressed - not verified (legal/questions.md C13).
- Sale proceeds: same 180-day rule unless reinvested [R1 para 17]; may buy another property abroad [R8 r.21(2)(ii)(e)].

## 6. Reporting

- Form A2 with each remittance, through one designated AD branch; PAN mandatory [R1 para 15-16].
- Schedule FA (calendar year): ITR-2 AY 2026-27 schema has `DetailsImmovableProperty` (6th FA table,
  Table C in form order - reading) with country, ZIP, address, ownership (direct/beneficial/
  beneficiary), date, total investment, income derived, schedule where taxed [T2, schema v1.2,
  2026-08-13]; Schedule AL has one too. Rent also in HP and FSI, foreign tax in TR (ask finance-tax).
- Black Money Act: Rs 10 lakh penalty for a missing or wrong foreign-asset disclosure (s.42, s.43)
  [tax/migration-indmoney-to-ibkr.md s.5]. The Rs 20 lakh relief from 1 Oct 2024 excludes immovable
  property for the PENALTY (s.42/43) [T3] and for prosecution (s.49/50, "other than immovable
  property") [T1, Clause 144]. So any missed property = Rs 10 L penalty. No BMA time limit [migration s.6].

## 7. Indian tax (one line each; not in tax/investment-types.md -> ask finance-tax)

- Foreign rent: slab rate as house property, 30% standard deduction and interest deduction (not verified).
- Foreign tax on rent/gain: credit under the treaty (AF s.159, r.76, Form 44) [tax/investment-types.md].
- Sale: long-term after 24 months at 12.5% (as other foreign assets, tax/investment-types.md); rest not verified.
- s.54/54F reinvestment exemption: new house must be in India (not verified here); ask finance-tax.

## 8. Practical

- Bank documents: RBI does not prescribe them; each AD bank sets its own list under FEMA s.10(5)
  [R1 B.2-4; R4 27(3)]. Account >= 1 year old [R1 B.7]; expect Form A2, PAN,
  sale agreement, seller bank details, title deed or valuation (not verified, ask the bank).
- Valuation for Schedule FA / ITR: cost in INR at the rule's FX rate (ask finance-tax).
- Succession: Indian Succession Act s.5 - succession to immovable property follows the law of the
  place where it is (lex situs), so the foreign country's law and probate decide [C1]. Whether Part
  II (s.5) applies to Hindus: not verified (legal/questions.md C11-C14). Nominee: not verified.
  US property: US estate tax above USD 60k [us-estate-tax]. RBI LRS sub-limit proposal: [platforms] s.1.

## Do and do not

- Do send each family member's share from their own account, in their own name, and put each on the title.
- Do use the "immovable property abroad" purpose and one designated branch; keep Form A2 and 27D.
- Do reinvest rent and sale money or bring it home within 180 days.
- Do report the property in Schedule FA every year, even with no income.
- Do not take an Indian bank loan or overdraft to fund the purchase.
- Do not take a foreign mortgage without RBI approval (B&L Regs reg 3, 4.B).
- Do not route money through another resident's foreign account or gift FX between residents.
- Do not leave rent idle abroad past 180 days.
- Do not rely on the Rs 20 L Black Money relief (penalty or prosecution): it excludes property.
