# Oman property

## Summary
Foreigners can buy only in ITCs and listed new cities (Ministry of Housing, 28 Jun 2026). A
new Owner residence permit (ROP 87/2026) has no minimum per IMI Daily; Sultan Haitham City units
give residency from OMR 50k (Rs 1.22 Cr). Sale prices and yields could not be verified.
Verdict: skip, or maybe as a cheap residency option.

Back to the index: [Gulf property](../gulf.md).

## Details

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, but only in designated places. In June 2026 news reports said the
whole country had opened. The Ministry of Housing said on 28 Jun 2026 that ownership is still
limited to designated places (Gulf News):
- Integrated Tourism Complexes (ITCs): Al Mouj Muscat, Muscat Hills, Muscat Bay, Jebel Sifah
  and others.
- New cities: Sultan Haitham City, Al Thuraya, Al Jabal Al Aali.
- Neighbourhoods such as Surouh.

There is a source conflict on the type of title. A Muscat lawyer (Times of Oman, Jul 2026) and
EnterpriseAM describe ITC title as freehold (usufruct of up to 99 years only in Musandam).
Economic Times and Times of India (Sep 2025) call ITC title usufruct of up to 99 years.

Outside ITCs, foreigners can hold usufruct only. Conditions (ET, Sep 2025):
- At least 2 years living in Oman.
- One unit per buyer, in a building of 4 or more floors.
- Minimum OMR 45k in Muscat.
- At most 40% foreign-owned units per building, and at most 20% for one nationality.

The user, living in India, cannot use the usufruct route.

**Prices.**
- Price per sq m for Muscat ITCs: not verified (no GPG Oman page; the Savills report gives rents
  only).
- National residential price index: 130.2 in Q2 2026, about 30% above the 2018 base (NCSI via
  EnterpriseAM, 30 Sep 2026).
- Q1 index +15.9% year on year (Oman Observer via EnterpriseAM).
- Sultan Haitham City units qualify for residency from OMR 50k (Rs 1.22 Cr).

**Yields.** Rents for a 2-bed apartment: Al Mouj OMR 664 a month (Rs 1.62 lakh), Qurum OMR 406
(Savills Q2 2026). Gross and net yield: not verified, because I found no sale prices for the
same units.

**Costs.** Registration fee 3% of value, cut from 5% in Nov 2020 (Oman Observer). It was not
re-checked for 2026. Agent and service charges: not verified.

**Tax.** There is no tax on individuals today. Personal income tax starts on 1 Jan 2028: 5% on
income above OMR 42k for tax residents. Leasing income and gains on real estate sales are listed
as taxable sources (PwC, 7 Jul 2026). How non-resident foreigners will be treated is still
waiting for the executive regulations: not verified. India-Oman DTAA exists (not re-checked).

**Visas.**

| Route | Property needed | Notes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Owner" residence permit (ROP Decision 87/2026, from 22 Jun 2026) | No minimum | Renewable every 6-12 months for as long as you own the property; off-plan counts | IMI Daily, 24 Jun 2026 |
| ITC residency | Completed unit and OMR 200k | Conflicts with IMI's "no minimum" | EnterpriseAM |
| Investor Residency (Silver / Golden) | OMR 250k (Rs 6.1 Cr) / OMR 500k (Rs 12.2 Cr) | 5 years / 10 years | IMI Daily |

**Liquidity and risk.** The market is small: OMR 1.43bn of transactions in H1 2026 (Savills).
"Not there yet" for offshore investors (EnterpriseAM). Resale is thin. The ITC rules are
complex, and a plot must be built on within 4 years.

**Verdict: skip, or maybe as a cheap residency option.** Minimum about Rs 1.3 Cr (OMR 50k
Sultan Haitham City unit plus 3%). The rental yield cannot be checked.
