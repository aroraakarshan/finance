## FX and currency trend

FX used: USD/INR 94.03 (2026-11-13, brief). AED/INR 25.60 (yfinance AEDINR=X close
2026-11-13). All four currencies are pegged to the US dollar, so the rest are derived from the
peg: SAR 3.75/USD -> Rs 25.07; QAR 3.64/USD -> Rs 25.83; OMR 0.3845/USD -> Rs 244.55.
Pegs are the official ones but were not re-checked today. GPG = Global Property Guide.

Currency trend (all four, through the USD peg): AED/INR 18.39 (2016-11-14) -> 20.28
(2021-11) -> 25.60 (2026-11-13), from yfinance. That is +39% in 10 years (about 3.4%/yr)
and +26% in 5 years (about 4.8%/yr). Over time, holding Gulf currency has protected against
rupee weakness about as well as holding US dollars. The risk to watch is the peg itself. It
has held for decades, but the 2026 war is putting pressure on the Gulf economies (see [UAE](uae.md)).

## War background

Background for all four: the US-Iran war began in late February 2026, there was a ceasefire on
8 April, and the conflict is still not settled. Shipping through the Strait of Hormuz has been
disrupted (GPG UAE, Oct 2026). Khaleej Times reported on 13 Nov 2026 that drones were launched
toward Bahrain.
