# Qatar property

## Summary
Freehold in designated zones (The Pearl, Lusail, West Bay Lagoon). Pearl 1-bed Rs 4.51 Cr,
gross yield 4.99% average, prices flat, and non-residents pay 10% tax on gains. Property of QAR
730k (Rs 1.89 Cr) gives a 1-year renewable residence permit. Verdict: skip.

Back to the index: [Gulf property](../gulf.md).

## Details

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, freehold in designated zones: The Pearl, Lusail, West Bay Lagoon
and others (IMI Daily; EnterpriseAM). Usufruct of 99 years is available in other listed areas.
I did not re-check the official list of zones.

**Prices.**
- Average apartment: QAR 12,865 per sq m, -2% year on year (Knight Frank Q4 2025, Gulf Times
  31 Jan 2026). That is about Rs 3.32 lakh per sq m.
- Pearl, per sq m: Viva Bahriya QAR 14,630; Porto Arabia QAR 11,787.
- The Pearl, studio / 1-bed / 2-bed: USD 329k / 479.8k / 658.1k = Rs 3.09 / 4.51 / 6.19 Cr (GPG,
  Sep 2026).
- Lusail figures are paywalled.

**Yields.** Gross: Qatar average 4.99% in Q3 2026, down from 5.17% in Q1. Pearl 1-bed 5.49%,
studio 6.26% (GPG). Apartment rents were -7% in Q4 2025 (Knight Frank). Net yield: not verified.

**Costs.** Registration fee: not verified (often quoted at 0.25%). Agent fee: not verified.
There is no annual property tax.

**Tax.** Rent received by individuals not running a taxable activity is not taxed. Capital
gains are exempt for residents, but Qatar-source gains of non-residents are taxed at 10% (PwC,
reviewed 17 Sep 2026). That 10% tax on sale would apply to this user. India-Qatar DTAA exists
(not re-checked).

**Visas** (IMI Daily, updated 6 Nov 2026).

| Property in the zones | Residency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| QAR 730k (USD 200k, Rs 1.89 Cr) | 1-year renewable residence permit | |
| USD 1m (QAR 3.65m, Rs 9.4 Cr) | Permanent residency | Capped at 100 a year; Arabic requirement |

**Liquidity and risk.** Prices are "at best stable", mostly old stock changing hands among the
same buyers, and the market has "flatlined" (EnterpriseAM, Jul 2026). Sales did rise: 2025
value +43.5% (Knight Frank), and July 2026 sales were QAR 2.25bn (Gulf Times, 14 Aug 2026).
Effect of the 2026 war on Qatar prices: not verified.

**Verdict: skip.** Minimum about Rs 1.95 Cr (the QAR 730k residency threshold plus costs; a
Pearl studio is Rs 3.09 Cr). Weak price growth and a 10% tax on gains.
