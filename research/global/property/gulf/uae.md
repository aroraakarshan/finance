# UAE property (Dubai, Abu Dhabi)

## Summary
Dubai is the only liquid Gulf market, but prices fell after the war (ValuStrat -4.5% a year in Q3
2026), rents are falling, and about 525,000 homes are due in 2027-2030. The minimum is about Rs 4.1
Cr all-in, 1.8 times the user's Rs 2.23 Cr. Verdict: maybe, not now; only a ready resale flat
after the 2027 supply wave makes sense. Golden Visa needs AED 2m (Rs 5.12 Cr).

Back to the index: [Gulf property](../gulf.md).

## Details

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, freehold, but only in designated freehold zones (Dubai examples:
Downtown, Marina, Palm, Business Bay, JVC; Abu Dhabi has investment zones such as Saadiyat
and Yas). I did not re-check the official zone lists. There is no surcharge for foreign
buyers, and there are no quotas.

**Prices.** These come from GPG's free tier; the cheaper Dubai areas (JVC etc.) are paywalled.

**What the war did to prices.** The REIDIN Dubai sales index was -1.27% year on year in Sep
2026, compared with +13% at the end of 2025. ValuStrat's Dubai index was -4.5% year on year in
Q3 2026, with the largest monthly fall (-6%) in March 2026. Palm, Business Bay and Downtown
apartments fell 7-9% in Q2 alone (CBRE). Q2 sales were down 29% in number and 43% in value
(CBRE). Abu Dhabi was still +11.2% year on year in Sep 2026 (REIDIN). The official Dubai index
(Dubai Data and Statistics) showed +9.81% for 2025, but I found no 2026 reading. Super-prime
sales held up better (The National, 13 Nov 2026).

**Oversupply, 2026-28.** About 525,000 homes are due in Dubai in 2027-2030 (Savills, Q3
2026), and S&P expects the housing stock to grow about 20% in two years (Khaleej Times, 7 Oct
2026). Only about 33,000 of the 131,000 units planned for 2026 had been delivered by
September (ValuStrat). The delays help prices in the short term but push supply into
2027-28.

**Off-plan vs ready.** Off-plan was 76% of Dubai sales in Q2 (ValuStrat). Off-plan buyers pay
in instalments, earn no rent until handover, and handovers are slipping. Ready-home sales rose
18.5% quarter on quarter in Q3. In a falling market, a ready resale flat gives rent from day
one and can be bought at a discount.

**Yields and running costs.**
- Gross yield: UAE average 4.94% in Q2 2026, down from 5.45% in Q4 2025. Downtown 1-bed 5.72%,
  studio 6.20% (GPG).
- Rents are falling: Dubai -6.81% year on year in Sep 2026 (REIDIN). Abu Dhabi has frozen rents
  since June 2026 (ADREC).
- Service charges: AED 8-25 per sq ft a year, paid by the owner (Excel Properties, Jul 2026).
  That is roughly 0.5-1% of value a year. There is no annual property tax.
- Net yield: about 3.5-4.5% after service charges and 1 month of vacancy. This is my estimate,
  not verified.

**Purchase costs.**
- Dubai: DLD transfer fee 4%, admin AED 580, trustee AED 4,000 + VAT, agent 2%. Total about
  6-6.5% (Excel Properties, Jul 2026; Gulf News Jan 2026 confirms the 4%).
- Abu Dhabi: 2% transfer fee (not verified).

**Tax.** The UAE has no personal income tax, no tax on rent and no capital gains tax. Corporate
tax at 9% does not count personal real estate investment income (PwC, reviewed 9 Sep 2026).
India-UAE DTAA: income from the property can be taxed where it sits, but India taxes its
residents on worldwide income. Since the UAE tax is zero, there is no credit, so the full Indian
tax is due.

| 2-year investor visa | No minimum for a sole owner since Apr 2026; AED 400k each for joint owners | | Gulf News |
| Retirement visa | AED 1m | Age 55+ | Gulf News |

**Liquidity and risk.** Dubai is the most liquid Gulf market (181,866 transactions and AED 412bn of sales in Jan-Oct 2026:
Emirates 24|7, 31 Oct 2026). Off-plan money goes into escrow. Fraud exists: a Dubai court
ordered AED 4.65m repaid in a Golden Visa property scam (IMI Daily, 9 Sep 2026). On the macro
side, the IMF expects UAE GDP of -3.9% in 2026, and the central bank raised its base rate to
3.90% on 17 Sep 2026.

**Verdict: maybe, not now.**
- A ready resale flat after the 2027 supply wave is the only case that makes sense.
- Minimum about Rs 4.1 Cr all-in (AED 1.5m plus about 6.5% costs). That is about 1.8 times the
  user's total Rs 2.23 Cr, so it does not fit today.
- The Golden Visa route needs Rs 5.4 Cr or more.
