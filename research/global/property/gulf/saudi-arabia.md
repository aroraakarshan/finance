# Saudi Arabia property

## Summary
Foreigners can buy since 2026, but non-residents only in zones (mostly PIF mega-projects in
Riyadh, about 55 zones in Jeddah, AlUla; Makkah and Madinah Muslims only). Costs about 7% or more,
about 90% of zoned supply is off-plan, no mortgages for non-residents, and residency needs SAR 4m
(Rs 10.0 Cr). Verdict: skip.

## Details

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, since January 2026. The law entered into force in January 2026, and
the zones and the Saudi Properties portal went live after Cabinet approval on 23 Jun 2026 (JLL,
8 Sep 2026).
- A non-resident can buy only inside zones in Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah and AlUla.
  Outside these five cities, zones do not apply.
- Riyadh zones are mostly PIF mega-projects: KAFD, Diriyah Gate, New Murabba, Qiddiya, Sedra,
  King Salman Park and others. Jeddah has about 55 zones.
- Makkah and Madinah: Muslims only, and only in designated areas (REGA via Saudi Gazette,
  7 Jul 2026).
- Before buying, you need a Ministry of Interior digital ID (from a Saudi mission abroad), a
  Saudi bank account and a Saudi mobile number. Payment goes through SAMA systems.
- One residence per foreign family.
- Fines go up to SAR 10m (Saudi Gazette, 3 Jul 2026).
- About 90% of zoned supply is off-plan, with 3-10 year build times. There is no mortgage for
  non-resident foreigners (EnterpriseAM, 10 Jul 2026).

**Prices.**
- Riyadh apartments: SAR 6,369 per sq m, +3.1% year on year (Knight Frank Q2 2026, Argaam
  16 Sep 2026). That is about Rs 1.60 lakh per sq m.
- GPG Riyadh (Oct 2026): 1-bed USD 238k (Rs 2.24 Cr), 2-bed USD 224.8k (Rs 2.11 Cr). A 1-bed
  priced above a 2-bed looks like a data problem.
- Jeddah: apartments +1.2% year on year. I did not find a price per sq m.
- National price index: +1.2% year on year in Q3 2026; Riyadh +4.6% (GASTAT, Saudi Gazette
  20 Oct 2026).

**Yields.** Gross: Saudi average 7.45% in Q3 2026; Riyadh 5.8% (2-bed 5.52%) (GPG). Riyadh has a
5-year freeze on all rents (EnterpriseAM, Jul 2026), so rental income cannot rise. Net yield:
not verified.

**Purchase costs.**
- 5% real estate transaction tax on every transfer.
- An extra 2% fee for non-Saudis in the Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah and Madinah zones (REGA, 7 Jul
  2026).
- Agent and legal fees: not verified.
- Total about 7% or more.

**Running costs.** Service charges in mega-projects: not verified. There is no annual property
tax on homes. The White Land Tax applies to undeveloped land, not to flats.

**Tax.** Saudi Arabia has no individual income tax. For a non-resident with no permanent
establishment, Saudi-source income falls under withholding tax rules (PwC, 29 Jul 2026). The
rate on rent paid to an individual: not verified. The 5% transaction tax applies on sale. A
separate capital gains tax for individuals: not verified. India-Saudi DTAA: a treaty exists
(PwC treaty table).

**Visas.** Premium Residency, "real estate owner" category: property worth SAR 4m or more (Rs
10.0 Cr). It lasts as long as you own the property (Deloitte, 25 Jan 2024; IMI Daily, updated
Nov 2026).

**Liquidity and risk.** There is no resale history in the zones, so you depend on developers
delivering. Residential transactions were -12% year on year in Q2 2026 (Knight Frank). Aramco
pipelines were attacked in the war (NYT, 3 Nov 2026).

**Verdict: skip.** It is new, off-plan heavy, has no mortgages, needs 7% or more in costs, and
the residency route needs Rs 10 Cr. Minimum budget about Rs 2.3 Cr for a Riyadh zone flat (GPG
2-bed USD 224.8k plus 7%).
