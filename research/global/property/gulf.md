# Gulf property for an Indian resident: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar

## Summary
Skip Gulf property for now. Every option would take 55-180% of the user's Rs 2.23 Cr and put it
into one illiquid flat in a war-affected, USD-pegged region; rent is tax-free locally but fully
taxed in India. Dubai is the only liquid market (maybe later: a ready resale flat after the
2027-28 supply wave, about Rs 4.1 Cr all-in). Saudi Arabia, Oman and Qatar: skip.

Research only, written 2026-11-15. Indian-side rules are in [india-rules.md](india-rules.md).
FX: USD/INR 94.03 (2026-11-13); AED/INR 25.60; SAR, QAR, OMR derived from their USD pegs.
