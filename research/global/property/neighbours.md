# Neighbours: buying a home in Mauritius, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, Maldives, Bangladesh

Research only, written 2026-11-15. Indian-side rules are in [india-rules.md](india-rules.md).

## Summary

- None of the six neighbours is worth buying in for you: verdict skip for all.
- Mauritius is the only real route (G+2 flat about Rs 1.3 Cr with duty, about 3% gross yield, 10% duty each side); its residence permit needs over USD 375k (Rs 3.53 Cr), above your net worth.
- Sri Lanka allows condos (from about Rs 75 L) but the rupee lost 40% vs INR in 10 years; Nepal, Bhutan and Maldives do not let an Indian own land; Bangladesh only via a company.
- Indian rules do not block buying in any neighbour; the FEMA list only stops their citizens buying in India.
- What to do: nothing here; keep the budget for options in the other property notes or financial assets.
