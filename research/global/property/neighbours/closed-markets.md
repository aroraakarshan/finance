# Neighbours: Nepal, Bhutan, Maldives, Bangladesh (no practical route)

Part of [Neighbours: buying a home in nearby countries](../neighbours.md), written 2026-11-15.

## Summary

- Nepal and Bhutan: an Indian individual cannot own land; the 1950 India-Nepal treaty reciprocity is not usable.
- Maldives: leasehold only (up to 99 yr); no visa via property; new Rasmale leases not final and contested.
- Bangladesh: only through a local company, with title-fraud complaints.
- What to do: skip all four. FX and price-source basis: [mauritius-sri-lanka.md](mauritius-sri-lanka.md).

Back to the index: [neighbours.md](../neighbours.md).

## Nepal

1. **Ownership**: foreign individuals cannot hold title to land; foreign investors under FITTA can own property
   only through a Nepali company, "title rests with the business ... rather than the foreign investor in an
   individual capacity" (US State Dept Investment Climate Statement 2025). The Nepali statute section: not verified.
2. **Indian citizens / 1950 treaty**: Article 7 of the 1950 India-Nepal Treaty promises, "on a reciprocal basis",
   the same privileges in "residence, ownership of property, participation in trade" (treaty text as reproduced on
   Wikipedia, read 15 Nov 2026). It is not self-executing: India itself requires RBI permission for Nepali citizens
   to buy property (RBI FAQ in [india-check.md](india-check.md)), and a High Court asked the Indian government in May 2026 whether Nepalis have
   any such right (Times of India, 22 May 2026, headline only). That Nepal lets Indian citizens register land:
   **no evidence found** - treat as no.
3. **Prices / yield** (for reference only): Numbeo Kathmandu USD 2,336/sq m centre (Rs 2.2 L), Pokhara USD 1,837
   (Rs 1.7 L); gross yield 2.3% Kathmandu, 1.6% Pokhara (Numbeo, Oct-Nov 2026).
4. **Costs, tax, visa**: not researched further - no legal route for an individual. No property visa.
5. **Currency**: NPR pegged at 1.6 per INR (yfinance -1% to -2% over 5-10 yr is quote noise).
6. **Risks**: nominee/benami holding in a Nepali's name has no legal protection - do not use.
7. **Verdict: skip (cannot buy).** Minimum budget: not applicable.

## Bhutan

1. **Ownership**: Land Act of Bhutan 2007 s.58 lists who may own land (monarchy, family, "individual person",
   government bodies, religious bodies, CSOs) and s.3(48) defines "Person means a citizen of Bhutan" (FAOLEX copy
   of the Act, read 15 Nov 2026). So **non-citizens, including Indians, cannot own land**; apartments sit on titled
   land, so no route either. India-Bhutan 1949/2007 treaties give no property right: not checked in detail.
2. **Prices, yield, costs, visa**: no foreign route, so not researched. Numbeo has no Thimphu data.
3. **Currency**: BTN is said to be pegged 1:1 to INR - not verified (yfinance has no BTN quote).
4. **Verdict: skip (cannot buy).** Minimum budget: not applicable.

## Maldives

1. **Ownership**: **no freehold**. Constitution Art 251 bars foreign ownership of land and caps leases to
   foreigners at 99 years; a 2015 amendment (freehold for USD 1bn+ projects, 70% reclaimed) was repealed in April
   2019 (Maldives Independent explainer 4 Nov 2026; US ICS 2025). A Sept 2025 cabinet plan to allow foreign
   ownership in "real estate development zones" has stalled (Adhadhu via Maldives Independent 26 Oct 2026).
2. **What is sold**: resort villas on leases that cannot outlast the resort lease (no visa), and from 2026-27
   99-year leases in Rasmale (Eagle Hills; term sheet signed 21 Sep 2026, not final, construction from Q1 2027 at
   the earliest) and Project Ayla (Noonu). Rasmale: 4% fee on each resale; each resale restarts a new lease
   (Maldives Independent 23 Sep and 4 Nov 2026). Politically contested (referendum demands, Nov 2026).
3. **Prices**: Hulhumale average MVR 7,500/sq ft = about USD 5,235/sq m (**Rs 4.9 L/sq m**; Dhauru via Maldives
   Independent, Nov 2026). Numbeo Male USD 2,264/sq m centre (Rs 2.1 L), USD 1,400 outside (Rs 1.3 L). Eagle
   Hills unit prices: not published.
4. **Yield**: Numbeo Male gross 10.1% centre / 12.4% outside - crowd data, inconsistent with Hulhumale prices,
   low confidence. Lease value decays toward zero at expiry.
5. **Costs and tax**: 4% transfer fee (Rasmale only). Income tax on rent and gains for a non-resident lessee:
   not verified. India-Maldives tax treaty coverage of property income: not verified.
6. **Visa**: Corporate Resident Visa needs USD 250k in a 5-year fixed deposit or listed business; the 2020 rules
   "make no mention of property"; the ministry has no record of a visa issued for real estate. Pearl Residence
   (Henley & Partners) still not launched; minimum not announced (Maldives Independent 4 Nov 2026).
7. **Currency**: MVR vs INR +23% over 5 yr, +34% over 10 yr (MVR held near USD; yfinance monthly).
8. **Liquidity/title**: no formal registration of leasehold titles on inhabited islands (US ICS 2025); opaque deals.
9. **Verdict: skip.** Minimum: not determinable (no published prices for foreign-eligible leases).

## Bangladesh (brief)

1. **Ownership**: foreigners can buy land only by setting up a local company recognised as a local entity; US
   citizens have complained of fraudulent land sales and extortionate title claims (US ICS 2025).
2. **Prices / yield**: Numbeo Dhaka USD 1,605/sq m centre (Rs 1.5 L), USD 790 outside (Rs 0.7 L); gross yield
   2.6-2.8%. Housing sales slumping (The Business Standard, 16 Jul 2026, headline).
3. **Tax**: rent taxed as income; individual slabs 0-30% for residents/Bangladeshi citizens (PwC, reviewed
   31 Jul 2026); foreign non-resident rate: not verified.
4. **Currency**: BDT vs INR -16% over 5 and 10 yr (yfinance). IMF programme tranche delayed in 2025 (US ICS).
5. **Verdict: skip (not practical).** Minimum: not applicable (company structure needed).
