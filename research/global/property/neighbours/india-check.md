# Neighbours: Indian rule check (FEMA and LRS)

Part of [Neighbours: buying a home in nearby countries](../neighbours.md), written 2026-11-15.

## Summary

- Indian rules do not bar a resident from buying in any neighbour; LRS excludes only FATF "non-cooperative" countries and listed terror-risk persons.
- The FEMA country list (Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, China, Nepal, Bhutan and others) only stops their citizens buying in India.
- The real barrier is the other country's own law (Nepal, Bhutan, Maldives).

## Details: does FEMA bar an Indian resident from buying in any neighbour?

- **No country bar on the outbound side.** RBI FAQ "Purchase of Immovable Property" (upd. 6 Apr 2023) Part I:
  a resident individual may buy property abroad with LRS money; no country list. RBI LRS FAQ (6 Apr 2023) Q2/Q11:
  only exclusions are capital remittances to FATF "non-cooperative countries and territories" and to listed
  terror-risk persons; "no restrictions towards remittances ... to Mauritius and Pakistan". Which list banks apply
  for "non-cooperative" today (FATF now publishes a "call for action" list, which has included Iran, North Korea
  and Myanmar): not verified with a bank - expect Iran and Myanmar remittances to be refused in practice.
- **The bar runs inbound only.** Same RBI FAQ Part II Q4: citizens of Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka,
  Afghanistan, China, Iran, Nepal, Bhutan, Macau, Hong Kong and North Korea cannot buy property in India (other
  than lease up to 5 years) without RBI permission; OCIs exempt. (NDI Rules 2019; the old FEMA 21(R)/2018 list.)
- Side note: LRS forex for private visits excludes Nepal and Bhutan (LRS FAQ Q3) - those use INR anyway.
- In practice the barrier is the other country's law: Nepal, Bhutan and Maldives do not let an Indian individual
  own land at all (below).
