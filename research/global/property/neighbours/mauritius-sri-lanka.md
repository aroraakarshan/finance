# Neighbours: Mauritius and Sri Lanka (the two that allow foreign buyers)

Part of [Neighbours: buying a home in nearby countries](../neighbours.md), written 2026-11-15.

## Summary

- Mauritius: only EDB schemes or G+2 flats from MUR 6m (about Rs 1.3 Cr with 10% duty); gross yield about 3%; residence permit needs over USD 375k (Rs 3.53 Cr). Skip unless a lifestyle buy.
- Sri Lanka: condos on any floor, 100% paid upfront from abroad; most Colombo sales Rs 71 L-1.41 Cr; 15% CGT; LKR -40% vs INR over 10 yr. Skip.
- What to do: nothing; neither beats keeping the money in Indian or global funds.

## Mauritius

1. **Ownership**: non-citizens may buy only in EDB-approved schemes - PDS (which absorbed IRS/RES from 2015),
   Smart City, legacy IRS/RES - or a flat in a **G+2** condo (ground + at least 2 floors) priced at least
   **MUR 6m** (Rs 1.19 Cr) (EDB residency.mu "Acquire", read 15 Nov 2026). EDB letter of authorisation needed.
   From 13 Dec 2024 (EDB FAQ, Feb 2025): on a first sale in a scheme, 85% of price paid to the developer in MUR;
   loans allowed only above USD 750k (first USD 750k from own funds sent from abroad). G+2 exempt from these.
2. **Prices**: GPG (Q3 2026) Grand Baie 1-bed USD 252,400 (Rs 2.37 Cr), 2-bed USD 441,300 (Rs 4.15 Cr); primary
   apartments average USD 3,934/sq m (Rs 3.7 L). Secondary area: Central region average apartment listing
   MUR 8.5m (Rs 1.68 Cr, Propertycloud 2025 via GPG Apr 2026; includes locals-only stock). Numbeo Port Louis
   centre USD 4,091/sq m (Rs 3.8 L), outside USD 1,056 (Rs 1.0 L). Prices +13.9% y/y Q3 2025 (Stats Mauritius RPPI).
3. **Yield**: GPG gross 3.29% (Q3 2026); Grand Baie 1-bed 3.04%, 2-bed 2.90%. Numbeo Port Louis 2.8% centre. Net
   estimate about 1.5% (GPG says net is usually 1.5-2 points lower). Short-let: AirDNA occupancy 48-64%, seasonal.
4. **Purchase costs**: registration duty for non-citizens **raised from 5% to 10% from 1 Jul 2026** (GPG citing
   KPMG Finance Act 2025 note; KPMG PDF itself not opened), payable in USD/hard currency (EDB FAQ Q10). Notary:
   not verified. Total about 10%+.
5. **Running**: no wealth/inheritance tax (PwC, 15 Jun 2026). Syndic/service charges, local rates: not verified.
6. **Tax**: rent taxed at 0% / 10% / 20% (first MUR 500k / next 500k / rest), applies to Mauritius-source income
   whether resident or not; **no capital gains tax** (PwC, reviewed 15 Jun 2026). But **land transfer tax 10%
   (was 5%) paid by the seller** when a non-citizen buys a scheme/G+2 home or on resale between non-citizens, from
   1 Jul 2026 (PwC corporate other taxes). India DTAA: rent and gains on Mauritius property taxable in Mauritius,
   India taxes as resident and credits Mauritius tax (standard Art 6/13; treaty text not re-read).
7. **Visa**: scheme home above **USD 375k** (Rs 3.53 Cr) -> residence permit for buyer, spouse and dependants while
   held (US State Dept ICS 2025; Capmad 14 Oct 2026). G+2 flats give no residence.
8. **Currency**: MUR vs INR +14% over 5 yr, flat (-0.4%) over 10 yr (yfinance monthly to Nov 2026).
9. **Liquidity/title**: notarial titles, EDB-controlled; 862 purchase authorisations in 2025 (EDB CEO, Feb 2026).
   Resale pool is non-citizens only (in schemes) and each resale costs about 20% in duty + LTT.
10. **Verdict: skip** (maybe only as a lifestyle/residence buy). Minimum: G+2 flat MUR 6m + 10% = **about Rs 1.3 Cr**;
    with residence USD 375k + 10% = about Rs 3.9 Cr, above total net worth.

## Sri Lanka

1. **Ownership**: Land (Restrictions on Alienation) Act 38 of 2014 bans freehold land to foreigners; land only by
   lease up to 99 years (Land Lease Tax abolished by Act 3 of 2017). Condos: the 2014 "4th floor and above" rule was
   **removed by Act 21 of 2018** - foreigners may buy a condo on any floor if the **full price is paid upfront by
   inward remittance** before the deed (Daily FT legal analysis, 30 Jun 2026; GPG Nov 2026). So no local mortgage.
2. **Prices**: GPG (Nov 2026) apartments average USD 1,909/sq m (Rs 1.8 L). Numbeo Colombo centre USD 3,660/sq m
   (Rs 3.4 L), outside USD 1,172 (Rs 1.1 L). CBSL Q2 2026: most condo sales LKR 25-50m (**Rs 71 L-1.41 Cr**);
   average 3-bed apartment asking LKR 99.1m (Rs 2.80 Cr, LankaPropertyWeb Q2 2026). New-condo price index +3.2% y/y,
   condo sales volume -6.7% y/y Q2 2026 (CBSL via EconomyNext 6 Oct 2026). Kandy/secondary city: not verified.
3. **Yield**: Numbeo Colombo gross 4.4% centre, 5.4% outside. GPG yield page is from 2005 (Colombo about 3.5%) -
   stale. Net estimate about 2.5-3% after tax and costs.
4. **Purchase costs**: stamp duty on transfer (commonly quoted 3% on first LKR 100k, 4% on the rest): not verified.
   Legal/notary: not verified. No foreign-buyer surcharge on condos.
5. **Running**: no annual property tax yet - the IMF-pledged property tax is deferred and not in the 2027 Budget
   (Daily FT via GPG, Nov 2026). Condo service charges: not verified.
6. **Tax**: capital gains tax **15% for individuals** (raised from 10%; Inland Revenue amendment from 1 Jul 2026,
   EconomyNext 26 Jun 2026; 2025 Budget, EconomyNext 17 Feb 2025). Rent: taxed at individual slab rates; the rate
   and withholding for a non-resident: not verified. India DTAA: property income/gains taxable in Sri Lanka, credit
   in India (standard articles; not re-read).
7. **Visa**: a USD 75k real-estate-based long-term residence visa was launched in 2022 (IMI Daily headline,
   14 Nov 2022); its terms and status in 2026: not verified.
8. **Currency**: LKR vs INR **-25% over 5 yr, -40% over 10 yr** (yfinance monthly to Nov 2026). 2022 default:
   rupee fell 44.8% vs USD in 2022, inflation peaked 69.8% (CBSL via GPG). LKR -6.8% vs USD in 2026 to 30 Oct.
9. **Liquidity/title**: deeds system, buyers mostly local residents for own use (CBSL); foreign resale pool thin.
   Policy risk: foreign-ownership rules changed in 2013, 2014, 2017, 2018.
10. **Verdict: skip.** Minimum: a Colombo condo in the common LKR 25-50m band plus about 5% costs = **about
    Rs 75 L**; rupee loss has wiped out local price gains for an INR investor.
