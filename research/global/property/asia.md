# Asia: buying a home abroad (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Philippines, Japan)

Research only, written 2026-11-15. Indian-side rules (LRS, FEMA, Indian tax) are in `india-rules.md`.

## Summary
- Rs 2.23 Cr is about USD 237k; every country except Thailand (and a small Japanese regional flat) needs over half of it for one flat.
- Skip Singapore (60% ABSD, no CECA relief for Indians), Malaysia, Vietnam, Indonesia and the Philippines.
- Maybe: a Thai condo from about Rs 86 L (THB 3m also gives a renewable 1-year stay) or a Fukuoka/Osaka flat from about Rs 1.36 Cr as a yen diversifier.
- New in 2026: Malaysia foreign stamp duty 8%; Japan nationality filing; Thai 75% quota and 99-year lease are not law.
- Net yields are about 1-4% after costs and tax; treat any purchase as lifestyle, not income.
