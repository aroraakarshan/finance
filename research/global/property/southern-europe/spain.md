## Details: Spain (Madrid; Malaga, Valencia)

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, freehold, no permit. **The 100% tax on non-EU buyers is not law:**
proposed Jan 2025, never debated in parliament by end-March 2026; parliament dissolved for the
29 Nov 2026 election, so all bills lapsed; polls favour PP, which would likely drop it (The Local
Spain 2026-10-19). Watch the result. The Canary Islands are pushing their own foreign-buyer
limits at EU level (Idealista 2026-09-21; details not verified).

- **Buy costs:** transfer tax 6-11% by region (new homes: VAT instead), legal 1-2%, notary
  0.5-1% -> 7.5-14% (GPG, Sep 2026).
- **Running:** IBI from about 0.4% of cadastral value (GPG). Owners not renting pay imputed
  income tax: 24% on 1.1-2% of cadastral value (GPG).
- **Tax (non-EU non-resident):** rent 24% of **gross** rent, no expense deduction; gains 19%;
  buyer withholds 3% of price on sale as an advance (GPG, Sep 2026).
- **Visa link:** golden visa ended 3 Apr 2025 (Idealista 2025-04-04; KPMG 2025-01-09). None now.
- **Other:** tourist-let rules tightening; Barcelona plans to end tourist licences by 2028
  (headline, Bella Caledonia 2026-06-10, not verified).
- **Liquidity/risk:** good liquidity; risk is policy (election, short-let, possible future surtax).
- **Verdict: skip** (maybe after the election if a non-EU surtax is formally dropped). Minimum
  ~Rs 2.5 Cr (Malaga 1-bed + ~10% costs).
