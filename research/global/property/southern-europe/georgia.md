# Georgia: property for an Indian resident

## Summary
Maybe. Cheapest entry (Tbilisi studio Rs 0.63 Cr, 1-bed Rs 1.04 Cr), ~1% buy costs, 5% tax on rent, ~5.1% net (estimate).
Risks: small illiquid market, off-plan developer risk, political risk. Residence permit now needs USD 150k (from 1 Mar 2026).
Minimum ~Rs 0.65-1.05 Cr.

## Details: Georgia (Tbilisi, Batumi)

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, apartments and non-farm land freehold, no permit (GPG Georgia guide;
not checked against the land registry). Agricultural land is closed to foreigners (VOA
2019-04-17; current exceptions not verified).

- **Buy costs:** transfer under 0.1%, legal 0.5-1%, notary under 0.1% -> 0.7-1.2% buyer;
  seller agent 3-5% (GPG).
- **Running:** property tax only if family income is over GEL 40k (GPG); vacancy high in Batumi
  off-season (summer-only demand, Georgia Today 2026-07-22).
- **Tax (non-resident):** residential rent 5% of gross; gain on a flat 5% (exemptions by holding
  period, not verified) (GPG, Aug 2026).
- **Visa link:** property residence permit now needs **USD 150k** (Rs 1.41 Cr) of property, from
  1 Mar 2026 (was USD 100k - the brief's USD 100k is out of date); 1-year renewable. A USD 300k
  investor route gives 5-year residence (IMI Daily 2026-02-02).
- **Currency:** GEL/INR 26.96 (2016-11-14) -> 23.64 (2021-11-13) -> 36.26 (NBG): +34% in 10
  years, +53% in 5 years.
- **Liquidity/risk:** small market; many off-plan sales (developer risk); political risk
  (foreign-agent law, BBC 2024-05-14; HRW 2026). Tighter mortgage limits from Feb 2027 may slow
  local demand (SOVA 2026-10-08).
- **Verdict: maybe.** Cheapest entry and highest net yield here, low costs; but small, illiquid,
  political risk. Minimum ~Rs 0.65 Cr (Tbilisi studio) or ~Rs 1.05 Cr (1-bed).
