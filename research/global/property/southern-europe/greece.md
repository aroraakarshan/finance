Maybe. Cheap Athens 1-bed EUR 99k (Rs 1.03 Cr) at 7.3% gross; entry costs ~6.5-8.5% today.
A bill (3 Nov 2026) would raise transfer tax to 15% for non-EU tax-residents from 1 Jul 2027 - buy before then or skip.

## Details: Greece (Athens, Thessaloniki)

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, freehold (some border areas need a permit - not verified).
**Proposed 15% transfer tax:** a bill submitted to parliament on 3 Nov 2026 (Article 16) raises
transfer tax from 3% to 15% for residential buys by individuals tax-resident outside the EU/EEA,
for deeds signed from **1 Jul 2027**. Not law yet; elections due spring 2027 (Get Golden Visa
2026-11-09; IMI Daily 2026-09-06 and 2026-10-23).

- **Buy costs:** transfer tax 3.09%, registry 0.5-0.7%, notary 0.8-1%, legal 1-2%, agent 1-2%
  -> about 6.5-8.5% today (GPG). 24% VAT on new builds is suspended only to 31 Dec 2026 (IMI Daily).
- **Running:** ENFIA annual tax, formula based (location, size, age), not a % of value (GPG).
- **Tax (non-resident):** rent 15% to EUR 12k, 25% to 24k, 35% to 36k, 45% above; 5% flat
  expense deduction. Capital gains tax on property suspended to 31 Dec 2026 (GPG, Nov 2026).
- **Visa link:** golden visa EUR 800k in Attica, Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Santorini and big islands
  (Rs 8.31 Cr); EUR 400k elsewhere (Rs 4.15 Cr), single property of 120 sq m+; EUR 250k for
  conversions/listed buildings (SchengenVisaInfo 2026-10-15). Golden-visa homes may not be
  short-let; minister warned in Oct 2026 that permits will be revoked for leasing or hidden
  rebates (same source).
- **Short lets:** new short-let registrations banned in central Athens since 2025 (AP 2025-01-10)
  and extended to central Thessaloniki (GPG, Jul 2026).
- **Liquidity/risk:** moderate; old buildings, inheritance disputes and split ownership are common
  (IMI Daily 2026-09-06). Use a lawyer for title search.
- **Verdict: maybe.** Only Europe market here where a flat fits the budget with 5-7% gross. Buy
  before July 2027 or the cost rises by 12 points if the bill passes. Minimum ~Rs 1.1 Cr
  (Athens 1-bed EUR 99k + ~7%).
