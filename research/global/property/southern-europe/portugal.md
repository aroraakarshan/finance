# Portugal: property for an Indian resident

## Summary
Skip. Since 25 May 2026 non-residents pay a flat 7.5% IMT, so entry costs are about 10%.
Net yield is about 2.3% (my estimate) and there is no visa link (property route ended 2023).
Minimum ~Rs 2.7 Cr (Porto 1-bed + costs), above budget.

## Details: Portugal (Lisbon, Porto)

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, freehold, no permit and no quota (not re-checked against an official
page). **New 2026 surcharge:** since 25 May 2026 a buyer who is not tax-resident in Portugal pays
IMT (transfer tax) at a flat 7.5% from the first euro on a home, with no exemptions
(Decree-Law 97/2026, published 20 May 2026 - Crowe Portugal; EIN/Black Prive 2026-10-06). It is
refunded if you become Portugal tax-resident within 2 years, or let at a capped "moderate" rent.

- **Buy costs:** IMT 7.5% (non-resident), stamp duty 0.8% (EIN 2026-10-06), legal 1.25-2%,
  notary 0.1-0.5% (GPG, Sep 2026) -> about 10%. Seller pays agent 3-6%.
- **Running:** IMI 0.30-0.45% a year of the tax value (VPT), not market value (GPG).
- **Tax (non-resident):** rent 25% of net rent (10% for qualifying moderate-rent leases to
  2029); gains: 50% of the gain taxed at progressive rates, worldwide income counts for the rate
  (GPG, Sep 2026).
- **Visa link:** golden visa property route closed in 2023 (SchengenVisaInfo; KNSI 2026-05-26);
  fund routes remain and the citizenship wait was raised to 10 years in 2026 (Forbes 2026-04-07;
  IMI Daily 2026-05-03). NHR tax regime closed to new entrants from 1 Jan 2024 (PwC Portugal).
- **Currency:** EUR/INR 72.77 (2016-11-15) -> 84.99 (2021-11-15) -> 103.85: +43% in 10 years
  (about 3.6%/yr), +22% in 5 years. Same for all euro countries below.
- **Liquidity/risk:** liquid in Lisbon/Porto; title registry is reliable (not verified). Sales
  falling in 2026 (Idealista 2026-10-01).
- **Verdict: skip.** Expensive, ~2% net yield, 10% entry cost. Minimum ~Rs 2.7 Cr (Porto 1-bed
  + costs), above budget.
