# Malta: property for an Indian resident

## Summary
Skip. Low yield (3.99% gross, ~2.4% net estimate), AIP permit outside Special Designated Areas.
MPRP needs a EUR 375k purchase plus fees, above budget. Minimum ~Rs 2.5 Cr.

## Details: Malta

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, but outside Special Designated Areas a non-EU buyer needs an AIP
permit and one property (Edinburgh Reporter 2026-05-28; the AIP minimum price was not verified).
GPG's Malta page says a non-resident may sell only to a Maltese buyer unless none is found; this
looks wrong or out of date and was not verified.

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Malta price | EUR 4,158/sq m (Rs 4.32 L) | GPG, Q3 2026 |
| Mainland 1-bed / 2-bed | EUR 220k / 285k (Rs 2.28 / 2.96 Cr), yield 4.1% / 4.2% | GPG, Aug 2026 |
| Gross yield | 3.99% (Q3 2026) | GPG |
| Net yield | about 2.4% | my estimate |

- **Buy costs:** stamp duty 3.5-5%, notary 1.5-2%, legal 1-1.5% -> 6-8.5% (GPG).
- **Tax (non-resident):** rent 15% final tax on gross (optional). Sale: GPG says a final tax of
  12% of the **sale price** (5% if sold within 5 years, 10% if bought before 2004) - rates not
  verified elsewhere.
- **Visa link:** MPRP from 1 Jan 2025: buy for EUR 375k (Rs 3.89 Cr) or rent at EUR 14k/yr,
  plus EUR 50k admin fee, EUR 30k (buy) / 60k (rent) contribution, and EUR 500k assets with
  150k liquid (IMI Daily 2024-11-20). No later change found.
- **Liquidity/risk:** small island market; AIP permit limits who you can sell to.
- **Verdict: skip.** Low yield, permit hassle, MPRP costs above budget. Minimum ~Rs 2.5 Cr.
