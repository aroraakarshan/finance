# Italy: property for an Indian resident

## Summary
Skip. Indians probably cannot buy directly (reciprocity) and need an Italian company.
1-euro homes are renovation projects in remote towns, not investments. No property visa route.
Minimum ~Rs 2.5 Cr plus company.

## Details: Italy (Milan, Rome; 1-euro homes)

**Can an Indian buy?** Probably **not directly**. Italy applies a reciprocity test, and an
Italian law firm (cited by Moneycontrol 2025-10-09) says Indians lack reciprocity and must buy
through an Italian company (S.r.l., ~EUR 10k capital). Reciprocity generally does not apply to
holders of an Italian residence permit (not verified). The Foreign Ministry reciprocity page
returned "not found" on 2026-11-15.

- **Buy costs:** transfer tax 2-9% (9% for second homes), agent 3%, legal 1%, notary 1-2%
  (GPG). A company structure adds setup and annual costs.
- **Running:** IMU about 0.86% of the cadastral base on second homes (GPG).
- **Tax (non-resident):** rent 21% flat (cedolare secca; 26% on short lets beyond one flat);
  gains taxable only if sold within 5 years (GPG, Jul 2026).
- **Visa link:** no property route (Italy's investor visa is for companies, funds and bonds - not
  verified this run).
- **1-euro homes:** dilapidated houses in remote towns, with a binding renovation commitment;
  often limited to EU citizens or residents (Moneycontrol 2025-10-09). Not an investment.
- **Verdict: skip.** Legal route for Indians unclear/costly. Minimum ~Rs 2.5 Cr plus company.
