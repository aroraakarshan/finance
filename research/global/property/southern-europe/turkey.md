## Details: Turkey (Istanbul)

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, freehold, but not in military or security zones; max 30 hectares
per person; foreigners may own at most 10% of private property in a district (Get Golden Visa
guide 2026-11-04). Residence-permit district closures were mostly lifted in June 2026 except
Fatih and Esenyurt (Turkiye Today 2026-06-06). A property residence permit needs USD 200k+
(same source).

- **Price trend:** Istanbul +26.8% y/y to Sep 2026 in lira but **-2.3% after inflation** (GPG,
  Nov 2026).
- **Buy costs:** title deed fee 4%, agent 2% each side, legal 0.5%, notary 0.1-0.2% (GPG); who
  pays the deed fee is negotiated (not verified). VAT exempt for first-time foreign buyers paying
  in foreign currency (IMI Daily 2026-05-12).
- **Running:** small annual property tax, DASK earthquake insurance EUR 30-100 (Get Golden Visa).
- **Tax (non-resident):** rent at 15-40% progressive after a TRY 58k exemption and 15% deduction
  (17% of gross at USD 18k/yr); gains tax-free after 5 years (GPG, Aug 2026).
- **Visa link:** citizenship for USD 400k (Rs 3.76 Cr) of property held 3 years (IMI Daily
  2026-05-12). In Aug 2026, 6,134 CBI citizenships were cancelled, mostly over inflated
  valuations; citizenship appraisals now only by state-owned GEDAS (IMI Daily 2026-08-06).
- **Currency:** TRY/INR 20.87 (2016-11) -> 7.49 (2021-11) -> 1.87: **-91% in 10 years**
  (yfinance USDTRY, USDINR). Rents are in lira.
- **Liquidity/risk:** liquid in Istanbul, but valuation fraud, earthquake risk and lira risk.
- **Verdict: skip.** Minimum ~Rs 1.0-1.1 Cr for an ordinary Istanbul 1-bed.
