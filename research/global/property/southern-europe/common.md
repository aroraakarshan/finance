# Southern Europe property: method, FX and common points

## Summary
FX rates (2026-11-13) used for every INR figure, how net yield is estimated, and the India tax
treaty point that applies to all eight countries.

Back to the index: [southern-europe.md](../southern-europe.md).

## Details
Research only, written 2026-11-15. Not tax or legal advice. Indian-side rules (LRS, FEMA,
Indian tax) are in `india-rules.md` and are not repeated here.

**FX used** (all for 2026-11-13): EUR/INR 103.85 (yfinance EURINR=X); USD/INR 94.03 (brief;
yfinance 94.03); TRY/INR 1.870 (derived: USD/INR 94.03 / USD/TRY 50.28, yfinance USDTRY=X);
GEL/INR 36.26 (National Bank of Georgia official rate, 100 INR = 2.7578 GEL).
"L" = lakh, "Cr" = crore. GPG = Global Property Guide (free tier; many city rows are paywalled).

**Common points**
- India has a tax treaty with all eight countries (PwC India treaty list, reviewed 2026-05-12).
  Treaty point for every country: rent and gains on the property can be taxed first where the
  flat is; India also taxes them and gives credit for the foreign tax, so the total is roughly the
  higher of the two rates.
- "Net yield" below is **my estimate**: gross yield minus 25% of rent for vacancy, upkeep,
  property tax and management, then minus the local tax on rent. Indian tax comes on top.
- 1-2 bed price = GPG asking prices where shown, else price per sq m x 55-80 sq m (my calc).
- User budget context: Rs 2.23 Cr in total. Any flat here is 30-100% of net worth in one asset.
