Skip all six for now; Ireland is the only "maybe (later)" (entry cost about 2.5-3%, Dublin gross

## Verdict for this user

Every market here would take most or all of the user's Rs 2.23 Cr (and nearly a full year of
one person's LRS limit) for one flat. Ireland has the lowest entry cost (about 2.5-3%) and the
best gross yields, so it is the only "maybe" if wealth grows a lot. The US adds estate-tax risk
to an already US-heavy portfolio. Canada and New Zealand are closed; Australia is new-build only
with 15%+ costs; UK entry costs about 8-10% and rent tax rises to 24-49% in 2027.
