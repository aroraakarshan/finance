# Malaysia: buying a home as a foreign (Indian) buyer

Part of [Asia property](../asia.md). FX rates and method: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- Freehold allowed with state consent and about RM1m minimum; foreign stamp duty flat 8% from 1 Jan 2026.
- KLCC gross yield 4.6-5.1%; MM2H Silver needs USD 150k deposit plus a RM600k home.
- Verdict: skip (maybe only with MM2H as a lifestyle plan); minimum about Rs 2.55 Cr (Rs 1.54 Cr via MM2H).

## Details

| Topic | Detail (source, date) |
|---|---|
| Ownership rules | foreigners can hold freehold or leasehold titles in their own name (EdgeProp MY, 28 Jul 2026). Every sale to a foreigner needs state consent under Sec 433B National Land Code. State minimum prices: "many states about RM1 million for strata", higher for landed, some lower (same article). Exact KL threshold: not verified. |
| Prices | GPG (Sep 2026) KLCC 1-bed USD 269,100 (Rs 2.53 Cr), 2-bed USD 330,300 (Rs 3.11 Cr). Numbeo KL centre USD 3,721/sq m (Rs 3.5 L), outside USD 1,571 (Rs 1.5 L); Johor Bahru centre USD 1,996 (Rs 1.9 L). Prime KL marketed at RM1,200-3,500 per sq ft (PropNex in EdgeProp, Jul 2026). Penang (George Town): Numbeo had no data. |
| Rental yield | GPG national gross 5.28% (Q3 2026); KLCC 1-bed 4.59%, 2-bed 5.07%. Net estimate about 2.8% on price (20% for service charge/vacancy, then 30% tax), about 2.5% on total cost. |
| Purchase costs | **stamp duty for non-Malaysians is a flat 8% on residential from 1 Jan 2026** (was 4%) (EdgeProp 28 Jul 2026; The Edge 21 Jan 2026). All-in 10-12% for a cash buyer incl. consent, legal, registration (lawyers quoted in EdgeProp). |
| Running costs | assessment and quit rent: not verified (reported as low). Service charges: not verified. |
| Tax on rent and sale (non-resident) | non-resident income tax 30% flat on Malaysian income incl. rent (PwC). RPGT for foreigners: 30% of gain if sold within 5 years, 10% after (PwC; EdgeProp). India DTAA: same standard point (treaty text not re-read). |
| Visa link | **MM2H** (2024 rules, 3 tiers): Silver fixed deposit USD 150k; Gold USD 500k with 15-year visa; compulsory home purchase, minimum RM600,000 (Rs 1.38 Cr) for Silver per Rehda; the 8% duty and state thresholds still apply (EdgeProp, 28 Jul 2026). 7,650 applications so far (NST, 26 Sep 2026). |
| Currency vs INR | MYR vs INR +30% over 5 yr, +50% over 10 yr (yfinance). |
| Liquidity and title risk | Torrens title, standard SPA protects buyers; resale to foreigners is limited by the same thresholds, so exit is mostly to locals at local prices (buyers report "resale discounts" - EdgeProp). Consent can take months. |
| Verdict and minimum budget | **Verdict: skip** (maybe only with MM2H as a lifestyle plan). Minimum: RM1m + 11% = **about Rs 2.55 Cr**; MM2H route RM600k + 12% = about Rs 1.54 Cr plus the USD 150k deposit. |
