# Philippines: buying a home as a foreign (Indian) buyer

Part of [Asia property](../asia.md). FX rates and method: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- Condo only within the 40% foreign cap; no land.
- Taguig gross yield 4.7-4.8%; 25% final tax on rent for non-residents; SRRV deposit can buy a condo.
- Verdict: skip; minimum about Rs 1.73 Cr (Cebu).

## Details

| Topic | Detail (source, date) |
|---|---|
| Ownership rules | no land (Constitution 60-40 rule); condo units allowed while foreign ownership of the condominium corporation stays within **40%** (RA 4726 Sec 5) (Aureada law firm, 11 Nov 2026; ASEAN Briefing, 8 Jul 2025). Land leases: a Sep 2025 law allows foreign investors to lease private land for up to 99 years (Bangkok Post, 6 Sep 2025), aimed at investment projects; use for a private home not verified. Anti-Dummy Law punishes nominee structures. |
| Prices | GPG (Oct 2026) Taguig (BGC) studio/1-bed USD 220,700 (Rs 2.08 Cr), 2-bed USD 428,600 (Rs 4.03 Cr). Numbeo Manila centre USD 3,974/sq m (Rs 3.7 L); Cebu centre USD 4,477 (Rs 4.2 L), outside USD 3,023. |
| Rental yield | GPG national gross 5.57% (Q3 2026); Taguig 4.70-4.80%. Net estimate about 2.5% (25% costs, then 25% final tax on gross rent). |
| Purchase costs | documentary stamp tax PHP 15 per 1,000 = 1.5% (PwC); local transfer tax and registration: not verified. Seller usually pays the 6% capital gains tax. |
| Running costs | real property tax up to 2% of assessed value in Metro Manila + 1% special education fund (PwC). Association dues: not verified. |
| Tax on rent and sale (non-resident) | non-resident alien not in business: 25% final tax on Philippine income (PwC); whether rent counts as "business" (then graduated rates) not verified. Sale: 6% of the higher of price or fair market value, withheld (PwC). India DTAA: standard point (treaty text not re-read). |
| Visa link | **SRRV** retiree visa; the deposit can be converted into a condo in PRA-accredited projects (PRA site; Gulf News, 28 Dec 2025). Deposit amounts and age rules: not verified this run. |
| Currency vs INR | PHP vs INR +1% over 5 yr, +8% over 10 yr (USD cross, yfinance). |
| Liquidity and title risk | title and developer-delay risks not researched this run (not verified); resale to foreigners only while the building is under the 40% cap. |
| Verdict and minimum budget | **Verdict: skip.** Minimum: Cebu 40 sq m about USD 179k + 3% = **about Rs 1.73 Cr**. |
