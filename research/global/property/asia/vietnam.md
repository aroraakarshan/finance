# Vietnam: buying a home as a foreign (Indian) buyer

Part of [Asia property](../asia.md). FX rates and method: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- No freehold: 50-year ownership (extendable once), 30% quota per building.
- Gross yield 3.3-3.6% (2025 data); few ownership certificates issued to foreigners.
- Verdict: skip; minimum about Rs 1.03 Cr.

## Details

| Ownership rules | foreigners who entered Vietnam legally may own apartments/houses in commercial projects for **50 years from certificate date, extendable once by up to 50 years**; cap **30% of units per building**, 250 houses per ward-sized area; no land ownership (VIR, 7 Feb 2025; law text not re-read). Defence zones excluded. |
