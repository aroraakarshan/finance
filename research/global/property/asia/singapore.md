# Singapore: buying a home as a foreign (Indian) buyer

Part of [Asia property](../asia.md). FX rates and method: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- Indians pay the full 60% ABSD (no CECA relief; verified on IRAS), so the minimum is about Rs 10.9 Cr.
- Yield about 3% gross, about 1% net on total cost; strongest currency here (SGD +52% vs INR in 10 yr).
- Verdict: skip.

## Details

| Topic | Detail (source, date) |
|---|---|
| Ownership rules | foreigners may buy strata condos/apartments freely; landed homes need Land Dealings Approval Unit approval, "rarely granted outside Sentosa Cove"; HDB flats closed (PropertyNet.SG guide, 21 Jun 2026, citing Residential Property Act). Leasehold (99-yr) and freehold condos both exist. |
| Prices | Numbeo apartment centre USD 22,466/sq m (Rs 21.1 L), outside centre USD 15,811 (Rs 14.9 L). GPG (Jun 2026) Tanglin/Holland/Bukit Timah 1-bed USD 1.09m (Rs 10.27 Cr), 2-bed USD 1.64m (Rs 15.40 Cr). |
| Rental yield | GPG national gross 3.06% (Q2 2026); Tanglin 1-bed 3.30%, 2-bed 3.03%. Net estimate: about 1.7% on price after property tax, 10% for fees/vacancy and 24% income tax; about 1% on total cost once ABSD is added. |
| Purchase costs | BSD 1-6% marginal (about 3% on S$1.5m) plus **ABSD 60% for foreigners** (from 27 Apr 2023) (IRAS ABSD and BSD pages, read 15 Nov 2026). **Indian nationals get no remission**: IRAS FTA remission covers only nationals/PRs of Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland and nationals of the USA (IRAS FTA page, read 15 Nov 2026). India-Singapore CECA is not on the list - verified. Total about 63% + legal fees. |
| Running costs | non-owner-occupied property tax 12% / 20% / 28% / 36% on annual value bands of S$30k / 15k / 15k / above S$60k (IRAS, from 1 Jan 2024). Example: S$3,869/month rent -> AV about S$46k -> tax about S$7.0k (about 15% of rent). Condo maintenance fees: not verified. |
| Tax on rent and sale (non-resident) | rent taxed at 24% flat for non-residents, 15% deemed expense allowed (PwC, 2026). No capital gains tax, but **SSD 16/12/8/4%** of price if sold within 1/2/3/4 years for purchases from 4 Jul 2025 (IRAS SSD page). India DTAA: rent and gains on Singapore property taxable in Singapore; India credits foreign tax (standard Art 6/13; treaty text not re-read). |
| Visa link | no property-linked residence (property gives no visa; ABSD applies until PR). |
| Currency vs INR | SGD vs INR +33% over 5 yr, +52% over 10 yr (yfinance monthly, to Nov 2026) - strongest here. |
| Liquidity and title risk | deep resale market, Torrens title, low fraud; foreign buyers limited to condos. |
| Verdict and minimum budget | **Verdict: skip.** Minimum: 45 sq m outside centre about USD 711k + 63% duties = **about Rs 10.9 Cr**. |
