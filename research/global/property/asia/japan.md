# Japan: buying a home as a foreign (Indian) buyer

Part of [Asia property](../asia.md). FX rates and method: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- Freehold with no approval; nationality filing from fiscal 2026; no ban on foreign home buyers as of Nov 2026.
- Tokyo gross yield 2.8-3.3%, regional higher; yen weak vs INR (-11% in 5 yr).
- Verdict: maybe (small regional flat, not Tokyo); minimum about Rs 1.36 Cr.

## Details

| Topic | Detail (source, date) |
|---|---|
| Ownership rules | foreigners can own land and buildings freehold with no approval. 2025-26 changes: nationality must be declared when registering property from fiscal 2026 (Japan Times headline, 16 Dec 2025; SCMP 18 Oct 2026 says it is now in force). A purchase licence near defence/nuclear sites is planned for **all** buyers, Japanese and foreign, by law change in autumn 2026 or 2027 (Asahi, 17 Mar 2026); wider curbs on foreign buyers were postponed (Japan Today, 3 Jun 2026). No ban on homes elsewhere as of Nov 2026. |
| Prices | Tokyo 23 wards used 70 sq m flat asking price JPY 127.41m (Rs 7.49 Cr), about JPY 1.82m/sq m (Rs 10.7 L) (Tokyo Kantei, June 2026, via Asahi). GPG (Aug 2026) central Tokyo studio USD 295,600 (Rs 2.78 Cr), 1-bed USD 552,800 (Rs 5.20 Cr). Numbeo Osaka centre USD 6,556/sq m (Rs 6.2 L); Fukuoka USD 4,518 (Rs 4.2 L). **Akiya** (empty rural houses): e.g. a 4-bed house in Iwamizawa (Hokkaido) for JPY 2.8m (Rs 16.5 L) (Straits Times, 12 Oct 2026) - cheap but hard to rent or sell. |
| Rental yield | GPG national gross 4.24% (Q3 2026); central Tokyo 2.76-3.29%. Net estimate about 2-2.5% in Tokyo, maybe 4% in regional cities (management + repair reserve + 1.7% tax on assessed value). |
| Purchase costs | acquisition tax 3% of assessed value until 31 Mar 2027 (PropertyNet.SG citing Shiga Prefecture), registration tax 0.1-2% of assessed value (PwC), stamp tax, scrivener; agent fee: not verified. All-in estimate 6-8% of price. |
| Running costs | fixed asset tax 1.4% + city planning tax up to 0.3% of assessed value (PwC; JETRO via PropertyNet). Assessed values are well below market. |
| Tax on rent and sale (non-resident) | rent: 20.42% withheld, then a yearly return on net rent (PwC; NTA via PropertyNet). Sale by non- resident: 15.315% (held over 5 yrs) or 30.63% (5 yrs or less); buyer withholds 10.21% of price unless price is JPY 100m or less and an individual buys it to live in (PropertyNet, 5 Nov 2026). India DTAA: standard point (treaty text not re-read). Japan inheritance tax up to 55% can hit heirs (PropertyNet citing MoF). |
| Visa link | no property-linked residence. |
| Currency vs INR | JPY vs INR -11% over 5 yr, -3.5% over 10 yr (yfinance) - the only weak currency here besides IDR. |
| Liquidity and title risk | very safe title and registry; Tokyo flats liquid; used Tokyo prices fell for the first time in 26 months in June 2026 (Asahi); regional and akiya houses very illiquid, buildings depreciate. |
| Verdict and minimum budget | **Verdict: maybe** (small regional flat, e.g. Fukuoka/Osaka, as a yen diversifier; not Tokyo). Minimum: 30 sq m Fukuoka about USD 136k + 7% = **about Rs 1.36 Cr**; akiya from about Rs 20 L but not an investment. |
