# Thailand: buying a home as a foreign (Indian) buyer

Part of [Asia property](../asia.md). FX rates and method: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- Condo freehold only (49% foreign quota); land not allowed; quota rise and 99-year lease are not law.
- Gross yield 5.2-5.6% in Bangkok; a THB 3m condo gives a renewable 1-year stay.
- Verdict: maybe (small condo as lifestyle asset); minimum about Rs 86 L.

## Details

| Topic | Detail (source, date) |
|---|---|
| Ownership rules | condo units freehold if total foreign ownership stays within **49%** of a building's saleable area; land cannot be owned; houses via 30-year registered lease. Since a March 2025 Supreme Court ruling, "30+30+30" renewal clauses are not enforceable beyond the first 30 years (Thaiger, 13 Mar 2026). Raising the quota to 70-75% is "being considered" (Thai Examiner, 22 Jun 2026) - not law. The 99-year lease plan was shelved (Nation Thailand, 16 Sep 2025). EEC zones can allow 100% foreign in some projects (Thai Examiner). |
| Prices | GPG (Aug 2026) Bangkok Watthana 1-bed USD 198,200 (Rs 1.86 Cr), 2-bed USD 457,400 (Rs 4.30 Cr). Numbeo Bangkok centre USD 6,195/sq m (Rs 5.8 L), outside USD 3,271 (Rs 3.1 L); Chiang Mai centre USD 2,396 (Rs 2.3 L). About two-thirds of 2025 foreign condo buys in Greater Bangkok were THB 5m or less (Rs 1.39 Cr) (AREA survey in Thai Examiner). |
| Rental yield | GPG national gross 6.54% (Q3 2026); Watthana 1-bed 5.57%, 2-bed 5.19%. Net estimate about 3.5-4% (20% for common fees, vacancy, agent; then Thai income tax). |
| Purchase costs | transfer fee 2% (cut to 0.01% where price and appraisal are THB 7m or less, extended by Cabinet 30 Jun 2026; Thaiger). Specific business tax 3% (+local, often 3.3%) on sales of immovable property (PwC), stamp duty if SBT does not apply; split between buyer and seller by contract. Buyer share about 1-3% (estimate). Foreign-currency inflow form (FET) for foreign condo ownership: not verified this run. |
| Running costs | land and building tax very low, e.g. 0.02% for a first home above THB 50m, on appraised values below market (Thai Examiner). Rate for let-out condos: not verified. Common-area fees: not verified. |
| Tax on rent and sale (non-resident) | rent taxed at progressive personal rates up to 35% (PwC); sale: SBT and withholding income tax (rate not verified). India DTAA: standard point (treaty text not re-read). |
| Visa link | **THB 3m property route** (Orders 237/238 of 2568, from Oct 2025): buy a qualifying condo of at least THB 3m (Rs 83 L) -> renewable 1-year stay, fees THB 4,000 + 27,000/yr (Thaiger, 13 Mar 2026; Phuket scheme in Thai Examiner). Standard THB 10m investment extension also exists. |
| Currency vs INR | THB vs INR +25% over 5 yr, +44% over 10 yr (yfinance). |
| Liquidity and title risk | 350,000 unsold units in Bangkok, foreign transfers -17.3% in Q1 2026 (Thai Examiner) -> resale is slow; resale to foreigners only while the quota has room. Nominee companies for land/villas are illegal and under crackdown (EdgeProp SG headline, 4 Jun 2026). |
| Verdict and minimum budget | **Verdict: maybe** (only a small Bangkok/Chiang Mai condo as a lifestyle asset). Minimum: about THB 3m + 3% = **about Rs 86 L**. |
