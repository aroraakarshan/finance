# Indonesia (incl. Bali): buying a home as a foreign (Indian) buyer

Part of [Asia property](../asia.md). FX rates and method: [sources.md](sources.md).

## Summary
- No freehold: Hak Pakai right of use; foreign minimum prices (Jakarta flat IDR 3bn, Bali flat IDR 2bn).
- High gross yields (8-9%) only on flats below the foreign minimum; nominee, leasehold-scam and rupiah risks are the highest here.
- Verdict: skip; minimum about Rs 1.1 Cr (Bali flat).

## Details

| Topic | Detail (source, date) |
|---|---|
| Ownership rules | no freehold (Hak Milik) for foreigners. Foreigners with any valid stay permit can hold **Hak Pakai** (right of use) on a house or a strata unit: 30 years + 20-year extension + 30-year renewal (NusaBali, 17 Oct 2024; Bali Sun, 9 Apr 2024, citing Ministerial Decree 1241/SK-HK.02/IX/2022). **Minimum prices for foreigners** (same decree): Bali house IDR 5bn (Rs 2.58 Cr), Bali flat IDR 2bn (Rs 1.03 Cr), Jakarta flat IDR 3bn (Rs 1.55 Cr), some regions IDR 1bn. Status of the decree in 2026: not re-verified. Bali villas are mostly bought as **leaseholds** (often 25+ years) or through a **PT PMA** company (minimum investment IDR 10bn per a Bali MP, BaliNews, 30 Jun 2026); nominee (Indonesian name) deals are illegal and under political attack (same source). |
| Prices | GPG (Aug 2026) South Jakarta 1-bed USD 67,100 (Rs 63 L), 2-bed USD 117,200 (Rs 1.10 Cr) - both below the IDR 3bn (about USD 164k) foreign minimum. Numbeo Jakarta centre USD 2,789/sq m (Rs 2.6 L); Denpasar (Bali) centre USD 2,306 (Rs 2.2 L). |
| Rental yield | GPG national gross 8.22% (Q3 2026); South Jakarta 8.05-9.42%. Net estimate 5-6% (10% final tax, 25% costs/vacancy), but at prices foreigners cannot buy. |
| Purchase costs | BPHTB acquisition duty up to 5% (PwC); VAT 11% on new units (PwC); notary: not verified. |
| Running costs | land and building tax (PBB) max 0.5% (PwC). |
| Tax on rent and sale (non-resident) | rent of land/buildings 10% final tax (PwC, resident rule); non-residents 20% general WHT unless treaty lower (PwC). Seller pays 2.5% of gross transfer value (PwC). India DTAA: standard point (treaty text not re-read). |
| Visa link | property gives no residence; a stay permit is needed to hold Hak Pakai. Second-home visa link: not verified. |
| Currency vs INR | IDR vs INR -1% over 5 yr, +2% over 10 yr (yfinance); rupiah near IDR 18,000/USD after a 2026 sell-off; house price index +0.62% y/y in Q1 2026, slowest since 2003 (Asia Property Awards, 3 Sep 2026). |
| Liquidity and title risk | highest risk in this list: nominee and leasehold disputes, an alleged USD 7m Lombok villa scam (The Australian headline, 4 Jun 2026), visa crackdowns in Bali (Tempo, 1 Nov 2026), thin resale market. |
| Verdict and minimum budget | **Verdict: skip.** Minimum: Bali flat IDR 2bn + 6% = **about Rs 1.1 Cr**; Jakarta flat about Rs 1.65 Cr. |
