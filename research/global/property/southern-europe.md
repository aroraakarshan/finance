# Southern Europe, Turkey and Georgia: property for an Indian resident

Research only, written 2026-11-15. Not tax or legal advice. Indian-side rules are in
`india-rules.md`. Details per country are in [southern-europe/](southern-europe/).

## Summary
Only Greece (Athens 1-bed ~Rs 1.1 Cr, 5-7% gross) and Georgia (Tbilisi ~Rs 0.65-1.05 Cr, ~5% net
estimate) fit the Rs 2.23 Cr budget; both are "maybe", and either would be a large share of net worth.
Greece: if the 15% non-EU transfer tax bill passes, buying only makes sense before 1 Jul 2027.
Skip Portugal, Spain, Cyprus, Malta, Italy and Turkey (costs, low yields, legal limits or lira risk).
No golden visa / residency route in this region fits the budget except Georgia's USD 150k permit.
