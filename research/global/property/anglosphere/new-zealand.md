## Details

FX and sources: [basics.md](basics.md), [sources.md](sources.md).

**Can an Indian buy?** No. "Overseas people usually cannot buy a house or land" (LINZ).
Allowed: NZ citizens, ordinarily resident visa holders, Australian/Singaporean citizens; and
qualifying investor visa holders (Active Investor Plus, Investor 1/2) may buy or
build a home over NZD 5m (Rs 26 Cr) with consent, fee NZD 2,040 or 3,500 (LINZ, updated
27 Mar 2026). AIP visa needs NZD 5m (Growth) or 10m (Balanced) invested (Immigration NZ).
Large apartment development exemption: not verified.
