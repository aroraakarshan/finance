# United Kingdom (London, Manchester)

## Summary

Skip. Anyone can buy, but a Manchester flat (Rs 2.0-3.1 Cr) plus about 8-10% stamp duty
(non-resident +2%, second home +5%) is more than the user's Rs 2.23 Cr. Rent tax rises to
24/44/49% from 6 Apr 2027 (Budget 2026). No visa link. Min budget about Rs 2.4 Cr.

## Details

FX and sources: [basics.md](basics.md), [sources.md](sources.md).

**Can an Indian buy?** Yes, no approval or zones; most flats are leasehold. Draft Commonhold
and Leasehold Reform Bill (gov.uk, 27 Jan 2026): ground rent cap GBP 250/yr, new leasehold flats
banned - still a draft. New service-charge rights from 1 Apr 2027 (gov.uk, 11 Nov 2026).

**Purchase cost (SDLT, England).** Normal bands 0/2/5/10/12%; +5% if you will own any other
home worth GBP 40k+ "anywhere in the world" (so an Indian home counts); +2% if not UK
resident (gov.uk SDLT pages, 2025-04-01). The 5% surcharge rose from 3% on 31 Oct 2024 (per
brief; gov.uk page does not show the date). My calculation: GBP 200k -> GBP 15,500 (7.75%);
GBP 400k -> GBP 38,000 (9.5%). Add legal/survey about 1-2% (not verified).

**Running costs.** Flat service charge and ground rent (not verified); new council tax surcharge
on homes worth GBP 2m+ from April 2028 (gov.uk, 28 Oct 2026).

**Tax for a non-resident.** Rent: letting agent or tenant (rent over GBP 100/week) withholds
basic-rate tax under the Non-resident Landlord Scheme unless HMRC approves gross payment
(gov.uk NRL guidance). Personal allowance only for British/EEA citizens or if the treaty gives
it (gov.uk); whether the India-UK treaty gives it: not verified. Property income rates: 20/40/45%
now; Budget 2025 set 22/42/47% from 6 Apr 2027, and Budget 2026 (28 Oct 2026) raised these to
**24/44/49%** from 6 Apr 2027 (gov.uk OOTLAR, 5 Nov 2026). Sale: non-residents pay UK CGT on
UK homes, 18% / 24% (gov.uk CGT page, rates from 6 Apr 2026), report and pay within 60 days
(gov.uk, updated 13 Jan 2026).

**Visa.** None (Tier 1 Investor closed, gov.uk). **Liquidity/title.** Land Registry, liquid,
low fraud; short leases and high service charges are the trap.
**Verdict: skip.** Manchester flat about Rs 2.2-3.1 Cr plus about 8-10% SDLT, i.e. more than the
user's whole Rs 2.23 Cr; rent taxed up to 44-49% from 2027. Min budget about Rs 2.4 Cr.
