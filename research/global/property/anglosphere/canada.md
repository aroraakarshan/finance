# Canada (Toronto, Calgary, Montreal)

## Summary

Skip. Foreign buyers banned to 1 Jan 2027; Budget 2026 proposes extending the ban to
1 Jan 2029 for existing/resale homes but freeing new homes (not yet law). Prices falling
(-3.24% YoY, Jul 2026), CAD weak vs INR. Min budget once allowed about Rs 2 Cr (not verified).

## Details

FX and sources: [basics.md](basics.md), [sources.md](sources.md).

**Can an Indian buy?** Not now. The Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by
Non-Canadians Act runs to 1 Jan 2027 (brief). **Budget 2026 (tabled early Nov 2026,
page modified 2026-11-05) proposes extending it to 1 Jan 2029 for existing and resale homes,
while lifting it for new residential properties**; legislation and regulations still to come
(budget.canada.ca, chapter 3 and annex 3). Current exceptions (work-permit holders, students,
permanent residents, areas outside big cities): not re-checked today.

**Purchase cost.** Provincial land transfer tax 0.04-5%, higher for non-residents in some
provinces; extra city tax in Toronto and Montreal; BC adds 20% for foreign buyers in Metro
Vancouver and other regions (PwC, Jun 2026). Ontario's non-resident tax rate: not verified.
**Running costs.** Federal Underused Housing Tax ended from the 2025 calendar year (PwC). BC
speculation and vacancy tax applies in listed areas (PwC).
**Tax for a non-resident.** Rent: 25% withholding on gross unless a net-basis election (not
re-checked today). Sale: half of the gain is taxable; buyer must withhold unless the seller has a
clearance certificate (PwC).
**Visa.** None linked to property.
**Liquidity/title.** Good land registries, title insurance common.
**Verdict: skip.** Banned for resale to 2029 if Budget 2026 passes; new-build only after that,
falling prices and weak CAD. Min budget (new condo, once allowed) about Rs 2 Cr (not verified).
