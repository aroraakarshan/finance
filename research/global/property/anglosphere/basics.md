India DTAA (all six): India has a tax treaty with each. Rent and gains from property are taxed
first where the property is; India taxes the same income and gives a foreign tax credit
(details in [india-rules.md](../india-rules.md)).

Net yield rule of thumb: GPG says net yields are "typically around 1.5% to 2% lower" than gross
(GPG Ireland rental-yields page, Jun 2026). Where no better data exists, net = gross - 1.5 to 2.
