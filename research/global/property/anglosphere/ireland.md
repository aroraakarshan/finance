Rental shortage: GPG (Sep 2026) reports a rent regulation overhaul in March 2026 that gave the
biggest quarterly rent rise in 20+ years, then eased. Rent controls apply (details not verified).

**Purchase cost.** Stamp duty 1% up to EUR 1m, 2% to 1.5m, 6% above (Revenue, from
2 Oct 2024). Legal fees not verified (about 1-1.5% + VAT is commonly quoted). Total about 2.5-3%.
**Running costs.** Local Property Tax on the 1 Nov 2025 valuation, fixed for 2026-2030
(PwC, Aug 2026); amount not verified. Landlord must register tenancies (not verified).

**Tax for a non-resident.** Rent: tenant or agent withholds 20% and files within 21 days
(Revenue NLWT, Jun 2026); the landlord files Form 11 at 20%/40% (40% above EUR 44,000 single,
2026, PwC). Sale: CGT 33% on Irish land (PwC). Selling a house over EUR 1m without a CG50A
certificate -> buyer withholds 15% (Revenue).
**Visa.** None; the Immigrant Investor Programme closed in Feb 2023 (not re-checked today).
**Liquidity/title.** Land Registry, good title; slow conveyancing, thin buyer pool outside Dublin.
**Verdict: maybe (later).** Cheapest entry cost and highest yields here, euro exposure, but a Cork
flat (Rs 1.6-3.4 Cr) is most of the user's wealth. Min budget about Rs 1.8 Cr.
