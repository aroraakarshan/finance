# Australia (Sydney, Brisbane, Melbourne)

## Summary

Skip. Foreigners may buy new dwellings only; established homes banned 1 Apr 2025 to
30 Jun 2029 (FIRB, Jul 2026; the brief said 31 Mar 2027). Entry cost 15%+ (8-9% state
surcharge + duty + FIRB fee), yield 4.94%, prices falling. Min budget about Rs 3.5 Cr.

## Details

FX and sources: [basics.md](basics.md), [sources.md](sources.md).

**Can an Indian buy?** New dwellings and vacant land (build within 4 years) only, with FIRB
approval. **Established dwellings are banned from 1 Apr 2025 to 30 Jun 2029** (FIRB residential
guidance, updated 1 Jul 2026; Guidance Note 6 v5, Jun 2026). The brief said 31 Mar 2027; the
official end date is now 30 Jun 2029. Developers can pre-clear sales to foreigners.

**Purchase cost.** FIRB fee 2026-27: AUD 15,600 up to AUD 1m, AUD 31,300 up to 2m (FIRB
schedule of fees v8). Foreign surcharge duty: NSW 9% (Revenue NSW, 4 Nov 2026), Victoria 8%
(SRO, 13 Nov 2026), Queensland 8% (not verified). Plus normal state duty (about 4-5.5%, not
verified). Example AUD 700k Melbourne flat: 8% + FIRB fee = AUD 71,600 (10.2%) before normal duty.
**Running costs.** Annual vacancy fee if not lived in or genuinely for rent 183+ days a year
(FIRB); state land tax and foreign-owner land-tax surcharges (rates not verified).
**Tax for a non-resident.** Rent taxed at 30% from the first dollar up to AUD 135,000, then
37%/45% (PwC, Oct 2026). Gains: no 50% discount for non-residents; from 1 Jul 2027 the discount
is replaced by indexation with a minimum 30% tax (new dwellings may choose) (PwC). 15% foreign
resident withholding on sale: not verified (ATO blocked).
**Visa.** None linked to property.
**Liquidity/title.** Torrens title, very safe; but foreigners can only sell to locals or as new.
**Verdict: skip.** 15%+ entry costs, low yield, falling market. Min budget about Rs 3.5 Cr.
