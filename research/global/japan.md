Written 2026-11-15. Market research, not advice; the user decides. Sources [J#] at the end.

## Summary
Japan is the best of the three East Asian markets for this user: Research Affiliates expects 9.2%/yr
(10y, USD, 31 Oct 2026), CAPE 27.3 is mid-range, and only ~21% is tech, so it diversifies away from
QCOM/MSFT. Main risk: the yen (USD/JPY near 160; BOJ 1.25%, hike to 1.5% expected in December 2026).
Verdict: overweight, conditional - fund it from a QCOM sell-down via IJPA (IE00B4L5YX21, acc, 0.12%)
or VJPA; direct TSE stocks (100-share lots) only for learning.

User: 81% of Rs 2.23 Cr in QCOM + MSFT; IBKR cash account; prefers Irish UCITS (accumulating).

## 5. Growth drivers

- Governance: since 2015 the Corporate Governance Code and TSE push "management conscious of cost
  of capital and share price" [J13]; the code was revised again in July 2026 [J14] (details of the
  revision not read).
- Buybacks: record JPY 18.7 trn in FY2024; JPY 14.2 trn authorised Apr-Dec 2025, on track for a
  fifth record year (NLI Research via Nippon.com, 12 Feb 2026) [J13].
- Inflation is back: BOJ said on 30 Oct 2026 that underlying inflation is consistent with 2% for
  the first time since 2013; FY2027 core forecast 2.6% [J15]. Nominal growth helps profits.
- AI demand supports exports and capex; profits elevated (Vanguard, 9 Nov 2026) [J16]. EWJ sectors:
  industrials 22%, technology 21%, financials 19% (Yahoo) [J4].

## 6. Currency (yen) and BOJ path

- USD/JPY 159.9 on 13 Nov 2026; yen 3.3% weaker vs USD over 1y, but INR fell 6.1% vs USD, so
  JPY/INR rose 2.7% (0.59) [J4]. Japan warned of intervention as USD/JPY neared 160 (Reuters
  headline, 12 Nov 2026) [J17].
- BOJ policy rate 1.25% (held 30 Oct 2026, 8-1 vote, one member wanted 1.5%) [J15]. Vanguard expects
  +25 bp to 1.5% in December 2026 [J16]; 13 of 16 economists in a CNBC survey expect 1.75-2% by
  mid-2027 [J15].
- For an INR investor, a stronger yen from BOJ hikes would add to returns of unhedged funds; a
  sudden yen rally can also hit exporters' share prices. Unhedged is the simple choice.

## 7. Risks

- Yen: a fast yen rally (carry-trade unwind) can knock exporters; intervention near 160 [J17].
- Global tech selloff: Nikkei, KOSPI and TAIEX all fell sharply on 5-6 Nov 2026 on fears of US AI
  regulation after the US midterms [J18].
- Governance fatigue: some business leaders say returns to shareholders crowd out wages and capex
  [J13].
- No capital controls; politics stable (Takaichi government; energy subsidies hold down CPI [J15]).
- Concentration low: top holding MUFG 4.5%, Toyota 3.6%, Advantest 3.1% of EWJ (Yahoo) [J4].
  Advantest and other chip-equipment names are a small semis overlap with QCOM.

## 8. Dividend withholding and DTAA

Japan withholds 15.315% on listed-share dividends (15% + 2.1% surtax); India-Japan treaty caps it
at 10% [J19]. FTC in India is capped at 10%; reclaim the rest from Japan. IBKR's applied rate not
verified (../../tax/investment-types.md).

Tax (one line each, from ../../tax/investment-types.md): Japan stocks and US-listed ETFs - LTCG 12.5%
after 24 months, else slab; dividends at slab with FTC. Irish UCITS acc - no tax till sale.
US-situs assets above $60k - US estate tax.
