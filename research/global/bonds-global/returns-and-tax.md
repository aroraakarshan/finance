## 5. Drivers, currency and risks

- Up: high starting yields (best predictor of 10y bond return [AQR]); positive curves; JPM and
  Vanguard both raised bond forecasts in Q3 2026 [JPM][V].
- Down: yields still rising (fiscal deficits, inflation volatility named by JPM); stock-bond
  correlation is higher, so bonds hedge equity crashes less [JPM].
- Currency: unhedged foreign bonds add EUR/GBP/JPY risk on top of USD/INR; hedging to USD costs
  or earns the rate gap (AQR: "you can only earn your own risk-free rate risk-free"). See
  [cash-and-currencies](../cash-and-currencies.md) for USD/INR.
- EM local: currency is most of the risk; EM HY and capital controls (country specific).

## 6. Tax for this user (one line per type, from tax/investment-types.md)

- Bond UCITS ETFs and money-market funds: may be "always short-term" under AF s.76 (>65% debt
  "mutual fund by whatever name") -> 35.88% at any holding period; CA question open.
- Distributing bond UCITS: distributions at slab (35.88%); Ireland withholds 0%, so no FTC.
- Accumulating bond UCITS: no tax until sale (no deemed-distribution rule found) -> defers tax.
- Direct foreign bonds: interest at slab; "unlisted bond" always-ST risk (AF s.76(2)(b)).

[investment-universe](../../themes/investment-universe.md)) beats every foreign bond line post-tax,
but is INR and capped at Rs 1.5 L/yr, and you chose not to use it (16 Nov 2026). Foreign bonds carry the full INR-depreciation upside but at
the worst tax rate.
