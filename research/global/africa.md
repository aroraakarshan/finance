## 7. Risks

- South Africa: power and logistics, high unemployment; SARB raised rates in Sep 2026 citing
  Iran-war price shocks (Reuters 2026-09-23 headline) [A10]. Index is miners + banks + Naspers/Prosus
  (Prosus is Tencent-linked: China exposure inside "South Africa").
- Frontier: capital controls and FX shortages (Nigeria still adjusting FX rules, 2026-03-26 headline;
  Egypt on an IMF program, 2026-02-28 headline) [A11]; tiny, illiquid markets; no clean ETF.
- Amundi Pan Africa is synthetic (swap counterparty risk) [A3].

## 8. Dividend withholding (PwC tax summaries, South Africa reviewed 2026-05-29) [A13]
