Written 2026-11-15. Market research, not advice; the user decides. Sources [T#] at the end.

## Summary
Taiwan is a near-pure semis market (TSMC over 40% of TAIEX, tech 71% of EWT) at a record CAPE of
49.0 (98th percentile), with the lowest Research Affiliates forecast of the three (4.1%/yr). For a
user already 57% in semis it adds risk, not spread. Verdict: skip; a global or EM fund already holds
some Taiwan. If ever wanted: XMTD (LU0292109187, acc, 0.65%). IBKR TWSE access for Indian residents
is not verified.

User: 81% of Rs 2.23 Cr in QCOM + MSFT; 57% in semis (QCOM, AVGO, NVDA); IBKR cash account;
prefers Irish UCITS (accumulating).

## 5. Growth drivers

- AI hardware: Taiwan is "well over 80%" exposed to AI-related revenue (Goldman's Tim Moe via
  CNBC, May 2026) [T8]. TSMC dominates leading-edge foundry and AI packaging (Tech Times, Oct 2026,
  headline) [T15].
- Breadth beyond TSMC: the TWSE chairman is pushing more listings to show a "technology island"
  (Fortune, 31 Aug 2026, headline) [T16]. EWT sectors: technology 71%, financials 16% (Yahoo) [T7].
- Taiwan's market passed India to become the 5th largest in May 2026 [T17]; India regained 5th on
  12 Nov 2026 (BeInCrypto headline) [T18].

## 6. Currency (TWD) vs USD/INR

USD/TWD 32.94 on 13 Nov 2026; TWD 6.0% weaker vs USD over 1y, roughly flat vs INR (INR also fell
6.1% vs USD; derived) [T7]. Taiwan has managed FX; the regulator was ordered on 6 Nov 2026 to
"stabilize" the stock market after a 9% intraday fall [T19].

## 7. Risks

- **Overlap with QCOM (flag)**: Taiwan is a semis market - TSMC 40%+ of TAIEX, tech 71% of EWT.
  MediaTek competes with Qualcomm in phone chips, and TSMC makes chips for QCOM, NVDA and AVGO
  (industry knowledge, not verified here). Taiwan moves with the user's existing holdings.
- Concentration and AI cycle: on 6 Nov 2026 TSMC fell up to 9.8% intraday on fears of US AI
  regulation after the midterms; TAIEX closed -4.5% [T19].
- Geopolitics and supply chain: Goldman flags geopolitical tension and chemical/gas supply
  disruption risk; Taiwan is a large energy importer [T8].
- Access friction: local registration rules and limited broker support [T4].

## 8. Dividend withholding and DTAA

Taiwan withholds 21% on dividends to non-residents; the India-Taiwan agreement caps it at 12.5%
[T20]. Whether IBKR applies the treaty rate: not verified.

Tax (from ../../tax/investment-types.md): foreign shares, ADRs and US-listed ETFs - LTCG 12.5% after
24 months, else slab; dividends at slab with FTC. Irish (and assumed Lux) UCITS acc - no tax till
sale. US-situs assets above $60k - US estate tax.
