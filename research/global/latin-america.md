## 5. Growth drivers

- Brazil: very high real rates leave room to cut - Selic 13.75% vs IPCA inflation 4.63% (12m to
  Oct 2026) = about 9% real (BCB) [L10]. But the central bank stopped cutting on 2026-11-04 after
  Lula's surprise re-election (25 Oct runoff); stocks and the real fell sharply on 2026-10-26 [L11].
- Mexico: nearshoring to the US; but new FDI stalled on USMCA review doubts (Reuters 2026-09-01
  headline) [L12]. Banxico rate 6.25% (2026-11-05, TradingView headline; not verified at Banxico) [L12].
- Chile: copper USD 6.08/lb, +19.5% in 1y (HG=F, 2026-11-13) [L4]; Codelco-SQM lithium venture
  approved (Jan 2026 headlines) [L13]; pro-market president Kast in office since 2026-03-10 [L13].

## 8. Dividend withholding (PwC tax summaries, read 2026-11-15) [L14]

- Brazil: 10% on dividends to non-residents from 2026 (Law 15,270/2025; was 0%); India treaty cap 15%.
  Mexico 10% (India treaty 10%). Chile 35% "additional tax" less credit for company tax (full credit
  for treaty residents; India is in PwC's Chile treaty list) - net cost not verified.
  Indian tax: dividends at slab with FTC; LTCG 12.5% after 24 months; Irish UCITS acc: no tax
  till sale; Indian international FoF: 24 m / 12.5% (`../../tax/investment-types.md`).
