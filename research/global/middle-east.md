# Middle East - Saudi Arabia, UAE, Israel

Written 2026-11-15. Market research, not advice; the user decides. Sources [M#] at the end.

## Summary
- Skip Saudi, UAE and Israel as separate holdings; any Gulf exposure via a broad EM fund (EIMI) is enough.
- IBKR lists Tadawul, ADX/DFM and TASE; Saudi opened to all foreigners 2026-02-01 (QFI removed, 10%/49% caps kept).
- Saudi flat over 5 yrs (KSA -7.0%); UAE cheap (P/E 10.1, yield 4.5%); Israel dear (P/E 18.2 vs 10y avg 11.7).
- 2026 Iran war is the main risk; only clean UCITS route is IKSA (Saudi, 0.60%) - at most 1-2% as an oil hedge.

Why look: oil, banks and (Israel) software/defence - little overlap with his QCOM/MSFT book.
Why careful: 2026 is a war year (US-Israel war with Iran; UAE markets shut 2-3 Mar 2026 [M14];
attacks on Saudi energy sites and Strait of Hormuz disruption, 12 Nov 2026 headline [M15]).

- SAR and AED are pegged to USD; the shekel is free-floating.
- Yahoo shows USD/SAR 3.678 on 2026-11-13 and -1.8% in a year [M7]; the long-standing peg is 3.75
  (SAMA, not re-read today). Discrepancy: check before relying on Yahoo SAR data.
- USD/ILS 3.19; shekel +17.5% stronger vs USD over 3 years [M7]. INR fell 6.1% vs USD in 1y
  (USD/INR 94.24) [M7], so peg markets added that much in INR terms.

## 7. Risks

- War: Iran conflict hit Gulf markets (UAE halt 2-3 Mar 2026) [M14]; Saudi energy sites attacked
  (2026-11-12) [M15]. Israel: election deadlock sent stocks and shekel down (2026-10-28 headline) [M13].
- Oil: Saudi deficit "more than doubles on war, Aramco fees" (Bloomberg, 2026-11-03 headline) [M15]
  even with high oil; a price drop would hurt more. Saudi index is 48% banks, 15% energy [M6].
- Concentration: IKSA 28 stocks [M6]. Foreign caps (49%) can stop buying in popular names [M4].
- Israel: Nvidia-linked risk to the economy flagged by the treasury (2026-11-14 headline) [M13] -
  adds to his chip exposure rather than diversifying it.
