## 7. Risks

- US-China: one-year truce from 10 Nov 2025 (tariff exclusions, export-control pauses) [C15];
  US DoD added Alibaba, Baidu, BYD to its Chinese military companies list on 13 Feb 2026, then
  withdrew the document [C15]; Nikkei 13 Nov 2026: "truce masks rising tensions" (headline).
- ADR delisting: under the HFCAA, US trading is banned after two consecutive years in which the
  PCAOB cannot inspect the auditor; the 2022 protocol restored access but the PCAOB can issue new
  determinations at any time [C16]. HK listings and UCITS funds holding HK lines reduce this risk.
- Policy risk (tax, sector crackdowns), property slump, VIE structures, Stock Connect quotas.
- Concentration: Tencent + Alibaba 23% of ICHN; MSCI China's 5y volatility 30%, max fall 63% (10y).
