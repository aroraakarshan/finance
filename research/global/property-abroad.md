## 1. Indian rules in one screen (india-rules.md)
- Allowed: buying with LRS money ($250k per person per year; family members can each use their
  own limit and co-own), with income or sale proceeds already held abroad (for example RSU sale
  money), by inheritance, or jointly with a non-resident relative (OI Rules 2022 r.21(2)).
- Not allowed: an Indian bank loan for it, or a foreign mortgage (no route for individuals under
  the Borrowing and Lending Regs; only up to $250k from a relative abroad). No gift-from-non-resident route.
- TCS 20% on LRS remittances above Rs 10 L a year (credited against tax).
- Rent and sale money: reinvest abroad or bring home within 180 days (FEMA 9(R) reg 7; LRS MD para 17).
- Report every year in Schedule FA; the Black Money Act Rs 20 L relief does not cover property.
- Succession follows the law of the country where the property is (probate abroad).

## 3. Changes found vs common belief (dated in the notes)
- Australia's ban on foreigners buying existing homes runs to 30 Jun 2029; Canada's to 1 Jan 2027
  (Budget 2026 proposes 2029 for resale homes).
- Greece: 15% transfer tax for non-EU buyers proposed from Jul 2027 (bill of 3 Nov 2026, not law).
- Portugal: 7.5% IMT for non-residents since 25 May 2026; Spain's 100% surtax bill lapsed.
- Georgia's residence threshold rose to USD 150k (1 Mar 2026).
- Malaysia foreign stamp duty 8% from 1 Jan 2026; Mauritius foreign registration duty 10% from 1 Jul 2026.
- Sri Lanka removed the "4th floor and up" condo rule in 2018.
- Singapore: no ABSD relief for Indians under CECA (IRAS).

## 4. Tax (one line per item; finance-tax)
From `finance-tax`, `tax/property-and-alternatives-2026-11.md` (2026-11-15; new regime, 15% surcharge):
- Rent: taxed at slab after a 30% standard deduction (AF s.22); new regime gives no interest deduction
  for self-occupied property and no house-property loss set-off (s.202); interest paid abroad needs TDS
  or an agent in India (s.22(6), CA flag); foreign tax on rent credited via FSI/TR.
- Sale: long-term after 24 months at 14.95% (property bought before 23 Jul 2024 may choose 20% with
  indexation); the s.54/54F-type exemptions (AF s.82/86) need a new house in India.
- TCS 20% on LRS remittances above Rs 10 L, credited against tax. Report in Schedule FA Table C, FSI, TR.
