## 1. Access for an Indian resident

- IBKR: **yes, since 7 May 2026**, but only through Interactive Brokers LLC and other non-Indian
  entities. IBKR's KRX page: "KRX is not available to residents of Korea, or clients of IBIN
  [IB India] and IBSJ" [K1][K2]. So an Indian resident at IB LLC can trade KRX; an IB India client
  cannot (Livemint experts say the same [K3]). Which entity holds this user's account: not verified
  (../themes/ibkr-global/rules.md section 2). Enable: Client Portal > Settings > Trading Permissions >
  Stocks > Korea [K1].
- Brief's lead "foreign investor registration, likely not for retail" is out of date: IBKR uses
  Korea's new omnibus account (with Samsung Securities), which removes the per-investor
  registration hurdle; retail clients are trading through it [K4]. 1,800+ KRX stocks; Nextrade
  ATS added 30 Jun 2026 [K2][K5].
- Commission: tiered only, 0.06% min KRW 4,000 (no fixed plan) [K6]. Lot size: not verified.
- LRS: listed shares/ETFs allowed. Korean ETFs via the omnibus account: not yet (regulators plan
  to extend it to ETFs/ETNs [K4]).

## 5. Growth drivers

- Corporate Value-up Programme (FSC, launched Feb 2024): 801 companies had published value plans
  by end Oct 2026, 83.8% of market cap; Value-Up Index +49.8% YTD (KRX via BigGo, 5 Nov 2026) [K20].
- Shareholder returns: buybacks KRW 8.2 trn (2023) -> 20.1 trn (2025); cancellations 4.8 -> 21.4
  trn; in Oct 2026 SK Hynix approved a KRW 20 trn buyback and Samsung about KRW 30 trn of dividends [K20].
- Law changes: Commercial Act (Jul/Aug 2025) adds shareholder loyalty duty, cumulative voting,
  one-third independent directors from Jul 2026; dividend tax reform Jan 2026 [K14].
- AI memory (HBM) demand: Samsung + SK Hynix were 55.5% of KOSPI market cap in Jul 2026 vs 22% at
  end 2023 [K14]. In EWY: SK Hynix 23.8%, Samsung 23.0%; technology 52.7% (Yahoo) [K11].

## 6. Currency (won) vs USD/INR

- USD/KRW 1,336 on 14 Nov 2026; won 9.0% stronger vs USD over 1y, and INR fell 6.1% vs USD, so the won rose about 17% vs INR (derived) [K11].
- Won is not freely convertible offshore; MSCI kept Korea in EM on 23 Jun 2026 for this reason and
  the rigid investor-ID system [K21]. 24-hour onshore USD/KRW trading began 6 Jul 2026 [K21].

## 7. Risks

- **Overlap with QCOM (flag)**: over half of KOSPI and ~47% of EWY is two memory makers. Memory is
  the most cyclical part of semis; adding Korea doubles the user's existing semis bet, not
  diversifies it.
- Volatility: KOSPI circuit breakers fired 7 times by Jul 2026, more than in all prior history;
  single-stock leveraged ETFs on Samsung/SK Hynix since May 2026 [K14]. RA volatility 30.7% [K15].
- Foreign selling: record net KRW 148.3 trn sold in H1 2026 [K14]; won convertibility limits.
- North Korea and chaebol governance (Korea ranked 8th of 12 in ACGA 2023) [K14].
- Energy importer: oil shocks hurt terms of trade [K22].

## 8. Dividend withholding and DTAA

Korea withholds 20% plus 10% local tax (22%) on dividends to non-residents; India-Korea treaty
caps it at 15% [K23]. Whether the omnibus route applies the treaty rate automatically: not verified.

Tax (from ../../tax/investment-types.md): foreign shares and US-listed ETFs - LTCG 12.5% after
24 months, else slab; dividends at slab with FTC. Irish (and assumed Lux) UCITS acc - no tax till
sale. US-situs assets above $60k - US estate tax.
