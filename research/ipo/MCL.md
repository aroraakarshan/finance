## 2. Business
- Mines in Talcher and Ib Valley coalfields, Odisha; 17 mines (14 opencast, 3 underground) at
  2026-06-30 [NT, citing DRHP]. 99.4% of FY26 coal from surface miners [LM].
- Production 206.10 MT (FY24), 225.17 MT (FY25), 218.31 MT (FY26); dispatch 210.74 MT FY26 [NT] [LM].
- Share: 28.3% of CIL output FY26 [NT, CRISIL] (28.4% per [MC]); about 21% of India output [LM] [MC];
  22.4% of India non-coking coal [NT].
- Mostly thermal non-coking coal; thermal power = 68.62% of product sales FY26; top 10 customers =
  55.94% of revenue; top 10 contractors = 60.02% of contract costs [NT, DRHP].
- Q1 FY27 (Apr-Jun 2026): production 42.53 MT (-17.55% YoY) but dispatch 54.80 MT (+5.91%) [NT].
- Realisation per tonne, cost per tonne, MCL's own FSA vs e-auction split: not verified (in the RHP).
- Coal India level (proxy): e-auction premium over FSA price 90% in Oct 2026, 94% Sep, 54% average
  H1 FY27 [CNBC 2026-11-02]; one AI summary says 140% for Oct SWMA auctions [TV 2026-11-02] -
  conflicting, treat 90% as better sourced. Long-run average premium about 60% [LM2 2026-03-12].

## 7. Risks
- Demand: about 40 GW renewables vs 8 GW thermal added in first 10 months of FY26 (CEA); thermal
  generation -4.4%; CIL volumes -2.7% in 11M FY26 [LM2 2026-03-12]. Short-term rebound: CIL Oct 2026
  production +19.3%, offtake +18.7% [CNBC 2026-11-02]; power-plant coal stocks 35.13 MT -> 18.60 MT
  (end-Jul to 1 Oct) [TV]. Near term looks firm; long-term volume is the question.
- Captive/commercial mines share of output 21% in FY26 vs 9% FY20 [LM2] -> CIL group losing share.
- Pricing: FSA (notified) prices set by CIL under government control, not market; e-auction premium
  swings (over 3x FSA in Sep 2022 quarter vs about 60% long-run) [LM2].
- Potential state mineral-levy liability for MCL estimated at Rs 31,227.80 cr by CIL legal dept (CAG
  note, DRHP risk factor 46) [NT]; about 1.7x FY25 net worth; MCL says impact "not ascertainable".
- Controlling seller: CIL keeps 90% and may sell up to 25% in total -> more shares later; dividend
  policy set by the parent/government. PSU discount and policy risk. ESG: many foreign funds avoid
  thermal coal (general point, no figure verified).
- Single-state (Odisha) concentration; clearances, land, rail evacuation [LM].
