## 4. Breaking the FDs early

HDFC rule (FD rate page, "Important notes" 1, since 22 Jul 2023): premature withdrawal earns the
booking-date rate for the period held minus 1%. Held 192 days to 18 Nov -> "6 months 1 day to 9
months" 5.65% (card from 4 Nov 2026; 10 May card not in hand, same 6.25% 1-year rate) -> 4.65%.
All 9 FDs, Rs 47.77 L. Breaking on 18 Nov loses Rs 40,824 (Rs 4,536 per FD: 17,604 at 6.25% vs
13,068 at 4.65%, 10 May - 18 Nov) and frees Rs 48.94 L to reinvest for 173 days. Net gain = after
