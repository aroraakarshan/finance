# Old vs new regime with rent (tax year 2026-27)

## Summary

- The new regime stays better: about Rs 1.75-1.93 L less tax than the old regime, even with the HRA exemption for Rs 33,000/month rent.
- HRA exemption under the old regime would be only about Rs 0.88 L (rent Rs 3.96 L minus 10% of basic Rs 3.08 L), worth about Rs 31,500 of tax.
- Rent agreement and landlord PAN: no tax use for him on the new regime (they matter only for an HRA claim, old regime). No TDS on rent: rent is under Rs 50,000/month.
- Nothing to do for tax. A rent agreement is still useful as a tenancy record (not a tax matter).

## Figures (approximate, 15 Nov 2026)

| Item | New regime | Old regime |
|---|---|---|
| Income before deductions | Rs 1.06 Cr (`employer-nps.md`, `fd-and-advance-tax.md`) | same |
| Standard deduction | Rs 75,000 | Rs 50,000 |
| HRA exemption | not allowed | about Rs 87,700 (least of: HRA received, rent - 10% of basic, 50% of basic; basic Rs 2,56,972/month, Oct 2026 payslip) |
| 80C (EPF covers it) | not allowed | Rs 1,50,000 |
| 80D health insurance | not allowed | Rs 25,000 assumed |
| 80CCD(1B) own NPS | not allowed | Rs 50,000 only if he invests (not now) |
| Tax incl. 15% surcharge and 4% cess | about Rs 32.74 L | about Rs 34.67 L (Rs 34.49 L with 80CCD(1B)) |
| Difference | - | old regime costs about Rs 1.75-1.93 L more |

Slabs: new regime 0-4 L nil, then 5% steps per Rs 4 L to 30% above Rs 24 L (Finance Act 2025, unchanged
for 2026-27 per `ca-questions.md`); old regime 0-2.5 L nil, 5%, 20%, 30% above Rs 10 L. Employer NPS
(80CCD(2)) is allowed in both regimes (14% new, 10% old) and is not used now, so it does not change
the comparison (`employer-nps.md`).

## Rent agreement, landlord PAN, TDS

| Point | Rule | For him |
|---|---|---|
| HRA exemption | Old regime only (1961 s.10(13A); 2025 Act salary exemption list); new regime bars it | not claimed |
| Landlord PAN | Needed by the employer for an HRA claim above Rs 1 L/yr (CBDT circular on TDS from salaries) | not needed |
| Rent agreement | Evidence for an HRA claim | not needed for tax |
| TDS on rent by an individual | 1961 s.194-IB (2025 Act TDS table): only if rent exceeds Rs 50,000 per month; 2% from 1 Oct 2024 | Rs 33,000/month: no TDS |
