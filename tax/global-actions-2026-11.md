It applies in proportion to the fund's US weight: XDEW (S&P 500 EW): all US; AVGS (global small-cap value), XDWH (MSCI World Health Care), XDW0 (MSCI World Energy): their US share of holdings (check each factsheet); EXUS (World ex USA) and IJPA (Japan): none. Non-US holdings suffer their own countries' withholding inside the fund, also not creditable.

**N10 Irish CAT on Irish fund units.** Ireland charges CAT on Irish-situs assets whatever the
residence of the giver or heir (PwC Ireland, reviewed 5 Aug 2026). But Capital Acquisitions Tax
Consolidation Act 2003 s.75(2) exempts a "unit of an investment undertaking" (TCA 1997 s.739B) in
a gift or inheritance if the disponer (at the disposition) and the heir (at the inheritance) are
neither domiciled nor ordinarily resident in Ireland. So the Irish UCITS rows (#1-3, 5, 8, 13, 22,
29, 31) carry no Irish CAT while you and your heirs stay outside Ireland. That each ETF (plc or
ICAV) is a s.739B investment undertaking is likely, not checked; text read is the enacted 2003
version (later amendments not checked). Directly held Irish shares (ACN, #23) are not units:
CAT can apply there. Sources (2026-11-15): https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2003/act/1/section/75/enacted/en/html ;
https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ireland/individual/other-taxes

**N11 Buybacks.** AF s.69(1) taxes as capital gain the consideration any shareholder gets "from
any company for the purchase of its own shares" (not limited to Indian companies). The omitted
AF s.2(40)(f) read "any payment by a company on purchase of its own shares from a shareholder as
per section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013" (Indian companies only); omitted w.e.f. 1 Apr 2026 by
Act 4 of 2026 (Memo p.64: "consideration received on buy-back shall be chargeable to tax under the
head 'Capital gains'", TY 2026-27 on). The brief's premise that s.69 does not apply to foreign
companies is not what the text says; either route gives a capital gain for AJOT. A UK trust tender
is often done by a broker buying as principal: then it is a plain sale (s.67), same tax. The
start date of the old rule (1 Oct 2024, Finance (No.2) Act 2024) is from memory. STT on exchange
tenders: SEBI tender-through-exchange mechanism, not re-fetched; check the contract note. Sources:
Act text and memo as below (2026-11-15).

**N12 G-secs.** Interest on Central/State Government securities is outside TDS (AF s.393(4) Table
Sl.6(a)(iii), except the taxable savings bonds/FRSB). AF s.2(112) "zero coupon bond" covers only
notified bonds of infrastructure companies, PSUs and banks, so a T-bill is not one; it is under
12 months anyway. Whether a G-sec held in RBI Retail Direct counts as "listed in a recognised stock
exchange in India" (12 months, 14.95%) or is an "unlisted bond" under s.76(2)(b) (always slab) is
not settled in the text; India.md dropped discounted G-secs (+0.1-0.2 pp), so plan on slab.
Secondary-market discount on a coupon bond is treated as capital gain, not interest (likely; CBDT
Circular 2/2002 on deep-discount bonds concerns original-issue discount, not re-fetched).

**N13 Tax-free bonds.** The AF Schedule II row reads "Any income covered under section 10(15)(iii)
or (15)(iv)(c) ... (15)(iv)(h) or (36) of the Income-tax Act, 1961 ... subject to the conditions as
provided therein", Column C Nil (Sl. No. likely 17; the text file loses the numbering). 1961
s.10(15)(iv)(h) exempts interest paid by a public sector company on notified bonds; the condition
is on the bond (holder registered with the issuer, met by demat), so a secondary buyer gets it too
(likely; notification texts not re-fetched). Capital loss set-off: AF s.108 bars only LT loss vs
ST gain; carry-forward s.111(1)(a)(ii).

**N14 Clubbing.** AF s.99(1)(a)(ii) clubs income "arising directly or indirectly ... from assets
transferred directly or indirectly" to the spouse "otherwise than for adequate consideration";
s.99(1)(b) son's wife, s.99(1)(c) minor child; nothing for adult children or parents. That
income on clubbed income (reinvested gains) is not clubbed, and that a loan at a market interest
rate is "adequate consideration", rest on case law, not the Act text (likely). A gift to the
spouse is still a transfer: keep a gift letter and a bank trail. Each PAN's Rs 2 L buyback cap is a
SEBI point (finance-legal); clubbing only moves the tax to you.
