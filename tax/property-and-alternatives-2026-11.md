# Property abroad and alternative investments: one tax line each (Nov 2026)

## Summary

- Tax line for property abroad and 16 alternative investments (plan only; open points in `ca-questions.md`).
- Foreign property: rent taxed at 35.88% after a 30% deduction; sale long-term after 24 months at 14.95%; foreign tax is credited; Schedule FA; TCS 20% above Rs 10 L a year on the money sent.
- A foreign mortgage is unworkable without a CA and the AD bank (note N1).
- P2P lending, invoice discounting, MLDs, small finance bank and GIFT City FDs and annuities are taxed at 35.88%; MLDs are always short-term.

## Notes (flags)

**N1 Foreign mortgage.** Interest payable outside India is not deductible unless tax was deducted
under Chapter XIX-B or there is an agent in India (AF s.22(6)) [Act]; interest a resident pays on a
non-business loan is probably deemed to accrue in India, so TDS by the user may be needed (CA).
FEMA limits residents borrowing abroad (Borrowing and Lending Regs 2018, unv). Treat a foreign
mortgage as unworkable without CA + AD bank.

**N2 Source-country tax.** DTAA immovable-property and gains articles let the property country tax
rent and sale gain; India credits up to its own tax on that income (r.76). US property (unv): 30% on
gross rent unless net-basis election (US return); FIRPTA 15% withholding on sale price; estate tax.

**N3 SM REIT / SDI.** AF s.2(21) names only REITs and InvITs registered under the 2014 Regs; SM
REIT schemes are registered under the same Regs (SEBI amendment Mar 2024, unv), so business-trust
treatment (12 m, AF s.196/198, STT) is likely; confirm with the issuer's tax note. SDIs are not
"bonds/debentures" by name; whether s.76(2)(b) catches unlisted PTCs is open.

**N4 P2P / invoice discounting.** No AF s.93 deduction for a non-business lender's bad loans;
capital-loss claim untested. A purchased receivable might give a capital gain (24 m) instead of
interest (CA). No platform-specific TDS in the Act.

**N5 Pre-IPO.** STT-on-purchase exclusions (1961 Notif. 60/2018) -> AF s.198(5) mapping unv;
lock-in is not a tax issue.

**N6 Personal effects.** Excluded from capital asset, so no gain or loss for genuine personal use;
watches/wine bought as investment may fail "personal use" -> capital asset (CA). Gold/numismatic
coins: "archaeological collections" -> capital asset. Schedule AL threshold
is total income above Rs 1 Cr (ITR-2 AY2026-27 validation rule 456); user is above it.

**N7 GIFT City.** LRS FAQ Q18 says an OBU is not a foreign branch for residents' FCY accounts;
later RBI circulars allowed LRS to IFSC (unv): AD bank to confirm. IFSC is in India, so probably no Schedule FA (`investment-types.md` s.2 row "GIFT
City IFSC funds"). FX gain on a deposit: capital gain vs other sources unsettled (CA).

**N8 ULIP.** Equity test must hold "throughout the term" (AF s.198(8)(II)) [Act]. Whether the
s.198 STT condition is met on ULIP redemption: likely yes, insurers collect STT on it (Finance Bill 2026 memo).

**N9 Royalties.** Royalty rights are not an OPI listed security; LRS fit under OI Rules 2022 unv
(AD bank). No cost amortisation against the income (likely; CA).
