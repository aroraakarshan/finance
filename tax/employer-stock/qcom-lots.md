### 3.3 Is choosing the lots allowed? (no CA; decision here)

| Source | What it says | For these shares |
|---|---|---|
| AF s.67(7)(c), (8) (1961 s.45(2A)) | FIFO for cost and holding period of securities held through a depository, meanings from the Depositories Act 1996 | IBKR holds the shares in the US, not in an Indian depository: not directly covered. No other lot rule; no averaging for shares |
| CBDT Circular 768 (24 Jun 1998) | FIFO for demat holdings because demat shares lose their identity; physical shares are "identified separately" | Same scope as s.45(2A) |
| CIT v. Bai Shirinbai K. Kooka, Supreme Court, 23 Feb 1962, AIR 1963 SC 477, 1962 Supp (3) SCR 391 ([Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/1879530/), read 2026-11-17) | Shares held as investment and later converted to stock-in-trade: the majority held that the trading profit is computed from the market value on the conversion date (1 Apr 1945), not the original cost; the "actual cost" view was the dissent (Sarkar J.). No word on FIFO, lot identification or which shares were sold | Does not support specific identification or FIFO either way; not relied on |
| DCIT v. Dr. Prannoy Roy, ITAT Delhi, 14 Jun 2019 ([Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/51561346/)) | FIFO applied to each demat account separately; lot identification across accounts rejected | Demat; shows identity is judged by the record that holds the shares |
| Megasolis Renewable Pvt. Ltd. v. ACIT 7(2)(2), ITAT Mumbai "D" Bench, ITA No. 7904/Mum/2025 (AY 2016-17), pronounced 5 May 2026 ([Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/4842525/), read 2026-11-15) | Physical shares; the company sold its high-cost lots. Para 8.1: "The purpose of following Last-in-First-Out or the so called method of "specific lot identification" to determine which shares had been sold was clearly a device to evade taxes." Para 8.3: "the principle embodied in Section 45(2A), namely application of the FIFO method, is equally relevant and should be extended to physical holdings as well to prevent arbitrary tax planning." Para 8.4: "in respect of shares held in physical form also, FIFO method should be applied." | Against lot choice. Facts differ: its books used average cost (return and books disagreed) and it was one bulk sale to one buyer |

Decision: **user decision 17 Nov 2026 (via finance-main): specific identification for foreign shares at
foreign brokers (Fidelity, IBKR, INDmoney) - report the actual lot sold; FIFO kept as the fallback if the
officer disagrees.** User's basis: AF s.67(7) (1961 s.45(2A)) and Circular 768 cover Indian demat holdings
only; Megasolis concerned physical shares of an Indian company; Fidelity and IBKR identify each lot. (The
user also cited Kooka; it does not decide this point, see the row above.) This replaces the FIFO decision
of 15 Nov.

| Selling a new MSFT vest or ESPP purchase at once | That new lot |

### 3.5 IBKR steps

IBKR's default is FIFO, and its "Highest Cost" ranks by IBKR's US cost (ESPP lots at the price paid, for
example 2022-01-31 ranks 9th in India but 19th at IBKR) and takes ST DRIP lots with a dollar loss, so it is
not the Indian order. Use **Specific Lot** on each sale day. IBKR's guides show no lot choice on the order
ticket; the match is made in the Tax Optimizer after the fill.

| Step | When | What | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | This week | TWS: Portfolio, right-click QCOM > Change Tax Lots, answer No to the Tax Loss Harvesting pop-up; check that the open lots show the dates and quantities in 3.1 (IBKR cost $101,633.47). If IBKR shows the Dec 2025 transfer date or merged lots, match by quantity and cost and keep the ETrade confirmations as the link | [Launching the Tax Optimizer](https://www.ibkrguides.com/traderworkstation/launching-the-tax-optimizer.htm) |
| 2 | Before P1 | Client Portal > Performance & Reports > Tax Documents (or Menu > Reporting > Tax Documents) > Tax Optimizer panel > Account Match Method: leave FIFO (the fallback if a match is missed; Specific Lot cannot be the account default) | [Tax Optimizer (Client Portal)](https://www.ibkrguides.com/clientportal/performanceandstatements/taxoptimizer.htm), [Change account default](https://www.ibkrguides.com/traderworkstation/change-account-default-match-method.htm), [FIFO is the default](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/campus/glossary-terms/first-in-first-out-fifo/) |
