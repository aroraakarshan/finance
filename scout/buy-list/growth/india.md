## How the list was built
1. Screen: 466 Nifty 500 stocks priced at Rs 50 or more (holdings and buy-list names removed). 76 passed:
   revenue and profit growth of 15% or more (latest quarter, year on year), ROE of 15% or more, P/E under 60.
2. Dropped: peak-cycle profits (refiners, shipping, metals: the sector view says profits may be at a peak,
   `research/sectors/ranking/india.md`), Nifty Bank members (already in BANKBEES), and names with a governance
   flag (pledge). Kept themes you do not hold: mid banks, small lenders, hotels, brokers, solar cells. Polycab and
   Premier sit next to your electrical and power shares.
3. Checked each one against its Q2 FY27 results (company filings, Oct-Nov 2026), screener.in (13 Nov close) and the
   NSE shareholding filing.

What "double" needs: 2x in 3 years is 26% a year; in 5 years 15% a year. The Nifty Smallcap 250 itself returned
12.96% a year over 5 years and 14.1% over 10 years (Anand Rathi and Strota index pages, 13 Nov 2026), so a
5-year double is only a little above the index; a 3-year double needs fast profit growth and a higher P/E.

Which of your past stocks doubled is not in the files (`portfolio/summary.md` and `details.md` list only the
current AngelOne shares: power and electrical gear (TECHNOE, KPIL, SUZLON), machinery (ELGIEQUIP, KIRLPNU, TEGA,
AZAD), pipes and cement). The list matches that style where the price allows (ranks 3 and 6).
