## Method
- Universe: index-provider or ETF holdings files, read 16 Nov 2026: iShares EXSA (STOXX Europe 600, 13 Nov, London lines removed), iShares ISF + MIDD (FTSE 100 + FTSE 250, 13 Nov, 57 investment trusts and funds removed), JPX `topixweight_e.csv` (Core30 + Large70 + Mid400 = TOPIX 500, as of 30 Sep 2026), iShares IOZ (S&P/ASX 200, 13 Nov). Proxies, because no free constituent file was found: Hong Kong = HK lines of iShares ICHN (MSCI China) and IPXJ (MSCI Pacific ex Japan) plus the 100 largest HK lines of ISFE (MSCI Far East ex Japan small cap), for Hang Seng Composite large + mid; Singapore = MSCI Singapore and MSCI Singapore small-cap lines (IPXJ, ISFE) plus the STI (Wikipedia), for STI + mid caps; Taiwan = the 50 largest TWSE lines of iShares IFFF (MSCI AC Far East ex Japan), for the Taiwan 50.
- Data: Yahoo Finance via yfinance 1.8.0, pulled 23:19 16 Nov to 02:40 17 Nov IST (cached, about 1 call/s, crumb checked, no 429s): 10-year split-adjusted closes (16 Nov closes for TSE/HKEX/SGX/TWSE taken from Yahoo info, the daily bars stopped at 13 Nov), info, 4 annual statements, EPS consensus. Ratios in the reporting currency (price converted with yfinance FX; GBp / 100); Yahoo EPS checked against net income / total shares and its unit chosen to match (rules in `finance-scout-screens/work/CHANGES.txt` items 7-8).
- Drops (as the India screen): price under $5 (trading rules), a loss in any of the last 3 fiscal years or TTM (continuing operations), 3-year average ROE under 12% (8% for Japan, where ROE is structurally lower), net debt/EBITDA over 3 or 3-year FCF/net income under 0.6 (non-financials). A pre-filter on Yahoo info skipped statements for TTM losses, TTM ROE under 4% (under 6% for names over $5).
- Score (percentile ranks inside each market, weights as the India and US screens: value 0.30, growth 0.25, quality 0.25, trend 0.20). Value: P/E (P/B for banks and insurers) and EV/EBITDA percentile in own monthly history (fiscal-year figures known 60 days after year end; 2-4.6 years, median 3.7) and TTM P/E or P/B vs the median of the same Yahoo sector in the same market. Growth: 3-year EPS CAGR (last 4 fiscal years) and consensus EPS growth per year from the last reported to the next fiscal year (estimate). Quality: 3-year ROE, ROIC (EBIT after tax / equity + debt), net debt/EBITDA, FCF/NI. Trend: price vs 200-day average, 6- and 12-month price return.
- Verdict (same rule as `us.md`): BUY = above the 200-day average and at or below its own median valuation; BUY BELOW X = above the own median (X = price at the own median multiple, in the quote currency); WATCH = below the 200-day average, chip or semicap name, data flag, no own-history valuation, or Taiwan. Corr = weekly USD returns vs QCOM over 52 weeks (the US screen uses daily returns; Asian closes come before the US open, so daily returns understate it).

Columns: Value = multiple on the last fiscal year (percentile in own history, years of history, own median); EV/EBITDA percentile; TTM P/E or P/B vs sector median. Growth = 3-y EPS CAGR; fwd = consensus per year (estimate, number of analysts). Quality = 3-FY averages. Withholding = dividend tax at source for an Indian resident (India credits at most the treaty rate; HIGH = over 20%); rates from `tax/new-investments.md` section 2 (finance-tax; PwC rates re-checked 17 Nov 2026; reclaim forms and IBKR's at-source rates unverified), others marked unverified. Lot = one 100-share TSE lot at JPY/INR of 16 Nov.
Indian tax on all of these: dividends at slab 35.88% less the credit; gains 14.95% after 24 months, 35.88% before; report in Schedule FA A3. Whether a reclaim is worth filing at a Rs 4.2 L position: [README tax lines](README.md#tax-lines).

## Data errors and checks
- Second source (stockanalysis.com, one page each, viewed 17 Nov): Visional P/E 22.53 (ours 22.38), easyJet 12.72 (12.76), TUI 6.71 (6.79); ROE and 200-day averages agree.
- Forward estimates: Yahoo's current-year consensus can be wrong (easyJet 0y EPS 0.117 vs last year 0.653 and stockanalysis forward P/E 23), which made +1y/0y growth +207%; growth is therefore taken from the last reported year to the next year, per year. 4 scored names have no consensus.
- Yahoo EPS unit: 54 lines had EPS in the quote currency although the company reports in another (Nordic and Swiss lines: Genmab, Vestas, Equinor, Evolution, Maersk, Novonesis, Hexagon) - the opposite of the rule for Shell, TTE and Rio; the unit that matches net income / total shares was used. 68 lines had Yahoo EPS over 30% off net income / shares (e.g. Eni, Orange, Ferrovial, Philips, Nifco 7988, Konami 9766): P/E uses net income / shares, flagged. 26 more differ by over 25% across currencies (e.g. Unilever, BP, Aena, Amundi): flagged.
- TTM EPS vs last fiscal year EPS outside 0.6-1.6x for 123 lines (e.g. Rakus 3923 1.85x, PARK24 4666 2.68x, Novartis, Shell, Eni): one-offs or Yahoo booking a cumulative quarter as one quarter (CHANGES.txt item 6); these are WATCH until checked against filings.
- Annual EPS history not split-adjusted for 46 lines (e.g. Safran, Ericsson, TUI, Monte dei Paschi, Puig): those years were rebuilt from net income at the latest EPS/NI ratio.
- Prices: Yahoo daily bars for Japan, Hong Kong, Singapore and Taiwan ended 13 Nov; the 16 Nov close from Yahoo info was added. Dividend-adjusted closes were replaced by split-only closes for the valuation history (dividend adjustment lowers old prices and makes today look dear). 39 lines had a one-day move over 40% in 3 years (e.g. Holcim -48% on 16 Jun 2025, the Amrize spin-off; Orsted -45% on 8 Sep 2025, the rights issue): trend may be distorted.
- No valuation history for 2 GEL reporters (Lion Finance BGEO, TBC TBCG: no GEL FX series). P/E under 2 dropped as unit errors for 0966.HK and 2343.HK. EVR.L (Evraz, suspended) and SSMXX.AX (a cash fund in the IOZ file) have no data.
- BUY BELOW X far under the price (Neuren 4.20 vs 22.98, BAM 5.31 vs 13.32, Codan 34.46 vs 53.81) means the stock is far above its own-history multiple; read it as "expensive vs its own past", not as a target.
- ROIC uses equity + debt (cash not deducted): net-cash companies otherwise showed ROIC over 500%.
