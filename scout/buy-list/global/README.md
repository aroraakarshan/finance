## Data caveats
- Scores are not comparable across screens: the same company scores 0.730 as ResMed on the ASX and 0.649 as RMD on the NYSE (US rank 42 of 409). The merge order is a rough guide, not a measurement.
- Valuation history: US up to 10 years; world about 3.5 years (Yahoo has 4 fiscal years), so "cheap vs own history" is weaker for the world names.
- Correlation with QCOM: US = daily returns over 2 years; world = weekly USD returns over 52 weeks.
- Prices: US intraday 16 Nov 2026 (21:45 IST), not the close; Japan, HK and Singapore closes of 16 Nov came from Yahoo info because Yahoo's daily bars stopped at 13 Nov.
- Data flags in the 30: Rakus and PARK24 (TTM EPS far above the last FY); Tenet (trailing one-offs); Exelixis (growth from a low base); H&R Block (ROE from tiny equity); Lion Finance (no valuation history).
- BUY BELOW far under the price (BFT, LPP, Neuren) means "expensive vs its own past", not a target.
- Non-US peers in the reasons (Ipsen, IONOS, Dassault, Fresenius, STJ, Amundi) are named by hand, not screened.
- Work file `world/screen_all.csv` holds older values that disagree with world.md (e.g. Visional JPY 10,050 and BUY BELOW 9,092; KDDI JPY 2,850 and BUY); `world/screen_scored.csv` matches world.md and was used here.
