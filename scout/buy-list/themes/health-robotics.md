**How much is already priced in.** Lilly at P/E 46.5 needs about 20% EPS growth a year for 10 years. Divi's at 90 needs 28%, Laurus at 90 needs 28%, Sai Life at 102 needs 30%. Those rates are being met today, but rarely last 10 years. Ypsomed (about 29 on this year's guidance) needs 14%. Bachem (about 29 on its 2026 guidance) needs 14%; on its trailing P/E of 40 it needs 18%. Novo at 11 needs only 4%. 3-year returns [Y]: Lilly +147%, Divi's +232%, Laurus +561%, Neuland +294%, Novo -56%. The supplier run is about 2-3 years old.

**What would kill it**
- Price: Lilly's realised price fell 15% in Q3 and 29% outside the US (S1). Every new price deal (TrumpRx, Medicare at $245) lowers the price per dose, so volume must keep rising faster.
- Pills replace pens: watch the Wegovy pill and Foundayo share of new prescriptions and Stevanato's GLP-1 revenue each quarter. Bad for Ypsomed and West; neutral for peptide API makers.
- Supply quality: Dr Reddy's stopped selling semaglutide in Jul 2026 after API batches failed specification (S5). Watch for any FDA warning letter at Divi's, Laurus or Bachem.
- Capacity glut: Lilly and Novo build in-house (Novo Holdings bought Catalent, Dec 2024, S12). If Bachem's H2 2026 falls short of +35-40%, the order book is not as safe as it looks.
- Next-drug failures: CagriSema rejected; retatrutide safety signals.

**Base rate (drug-maker wave 2017-21, monthly prices [Y]):** Lonza 4.8x at the peak (Aug 2021), then -59%. WuXi Biologics 14.5x, then -92%. Samsung Biologics 6.0x, then -29%. West 5.7x, then -51%. Catalent was taken over in Dec 2024 at $63.50 (S12), below its 2021 high (not re-checked). So 4 of 4 multi-bagged, and 3 of 4 then lost half or more when Covid demand ended.

**How much is already priced in.** Nabtesco (P/E 20.5 trailing, research; 24.3 here used the old EPS 142 instead of 174.5) and Fanuc (25.5) need about 12-13% EPS growth a year, and both are inside their own P/E range of the last 4 years (22-39 and 22-30). Harmonic Drive at P/E 102 trailing (57 on the Yahoo forward EPS) needs 22-30%. Keyence (33) needs 16%. The humanoid premium has mostly gone from Fanuc and Nabtesco, but not from Harmonic Drive.

**What would kill it**
- Humanoid demos without orders: Harmonic Drive has 1 AI-robot customer in mass production (S15). If its US AI-robot orders stop after two quarters of JPY 1.2 bn, the premium goes.
- The chip cycle: Yaskawa and Harmonic Drive say chip-factory spending drives their orders. A chip-spending pause hits these and QCOM together.
- China: cheap Chinese reducers and robots (Leaderdrive, Estun, Unitree at $16,000 a robot, S19) cut prices; China blocked SoftBank from buying ABB's China robot unit (4 Nov 2026, S22).
- Orders turning: watch the JARA quarterly orders (next about late Jan 2027). Two quarters below last year end the upturn.

**Base rate (robot wave Jan 2015 to Dec 2018, monthly prices [Y]; iRobot had no data):** of 10 leaders, 3 reached 3x or more at the peak (Harmonic Drive 4.1x, Yaskawa 3.8x, Intuitive 3.5x), 2 reached 2-3x (Teradyne 2.6x, Keyence 2.4x), and 5 stayed under 2x (Fanuc 1.6x, ABB 1.6x, THK 1.7x, SMC 1.7x, Nabtesco 1.8x). Most peaked in Jan 2018; 7 of the 10 then fell 37-60% by Mar 2020 (Keyence, Intuitive, ABB and Teradyne fell 17-31%). At the end of 2020 only Teradyne (3.1x), Intuitive (3.0x), Keyence and Harmonic Drive (2.6x) were up 2.5x or more; Fanuc, Nabtesco and THK were below their 2015 price. Rethink Robotics, the 2015 cobot start-up, closed in 2018; Universal Robots was sold to Teradyne for $284 m (S23).
