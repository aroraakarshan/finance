## 4. Method

- Deal list: the dealroom.net tracker of pending deals (updated 2026-11-16) [D1], plus searches for UK schemes, Japanese tender offers, HK privatisations, German tender offers, and US votes and tender offers in the window. Each deal's date was checked against a company release, a filing or an exchange notice where one was found (sources below). The Japan list is from tobradar (as of 2026-10-25) [D13]; only Japan deals with dates in the window and a price over Rs 50 are listed.
- Prices and market caps: yfinance closes on 2026-11-17 (Tokyo 2026-11-16), pulled 2026-11-18 with a 0.4 s throttle.
- Spread and annualising: see the header. The payment date is assumed at the end of the guided close period; each row states it.
- Downside if a deal breaks: the price before the bid (Yahoo month-end closes). Implied chance of closing = (price - downside) / (offer - downside): DHER 76% (downside EUR 20.49), EZJ 92% (398p), PTC 85% (USD 144.03).
- SEC EDGAR was not queried directly. US deal facts come from company 8-Ks quoted by secondary sites (stocktitan, boardroomalpha, dealroom); not verified against the primary filing.

### Not verified / open
- Delivery Hero: whether the broker (IBKR) accepts instructions to tender in the additional acceptance period, and its cut-off date; the closing date; whether a separate "tendered shares" line trades after 24 Nov; the size of the remaining antitrust risk (Uber's earlier Taiwan foodpanda deal is a precedent, not checked).
- Integer: the price per share and whether the deal has already closed.
- Eleco: the offer price. Guotai Junan International: the offer price.
- Penumbra: the exact proration of the cash/stock mix (73% cash is approximate [D1]).
- Dates of the STOXX/DAX and Nasdaq-100 December announcements.
- The cause of Rheinmetall's fall in November 2026.
