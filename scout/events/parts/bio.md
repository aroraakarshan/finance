## Method

Base rates (cited in the table):
- Phase III to filing: 57.8% average, oncology 47.5% (lowest), ophthalmology 51.2%; Phase II to III 28.9%;
  filing to approval 90.6% (oncology 92.0%, range 82.5% cardiovascular to 100% allergy). Source: BIO,
  Informa Pharma Intelligence and QLS Advisors, "Clinical Development Success Rates and Contributing
  Factors 2011-2020" (Feb 2021).
- Phase 3 to approval (Wong, Siah and Lo, Biostatistics 2019, 2000-2015 data): 59.0% for all drugs,
  oncology 35.5%, orphan drugs 46.7%.
- First-cycle FDA decision: FDA CDER "First-Cycle Complete Response Rates for CDER NDA and BLA
  Applications, FY2008-2024" (2026): CR rate fell from 47% (FY2008) to about 27% (FY2024); median
  first-cycle approval about 64% over the whole period, about 70% in recent years.
- AdCom: FDA decisions agreed with panel votes about 78% of the time (Zhang AD et al, Milbank Quarterly
  2019). Not re-checked this session; treat as approximate.
- CHMP meeting dates (7-10 Dec 2026, 25-28 Jan 2027, 22-25 Feb 2027) are taken from merlintrader.eu (15 Nov
  2026), which quotes EMA; I did not open the EMA calendar itself.

How I sized moves: from real price moves in 2026 (yfinance closes):
- Phase 3 wins in small caps: SLS +156% (16 -> 17 Nov 2026, final close 19.15), KOD +178% (25 -> 28 Sep 2026), VIR +12%
  (6 -> 9 Nov 2026, mid cap, then gave it all back).
- Failures: TENX -90% (7 -> 10 Aug 2026, LEVEL missed), MLTX -90% (26 -> 29 Sep 2025, VELA), CAPR -79%
  around its AdCom (24 -> 30 Jul 2026).
- FDA rejections: CORT -50% (30 -> 31 Dec 2025), SMMT -16% (13 -> 16 Nov 2026, CRL 13 Nov), and approvals
  often sell off in larger names (CYTK -5% after its 14 Nov 2026 approval).
- Population data: pdufa.bio study of 1,752 readouts (2022-2026): median move 3.8%; 7.6% fell 30%+ and
  3.1% rose 50%+; micro caps fell 30%+ 11.7% of the time vs 3.9% for large caps; no systematic run-up
  before readouts (median D-30 to D-1 -0.07%) or PDUFA dates (+0.43%).
