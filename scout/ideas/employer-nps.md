- Idea: opt in to employer NPS at the cap-fit rate, about 12.3% of basic = Rs 31,663/month =

  year at 35.88%; take-home falls about Rs 2,43,628. Keep VPF at 0. PPF was declined by the user on

- Tax answer (finance-tax, 2026-11-15; numbers checked by the scout in `tax/FY2026-27/employer-nps.md`

  - Employer NPS: up to 14% of basic + DA in the new regime (2025 Act s.124(1)-(2), s.202(2)(a)(xii)).
  - Rs 7.5 L cap on employer PF + NPS + superannuation (AF s.17(1)(h)); employer PF 12% of basic =
    about Rs 3.70 L a year from FY2027-28 (Rs 3,34,816 in FY2026-27), no superannuation -> room about
    Rs 3.80 L (12.3%). 14% (Rs 4,31,712) saves no more: Rs 51,756 over the cap is taxed back.
  - FY2026-27 if it starts with Jan 2027 payroll: 3 months at 14% = Rs 1,07,928, tax saved about
    Rs 38,725.
  - VPF: no deduction in the new regime (AF s.123 old regime only); interest on employee
    contributions above Rs 2.5 L a year is taxed at slab; employee PF is already Rs 3,34,816 in
    FY2026-27, so extra VPF earns about 8.25% x 0.6412 = 5.3% after tax. Keep at 0.
  - Checked: 3,79,956 / 30,83,664 = 12.3%; 3,79,956 x 0.3588 = 1,36,328; 7,50,000 - 3,70,044 =
    3,79,956. Match. Note: the cap-fit figure uses the full-year basic; at the FY2026-27 projected
    basic (Rs 27,90,115) employer PF is Rs 3,34,816 and the room would be larger.
